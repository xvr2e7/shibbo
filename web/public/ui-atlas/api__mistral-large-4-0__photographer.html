01 — The eye behind the lens

I believe the most beautiful photographs are the ones you didn't pose for. My work lives in the in-between moments — a trembling hand, a held breath, a laugh that escapes.

Based in Paris and endlessly travelling, I photograph weddings with an editorial eye and a documentary heart. No stiff poses, no forced smiles — just your day, exactly as it felt, preserved on fine-art film and digital.

When I'm not shooting, you'll find me developing film in my darkroom, drinking too much espresso, or planning the next road trip with my partner and our two dogs.
