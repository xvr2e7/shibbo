The Album

Four years in the making, Static Bloom is Hollow Wire's most expansive record yet — ten tracks that move between distorted shoegaze walls and quiet, close-mic'd confessionals. Recorded in an abandoned grain silo outside town, the album leans into the room's natural decay.

About the Band

Formed in a basement in 2018, Hollow Wire started as a bedroom-recording project between two friends trading cassette tapes. Six albums later, the band — now a tight four-piece — has built a reputation for turning noise into something tender.

Static Bloom marks a turn inward: fewer walls of distortion, more room left to breathe. "We wanted the silence to do some of the work this time," says vocalist/guitarist Rowan Tate. "The silo gave us that for free."

The band is currently based out of a converted warehouse practice space, where they're already demoing material for the follow-up EP.
