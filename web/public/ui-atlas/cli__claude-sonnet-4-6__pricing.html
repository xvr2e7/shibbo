Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans at any time? −

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your workspace settings. When upgrading, the difference is prorated immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

What happens when my free trial ends? +

Your workspace is automatically moved to the Free plan after the 14-day trial — no credit card needed to try. Your data is fully preserved. If you choose not to upgrade, you'll simply be limited to Free plan quotas.

How does per-seat billing work? +

You're charged only for active members — people who have sent at least one message in the billing period. Deactivated or invited-but-not-joined accounts never count toward your seat total.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education? +

Yes — verified nonprofits and accredited educational institutions receive 50% off Pro and Business plans. Reach out to our support team with documentation and we'll apply the discount to your account.

Is my data secure and compliant? +

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We are SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Business and Enterprise plans add audit logging, data residency options, and DPA agreements.

Can I export my data if I leave? +

Workspace admins on Pro and above can export all messages and files at any time from Settings → Data. The export is delivered as a standard JSON archive. We'll retain your data for 90 days after cancellation before deletion.
