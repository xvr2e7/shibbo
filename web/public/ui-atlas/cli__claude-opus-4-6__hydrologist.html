About

LC

Dr. Lane Carver is a hydrologist and writer based in the Pacific Northwest. They study river geomorphology at the watershed scale — how channels migrate, how floodplains store water, and how decades of infrastructure reshape the systems we depend on. Their essays explore the science and politics of rivers for a general audience. Lane holds a Ph.D. in Earth Sciences from Oregon State and has spent field seasons on rivers from the Mekong to the Missouri.
