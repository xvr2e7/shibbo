Why teams keep it

Less triage. More signal.

Meaning-based clustering

“Can't log in,” “SSO loops,” and “Okta bounced me” land in one theme, even when no two customers use the same words.

Trend, not just totals

Each theme is compared to its own baseline, so you see what's new or accelerating instead of what's always been noisy.

Weighted by revenue

Sync plan and ARR from Stripe, HubSpot or Salesforce. Ten tickets from enterprise accounts can outrank a hundred from free users.

Receipts for every claim

Each number links to the tickets behind it. Click any theme and read the original conversations in your helpdesk.

Privacy by default

Names, emails and card numbers are redacted before analysis. SOC 2 Type II, EU data residency, and no model training on your data.

Lands where you work

FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else? Write to hello@relay.example and a human will answer.

How accurate is the theme grouping?

Product leads rate 92% of themes as accurate in our customer surveys. You can merge, split or rename any theme and Relay remembers those corrections the following week.

What happens to sensitive customer data?

Personal data like names, emails, phone numbers and payment details is redacted before analysis. Your tickets are never used to train models, and you can delete all synced data at any time.

Do I have to tag or clean up tickets first?

No. Relay works on the raw conversation. If you already use tags, it can use them as a hint, but it never depends on them.

Can I change when the report arrives?

Yes. Pick any day and time, and a different channel for each team. Scale customers can also get a daily digest for launch weeks.

What if I have fewer than 100 tickets a week?

Relay still works, but themes will be smaller and the report shorter. Many early-stage teams use it to make sure the first 50 customers are really heard.
