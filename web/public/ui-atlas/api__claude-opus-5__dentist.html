Frequently asked questions

Do you take my insurance? +

We're in-network with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, Guardian and Regence, and we happily bill most out-of-network PPO plans. Send us your plan details when you book and we'll verify your benefits before your visit — no surprises.

What happens at a first appointment? +

Plan on about 60–75 minutes: a health chat, digital X-rays, an intraoral camera tour so you can see what we see, a gum health check, and a cleaning if your gums are healthy. You'll leave with a written plan and cost estimate.

I'm genuinely anxious about dental work. +

You're in good company — roughly a third of our patients tell us the same. We offer numbing gel before injections, headphones and blankets, hand-signal breaks, nitrous oxide, and oral sedation for longer visits. Tell us on the form and we'll book extra time.

Can I pay over time? +

Yes. We offer 0% interest plans through CareCredit and Cherry for 6–24 months, plus an in-house membership plan ($29/month) that covers two cleanings, exams, X-rays and 15% off treatment for uninsured patients.

What are your hours? +

Monday–Thursday 8am–6pm, Friday 8am–3pm, and two Saturdays a month 9am–2pm. Existing patients can reach our on-call line any evening for emergencies.
