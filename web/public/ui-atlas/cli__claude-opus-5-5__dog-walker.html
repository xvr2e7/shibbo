FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Are you insured and background-checked?

Yes. We carry full pet-care liability insurance and bonding, and every walker passes a background check and holds a current Pet First Aid & CPR certification.

How do you handle house keys and access?

Keys are coded (never labeled with your address) and stored securely. We also work with lockboxes, smart locks, and garage codes — whatever works best for you.

Do you walk dogs in groups?

Quick Strolls may include up to 3 compatible dogs if you opt in. Adventure Walks are always one-on-one. Reactive or nervous dogs always get solo walks.

What happens in bad weather?

We walk rain or shine! In extreme heat, cold, or storms we shorten the walk and add indoor play and enrichment so your pup still gets attention.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule free with 24 hours' notice. Same-day cancellations are charged at 50% of the visit rate.

How do I pay?

We invoice weekly and accept all major cards, Venmo, and Zelle. Recurring clients can set up autopay.
