04 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Write to us — a real human replies, usually within a few hours.

Do other people on the call know I'm using Murmur?

Murmur records from your device, so no bot joins the meeting. We do recommend letting participants know — and you can turn on an automatic courtesy message in chat if you'd like.

Is my audio used to train AI models?

Never. Your recordings and notes are encrypted, belong to you, and are not used to train any models — ours or anyone else's.

Does it work for in-person conversations?

Yes. Open the mobile app, set your phone on the table, and Murmur will handle speaker separation for rooms of up to 12 people.

Can I edit the summaries?

Every note is a fully editable document. Rewrite, highlight, add images, or ask Murmur to rephrase a section in a different tone.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep everything. Export your notes as Markdown, PDF or Docx any time, and your account drops to the free Whisper plan.
