How it works

From ticket pile to product clarity in three steps

Set it up once in about five minutes. After that, Relay works quietly in the background.

Connect your helpdesk

One-click integrations with Zendesk, Intercom, Help Scout, and Front. Relay starts reading new tickets immediately and backfills the last 90 days for context.

Relay finds the themes

Tickets are clustered into plain-language themes — bugs, feature requests, confusion points — and tracked week over week so you can see what's rising and what's fading.

Your report arrives Monday

A prioritized summary lands in Slack and email: top themes, trend changes, representative customer quotes, and suggested next actions. Readable in four minutes.

Why teams choose Relay

Built for product teams, not support ops

Relay isn't another analytics dashboard. It's the weekly briefing that keeps your roadmap honest.

◎

Themes, not tag soup

No manual tagging taxonomy to design or maintain. Relay names themes in plain language and merges duplicates automatically.

↗

Trends that matter

Every theme is tracked week over week, so a quiet issue becoming a loud one gets flagged before it hits your churn numbers.

❝

Real customer voices

Each theme comes with representative quotes, linked back to the original tickets, so debates end with evidence instead of opinions.

Revenue-weighted priority

Connect your CRM and Relay weights themes by the accounts behind them — ten tickets from your biggest customer aren't the same as ten from free trials.

✓

Closes the loop

⬡

Fits your workflow

Reports arrive in Slack, email, or Notion. Push any theme to Linear or Jira as a ticket with all the supporting evidence attached.
