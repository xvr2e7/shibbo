Why Urban Forestry Matters

Cities are often "heat islands," where concrete and asphalt trap heat, making urban areas significantly hotter than surrounding rural areas. This affects health, energy costs, and quality of life.


UrbanCanopy partners with local governments and community leaders to identify the "greenest needs" of a city, ensuring that the most vulnerable neighborhoods get the shade and air filtration they deserve.

🌡️

Cooler Streets

Strategic canopy cover can reduce surface temperatures by up to 20°F.

🌬️

Cleaner Air

Trees filter particulate matter and absorb harmful pollutants from traffic.

🧠

Mental Wellness

Access to green space is proven to lower stress and anxiety levels in city dwellers.
