Our Story

A table set with memory & spice

In 1987, Nadia and Georges Khoury left their hometown of Tripoli with a suitcase, a cedar wood mortar, and every recipe Georges's mother had ever whispered to them. They opened a small takeaway counter, feeding fellow newcomers with kibbeh and fattoush until the line stretched around the block.

"Teta always said the secret is saffron, patience, and feeding people as though they are family — because they are."

Today their daughter Lara runs the kitchen with the same wood-fire taboun oven her father built by hand. Nothing is frozen. Nothing arrives in a bag. The pomegranate molasses is still reduced on a Tuesday morning, just like it always was.

— Nadia, Georges & Lara Khoury

The Experience

More than a meal

We believe dinner should feel like you have been welcomed into a home — loud, generous, unhurried, and full.

🫒

Family Recipes

Every dish traces back to Teta Mariam's handwritten notebook. No shortcuts, no outsourcing.

🔥

Wood-Fire Taboun

Georges built our taboun oven himself in 1991. It burns cedar offcuts and gives everything that irreplaceable char.

🌿

Seasonal & Local

We source produce from two local farms and the Lebanese spice importer our family has trusted since the beginning.
