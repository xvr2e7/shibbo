Why Ceramic

Crafted for the Perfect Brew

Thermal Stability

Stoneware retains heat beautifully, keeping your brew temperature consistent throughout the entire pour — no thermal shock to your coffee.

Hand-Thrown Form

Each dripper is individually thrown on a pottery wheel by skilled artisans, resulting in a unique object that becomes more personal with every use.

Taste Neutral

Unlike plastic or metal alternatives, fired ceramic is completely taste-neutral and non-porous, so nothing interferes with the true flavor of your coffee.

Precision Ribs

Spiral interior ribs guide water flow uniformly, creating optimal contact time between water and coffee grounds for even extraction.

Heirloom Quality

High-fire stoneware is built to last generations. With proper care your dripper will develop a subtle patina and become even more characterful over time.

Sustainably Made

Our studio runs on 100% renewable energy. Clay is locally sourced and all packaging is fully recyclable or compostable.

The Ritual

How to Brew

1

Rinse & Preheat

Place a paper filter, pour hot water through to rinse, and let it drain to preheat the ceramic and mug.

2

Add Coffee

Use 15–17g of freshly ground coffee (medium-fine) per 250ml water. Level the bed gently.

3

Bloom

Pour 2× the coffee weight in water (30ml) and wait 30–45 seconds for the grounds to degas and bloom.

4

Pour & Enjoy

In steady concentric circles, pour the remaining water over 2–3 minutes. Lift the dripper and savor.
