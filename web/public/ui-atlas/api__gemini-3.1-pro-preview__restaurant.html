Our Story

From Beirut to Your Plate

Founded in 1998 by the Haddad family, Al-Arz Heritage began as a small dream to bring the vibrant, rich flavors of Lebanon to our new home. The name 'Al-Arz' translates to 'The Cedar', a symbol of resilience, eternity, and our deep-rooted heritage.

Every dish we serve tells a story. From our hand-rolled stuffed grape leaves to our slow-roasted shawarma, we use recipes passed down through three generations. We believe in fresh ingredients, generous portions, and treating every guest like family.
