A few things you might be wondering

Questions?
We’re all ears.

Still curious? Call or text (555) 013-7297. A real human will happily help.

What happens at the free meet & greet?

We’ll visit you at home for about 20 minutes, get to know your dog, review their routine, and collect entry details. You can ask questions, show us their favorite walking gear, and make sure everyone feels comfortable. There’s no commitment.

Will my dog walk with other dogs?

Only if you’d like them to. We offer solo walks and carefully matched buddy walks with a maximum of two compatible dogs. We consider temperament, size, energy level, and walking style before making any introductions.

Do you walk in bad weather?

Usually, yes! We walk in light rain and cold weather with appropriate breaks and paw care. During extreme heat, storms, ice, or unsafe air quality, we’ll contact you to shorten the outing or switch to indoor play and enrichment.

Can you handle puppies, seniors, or reactive dogs?

Absolutely. Our VIP Walk is designed for dogs who need extra patience or a predictable one-on-one routine. We’ll discuss triggers, mobility, training goals, and any medical needs during your meet & greet.

How do scheduling and cancellations work?

You can request recurring walks or book as needed, subject to availability. We’ll confirm your schedule directly. Cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours’ notice at no charge; later cancellations are billed at 50%.
