Details

Each dripper is slip-cast from high-fired stoneware, glazed by hand, and kiln-fired twice at 1240°C. The sixteen interior ribs run in a gentle spiral so the paper filter lifts away from the wall, letting the bed breathe and water flow evenly. One wide outlet keeps your pour, not the brewer, in control of the flow rate.

Care

Dishwasher safe on any rack. Avoid pouring boiling water into a dripper straight from the freezer or a cold car, since fast temperature swings stress any ceramic. Glaze variation and tiny pinholes are normal and part of the piece.

Why ceramic

Thick walls hold heat. Heat holds flavor.

Thin plastic and metal brewers lose temperature fast, which pulls your extraction toward sour. Our stoneware wall is 6 mm thick. Pre-warm it with a rinse and it holds within a few degrees of your kettle through the whole brew.

Every dripper is made in a small studio in Oregon. The same two hands glaze each one, which is why no two are exactly alike.

Design

Small decisions, better cups

We spent two years brewing with every cone dripper we could find before shaping this one. These are the details that matter.

Spiral ribs

Sixteen raised ribs twist gently toward the outlet, keeping the filter off the wall so air escapes and water moves through the whole bed, not just the center.

Single wide outlet

A 22 mm opening means the dripper never restricts flow. Pour faster for a lighter cup, slower for more body. The control is yours.

Thermal mass

Six millimetres of stoneware holds brew temperature steady from first bloom to last drip, so your coffee tastes the same on a cold morning as a warm one.

Steady base

A wide 90 mm foot sits securely on mugs, servers, and carafes from 70 to 95 mm across. Three small notches let you see the level below.

Hand-applied glaze

Each piece is dipped and brushed by hand. Expect subtle pooling at the rim and tonal shifts across the cone. That's not a flaw. That's a signature.

Standard filters

Fits any #2 cone filter (Size 01) or #4 cone filter (Size 02). Paper, cloth, or metal, use what you already own.

Brew guide

Our house recipe

A simple, forgiving method that works for most light and medium roasts. Adjust grind first, then ratio.

Size 01 · One cup

Coffee18 g
Water300 g
Ratio1 : 16.7
Water temp94°C / 201°F
GrindMedium-fine, like coarse sand
Total time2:45 – 3:15
  1. 1

    0:00

    Rinse and warm

    Set a filter in the dripper and pour hot water through it. This removes paper taste and, more importantly, warms the ceramic. Discard the rinse water.

  2. 2

    0:00 – 0:45

    Bloom

    Add 18 g of coffee, level the bed, and pour 40 g of water in a slow spiral. Give the dripper a gentle swirl to wet every grain. Wait until bubbling slows.

  3. 3

    0:45 – 1:30

    First pour

    Pour steadily to 180 g in slow circles the size of a coin, staying off the paper. Keep the water level roughly even, about two-thirds up the cone.

  4. 4

    1:30 – 2:00

    Second pour

    When the bed drops to a quiet surface, pour to 300 g. Finish with a small swirl to knock grounds off the wall and flatten the bed.

  5. 5

    2:45 – 3:15

    Drawdown

    Let it drain fully. You're looking for a flat bed with no high-and-dry grounds. Too fast and sour? Grind finer. Too slow and bitter? Grind coarser.

Questions

Good to know

Anything else? Write to us at hello@kilnandco.example and a real person replies within a day.

Which filters fit?

Size 01 takes any standard #2 cone filter. Size 02 takes a #4. Both work with paper, cloth, and reusable metal cones. We include twenty unbleached paper filters to get you started.

How is this different from a V-shaped plastic dripper?

Geometry is similar, so your existing recipes transfer. The differences are thermal mass, which keeps brew temperature stable, a slightly more open outlet, and ribs that spiral rather than run straight. Most people find the cup a little sweeter and more consistent.

Will it fit my mug or carafe?

Size 01 sits securely on openings from 70 to 95 mm, which covers nearly every mug and most small servers. Size 02 fits 80 to 115 mm. Measure the inner diameter of your vessel's opening if you're unsure.

Is the glaze food safe?

Yes. All five glazes are lead- and cadmium-free, fired to full maturity, and tested by a third-party lab to FDA and California Prop 65 limits.

What if it breaks?

If it chips or cracks in normal use, ever, we replace it. Send us a photo and we'll ship a new one. If it arrives damaged, same thing, no need to send the broken piece back.
