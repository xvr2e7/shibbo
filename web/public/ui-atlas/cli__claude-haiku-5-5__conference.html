About the conference

Built for people who ship interfaces.

Frontend Tejo brings together practitioners for honest talks, hands-on workshops and long conversations over pastéis de nata. No product pitches on stage, just what we learned building real things.

⚡

Performance

Rendering strategies, bundle budgets, Core Web Vitals in the wild and what actually moved the numbers.

🧩

Systems & Architecture

Design systems at scale, component APIs, monorepos, and keeping frontend codebases maintainable for years.

♿

Accessibility & Craft

Inclusive interaction design, semantic HTML, motion, typography and the details that make software feel right.

FAQ

Good questions.

Will talks be recorded?

Yes. Recordings of all main-stage talks are shared with ticket holders within two weeks of the event.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes, and it is enforced. Everyone attending is expected to treat others with respect. Reports go to the organizing team in confidence.

Can I get a refund or transfer my ticket?

Tickets can be transferred to another person free of charge up to 7 days before the event. Refunds are available until 31 March 2027 minus a processing fee.

Do you offer invoices or group discounts?

Yes. We issue invoices with VAT for companies. Groups of five or more get 15% off; email the team to arrange it.

What about dietary needs and accessibility?

Please note any requirements during registration. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and halal options are provided, and we are happy to arrange further support.

See you by the Tagus.

Early bird pricing ends 31 January 2027. Limited seats for the workshops.
