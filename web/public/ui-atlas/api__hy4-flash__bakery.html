Our craft

Slow bread for people in a hurry to eat it.

There’s no secret trick here. We use a wild starter, local stone-milled flour, good salt and a long, cold ferment. The rest is patience — something we’ve learned cannot be rushed, even when the line starts forming at 7:10.

Every loaf is shaped by hand and baked in small batches. That means a crackly crust, a soft interior, and a neighbourhood that knows exactly when the oven door opens.

Mix

Flour, water, salt and starter. Nothing clever. Everything necessary.

Fold

Gentle stretches build strength without knocking the air out.

Rest

A long cold proof develops flavour, texture and that familiar tang.

Bake

Steam first, colour second. Out by dawn, on your table by breakfast.
