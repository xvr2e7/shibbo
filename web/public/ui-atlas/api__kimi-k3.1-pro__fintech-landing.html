Good questions

A little more
clarity.

Still wondering about something? Talk to a real human.

Do we need to combine our finances?
Not at all. Tandem works whether you share every account, only split rent, or fall somewhere in between. You decide which accounts, budgets, and transactions belong in your shared space.
Can I keep some purchases private?
Yes. Personal accounts and purchases stay private unless you choose to share them. Your partner will see shared balances and plans, not a play-by-play of everything you buy.
Do we have to connect our bank accounts?
No. You can securely connect eligible accounts for automatic updates, or add balances and transactions manually. Both of you can choose a different setup.
What if our incomes or arrangement change?
Change your split rules, contribution amounts, or shared budgets whenever you need. Tandem is designed to evolve with new jobs, new homes, growing families, and everything else life brings.
Can we cancel during the free trial?
Anytime, in just a couple of taps. Your 30-day trial doesn’t require a credit card, so there’s no surprise charge waiting at the end.
