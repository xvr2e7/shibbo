What’s included

One Terra stoneware pour-over dripper and a naturally renewable paper filter. Brew guide and care instructions are included in the recyclable packaging.

Size & material

Approximately 14 cm in diameter and 13 cm tall. Made from high-fired, food-safe stoneware with a lead-free matte glaze.

How to brew

Rinse the filter, add 20 g of medium-fine coffee per 300 ml of water, and pour slowly in concentric circles. Begin with a 45-second bloom.

Care & cleaning

Allow the dripper to cool before washing. It is dishwasher safe, though hand washing will preserve the matte glaze for longer. Avoid sudden temperature changes.
