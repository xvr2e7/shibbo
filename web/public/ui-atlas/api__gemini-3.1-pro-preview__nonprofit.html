Why Urban Trees Matter

Trees are not just scenery; they are critical urban infrastructure that saves lives and improves communities.

Combat the Heat Island Effect

City streets can be up to 10°F hotter than surrounding areas. Tree canopies act as natural air conditioning, dramatically cooling neighborhoods.

Clean the Air We Breathe

Urban trees filter out harmful particulate matter and smog, significantly reducing asthma rates and respiratory diseases in densely populated areas.

Foster Community Well-being

Green spaces lower stress, improve mental health, and provide safe, shaded areas for neighbors to gather and children to play.
