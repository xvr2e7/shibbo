Our Mission

We believe every city deserves the beauty and benefits of urban trees. Here's how we're making it happen.

Clean Air Initiative

Trees are nature's air purifiers. Each tree we plant removes up to 48 pounds of CO₂ per year while releasing fresh oxygen into our neighborhoods.

Cooling Communities

Urban heat islands can be 10°F hotter than surrounding areas. Strategic tree planting creates natural shade and reduces temperatures for everyone.

Building Community

Our planting events bring neighbors together, creating lasting bonds while beautifying shared spaces. Together, we're growing more than just trees.

Our Journey

From a small group of passionate environmentalists to a nationwide movement.

2018

Founded in Portland with just 12 volunteers and a dream of greener cities.

2020

Expanded to 15 cities and planted our 25,000th tree despite global challenges.

2023

Reached 48 cities with over 125,000 trees and 15,000 active volunteers.

2025

Goal: 250,000 trees in 100 cities. Join us in making this vision a reality!
