FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How long does it take to set up?
Most teams are live in under 5 minutes. Connect your helpdesk via OAuth, choose your Slack channel or email, and Relay starts analyzing immediately. The first report arrives the following Monday.
Does Relay store my ticket data?
Relay processes tickets in your environment and stores only the aggregated analysis — never raw customer messages. All data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.3 and we're SOC 2 Type II compliant.
Can I customize what goes in the report?
Yes. You can configure custom categories, set priority thresholds, exclude certain ticket tags, and choose which sections appear in your report. Enterprise customers can also define custom AI prompts.
What if I want reports more often than weekly?
Pro and Enterprise plans support daily digests in addition to the weekly summary. You also get real-time spike alerts whenever a new issue suddenly trends above your threshold.
Which AI model powers Relay?
Relay uses a combination of fine-tuned models optimized specifically for support ticket analysis. We continuously improve accuracy using aggregated, anonymized feedback from product teams.
Can I try it before entering a credit card?
Absolutely. The Starter plan is free forever and requires no credit card. The Pro 14-day trial also doesn't ask for payment upfront — we only collect billing info if you choose to continue.
