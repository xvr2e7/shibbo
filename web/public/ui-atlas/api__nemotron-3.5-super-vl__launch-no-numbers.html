Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to use a brand-new app for my books?+
Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. You can export your records anytime as CSV or PDF, so you’re never locked in.
Do I still need an accountant?+
Tally Hall organizes your books so your accountant spends less time cleaning them up—but it doesn’t replace professional tax advice.

Yes. Cancel from your account settings and you won’t be billed again. You keep access until the end of your billing period.
