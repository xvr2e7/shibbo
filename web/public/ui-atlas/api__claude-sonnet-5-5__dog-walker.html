Good questions

Things dog parents ask us.

Anything else on your mind? Send a text and we'll get back to you within the hour.

Do I need to be home for walks?

Nope. Most clients give us a key or lockbox code after the meet & greet. We always lock up behind us and text you when we arrive and leave.

What if my dog is reactive or anxious?

We'll recommend Solo Strolls and take things slowly, with quiet routes and plenty of space. Dogs who don't mix well are never put in a group.

Are my dog's vaccinations required?

Yes. We need current rabies, DHPP and (for group walks) Bordetella records. We'll collect them at sign-up.

What about bad weather?

We walk in rain, snow and cold with proper gear. For extreme heat or dangerous conditions we shorten walks or swap to indoor enrichment, and always tell you first.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule free up to 12 hours before. Later than that, we charge 50% so we can hold the spot for your pup.
