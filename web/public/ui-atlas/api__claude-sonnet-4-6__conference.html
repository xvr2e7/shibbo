About the conference

Frontend at the Edge
of What's Possible

FrontCon Lisbon brings together the brightest minds in frontend development for two intensive days of learning, building, and connecting — all in one of Europe's most beautiful cities.

🎤
World-class Talks
Carefully curated sessions covering React, Web Performance, Accessibility, Design Systems, and the future of CSS.
🛠️
Hands-on Workshops
Small-group workshops led by industry experts. Build real things, ask real questions, leave with real skills.
🌐
Open Source Focus
Meet maintainers of the tools you use daily. Contribute to open source during our dedicated hacking sessions.
🤝
Networking
Structured networking, evening social events, and curated connections that last well beyond the conference.
🏛️
Historic Venue
Held at the stunning Centro Cultural de Belém with views of the Tagus River. Lisbon at its finest.
🌱
Carbon Offset
We offset 100% of the event's carbon footprint. Sustainable by default — because the web should be too.

FAQ

Common Questions

Is there a Code of Conduct?
Yes. FrontCon Lisbon is dedicated to providing a harassment-free conference experience for everyone. All attendees, speakers, sponsors, and staff are required to agree to and follow our Code of Conduct. Violations will be handled swiftly.
Will talks be recorded?
Yes! All main stage talks will be professionally recorded and published freely on our YouTube channel within 2 weeks of the conference. Workshops are not recorded out of respect for participants.
What's the refund policy?
Full refunds are available up to 30 days before the event (August 18, 2025). Between 30 and 14 days before, we offer a 50% refund or a full credit for the following year. Within 14 days, tickets are non-refundable but fully transferable.
Can I get an invoice for my company?
Absolutely. During registration you can enter your company VAT number and billing details. A proper invoice will be automatically generated and sent to you. We're a Portuguese company (NIF available on request).
Are there student discounts?
Yes! Students with a valid student ID can apply for a 60% discount via email at students@frontconlisbon.io. We also have a scholarship program — apply by July 1st.
What about visa support letters?
We're happy to provide official invitation letters for visa applications. Please reach out after purchasing your ticket and we'll send you a signed letter within 2 business days.
Is there childcare available?
We're partnering with a local childcare provider to offer supervised childcare during conference hours. It's free for registered attendees — please indicate your needs at registration so we can plan capacity.
