◦ the method

Four quiet moves.

Murmur does what a good friend with a perfect memory would do: listen first, tidy later, and never interrupt while you're still thinking.

01
capture → compose
01 — capture

Everything lands softly.

Voice memos mumbled on the walk home. Sentences typed one-handed. Screenshots, scraps, half-lyrics. Murmur takes them as they come — timestamped, unjudged, never lost in the couch cushions.

recording — kitchen table 00:14
◉ voice✎ text▣ photo⇪ share-sheet
02 — distill

The quiet edit.

While you move on, Murmur works softly: a two-line summary, honest tags, action items pulled from your “remind-me-tos.” All on-device. Your raw thoughts stay yours — they never leave, never train.

“…onboarding as a conversation, one question at a time, email Priya the numbers before Friday…”

03 — recall

Ask like you'd ask a friend.

No folders. No filing. Just “what did I think about pricing in March?” or “that book June recommended.” Semantic recall finds the note — and the notes it was flying with.

04 — compose

From murmur to manuscript.

When a draft needs to exist, Murmur gathers the threads — three murmurs, one meeting, one dream — and lays them out in order. You write the last mile. It walked the other ninety.
