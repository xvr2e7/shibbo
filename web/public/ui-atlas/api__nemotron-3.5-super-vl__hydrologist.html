Elara Moss

Hydrologist & Essayist — Notes on rivers, flow, and the quiet work of water.

Elara Moss is a hydrologist studying river morphology and groundwater-surface water exchange. When she isn't measuring streamflow or tracing sediment through catchments, she writes essays about the stories rivers carry in their currents, banks, and beds.

She holds a PhD in Water Resources and has worked on mountain headwaters, alluvial floodplains, and urban watersheds. Her writing has appeared in The Drift, Current Affairs Quarterly, and regional watershed newsletters.
