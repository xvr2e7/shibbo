Our family story

Bayt means home.

In our Beirut kitchen, dinner began long before anyone sat down. Someone was picking parsley, someone was squeezing lemons, and someone was tearing the first piece of warm bread.

Since opening Bayt Olive in 1998, our family has kept that rhythm. The table is a little bigger now, but the welcome hasn’t changed.
