What it does

Just the books. None of the busywork.

We built Tally Hall for people who'd rather be doing the work than tracking it. Here's what you get on day one.

Sort expenses fast

Connect your bank or upload a CSV. Tally Hall suggests a category for each transaction. You confirm it with one tap.

A running tax estimate

See an estimate of what to set aside each time you get paid, so a big bill in April doesn't catch you off guard.

Simple invoices

Send clean, professional invoices and mark them paid. Income shows up in your ledger automatically.

Receipts in one place

Snap a photo or forward an email. Each receipt gets attached to its transaction, so it's there when you need it.

Reports in plain words

Profit and loss, income by client, spending by category. Each report is written so a non-accountant can follow it.

Your data, portable

Export everything to CSV or PDF whenever you like, whether it's for your accountant or for you.

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

You just launched. Is it safe to trust you with my finances?

It's a reasonable worry. Bank connections run through an established third-party provider, so we never see or store your banking password. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. You can export everything or delete your account at any time.

Does Tally Hall file my taxes?

No. Tally Hall keeps your books organized and gives you a set-aside estimate, but it doesn't file returns or give tax advice. You get clean records and reports that you or your accountant can use at tax time.

Who is it for?

Solo freelancers and independent contractors such as designers, writers, developers, consultants and photographers. If you have employees or run payroll, a fuller accounting package will probably suit you better.

What happens when my trial ends?

We'll email you before it ends. If you don't subscribe, your account becomes read-only and you can still export everything. You won't be charged unexpectedly.

Can I import my old spreadsheet?

Yes. Upload a CSV and map the columns. We'll help you sort it out if it's messy.
