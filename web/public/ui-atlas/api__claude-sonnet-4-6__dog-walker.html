About Me

Hi! I'm Jamie, Your Dog's New Best Friend

I've been a lifelong dog lover with over 5 years of professional pet care experience. I treat every dog like my own — with patience, kindness, and a whole lot of tail wags.

I'm fully insured, certified in pet first aid, and passionate about keeping dogs happy, healthy, and well-exercised. When I'm not on a walk, I'm volunteering at the local shelter.
