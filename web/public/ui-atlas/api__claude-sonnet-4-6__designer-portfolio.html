How I Work

My Design Process

Every great product starts with understanding people. Here's how I turn ambiguous problems into clear, beautiful solutions.

01
🔍
Discover

Deep dive into user research, stakeholder interviews, competitive analysis, and data to uncover real problems worth solving.

02
💡
Define

Synthesize research into clear problem statements, opportunity areas, and design principles that guide every decision.

03
✏️
Design

Explore a wide solution space through sketches, wireframes, and high-fidelity designs that balance beauty and usability.

04
🚀
Deliver

Prototype, test, iterate, and hand off pixel-perfect specs with thorough documentation to ensure flawless implementation.

About Me

Crafting experiences, not just interfaces

Hey! I'm Alex, a product designer based in San Francisco. I believe the best designs are invisible — they just work, feel intuitive, and bring a little joy to everyday tasks.

I've had the privilege of working with early-stage startups finding their footing, to global companies scaling their products to millions. My work spans mobile apps, complex SaaS platforms, and comprehensive design systems.

When I'm not pushing pixels, you'll find me hiking in Marin, obsessing over specialty coffee, or mentoring junior designers.
