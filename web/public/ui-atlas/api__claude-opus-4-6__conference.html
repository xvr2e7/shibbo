Centro de Congressos de Lisboa

Situated along the banks of the Tagus River in the vibrant Belém district, the Centro de Congressos de Lisboa offers a stunning, modern venue with panoramic river views — the perfect backdrop for two days of frontend excellence.

Free high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the venue
1,000+ seat auditorium with modern A/V
Dedicated workshop rooms with power at every seat
10-minute tram ride from city center

Lisbon Airport (LIS) is about 25 minutes by taxi/Uber from the venue. You can also take the Metro (Red Line) to Oriente, then the Tram 15E to Belém. We'll send detailed travel guides to all attendees before the event.

Full refunds are available up to 60 days before the event. 50% refunds up to 30 days before. After that, tickets are non-refundable but fully transferable to another person.

We've partnered with several hotels in the Belém and Santos area for discounted rates. Ticket holders will receive a discount code for partner hotels. We recommend booking early — September is peak season in Lisbon!
