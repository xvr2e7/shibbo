FAQ

Questions, answered

Which helpdesks does Relay support?

Relay has native integrations for Zendesk, Intercom, Front, and Help Scout. If you use something else, you can import tickets via CSV or our open API.

Do I need to tag tickets before Relay can read them?

No. Relay clusters tickets by root cause automatically using the ticket content itself. Existing tags and categories are used as a bonus signal, not a requirement.

How is this different from a helpdesk's built-in analytics?

Helpdesk analytics tell you volume and response time. Relay tells you what customers are actually struggling with and asking for, grouped and prioritized in plain language — the thing product teams actually act on.

Is my ticket data used to train any models?

No. Your data is processed to generate your reports and is never used for training. You can request full deletion at any time.

Can I customize what goes into the report?

Yes. On Growth and Enterprise plans you can set custom clustering categories, exclude certain ticket types, and choose which channels receive the report.
