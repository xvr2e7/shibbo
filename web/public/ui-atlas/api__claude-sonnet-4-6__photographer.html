About Me

A Storyteller
with a Camera

Hello, I'm Evelyn — a documentary wedding photographer based in New York City, available worldwide. I believe that weddings are living, breathing stories, and my role is simply to witness and preserve them with honesty and artistry.

My style blends photojournalism with fine art portraiture. I work quietly in the background, capturing candid emotions as they unfold, while also creating beautifully crafted portraits that you'll treasure for generations.
