№ 03 — What it does

Four things,
done properly.

No dashboards you’ll never open. No “AI insights.” Just the books — ruled, dated, and honest.

3.1

Income that files itself

Connect your bank, Stripe, or your invoicing email. Every payment lands as a line — labeled, categorized, dated — before you’ve closed the tab.

Bank sync · Stripe · PayPal
3.2

Tax day, demoted

With every payment, Tally Hall skims off what you’ll owe — self-employment tax included — so the number never surprises you. April becomes a formality, not a fire drill.

Set-aside, updated per payment
3.3

Receipts become records

Photograph a receipt; it reads itself, matches its expense, and stops living in your pocket or your junk drawer.

Photo in → ledger line out
3.4

The monthly square-up

One page, first of each month: earned, spent, set aside, kept. Your accountant gets clean CSV or PDF exports — or nothing at all, if you’d rather keep it between us.
