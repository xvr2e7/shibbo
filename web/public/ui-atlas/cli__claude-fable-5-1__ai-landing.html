Features

Capture without friction. Retrieve without searching.

Murmur does the organizing so you can keep thinking. Every note is understood, linked, and ready to come back the moment it becomes relevant.

Speak it, see it

Voice memos become clean, punctuated notes in seconds, with filler stripped and the key decisions pulled to the top.

Threads, not folders

Murmur quietly links notes that belong together, across months and formats, so ideas grow into something instead of getting buried.

Right thought, right time

Open a doc, start a meeting, or revisit a project, and the notes that matter resurface on their own. Nothing to remember to look for.

Ask your notes anything

Plain questions, honest answers, every claim cited back to the exact note it came from. If Murmur doesn’t know, it says so.

Private by default

End‑to‑end encrypted at rest, processed on-device where possible, and never used to train anyone’s model. Your notes stay yours.

How it works

Three habits, zero maintenance.

Capture anywhere

Type, dictate, clip a page, or forward an email. Murmur lives on your phone, desktop, and browser, and everything lands in one quiet stream.

Let it settle

Overnight, Murmur reads what you captured, finds the threads, and surfaces open loops. You wake up to a tidier mind, not a to‑do list.

Think with it

Ask questions, draft from your own words, or just notice what comes back. The more you capture, the more it has to offer.
