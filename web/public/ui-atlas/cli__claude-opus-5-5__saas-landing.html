What's in the report

Signal your roadmap can trust.

Every number in a Relay report traces back to real conversations. When someone asks "says who?", you click once and show them.

Trends, not snapshots

Themes persist week over week, so you can see what's rising, what's new, and whether last sprint's fix actually reduced tickets.

Quotes with receipts

Representative customer quotes for every theme, each linked to the original ticket, so nothing gets paraphrased into mush.

Weighted by revenue

Sync your CRM and Relay ranks themes by the ARR behind them, not just volume. Forty enterprise seats outweigh a few loud free users.

Spike alerts

Don't wait for Monday when something breaks. Relay pings you mid-week when a theme doubles in a day.

One click to the backlog

Send a theme to Linear, Jira, or GitHub Issues with the summary, impact, and source tickets already filled in.

Private by default

Names, emails, and card numbers are redacted before analysis. SOC 2 Type II, EU data residency, and your data never trains shared models.

Questions

Good things to ask before you connect.

Do we need to tag or categorize our tickets first?

No. Relay builds themes from the content of the conversations themselves. If you already use tags, Relay can read them as an extra signal, but nothing breaks without them.

How accurate are the themes?

Every theme shows its source tickets, so you can check any of them in a click. You can also merge, split, or rename themes, and Relay remembers your edits in future reports.

What happens to customer data?

Personal information is redacted before analysis. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in your chosen region, and is never used to train shared models. You can delete everything from settings at any time.

Can we change when and where the report is sent?

Yes. Choose the day, time, and timezone, and send to any mix of Slack channels, email lists, and Notion pages. You can also run an on-demand report for any date range.

What if we go over our ticket limit?

Relay keeps reading. We'll let you know and suggest a plan change at the end of the month. We never silently drop tickets from your report.
