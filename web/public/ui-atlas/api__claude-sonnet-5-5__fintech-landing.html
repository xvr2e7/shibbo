FAQ

Good questions, honest answers.

Can my partner see everything I spend?

Only if you want them to. Every account and category is set to Mine, Yours, or Ours. Private items never appear in your partner's view, and shared totals show only what you've chosen to share.

Do we both need to pay for Tandem?

No. One subscription covers both of you. Invite your partner for free and you'll each have your own login.

Is it safe to connect our bank accounts?

Yes. Connections are read-only and encrypted, so Tandem can never move your money. We use industry-standard aggregation partners and never sell or share your data.

What if we aren't married or don't live together?

Tandem works for any two people sharing money: dating, engaged, married, or roommates. You decide what's shared.

Can I cancel anytime?

Of course. Cancel in two taps from Settings. Your data stays exportable for 90 days after.
