FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Still curious? Email us at hello@tandem.money. A real human replies.

Can my partner see all my transactions?

Only if you want them to. Every account and category has its own visibility setting: fully shared, totals only, or completely private. You're always in control.

Do we need a joint bank account?

Nope. Tandem works whether you share one account, keep everything separate, or have a mix. It tracks who paid for what and settles up fairly.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. We use 256-bit encryption, read-only connections through trusted partners, and never sell your data. Tandem can't move your money. Ever.

What if we break up?

We hope it doesn't happen, but if it does, either of you can leave the shared space at any time. Your private data stays yours, and shared history can be exported.

Does it work for roommates or families?

Tandem is designed for two partners, but plenty of roommates use it too. Family plans for up to five people are coming in 2025.
