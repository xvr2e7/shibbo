About Brightside

Dentistry that feels like coming home.

Brightside opened in 2011 with a simple idea: dental care should feel human. We're a family-owned practice that takes time to listen, explain every option, and never rush your visit.

Our state-of-the-art office uses digital imaging, low-radiation X-rays, and modern tools to keep appointments efficient and comfortable.

  • Transparent, itemized estimates before any treatment
  • Sterilization that meets and exceeds state standards
  • Evening and Saturday hours for busy schedules
