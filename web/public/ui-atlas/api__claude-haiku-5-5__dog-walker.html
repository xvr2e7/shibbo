FAQ

Questions, answered

Are your walkers insured and background-checked?

Yes. Every walker passes a criminal background check, reference verification, and pet first-aid training. We're fully bonded and insured for your peace of mind.

How do I get access to my home?

You can share a key, a lockbox code, or a smart-lock PIN. All access details are stored securely and only shared with your assigned walker.

What happens if my dog has an emergency?

Our walkers carry pet first-aid kits and are trained to respond to emergencies. We'll contact you immediately and, if needed, take your dog to your chosen veterinarian or the nearest emergency clinic.

Can you walk dogs that don't get along with others?

Absolutely. We offer solo walks for dogs that are reactive or anxious, and group walks are only arranged after a temperament assessment during the meet-and-greet.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel or reschedule any walk at least 4 hours before the start time at no charge. Late cancellations within 4 hours are billed at 50% of the walk price.

Do you offer discounts for regular clients?

Yes! Recurring bookings save 10%, and clients who refer a friend receive a free 30-minute stroll after their friend's first paid walk.
