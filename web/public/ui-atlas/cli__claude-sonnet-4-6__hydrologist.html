Mara Linden

Rivers in Motion

Hydrology, landscape, and what water remembers.

I study how rivers move through the land — how they carve, deposit, flood, and find new channels. I'm a research hydrologist at the Mountain West Water Institute, where my work focuses on fluvial geomorphology and the long-term effects of climate on drainage networks.

Alongside the fieldwork, I write essays that try to hold the science and the strangeness of rivers together in the same sentence. I'm drawn to places where a river has changed course, where it refuses to stay put, where it asks us to pay closer attention than we thought we needed to.

I live in Missoula, Montana, two blocks from the Clark Fork.
