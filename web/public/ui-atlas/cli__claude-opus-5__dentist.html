Do you take my insurance?
We're in-network with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, Guardian, Regence, and Ameritas, and we file claims as a courtesy for every other PPO plan. Send us your member ID through the form below and we'll verify your benefits — usually within one business day — before your first appointment.
What if I don't have dental insurance?
Two options. Our $89 new-patient visit covers exam, X-rays, and a cleaning. Or join the Rivermark Membership at $32/month, which includes two cleanings, exams, X-rays, and 20% off everything else — no deductibles, no annual maximum, no claim forms.
Can I pay over time?
Yes. We offer interest-free in-house payment plans for treatment over $500, and we're approved for CareCredit and Sunbit if you'd prefer longer terms. We'll lay out the monthly figure alongside your estimate so there's nothing to work out later.
I'm genuinely afraid of the dentist. What can you do?
More than you'd expect. You can come in just to meet your dentist and sit in the room with nothing happening. We use topical numbing before every injection, agree on a hand signal to stop at any point, and offer nitrous oxide or oral sedation for anything from a cleaning upward. Tell us when you book and we'll schedule extra time.
How quickly can I be seen for pain?
We hold emergency slots every weekday. Call before 3pm and you'll almost always be seen the same day. Evenings and weekends, the main line routes to the on-call dentist — a real person, not a message service.
How often should I come in?
Most people do well on six-month cleanings. If you have gum disease, a history of decay, diabetes, or you smoke, we'll often recommend every three or four months. Your hygienist sets the interval with you — it's not a sales script.
Where do I park?
Free lot behind the building, entered from NW 5th. There's also street parking on Thorne and we're a four-minute walk from the Thorne & 8th streetcar stop. The entrance and both restrooms are step-free.
