How it works

Three steps. Then it runs itself.

Most freelancers are fully set up before their coffee gets cold.

01

Read-only · two minutes

Connect your accounts

Securely link your bank, Stripe, and PayPal in about two minutes. The connection is read-only — Tally Hall can see transactions but can never touch your money — and you can revoke it at any time.

02

Photo · email · browser

File receipts your way

Snap a photo, forward an email to your Tally Hall address, or let the browser extension catch online orders. OCR reads the merchant, date, and total — you just tap to confirm. Every correction teaches it your habits.

03

Always on

Your books close themselves

Invoices, expenses, mileage, and a 25% tax set-aside are sorted the moment money moves. When quarter-end arrives, everything is already organized — one click exports a clean packet for your accountant.
