Nice to meet you

Hello, I'm Elara

A storyteller at heart, with a camera in hand

For over eight years, I've had the honor of documenting love stories across the Pacific Northwest and beyond. From misty forest elopements to grand ballroom celebrations, I believe every couple deserves photographs that feel like coming home.

My approach is simple: I create a space where you can be completely yourselves. The stolen glances, the happy tears, the uncontrollable laughter—these are the moments I live for. I'm not here to pose you into perfection; I'm here to capture your perfectly imperfect love.

When I'm not behind the lens, you'll find me hiking with my golden retriever, Luna, planning my next adventure, or curled up with a good book and an oat milk latte.

— Elara
