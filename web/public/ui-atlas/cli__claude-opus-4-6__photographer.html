About

Hello, I'm Elena

I'm a natural-light wedding photographer based in Northern California, drawn to the quiet, in-between moments — a stolen glance during the first look, the way afternoon light catches a veil, the laughter between posed portraits.


My approach is unhurried and documentary-led. I blend into your day, creating space for genuine emotion while crafting images with a fine-art sensibility — soft, luminous, and timeless. Every couple deserves photographs that feel like coming home.

The Process

From inquiry to heirloom

Say Hello

Share your story, your date, and your vision. I'll reply within 48 hours with availability and a tailored proposal.

Let's Connect

A relaxed video call or coffee date to make sure we're a perfect fit — personality matters as much as portfolio.

Plan & Prepare

A detailed timeline consultation, a venue walkthrough when possible, and an optional engagement session to get comfortable in front of the lens.

Your Wedding Day

I arrive early, stay late, and move gently through your day — capturing real moments with an artist's eye.

The Reveal

Within 6–8 weeks, your private gallery is ready — a curated collection of 500+ images, each one hand-edited in my signature warm, luminous style.
