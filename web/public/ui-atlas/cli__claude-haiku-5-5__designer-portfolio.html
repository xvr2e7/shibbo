About

Clarity is a craft.

I believe the best products disappear. My work sits at the intersection of research, interaction, and visual design, with a focus on making hard decisions feel simple.

Before going independent, I led design teams at two early-stage startups and a mid-sized software company. I'm happiest when I'm close to users, close to engineers, and close to the messy middle of a problem.

Outside of work I sketch, cook, and collect old camera manuals. I'm currently exploring how AI tools can make design research more humane.

How I work

A simple, honest process

Every project is different, but the rhythm stays consistent: understand, explore, build, learn.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Interviews, data review, and stakeholder workshops to find the real problem worth solving.

  2. 02

    Explore

    Rapid sketches and low-fidelity flows that test many directions before committing to one.

  3. 03

    Refine

    High-fidelity design, prototypes, and close collaboration with engineering on every detail.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Launch with clear success metrics, then iterate based on what real people actually do.
