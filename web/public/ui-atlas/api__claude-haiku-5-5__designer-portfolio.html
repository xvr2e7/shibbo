About me

I care about the details people feel but rarely notice.

I started as a visual designer, then fell in love with the messy middle of product work: edge cases, permissions, empty states, the moment before someone gives up. That's where I spend most of my time now.

My process blends research, rapid prototyping, and close collaboration with engineering. I believe the best design is the one that quietly removes friction, and I'm happiest when a team can ship faster because the path is clear.

Outside of work you'll find me restoring vintage typewriters, cycling along the coast, or badly singing karaoke.

How I work

A process built for momentum.

Every project is different, but the rhythm usually follows these four phases. Each ends with something a team can react to.

01

Understand

Interviews, data review, and stakeholder workshops to define the problem before touching a pixel.

02

Explore

Rough sketches and flows, shared early and often. I aim for many wrong answers before the right one.

03

Prototype

Interactive prototypes tested with real users, so decisions rest on behavior rather than opinion.

04

Deliver

Specs, components, and close pairing with engineers through build, QA, and launch.
