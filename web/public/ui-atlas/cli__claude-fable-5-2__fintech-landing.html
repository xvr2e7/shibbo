How it works

Set up tonight. Sorted by Sunday.

Questions

The things couples ask first.

Do we need a joint bank account?

No. Twofold works with whatever you have: fully joint, fully separate, or somewhere in between. You choose which accounts and purchases count as shared.

Can my partner see everything I spend?

Only what you mark as shared. Private pockets and personal accounts are visible to you alone, and your partner sees nothing about them: no amounts, no shop names, no hints.

What if we earn very different amounts?

That's what the by-income split is for. Each of you contributes the same percentage of your take-home, so the amounts differ but the effort matches. You can also set any custom ratio you both agree on.

Is our financial data safe?

Bank connections are read-only, so Twofold can never move your money. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we don't sell it to anyone.

What happens if we split up?

Either of you can leave a shared budget at any time. You each keep a full copy of your own history, and private pockets stay private.
