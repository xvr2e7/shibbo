A little taste of home
The heart of our kitchen

It all started at
Sitti's table.

“Sitti” means grandmother in Arabic. Ours believed that the best way to tell someone you love them was to feed them—then insist they have a little more.

Today, we carry that same spirit into everything we make. From the first squeeze of lemon to the last warm piece of pita, this is food made to be passed around, lingered over, and remembered.
