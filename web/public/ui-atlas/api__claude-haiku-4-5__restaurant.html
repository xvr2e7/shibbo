About Al-Zaytoun

Our Story

For nearly 30 years, Al-Zaytoun has been serving authentic Lebanese cuisine to our beloved community. Founded by the Khalil family, our restaurant is built on a foundation of tradition, quality ingredients, and genuine hospitality.

Every dish is prepared with love and respect for Lebanese culinary heritage. We use only the finest ingredients, many sourced directly from Lebanon, to ensure that every bite transports you to the Mediterranean.

Our restaurant isn't just a place to eat—it's a gathering place where families come together to share meals and create memories, just as families do in Lebanon.
