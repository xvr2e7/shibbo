About

One walker. Every time.

Long Lead is a one-person business, which means your dog isn't handed to whoever's on shift. They get the same familiar face at the door, someone who knows that they hate the vacuum, love the ginger cat at number 12, and need a minute before the harness goes on.

I keep groups small, routes varied and the pace relaxed. Walks are for the dog, not the stopwatch.

FAQ

Good questions.

What happens when the weather is bad?

We walk in rain, drizzle and cold, and your dog gets towelled off at the door. In extreme heat, storms or ice, walks are shortened or swapped for an indoor play-and-toilet visit, and I'll always message you first.

How do you get into my home?

Most clients provide a spare key or a lockbox code at the meet & greet. Keys are coded rather than labelled with your address, and stored securely.

Will my dog be let off the lead?

Only with your written permission, only in safe enclosed areas, and only once I've seen reliable recall for myself. On-lead is the default.

My dog doesn't get on with other dogs. Is that OK?

Absolutely. That's exactly what Solo Strolls are for. We'll pick quieter routes and times, and go at whatever pace keeps your dog comfortable.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel by 6pm the evening before and there's no charge. Same-day cancellations are billed at 50%, because that slot can't be offered to anyone else.
