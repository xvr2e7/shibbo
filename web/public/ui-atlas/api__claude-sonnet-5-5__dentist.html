Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Call us and a real human will pick up.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! We welcome new patients of all ages. Most new patients can be seen within a week, and often sooner if you’re in pain.

I’m nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. A large number of our patients feel the same way. We go at your pace, explain everything, and offer nitrous oxide or oral sedation for anxious patients.

How often should I get a cleaning?

Most people do well with a checkup and cleaning every six months. If you have gum disease or other risk factors, we may recommend every three to four months.

Do you take my insurance?

We’re in-network with most major PPO plans. Send us your plan details and we’ll verify your benefits before your visit. Without insurance, our in-house membership plan saves up to 20%.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call (503) 555-0142 right away. If a tooth is knocked out, hold it by the crown, rinse gently and keep it in milk until you reach us. Time matters.
