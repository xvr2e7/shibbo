Candid answers to skeptical questions

Is my bank data safe with a brand-new company?

We never see or store your bank login credentials. Bank connections run through Plaid (the same infrastructure used by Venmo and Wise) with strictly read-only tokens. Prefer not to link a bank at all? Tally Hall works 100% via drag-and-drop CSV/OFX statements.

What happens if Tally Hall shuts down in six months?

First: we’re profitable at 42 customers because we have no office and no VC burn rate. Second: your data isn't trapped in a proprietary format. Every screen has a "Download Full Archive (.ZIP)" button that exports clean CSV ledgers and organized receipt PDFs named by date and vendor.

Who is Tally Hall NOT for?

If you have W-2 employees on payroll, manage physical warehouse inventory with COGS, or file as a multi-partner S-Corp requiring complex balance sheet audits, don't use us. We built Tally Hall specifically for Schedule C sole proprietors and single-member LLCs.

Can I backfill the rest of this tax year?

Yes. When you connect an account or drop in a CSV, you can pull transactions all the way back to January 1st. Most freelancers catch up on an entire quarter of neglected bookkeeping in about 15 minutes using bulk vendor rules.
