Everyone belongs here

Good company, by design.

Frontrow is dedicated to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

Be kind. Respect boundaries and different perspectives. Ask before photographing others. Harassment, discrimination, and intimidation are not welcome, at the venue or in our online spaces.

If you need support or notice an issue, speak to any event team member or contact ola@frontrowconf.com. Organizers may remove attendees who violate these expectations.
