A shady street shouldn’t be a privilege. But in too many cities, your zip code determines how much nature you get to enjoy.

We’re here to change that. Since 2016, Groundwork has worked alongside local neighbors to bring trees, care, and connection to overlooked urban spaces. Because greener neighborhoods are stronger neighborhoods.
