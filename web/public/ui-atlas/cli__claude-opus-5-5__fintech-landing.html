FAQ

Questions, answered honestly.

Still curious? Our support team is made of real humans — several of them happily coupled.

Do we need a joint bank account? +

Nope. Twofold works whether you share everything, nothing, or something in between. Many couples use it with entirely separate accounts and just track shared costs.

Can my partner see all my spending? +

Only what you choose to share. Accounts and categories are private by default — you decide what moves into "Ours." Your partner will never see private transactions.

How does income-based splitting work? +

Enter your take-home pay (or let us estimate it from synced deposits) and Twofold calculates a proportional split. If one of you earns 60% of household income, you'd cover 60% of shared costs.

Is my financial data secure? +

Yes. We use 256-bit encryption, read-only bank connections, and two-factor authentication. We're independently audited and we never sell your data to anyone.

What happens if we break up? +

It happens, and we've made it painless. Either partner can unlink at any time. Your personal data stays yours, shared history is exported to both, and any open balances are shown clearly so you can settle up.
