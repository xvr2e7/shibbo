// FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Relay decide what counts as an issue?

Relay classifies each ticket by intent (bug, request, friction, or praise) and clusters similar tickets together. A theme is only reported when it crosses a volume threshold, so one-off complaints don't create noise.

Does Relay read our customers' personal information?

Relay removes names, emails, phone numbers, and payment details before analysis. Quotes in reports are redacted by default. You control retention, and you can delete your data at any time.

Do you train AI models on our data?

No. Your ticket data is used only to generate your reports. It is never used to train shared models.

How accurate is the classification?

In internal benchmarks, Relay matches human labels about 92% of the time. You can correct any classification in the dashboard, and those corrections improve future reports for your workspace.

Can I change the day or recipients of the report?

Yes. Choose any day and time, add as many recipients as you like, and send to channels in Slack or pages in Notion.
