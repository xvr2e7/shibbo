Why we exist

Every neighborhood deserves shade, clean air, and green space.

Urban tree cover isn't just beautiful — it lowers summer temperatures, cuts energy bills, reduces flooding, and improves mental health. Yet low-income neighborhoods have, on average, 30% less tree canopy than wealthier ones.

We're closing that gap, one block and one community partnership at a time.

  • Free trees & planting for residents and schools
  • 3-year watering & care program for every tree planted
  • Paid training & green job pipelines for local youth

What we do

Programs that put down roots

From sidewalks to schoolyards, we design every planting with the neighborhood — not just for it.

01

Street Tree Planting

Free native street trees for homeowners and renters, matched to sidewalk conditions and utility lines.

02

School Greening

Transforming asphalt schoolyards into shaded outdoor classrooms and gardens for students.

03

Community Orchards

Fruit and nut trees on public land, cared for and harvested by the surrounding community.

04

Green Jobs Training

Paid arborist and landscaping apprenticeships for local young adults, building long-term careers.

Get involved

How a tree gets planted

Simple, transparent, and built around your neighborhood's needs.

1

Request a Site

Residents or community groups submit a planting request for their block or school.

2

Site Assessment

Our arborists survey the space and pick the right species for the spot.

3

Community Planting Day

Volunteers and neighbors plant together, trained by our field crew.

4

3 Years of Care

We water, mulch, and monitor every tree until it's established.
