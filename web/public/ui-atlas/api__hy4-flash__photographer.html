Meet Mara

A photographer who believes in feeling the day.

Hi, I’m Mara. I photograph weddings for couples who value beauty but care more about connection. My approach is editorial in feeling and documentary at heart, with an eye for the soft, unscripted moments that become the ones you return to most.

Based in Florence and traveling wherever love takes me, I create images that feel timeless rather than trendy—warm, layered, and full of life.
