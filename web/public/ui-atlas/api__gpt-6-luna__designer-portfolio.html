A little about me

Good design starts with good questions.

I’m Mara, a product designer and curious collaborator based in Brooklyn, NY. For the last 9 years, I’ve helped small teams and big-hearted organizations make digital products that are easier to understand—and a joy to use.

My practice moves between product strategy, research, and hands-on design. I care about the details, but I’m always looking at the bigger picture: who we’re designing for, what they need, and whether we’re making something genuinely worth having.
