How It Works

We’re a one-oven operation. Here’s how to make sure you go home with bread.

1

We bake at dawn

Dough shaped the night before goes into the oven at 5 am. The whole block smells it by 7.

2

Doors open at 8

Come early for the full lineup. Regulars bring coffee and chat in the queue — it’s half the fun.

3

Gone by noon

When the shelf is empty, we flip the sign, sweep the flour, and start tomorrow’s levain.

🍞
Our story

It started with one odd loaf.

In 2019, our first attempt at sourdough came out lopsided, scorched on one end, and — somehow — the best bread we’d ever eaten. We baked another. Then ten. Then the neighbours started knocking.

Seven years later we’re still on the same corner, still using the same starter (her name is Doris), and still refusing to bake more than our two hands can shape. Every loaf is a little different. That’s the point.

— Sam & Rio, bakers & co-owners
