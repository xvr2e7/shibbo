Which neighbourhoods do you cover?

We walk all over Oakwood, Riverside, the North End and the western edge of Maple Hill, plus a few streets just over the line into Fairview. If you're within roughly 10 minutes of Alder Street, we'll say yes. Ask about group walks and we'll also head to the fenced park off 9th.

Are your walkers insured and background-checked?

Yes. Every walker is covered by general liability and pet-liability insurance, is CPR and first-aid certified, and passes a criminal background check plus a reference check with two previous clients before they walk a dog solo.

What if my dog is anxious or reactive?

We start with a free behaviour consultation and a meet-and-greet. Reactive dogs are walked solo on quieter routes, at a distance from other dogs, with a slower pace — and we'll tell you honestly if we're the right fit. We never use aversive methods, and we're happy to work alongside your trainer.

How do group walks work?

We match dogs by size, energy, age and temperament, and cap groups at three. If your dog doesn't click with the group, we move them to solo walks — no awkwardness, no extra charge.

What happens in bad weather?

Light rain and snow are fine — we'll add a waterproof layer and booties if your dog wears them. For heat above 80°F or severe storms we reschedule to a cooler slot at no charge, and you can pause your plan entirely.

Do I need to be home?

No. We use your key, a lockbox or a garage code, and our walkers are photographed and ID-badge carrying for your peace of mind. We also keep your door code secure and never share it with other clients.

How do I book and what does it cost?

Fill in the form on this page or call us. Your first meet-and-greet is free, and standard walks start at $22 for a 20-minute break, $32 for a 45-minute Adventure Walk or $48 for a 90-minute Extended Explore. Payment is per walk, no membership required.
