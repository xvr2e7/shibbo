Our Mission

A city isn't complete until everyone has shade.

Urban heat islands disproportionately affect lower-income neighborhoods, where concrete outnumbers canopy by 10 to 1. We're changing that ratio.

By working directly with local residents, we don't just drop saplings in the ground—we ensure every tree has a neighborhood ready to help it thrive for generations.

  • Cooler Streets

    Strategic planting reduces surface temperatures and lowers summer energy bills.

  • Cleaner Air

    Mature canopies naturally filter particulate matter and absorb ambient CO₂.

  • Stronger Roots

    Paid local youth crews lead the planting and long-term maintenance.
