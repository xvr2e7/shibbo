The problem

Your backlog is arguing
with your inbox.

Support holds the most honest signal your company produces. It arrives in 400-word fragments, at 2am, buried in threads nobody on the product team will ever open.

01 / SIGNAL LOSS

The insight dies in the queue

An agent tags a ticket, resolves it, and moves on. The pattern that would have changed your roadmap stays locked in a spreadsheet nobody reads.

02 / MANUAL LABOUR

Someone spends Friday copying rows

Every week, a PM or support lead reconstructs the same report by hand — cherry-picking tickets, guessing at volume, and shipping a doc that's out of date on arrival.

03 / NO EVIDENCE

Prioritisation becomes opinion

Without counts, quotes and trend lines, the loudest stakeholder wins. Then the team ships the wrong thing and wonders why the tickets keep coming.

Inside the report

Not a dashboard.
An editor's briefing.

Relay doesn't hand you more charts to interpret. It writes the conclusion first, then shows the evidence — themes, volume, segment impact and the customer's own words.

  • Ranked themes with ticket counts, week-over-week movement and severity weighting.
  • Automatic merge of near-duplicate issues across languages and phrasings.
  • New-theme alerts the moment volume crosses your threshold.
  • Tie each theme to plan tier, MRR at risk and churn-conversation frequency.
  • Segment splits for enterprise, self-serve and trial users side by side.
  • Regression flags when a shipped fix stops working.
  • Hand-picked verbatims that carry the emotional weight of the theme.
  • Redaction of personal data before anything leaves your helpdesk.
  • One click from quote to source ticket for full traceability.
