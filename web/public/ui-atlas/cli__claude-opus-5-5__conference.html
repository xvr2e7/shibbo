Why Cascata

A conference that flows like a good stylesheet.

Modern CSSPerformanceAccessibilityDesign systemsJavaScript & frameworksBrowser APIsToolingMotion

Cascata is a small, independent, single-track conference for people who build for the browser. No parallel rooms, no fear of missing out — everyone sees every talk, and every talk is worth seeing.

We keep talks to 30 minutes, leave long breaks for real conversations, and end each day with something properly Lisboeta: sunset on the river, sardines, and a little too much vinho verde.

The venue

A former warehouse on the river.

Armazém 7 is a restored 19th-century riverside warehouse in Marvila, Lisbon's creative east side — high ceilings, tiled walls, a terrace facing the Tagus, and step-free access throughout.

MetroRed line to Chelas, then a 12-minute walk downhill (or our free shuttle every 15 min).
TrainBraço de Prata station is 6 minutes on foot.
Airport~15 minutes by taxi or ride-hailing from Humberto Delgado (LIS).
StayAttendee rates at three partner hotels — details in your ticket email.

Good to know

Perguntas frequentes.

What language are the talks in?

All talks and workshops are in English, with live captions on the main screen for every session.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes. Armazém 7 is step-free, with an accessible toilet on every floor, a quiet room, reserved seating near the stage and induction loops. Let us know your needs when booking and we'll take care of it.

Do you offer support or diversity tickets?

We set aside 10% of all tickets for free and discounted places for students, people from under-represented groups in tech, and anyone for whom cost is a barrier. Applications open in December.

Can I get an invoice for my company?

Absolutely — enter your company details and VAT number at checkout and a valid EU invoice is issued automatically. Group rates start at 5 tickets.

What's your refund policy?

Full refunds until 1 April 2027. After that, tickets are fully transferable to a colleague at no cost.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes, and we enforce it. Cascata is dedicated to a harassment-free experience for everyone. Our safety team is on site and reachable by phone throughout both days.
