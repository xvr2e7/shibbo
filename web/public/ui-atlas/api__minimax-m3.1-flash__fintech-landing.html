FAQ

Questions, answered

Still curious? Our support team is made of humans who like spreadsheets a little too much. Write to hello@hearth.app.

Do we both need our own account?

Yes — and it's the point. Each of you gets a private login, and you link your accounts together into one shared household budget. You both see the same picture, and you can each add expenses, set a private category, or snooze a reminder without the other seeing a thing.

How does the bill splitting actually work?

Tap a receipt, and Hearth suggests a split based on what you usually order, who's home more, or a straight 50/50 — you decide. It handles unequal contributions, rounding, shared subscriptions and one-off splurges. Everything is adjustable before it posts.

What if we have very different incomes?

Plenty of couples do. Hearth lets you set a shared spending pot funded proportionally to income, or equally — whichever feels fairer to you. There's no default assumption about whose money is "ours", and no percentage-of-income warnings we don't ask for.

Is my financial data safe?

Connections are read-only and encrypted with 256-bit AES, secured with bank-grade tokenisation. We never sell your data, and we don't run ads. You can revoke access or delete your account at any time, and we'll wipe your data within 30 days.

Can I use Hearth for just one account?

Absolutely. Plenty of people start solo, add their partner later, or keep Hearth purely as a shared goal tracker. The free plan works on its own for as long as you like.

What happens after the free trial?

You'll drop to the free Kindling plan — no card charged, no features disabled mid-month. Upgrade whenever you want, and we'll prorate instantly. Yearly billing saves you 30%.
