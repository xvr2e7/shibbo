Hydrologist & Essayist

I trace the invisible lines of watersheds and measure the pulse of flowing water. By day, I model groundwater recharge and streamflow dynamics; by night, I write about the rivers that shape our landscapes and our imaginations.

These essays are field notes from the confluence of science, history, and mud.
