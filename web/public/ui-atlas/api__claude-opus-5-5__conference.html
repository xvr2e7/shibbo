Why Calçada

Small enough to talk. Big enough to matter.

Lisbon’s pavements are hand-laid, one limestone cube at a time. We think the web deserves the same care. Calçada is a single-track conference with no sponsor pitches, generous breaks and long lunches — because the best ideas usually arrive over a pastel de nata.

  • 01

    One track, no FOMO

    Every attendee sees every talk. Conversations at lunch start from shared ground.

  • 02

    Platform first

    Talks focus on durable web skills over framework-of-the-month hype.

  • 03

    Hands-on Day 2

    Afternoon workshops in rooms of 25, run by the speakers themselves.

Venue

A former military bakery by the river

Hub Criativo do Beato is a converted 19th-century industrial complex in Lisbon’s eastern waterfront — high ceilings, brick walls and light pouring in from the Tagus. Fifteen minutes from the airport, ten from the historic centre.

Hub Criativo do Beato

Rua da Manutenção 71, 1900-500 Lisboa, Portugal

FAQ

Good questions

Can’t find what you need? Write to ola@calcada.dev — a human answers within a day.

What language are the talks in?

All talks are in English, with live captions displayed on screen throughout both days.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes. We fund 40 free tickets for people from groups underrepresented in tech, including travel support for 10 of them. Applications open in February.

Is there a code of conduct?

Absolutely. Every attendee, speaker, sponsor and volunteer agrees to it. Trained staff are reachable in person and by phone at all times.

What about food and dietary needs?

Lunch is fully vegetarian with vegan and gluten-free options, cooked by a local Marvila kitchen. Tell us about allergies at checkout.

Can I transfer or refund my ticket?

Transfers are free until the day before. Full refunds are available until 1 April 2026.

Will talks be recorded?

Yes — ticket holders get recordings within two weeks; they go public on our channel a month later.
