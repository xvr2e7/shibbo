Frequently Asked Questions

Have a specific inquiry? We're here to help.

Can I mix and match tiers between departments?
Workspace tiers apply to all active accounts within an individual workspace. However, multi-workspace organizations on our Enterprise plan can provision different workspaces with custom policies suited to individual teams.
How does billing work when we add or remove seats mid-cycle?
We employ a fair-billing policy. If you add seats halfway through your month or year, you’ll be billed pro-rata only for the fraction of time those seats were active. If users are deactivated, credit is automatically rolled over to future cycles.
Are guest accounts counted against our billable seats?
Single-channel guests (e.g. freelance contractors or clients joined to a single topic room) are 100% free up to 5 guests per paid seat on Pro and Business+ plans. Multi-channel guests are billed at standard user rates.
Can we import our existing chat history from Slack or Discord?
Yes! Our automated migration assistant lets you import team channels, message history, users, and attachments from Slack or Discord zip exports with one click.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits and educational institutions?
Absolutely. We provide up to an 85% lifetime discount for registered 501(c)(3) nonprofits and accredited universities. Reach out to our community relations squad with your documentation to apply.
