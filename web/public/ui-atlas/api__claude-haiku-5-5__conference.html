About the conference

Built by people who ship the web every day.

Frontend Tejo is a community-run conference for developers, designers and engineering leads who care about the quality of what reaches the browser. No vendor keynotes, no product pitches, just practical knowledge from people in the trenches.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Write to hello@frontendtejo.example.

Do I need to attend both days?

Passes cover both days. You can attend a single day at a reduced rate if you contact us before 1 November.

Are the talks in English?

Yes. All sessions are in English with live captions. A small number of community lightning talks are in Portuguese and Spanish, with English summaries posted in the app.

What is included in the workshops?

Workshops are 75 minutes and run in small groups of up to 30 people. Bring a laptop with a modern browser installed. Materials are provided.

Can I get a refund or transfer my ticket?

Tickets are transferable to a colleague at no charge. Full refunds are available until 31 October, less a 10% processing fee.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes. Every attendee, speaker and sponsor agrees to our code of conduct. Incidents can be reported at the registration desk or through the conference app at any time.

Do you offer scholarships or financial aid?

We reserve 30 places for under-represented groups in tech, covering the Community pass. Applications open in August on this site.

Stay in the loop.

Speaker announcements, early-bird reminders and the full schedule, once a fortnight. No spam, and you can unsubscribe in one click.
