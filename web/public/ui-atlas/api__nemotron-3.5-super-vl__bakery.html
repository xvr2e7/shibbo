A tiny oven on the corner.

Odd Loaf started as a weekend experiment in a converted garage. Now it’s a neighbourhood ritual: sourdough milled fresh each morning, shaped by hand, and baked in a wood-fired oven that smells like home.

We use heritage grains, a 40-year-old starter, and no commercial yeast. Every loaf ferments for at least 18 hours. It’s not fast bread—it’s good bread.
