The studio

A practice built like a quiet studio, not a waiting room.

We see fewer patients each day on purpose. Appointments start when they say they will. Music is low. Explanations come before instruments. If something can wait, we will tell you.

  • ✓ Digital records you can take with you — nothing held hostage.
  • ✓ Nitrous available, never pushed. Anxiety is treated as clinical information.
  • ✓ A single fee schedule, posted. Insurance estimates before you sit down.
  • ✓ Evening hours Tuesdays, for people who cannot leave work at two.
