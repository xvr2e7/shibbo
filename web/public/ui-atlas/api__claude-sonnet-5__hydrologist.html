About

I'm a hydrologist who spent a decade measuring discharge in places most maps leave blank, and I still think the best instrument for understanding a river is a slow afternoon on its bank.

My essays sit somewhere between field notebook and letter — half data, half the strange tenderness that comes from watching the same water never arrive twice. I write about flood pulses, sediment memory, dams, deltas, and the rivers that no longer reach the sea.

Ph.D. Hydrology, formerly with river basin research in the Mekong and Mississippi watersheds. Based near a river, naturally.
