FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Relay replace our support tool?

No. Relay sits on top of the tools you already use. It reads from Zendesk, Intercom, Front, Help Scout and email, then writes the weekly product report. Your team keeps working in the inbox they know — they just stop doing manual triage on Mondays.

What about customer privacy and PII?

Relay is read-only and never writes to your support tool. Customer contact details are stripped at ingestion and replaced with stable pseudonyms, so you can share a report externally without exposing an email address. Team and Scale plans support SSO, configurable retention and EU data residency.

How accurate is the clustering?

On a typical support week, teams see 90–95% of tickets assigned to a correct theme. Relay always shows its work — every theme links to the tickets behind it — so a human can reassign a misfiled ticket in one click and the model learns from the correction.

Can we control the tone and structure?

Yes. Bring your own template, or start from Relay's default structure (summary, top themes, what changed, roadmap suggestions, appendix). You can set the tone of voice, the audience — support, product, exec — and even the vocabulary your team uses internally.

When does the report get delivered?

Whenever you want. Most teams pick Monday at 8:00 AM local time, but weekly, fortnightly and monthly cadences are all supported, and you can send an on-demand report the moment you need one before a customer review.

Which languages are supported?

Relay reads and writes in nine languages out of the box — English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Dutch, Japanese, Korean and Swedish — and will report in a different language than your tickets if you prefer.

What happens when we cancel?

Your data is exported as JSON and Markdown reports on request, then deleted from our servers within 30 days. There are no data-retention surprises and no long-term contracts on Team or Scale.
