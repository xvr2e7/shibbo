Rinse Filter

Place filter and rinse with hot water to remove paper taste

Add Coffee

Use 20g of medium-fine ground coffee for 300ml water

3

Bloom

Pour 40ml water in circular motion, wait 30 seconds

4

Pour

Continue pouring slowly in spirals until you reach 300ml

Cleaning
Rinse with warm water after each use. Dishwasher safe on top rack.
Storage
Store in a dry place. Avoid stacking with other ceramics.

Soak in mixture of water and baking soda monthly to remove oils.

Avoid sudden temperature changes. Do not place on direct heat.
