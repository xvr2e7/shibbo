Frontend Conf Lisbon 2024

Two days of talks, workshops, and networking with the best frontend developers in Europe

About the Conference

Join Europe's Premier Frontend Conference

Frontend Conf Lisbon brings together frontend developers, designers, and tech leaders for two days of inspiring talks, hands-on workshops, and meaningful networking.

Whether you're interested in React, Vue, Angular, or emerging web technologies, we have something for everyone.

Join 500+ developers in beautiful Lisbon for the conference that's shaping the future of frontend development.

What to Expect

20+ Expert Talks covering the latest trends and best practices in frontend development.

Hands-on Workshops led by industry leaders where you can learn new skills.

Networking Sessions to connect with fellow developers and companies.

Expo Area featuring the latest tools and services for frontend developers.
