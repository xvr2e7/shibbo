Features

Built for people who talk
faster than they type.

Ambient capture

One hotkey, any app, any device. Murmur buffers the last 30 seconds too, so the idea you had before you started recording isn't lost.

Knows who said what

Diarization that holds up on bad conference audio. Learns voices over time, so attributions and action-item owners land on the right person.

Ask your archive

"What did we decide about pricing in August?" Semantic search across every note, with citations back to the exact minute of audio.

Private by default

Transcription runs on-device. Audio never leaves your machine unless you turn on cloud summaries. SOC 2 Type II, zero training on your data.

Threads, not folders

Murmur links new notes to the conversations they continue, so a project reads as one evolving story instead of forty orphaned files.

Lands where work already lives

Action items become real tickets. Summaries post to the right channel. Notes sync both ways with your existing system — Murmur doesn't ask to be another place you forget to check.
