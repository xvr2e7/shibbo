02 — Method

How a street gets its shade back.

  1. 01

    Survey

    We overlay canopy maps with heat-sensor data to find the city's hottest, barest blocks — then walk them on foot, counting every curb pit a tree could live in.

    Weeks 1–3
  2. 02

    Vote

    Neighbors pick the species for their own street at a block meeting. We bring the horticulture; they bring the stakes.

    Week 4 · Block meeting
  3. 03

    Plant

    Crews cut the pit, break the compaction, amend the soil, and set saplings grown for city stress — salt, drought, exhaust, and the occasional bike lock.

    Spring & fall
  4. 04

    Water

    Two full summers of watering, mulching, and structural pruning by paid neighborhood crews. A tree that makes it to year three will outlive us all.

    Years 1–2 · 92% survival
