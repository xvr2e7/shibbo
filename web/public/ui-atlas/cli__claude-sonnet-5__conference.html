About Edge/27

Built by frontend engineers, for frontend engineers.

FAQ

Good to know.

Is the conference recorded?+
Yes — mainstage talks are recorded and made available to Standard and Team ticket holders within two weeks after the event.
Do you offer diversity scholarships?+
Yes, we set aside 40 free tickets each year for underrepresented groups in tech. Applications open in January 2027.
What language are the talks in?+
All talks are in English. Portuguese-speaking volunteers will be on-site to help with anything else.
Can I get a refund if I can't attend?+
Tickets are fully refundable up to 30 days before the event, and freely transferable to a colleague at any time.
Is there a code of conduct?+
Yes, all attendees, speakers, and sponsors are expected to follow our code of conduct, available on request and at check-in.

Join us on the Tagus this March.
