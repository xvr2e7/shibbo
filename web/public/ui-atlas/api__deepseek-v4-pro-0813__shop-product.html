The Ridge Ceramic Pour-Over Dripper is the result of hundreds of test brews, tweaks to ridge depth, and hands-on ceramic expertise. Each dripper is thrown on a potter's wheel, then sculpted with precise spiral ridges that regulate water flow through the coffee bed for even saturation and a clean, complex cup.

What makes it special

  • Spiral ridge design — promotes even extraction and prevents channeling for a sweeter, more balanced cup.
  • High-fired stoneware — retains heat better than glass or plastic, keeping your brew at the ideal temperature.
  • Reactive glaze finish — every piece is individually hand-glazed, so no two are exactly alike.
  • Double-walled cone — provides insulation and a comfortable, cool-to-touch exterior.
  • Matching serving cup included — perfectly sized to catch exactly 500ml of hot coffee.

Care Instructions

  • Rinse with warm water after each use — no soap needed for daily use.
  • Dishwasher safe on the top rack for deep cleaning.
  • The reactive glaze will develop a subtle patina over time — this is intentional and adds character.
  • Allow to cool before rinsing with cold water to prevent thermal shock.
  • Store with the filter basket removed to allow air circulation.
