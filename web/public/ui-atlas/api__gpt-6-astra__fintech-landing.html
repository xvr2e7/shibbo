Do we need a joint bank account?

Nope. A shared plan doesn’t have to mean a shared bank account. Keep your accounts separate and agree on what’s yours, mine, and ours. This demo lets you explore that shared view without connecting a bank.

What if we earn different amounts?

Fair looks different for every couple. You can plan around proportional contributions instead of splitting everything 50/50. Start with your combined income, then decide together what feels comfortable for each of you.

Can we try it before signing up?

Absolutely. Choose “Try Tandem free” to create a sample budget right here. No email, account, bank connection, or credit card required. Your demo details are kept only for this page session.

Will this move or manage our money?

No. This is an interactive budgeting preview, not a banking service. The numbers help you explore a plan; no funds are moved, no accounts are connected, and no personal information is sent to a server.
