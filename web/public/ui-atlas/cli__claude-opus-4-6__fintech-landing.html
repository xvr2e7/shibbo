\n Features\n

Built for partnerships,
not spreadsheets

\n

Every feature is designed so both partners see the same clear picture — no awkward money conversations needed.

\n
\n
\n
\n
🔗
\n

Linked Accounts

\n

Connect both your bank accounts and credit cards. Transactions flow in automatically and get tagged to the right person.

\n
\n
\n
⚖️
\n

Smart Splitting

\n

50/50, income-proportional, or custom — pick the split ratio that feels fair. Pairwise calculates who owes what instantly.

\n
\n
\n
🎯
\n

Shared Goals

\n

Saving for a trip, a home, or a ring? Create goals together and watch both contributions add up in real time.

\n
\n
\n
💬
\n

In-App Nudges

\n

Gentle reminders for bills, budget check-ins, and weekly spending summaries — delivered as a shared notification, not a nag.

\n
\n
\n
📊
\n

Dual Dashboard

\n

One view shows your combined picture. Toggle to see personal spending. Full transparency with built-in privacy for individual purchases.

\n
\n
\n
🔒
\n

Bank-Level Security

\n

256-bit encryption, SOC 2 compliant, and read-only bank connections. Your data stays between the two of you.

\n How It Works\n

Three steps to financial harmony

\n

Get set up in under five minutes — no joint bank account required.

\n
\n
\n
\n
1
\n

Create Your Pair

\n

Sign up and invite your partner with a simple link. You'll both land in a shared financial space.

\n
\n
\n
2
\n

Connect & Customize

\n

Link accounts, set your budget categories, and choose how you want to split. Pairwise adapts to your style.

\n
\n
\n
3
\n

Budget as a Team

\n

Track spending, settle up with a tap, and hit savings goals together. Weekly recaps keep you both aligned.
