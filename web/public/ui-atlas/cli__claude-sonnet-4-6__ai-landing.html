Features

Built for the way
your mind actually works

Murmur is designed around how ideas actually form — messy, nonlinear, and usually at the worst possible time.

🎙

Voice-first capture

Speak naturally and Murmur transcribes with near-perfect accuracy. No formatting required — just think out loud.

✦

Instant AI summaries

Every recording becomes a structured note in seconds. Key points, action items, and decisions — extracted automatically.

🔗

Automatic connections

Murmur finds threads between your notes that you'd never spot yourself. Resurfaces relevant context exactly when you need it.

🔍

Semantic search

Forget keyword matching. Ask what you mean: "that idea about onboarding from last month" and Murmur finds it.

📱

Capture anywhere

Web, iOS, Android, and browser extension. A thought in the shower becomes a note before you've dried off.

🔒

Private by design

Your notes are end-to-end encrypted. Murmur never trains on your data, and you can export or delete everything anytime.

How it works

From fleeting thought
to lasting knowledge

Three simple steps, completely in the background.

1

Speak, type, or paste

Open Murmur and talk — during a walk, after a meeting, or when inspiration hits at 2am. You can also paste text, drop in a URL, or upload a voice memo. There's no wrong format.

AI does the structuring

Murmur transcribes, summarizes, and tags your note in the background. It identifies action items, questions, and key decisions — all without you lifting a finger.
