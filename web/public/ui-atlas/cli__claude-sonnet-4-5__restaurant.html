Our Story

Welcome to Cedar & Vine, where every dish tells a story of our family's journey from the mountains of Lebanon to your table. For over three decades, we've been sharing the authentic flavors and warm hospitality that define Lebanese culture.

Our recipes have been passed down through generations, each one carefully preserved and lovingly prepared. From our grandmother's hummus to our mother's signature kibbeh, every bite connects you to our heritage.

We use only the freshest ingredients, imported spices, and traditional cooking methods to ensure that each meal captures the true essence of Lebanese cuisine. When you dine with us, you become part of our family.
