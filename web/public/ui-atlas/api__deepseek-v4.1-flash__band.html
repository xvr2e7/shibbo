01 — The Record

Ten songs for the
blue hour.

Static Bloom began as a set of voice memos recorded on a phone in a tour van, and ended as the loudest, softest thing we've ever made. It's a record about the space between a city at night and the person sleeping next to you.

Mixed on tape, mastered quiet, and meant to be played loud.
