      Four friends, one van

      The Lantern Coast started in a bait-shop back room and never quite left. Their songs are about small towns, big weather, and the people who stay.

      Salt & Static is their third record, and the first one they produced entirely on their own.

      Mara QuellVocals, guitar
      Odie FennBass, synths
      Jules ArdenDrums
      Sol VargaGuitar, pedal steel
