Our Story
\n

Bread worth
crossing town for

\n

Odd Loaf started with a starter — a 12-year-old culture named Gus that lives in a ceramic crock behind the oven. We bake in small batches, using stone-milled flour from farms within 100 miles.

\n

There's no factory line, no preservatives, no rush. Just flour, water, salt, and time. We open the doors when the bread is ready and close them when it's gone.

\n

That's the whole trick.
