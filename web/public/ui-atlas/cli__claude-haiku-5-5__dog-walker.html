Meet the Team Behind the Leash

Happy Paws started with one simple belief: every dog deserves daily exercise, companionship, and care, even when their owners are busy. What began as a single walker with a few neighborhood clients has grown into a team of animal lovers who treat every pup like family.

We're fully insured, bonded, and trained in pet first aid and canine behavior. We keep it personal, small enough to know your dog by name.

  • Background-checked and CPR/First Aid certified
  • Licensed and fully insured
  • Vaccination records required for all dogs
  • Serving the local community since 2019
