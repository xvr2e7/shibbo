Our Heritage

"Food is the ingredient that binds us together."

Founded in 1974, Al-Safa Kitchen began in the small home kitchen of our grandmother, Mariam. Today, we continue her legacy by using the same organic spices imported from the mountains of Lebanon and the same slow-cooking methods that make our stews legendary.

We believe in hospitality, generosity, and the joy of sharing a large mezze platter with people you love. Every dish is prepared fresh daily with love and tradition.
