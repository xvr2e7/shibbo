About the Photographer

Quiet moments, honestly kept

I believe the most meaningful parts of a wedding happen between the posed frames: a grandmother's hand on a cheek, a laugh caught mid-sentence, the stillness before the first dance. My work is about noticing those moments and holding onto them.

With over a decade behind the lens, I photograph weddings with a documentary eye and an editorial finish, blending natural light with careful composition so your day reads the way it felt.
