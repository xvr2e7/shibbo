About

I'm Mara — a product designer who believes clarity is a feature, not a style.

I've spent the last decade embedded with engineering teams at startups and scale-ups, doing everything from discovery research and prototyping to shipping production CSS. My sweet spot is messy, workflow-heavy products where the hard part is deciding what not to show.

Outside of work you'll find me letterpress printing, trail running badly, and photographing doorways in cities I visit.

How I work

Every project is different, but the shape of the work is consistent.

01

Understand the real problem

Interviews, shadowing, and support-ticket archaeology before any pixels. The brief you're given is rarely the problem worth solving.

02

Prototype to learn, not to impress

Rough, testable flows in days, not weeks. I'd rather kill three directions cheaply than defend one expensively.

03

Ship with the team

I stay through implementation — specs, edge cases, QA passes — because the design isn't done until it's in users' hands.
