A note from the band
“We wanted to make a record that felt like being let in.”

We made these songs together in a rented house on the coast, with the windows open and no real plan for what came next. The kettle was always on. Someone was always playing something in the next room. Somehow, that became the whole record.

Thanks for listening, for singing along, and for making a little room for us in your day.

— June, Max, and Theo
