Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change plans later?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any charges or credits.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and PayPal. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice.
Is there a free trial?
Yes! All paid plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start your trial.
What happens if I exceed my plan limits?
We'll notify you when you're approaching your limits. You can upgrade at any time to access more features and capacity.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?
Yes! We offer special pricing for nonprofit organizations and educational institutions. Contact our sales team for more information.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. No questions asked, no cancellation fees.
