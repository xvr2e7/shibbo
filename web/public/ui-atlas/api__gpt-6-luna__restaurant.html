A home away from home

Our recipes have a little history.

Beit Zaytoun began with our mother’s kitchen: the counter dusted with flour, the kettle always on, and a table that somehow held one more plate.

We brought those recipes to Brooklyn just as she made them—with patience, generous handfuls of herbs, and the belief that food is how we take care of each other.
