Made by the water

Slow Tide was written over one winter in a rented fishing cabin on the coast, with a borrowed piano, a four-track recorder, and a heater that only worked when it wanted to.

The band tracked most of it live to tape in a single room, leaving in the creaks, the breaths, and the occasional gull. Strings and horns were added later by friends from the city, recorded in a stairwell for the reverb.

It's a quieter record than the last two, and a braver one.

“The kind of album that makes you want to call someone you've been meaning to call.” — The Evening Signal
