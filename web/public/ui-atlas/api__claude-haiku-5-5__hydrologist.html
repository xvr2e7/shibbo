About

Mara Okafor is a hydrologist who has spent nearly two decades measuring, modeling, and occasionally falling into rivers across the Midwest and the Pacific Northwest.

She works on flood forecasting and sediment transport, and writes about the gap between what instruments record and what communities actually live through. Her essays have appeared in regional journals and in a small collection, Where the Water Keeps Its Shape.

When she is not on a gauge or in a spreadsheet, she is probably reading old survey notebooks or trying to identify a sedge she has seen a hundred times without learning its name.
