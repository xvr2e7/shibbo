01 — The practice

We opened Linden in 2012 with one chair and one rule: nobody leaves confused. You’ll always know what’s happening in your mouth, what it costs, and what happens if you do nothing at all.

Thirteen years on we have two surgeries, four clinicians, and a waiting room that smells of coffee rather than disinfectant. We still run on that rule.

We’re deliberately small. It means your dentist remembers your name, your history, and that thing you mentioned last time about cold water.

  • AccessGround-floor surgery, step-free
  • LanguagesPolish & Urdu spoken in the chair

02 — Care

What we do, done properly.

Every treatment starts with a conversation — and a written plan with fixed prices before anything begins. No surprises, ever.

Examinations, hygiene visits, x-rays and the small interventions that stop big ones. This is ninety percent of what we do — and we’d rather it stayed that way.

  • Routine examination
  • Hygiene therapy
  • Digital x-rays
  • Fissure sealants
  • Mouth cancer screening

Fillings, crowns and onlays built to last — matched to the colour of your teeth so well your own mother won’t spot them.

  • Composite fillings
  • Crowns & onlays
  • Root canal therapy
  • Dentures

Whitening, bonding and veneers — for people who want to look like a better version of themselves, not somebody else.

  • Home whitening
  • Composite bonding
  • Porcelain veneers
  • Smile reviews

Discreet aligners and fixed options, planned entirely in-house. And we’ll tell you honestly if your teeth don’t need it.

  • Clear aligners
  • Fixed braces
  • Retainers
  • Free orthodontic reviews

First visits that feel like nothing much at all — a ride in the chair, a count of the teeth, a sticker. That’s the whole trick.
