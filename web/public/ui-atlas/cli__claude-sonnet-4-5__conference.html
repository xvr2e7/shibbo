About the Conference

Join us for two incredible days of cutting-edge frontend development insights, networking, and inspiration in the beautiful city of Lisbon. Frontend Lisbon brings together industry leaders, passionate developers, and innovative thinkers to explore the latest trends in web development, from React and Vue to performance optimization and emerging technologies.

Whether you're a seasoned professional or just starting your journey, you'll find valuable content, make lasting connections, and leave inspired to build better web experiences.
