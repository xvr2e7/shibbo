Frequently asked questions

Which help desks does Relay support? ▼
Relay integrates with Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, and any email-based support inbox. More integrations are added regularly.
Can I customize the report? ▼
Yes. You can choose which ticket tags to include, set delivery days, and add custom branding to your PDF reports.
Is there a free trial? ▼
Absolutely. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial—no credit card required.
