About

a short bio

I'm Mara Ellison, a hydrologist based in Missoula, Montana. For fifteen years I've studied how mountain watersheds move water and sediment — first for the U.S. Geological Survey, now for a small research cooperative and, increasingly, for myself.

My scientific work lives in journals with paywalls and figures nobody reads. These essays are the other half of the work: what the data feels like from inside a wading rod's reach. I write about rivers as archives, the ethics of measurement, and the gap between a hydrograph and a home.

When I'm not writing, I'm probably standing in cold water somewhere upstream, holding a current meter and losing track of time — which is, I've come to think, the whole method.
