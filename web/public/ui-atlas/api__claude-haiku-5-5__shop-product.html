Description

The Ember Dripper is thrown and trimmed by hand in a small studio, then glazed and fired twice. The 60° cone and internal spiral ribs slow the water just enough to extract sweetness without bitterness. The wide outlet means you can brew at a steady, forgiving pace, whether you're using a gooseneck or a standard kettle.

Brewing guide
  • Rinse the filter and dripper with hot water to warm the ceramic.
  • Add 20 g of coffee, medium-fine, and level the bed.
  • Bloom with 40 g of water for 40 seconds.
  • Pour in slow circles to 320 g total over 2:45–3:15.
  • Let drain fully. Aim for a finish near 3:30.
Care

Hand wash with warm water and a soft sponge, or place on the top rack of the dishwasher. Avoid abrupt temperature shifts. Stoneware is durable but can chip if dropped on a hard surface. Use a paper or metal filter; cloth filters are not recommended with this shape.

Why it brews better

Built around the water, not the other way around.

Every detail of the Ember Dripper is designed to make extraction predictable, cup after cup.

01

Dense stoneware

Fired to vitrification, the clay holds heat through the full brew and won't absorb oils or odors over time.

02

Spiral flow ribs

Internal ribs guide water evenly across the bed, reducing channeling and giving you a balanced, clean cup.

03

Single-hole outlet

A generous, centered drain keeps the drawdown steady so you can focus on pour technique, not flow restriction.
