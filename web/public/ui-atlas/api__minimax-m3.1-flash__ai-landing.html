Questions, answered

A few things you might be wondering.

Still curious? We'd love to talk.

hello@murmur.app ↗
What makes Murmur different from other note apps?

Murmur is built around active recall. It doesn't just store your notes — it connects them, summarizes them, answers questions about them, and surfaces relevant ideas while you're working.

Can I import my existing notes?

Yes. Bring your notes from Notion, Apple Notes, Evernote, Markdown files, or plain text. Murmur preserves your content and helps rebuild your structure with AI.

What can I capture in Murmur?

Type a thought, record a voice memo, paste a link, upload an image or PDF, or drop in a web clip. Quick Capture is available from every app and device.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your notes are encrypted in transit and at rest, AI processing is opt-in, and your data is never used to train shared models. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The free plan doesn't require a payment method, and you can use Murmur for as long as you like before deciding whether Pro is right for you.
