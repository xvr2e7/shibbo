A Note from Aurelia

More than photographs—an ode to how your love truly felt.

Over the past decade, I've had the profound privilege of documenting celebrations across Tuscan olive groves, Parisian châteaux, and intimate coastal cliffs. My philosophy marries the quiet grace of photojournalism with the elevated aesthetics of modern fashion editorials.

I don't believe in rigid posing or staged drama. I look for the fleeting whispers: the tremble in a father's hand, the quiet exhale after the vows, and the wild, champagne-soaked laughter under candlelight.

Frequently Asked Inquiries

Answers to common questions regarding travel, turnaround times, gear, and booking procedures.

Yes, worldwide! Over 70% of my commissions take place outside my home base in France. Travel expenses (flights, accommodations, car rental) are transparently quoted upfront with no surprise hidden fees.
I am a hybrid photographer. I shoot both modern high-dynamic-range digital cameras (for low-light evening celebrations and fast-paced ceremony moments) and vintage 35mm and 120 medium format film cameras for their unmatched organic tones, grain, and softness.
You will receive an editorial preview suite of 40–60 images within 48 to 72 hours of your wedding day to share with family and social media. Your complete, meticulously edited gallery is delivered within 6 to 8 weeks.
To guarantee absolute devotion and quality for every couple, I accept only a limited number of 18 weddings per calendar year. Dates are secured on a first-come basis via a signed contract and a 30% retainer deposit.
