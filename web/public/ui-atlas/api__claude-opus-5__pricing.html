Questions, answered

Still unsure? Our team replies in under an hour on weekdays.

How does per-user billing actually work?

You're only charged for people who are active in the workspace. If someone is deactivated mid-cycle, we automatically credit the unused portion toward your next invoice — no tickets, no waiting.

Can I switch between monthly and annual later?

Yes. Move to annual at any time and we'll prorate your remaining monthly balance against the new term. Switching back to monthly takes effect at the end of your current annual period.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Nothing breaks. Your workspace rolls back to the Starter plan and all your data stays intact — upgrade whenever you're ready and everything comes right back, including full history.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or students?

Registered nonprofits and educational institutions get 85% off the Team plan, and verified student organizations get it free. Email community@pulse.app with your documentation.

Where is my data stored, and who can see it?

Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest with AES-256. Business and Enterprise customers can pin data residency to the US, EU, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, or India. We never use customer content to train models.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards and ACH for monthly plans. Annual plans over $5,000 can be paid by invoice with NET-30 terms and a purchase order.
