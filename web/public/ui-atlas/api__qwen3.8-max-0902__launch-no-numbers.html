Asked, or soon to be

Fair questions, plain answers.

You have zero customers. Why should I trust you? +

You shouldn't, yet — that's the correct posture. What we offer instead of trust: plain-CSV export of everything, no card for 30 days, cancel in one click, and a founder who answers email within a day. Week-one software earns confidence in small entries, and we intend to post every one of them.

Is this real double-entry bookkeeping? +

Yes — every line posts as debits and credits behind the scenes, so your books always balance to the penny. You never have to know what a journal entry is; the ledger just quietly refuses to lie to you.

What does it do about quarterly estimated taxes? +

It keeps a running estimate of what you'll owe, skims a percentage of each payment into a tax jar you choose, and shows the four due dates with countdowns. Safe-harbor mode available for the cautious (correct).

Can I get my data out? +

Always. One click exports your full ledger, invoices, and reports as CSV and PDF. Your books are yours — a lock-in would be the one thing we refuse to balance.

Do you file my taxes? +

No — and we won't pretend to. Tally Hall hands you (or your accountant) a clean Schedule C draft and tidy reports. Filing stays with credentialed humans; we just make them faster and nicer about it.

What happens at seat 101? +

The price becomes $19/month for everyone new, and stays that way. The first 100 keep $9 forever — it's written into the receipt, and receipts are kind of our whole thing.
