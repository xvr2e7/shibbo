OUR CREED

Why we never bake a second batch for the afternoon.

Real sourdough does not respect modern retail convenience. Our dough demands 48 hours: 6 hours of levain development, 4 hours of gentle hand stretch-and-folds, and 36 hours of cold retard in our cellar.

If we threw together baguettes at 1:00 PM to capture after-work crowds, we'd have to spike them with commercial dry yeast. We'd rather sweep the deck oven, hand over the crumbs to the neighbourhood hens, and start kneading tomorrow's bread.

100% Wild Levain

Barnaby, our starter, is fed twice daily on unbleached stone-milled rye. Zero powdered yeast packets ever cross the threshold.

True 48h Ferment

Slow fermentation breaks down gluten proteins and phytates, rendering bread that sits peacefully in sensitive bellies.

Farmers We Hug

Our grains are grown less than 160 miles away, milled fresh weekly to preserve the wheat germ oil and floral bran aroma.

Zero Loaf Waste

On the rare days a loaf remains at noon, it is immediately converted to croutons for the corner soup kitchen or kvass beer.

The Loaf Roster

Baked fresh six mornings a week. Rotate seasonally.
