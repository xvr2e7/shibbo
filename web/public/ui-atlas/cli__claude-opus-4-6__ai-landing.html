Features

Everything your notes should do, but never could.

✦

Auto-Linking

Murmur detects themes, people, and projects across your notes and connects them — no manual tagging required.

⚡

Instant Capture

Voice, text, paste, screenshot. Capture any way you think. Murmur structures it on the fly.

◈

Resurface & Recall

Ask your notes in plain language. "What did I decide about pricing?" gets a real answer, with sources.

▣

Smart Summaries

Daily and weekly digests of your captured thoughts, decisions, and open threads — delivered where you want them.

How it works

Three steps to a second brain.

Capture anything

Jot a note, dictate a thought, or paste a link. Don't worry about formatting — Murmur handles the structure.

Murmur connects the dots

AI finds relationships between your notes, groups related ideas, and highlights contradictions or open questions.

Retrieve, don't search

Ask a question in natural language and get a synthesized answer drawn from everything you've ever captured.
