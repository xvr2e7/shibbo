The thoughtful details

High-fired stoneware with a food-safe, lead-free satin glaze. Spiral interior ribs encourage an even extraction, while the generous handle keeps your hands comfortable.
11.5 cm wide × 9 cm tall · approximately 320 g. Each glaze has subtle, one-of-a-kind variations.

A better brew, simply

Fits standard size 02 cone filters and brews 1–2 cups. Rinse your filter, add 20 g of medium-ground coffee, then slowly pour 320 ml of water just off the boil. Give your coffee 30 seconds to bloom. Take your time — that’s the good part.
