Our Mission

Every neighborhood deserves shade

Low-canopy urban areas are up to 12 °F hotter in summer, with higher rates of asthma and heat-related illness. We partner with cities and residents to change that — for good.

Equity-First Planting

We prioritize neighborhoods with the lowest canopy coverage and highest heat vulnerability.

Community-Led

Residents choose species, locations, and help care for new trees through our stewardship program.

Long-Term Care

Every tree gets three years of professional monitoring and watering to reach self-sufficiency.

Our Programs

Three ways we grow urban forests

From sidewalk saplings to schoolyard orchards, our programs are designed for lasting impact.

Streetside Trees

We work with municipal arborists to plant native shade trees along sidewalks and medians in heat-vulnerable corridors. Each block gets a customized planting plan.

Schoolyard Canopies

We transform bare playgrounds into shaded learning landscapes with fruit trees, pollinator gardens, and outdoor classrooms — reaching 45,000 students so far.

Green Corridors

Multi-block tree-lined pathways that connect parks, schools, and transit — creating cooler, safer walking routes and contiguous wildlife habitat in the urban core.
