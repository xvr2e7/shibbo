FAQ

Good questions, honest answers.

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most patients benefit from a checkup and cleaning every six months. If you have gum disease or other conditions, we may suggest more frequent visits.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! We welcome new patients of all ages. Your first visit includes a full exam, X-rays and a personalised treatment plan.

What if I'm nervous about treatment?

You're not alone. We offer nitrous oxide and oral sedation, and we'll go at whatever pace feels comfortable for you.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us right away. We keep same-day slots open for urgent problems such as severe pain, swelling or broken teeth.
