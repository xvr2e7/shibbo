"One of the most quietly devastating records of the year — music that knows when to stay still." — Pitchfork, Best New Music

Hollow Atlas is the project of siblings Mara and Owen Calloway, formed in Portland, Oregon in 2019. After two EPs and constant touring, Pale Meridian is their first full-length.

Recorded over three weeks at a rented house outside of Tucson with producer Jess Tanaka, the album trades the band's earlier urgency for something slower and more certain — wide-angle songs about grief, geography, and the strange comfort of motion.

Mixed by Alicia Voss. Photography by Daniel Freire.
