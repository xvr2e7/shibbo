About Pawsitive Steps

Welcome to Pawsitive Steps, your trusted dog walking partner in the community! With over 8 years of experience caring for dogs of all sizes and temperaments, we're passionate about providing your furry friends with the exercise and attention they deserve.

Our certified dog walkers are trained in pet first aid, dog behavior, and safety protocols. We treat every dog like our own, ensuring they get proper exercise, fresh air, and lots of love during their walks.

Whether your dog needs a quick potty break, an energetic adventure, or socialization with other pups, Pawsitive Steps has the perfect solution for your pet's needs.
