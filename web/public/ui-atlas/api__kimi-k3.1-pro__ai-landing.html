Ask us anything
Do I have to record my voice?

Not at all. You can type, paste, import documents, or record voice notes. Murmur works with whatever feels natural in the moment—and you can mix formats inside the same note.

Will Murmur change what I meant to say?

Murmur keeps your original recording or text alongside every summary. AI-created structure is always editable, so you stay in control of the words, tone, and final thought.

Can I bring my existing notes with me?

Yes. Import common text and document formats, then let Murmur help connect them with new ideas. You can also export your notes whenever you like—no lock-in, no awkward goodbye.

How does Murmur handle privacy?

Your notes are private by default. We don’t sell personal data or use advertising trackers, and sharing is always something you choose explicitly. Your inner life deserves boundaries.

What does the free plan include?

The free plan includes voice and text notes, AI summaries, semantic search, and enough monthly recording time to make Murmur part of your routine. No credit card is needed to start.
