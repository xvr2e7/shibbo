What it is

A small conference with a big balcony.

Six hundred people, one room, two days. We keep it deliberately intimate so that the hallway track is as good as the stage — and so you can actually meet the person whose talk changed your mind.

◼

One track, zero FOMO

Every attendee sees every talk. 25 minutes each, hard stop, generously spaced breaks for the conversations that follow.

⚑

Craft over hype

Speakers bring real production stories, real numbers and real trade-offs. If a slide has a logo on it, it had better be a bug report.

☀

Lisbon in May

22 °C, long afternoons and the best coffee-per-euro ratio in Europe. The venue terrace looks straight over the river.

♲

Open by default

All talks recorded, captioned and published free within three weeks. Transcripts and slides in the same repo.

Venue

Teatro da Graça, on the hill.

A restored 1920s theatre five minutes from the Graça viewpoint. Steep velvet seating, a stage you can actually see from row 20, and a terrace with the whole city underneath it.

  • Rua da Graça 44, 1170-166 Lisboa — tram 28 and 12, bus 734
  • Step-free entrance, lift to all levels, wheelchair spaces in rows A and K
  • Live captions on the side screens, both days, both languages
  • Quiet room with low light on the first floor, open all day
  • Vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free and halal lunches as standard

FAQ

Practical things.

What language are the talks in?

English, all of them. Live captions in English and Portuguese appear on the side screens, and the published recordings ship with both transcripts.

Where should I stay?

Graça, Alfama and Mouraria are all walkable to the venue. We have negotiated rates at three hotels within 800 metres — the codes are emailed with your ticket. Anywhere on the green or blue metro line also works; the 28 tram stops outside the door but is slow and full of suitcases.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes, and we enforce it. It covers the venue, the reception, the side events and the Discord. Two independent contacts (one on site, one off) are listed on every badge and in the programme. Reports are answered within the hour.

Can I get an invoice for my employer?

Of course. Add your company details and VAT number at checkout and you will get a proper invoice immediately. For teams over eight people, write to hello@miradouro.conf and we will handle it by email.

What if I can’t make it after all?

Free name transfers until 1 May 2026, and a full refund minus the €12 payment fee until 1 April. After that we will happily help you find a buyer from the waiting list.

Is there a call for speakers?

The main line-up is curated and partly blind-reviewed, and it closes in January. Lightning talk submissions open in March and are open to ticket holders only — five minutes, any format, first-timers very welcome.

See you on the hill.

Get the occasional email: line-up announcements, ticket releases and a short list of where to eat. Nothing else, ever.
