Our Story

A kitchen that began at Sitto's stove

"Beit Sitto" means grandmother's house. When Layla Haddad arrived with two suitcases and a notebook of recipes, she began cooking for neighbors on Sundays. The neighbors told their neighbors, and in 1994 the family opened these doors.

Today her son Karim runs the grill, her daughters Nour and Maya run the floor and the pastry counter, and the grandchildren fold sambousek after school. Everything is made from scratch each morning: the labneh, the pickles, the bread, the sweets.

— The Haddad Family
