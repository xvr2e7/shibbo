About Me

With over 10 years of experience in wedding photography, I specialize in capturing authentic moments that tell your unique love story. My approach blends photojournalistic storytelling with elegant portraiture.

I believe in building genuine connections with my clients to ensure comfort and authenticity in front of the camera. Every wedding is different, and I'm passionate about creating images that you'll treasure forever.

Based in California, I travel nationwide and internationally to document weddings of all styles and sizes.
