02 / A little about me

Equal parts systems thinker and detail obsessive.

I believe good design isn’t decoration. It’s how a product keeps its promise.

For the past eight years, I’ve worked alongside founders, engineers, and researchers to make difficult things feel clear. My sweet spot is the messy middle: finding the signal, shaping the system, and sweating the interactions that make it all feel effortless.

I stay close from strategy through shipped pixels, asking “why” just as often as “what if?”
