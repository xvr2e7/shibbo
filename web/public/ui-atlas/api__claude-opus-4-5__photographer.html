About Me

Hello, I'm Elena

For over a decade, I've had the honor of documenting love stories across the globe. My approach to wedding photography is rooted in authenticity—capturing the genuine emotions, stolen glances, and joyful tears that make your day uniquely yours.

Based in San Francisco but available worldwide, I believe every couple deserves photographs that feel as timeless as their love. My style blends fine art elegance with photojournalistic spontaneity, creating a collection of images that tell your complete story.

When I'm not behind the camera, you'll find me exploring coastal trails, curating vintage vinyl, or planning my next adventure with my rescue dog, Maple.

— Elena Rose
