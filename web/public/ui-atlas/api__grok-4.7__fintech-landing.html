Once a month, Hearth opens the agenda. You bring the coffee. The numbers are already there.

Fair splits

Fair doesn’t have to mean equal.

Drag the incomes. Shared bills follow take-home, the way you might do it at the table if the table were calmer. You can still choose 50/50. The point is that the rule is named.
