About

Marlow Reyes is a hydrologist who studies how water moves through landscapes: how rain becomes streamflow, how rivers carry sediment, and how communities live alongside both. Their fieldwork has taken them from alpine headwaters to slow, muddy deltas.

These essays began as field notebooks and grew into something closer to letters to the river. They are written for anyone curious about the water nearby, with no equations required.

When not in the field, Marlow teaches, reviews other people's models, and walks the banks of whichever river is closest.
