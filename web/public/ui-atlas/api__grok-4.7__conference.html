Why this room

A pattern walked on by everyone.

Most frontend conferences are a hallway with a stage attached. Calçada is the other way around: one sequence, nowhere to hide a thin talk, and time between sessions that is actually long enough to finish a sentence.

The name is the stone under Lisbon. Calçada is laid by hand, repaired in public, and used by whoever happens to be walking. That is close to how we want interfaces discussed — as material, not as mythology.

Eight invited talks, a panel, lightning, lunch both days. Captions on the main screen. A quiet room if the room gets loud.

I wanted a conference I would attend even if I were not running it. Fewer slides about scale. More about the last eight pixels.

Leonor Pinto, curator

The room

Sala do Rio, on the Santos waterfront.

A single hall between Cais do Sodré and Alcântara, river side of Avenida 24 de Julho. Converted warehouse, high windows, no basement ballroom. You can hear the tram if the talk goes quiet.

  • ArriveCais do Sodré station, about a twelve-minute walk. Tram 15E along the river. Buses on 24 de Julho.
  • AccessStep-free entrance and seating. Live captions on the main screen. A quiet room off the hall.
  • FoodLunch both days, included. Vegetarian by default; say so on the note if you need something else.
  • StayNo partner hotel. Santos, Chiado, or Alcântara, and walk.
  • ConductBe decent. Harassment ends in removal, without a panel about it. The short note is at the door.

Practical

Before you ask the curator.

What language are the talks?

English. Questions too. Lisbon outside the hall will not be, which is part of the point of holding it here.

Are talks recorded?

Audio for people who were in the room, sent later. No camera crane, no “content track.” If a speaker declines, that talk stays in the room.

Can I give a lightning talk?

Eight slots on Friday, seven minutes, no slides required. Use the note above and choose a lightning slot. A bell ends you. That is the whole production.

What if I cannot afford a seat?

Twenty scholarship seats are part of the plan, funded by mecenas passes. Say so in the note. Cost should not be the thing that decides the room.

Is this a real ticketed event?

No. This page is a self-contained design specimen for a conference that is not on sale. Do not book travel from it.
