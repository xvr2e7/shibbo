The ledger

Four things freelancers actually lose.

A spreadsheet can do this. A lot of them become a file you are afraid to open. Tally Hall is the page you open instead.

  1. Received and spent

    Enter income when it lands and expenses when you pay them. Categories stay in plain language: software, travel, contractors, fees, home office. Not a chart of accounts on day one.

  2. Who has not paid

    Unpaid invoices sit on the month until they clear. They are not mixed into money you already received. A late client is a line, not a feeling you try to reconstruct in June.

  3. What to set aside

    Pick a percentage of what is left. The number stays on the month, so the whole balance does not look spendable. It is a pocket, not a filing and not a bill.

  4. A file you can leave with

    Export a CSV of the month or the year. No proprietary binder. If you stop using Tally Hall, the books are still yours.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this tax or legal advice?

No. Tally Hall adds and remembers. It does not file, represent you, or know your local rules. If a government cares about the number, ask a tax professional. The set-aside tool on this page is a rough pocket, not an amount due.

Do you connect to my bank?

Not in this first release. You enter what you received and what you spent. It is more typing, and a sync error cannot invent a purchase you did not make. If that changes, it will be optional and written on this page first.

Who is it for?

Freelancers and solo consultants who send invoices and want the month to make sense: design, writing, development, photography, consulting, and the work that does not fit a department. Not for firms that need payroll, inventory, or a full chart of accounts.

What happens to the price?

Free stays free. Hall is $12 a month during launch month, and that rate holds for twelve months from the day you start. After that it is $18 a month, or you can return to the free ledger. Cancel whenever. There is no annual contract.

Can I leave and take the books?

Yes. CSV export is on the free ledger and on Hall. If you stop paying, you can still export. We would rather lose a subscription than hold your year hostage.

What about currency?

The prices and the specimen on this page are in US dollars. The first release does not convert currencies, so keep one currency per ledger and enter figures the way you invoice.

Why are there no reviews?

Because we launched last week and have no customers. We are not going to buy quotes, borrow logos, or write praise ourselves. If you want a reference, we cannot give you one. We can give you the ledger.
