A little about me

Science, with room for wonder.

I’m a hydrologist studying how water moves through landscapes—and how our choices change its course.

For the past decade, I’ve worked with river communities and research teams across the Pacific Northwest. These essays are a place to bring fieldwork into conversation with history, ecology, and everyday life. When I’m not taking measurements, you’ll usually find me walking upstream.

For notes, questions, or speaking inquiries, write me a line.
