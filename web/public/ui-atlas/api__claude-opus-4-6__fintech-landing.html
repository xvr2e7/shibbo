Features

Everything you need to budget as a team

No more spreadsheets, awkward conversations, or wondering who paid for what. DuoBudget makes shared finances simple.

Shared Dashboard

Both partners see the same real-time view of all accounts, spending, and budgets. Full transparency, zero surprises.

Smart Bill Splitting

Split expenses 50/50, by income ratio, or any custom arrangement. Automatic tracking of who owes what.

Couple Goals

Save for your wedding, vacation, or first home together. Visual progress tracking keeps you both motivated.

Smart Alerts

Get notified when you're close to budget limits, bills are due, or unusual spending is detected. Stay ahead, together.

Spending Insights

Beautiful charts showing where your money goes — individually and as a couple. Spot trends and optimize together.

Personal Allowances

Keep some spending private with personal "fun money" budgets. Because healthy finances include healthy boundaries.
