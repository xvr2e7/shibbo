About

Designing at the intersection of craft and strategy

I'm a product designer with 8+ years of experience working with startups and scale-ups. I've shipped products used by millions at companies like Stripe, Figma, and several YC-backed startups.

My approach blends deep user research with systems thinking. I believe the best products come from understanding not just what users want, but the constraints of the business and the possibilities of the technology.

When I'm not pushing pixels, you'll find me hiking coastal trails, experimenting with ceramics, or mentoring junior designers through ADPList.
