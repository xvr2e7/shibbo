Questions you’re probably asking

Because trusting your money to a 6-day-old app is scary.

What if you go out of business in six months?
Fair question. We run on near-zero overhead (static architecture + serverless backend). Even if we only had 10 users, our server costs are under $30/month, meaning we aren't going anywhere. Furthermore, you can enable "Daily Dropbox Sync" which dumps a raw SQLite & CSV file into your personal drive every night. You can never be locked out.
Can you see my bank account login or passwords?
Never. We use Plaid, the same SOC-2 certified financial layer used by Venmo and American Express. We only get encrypted read-only transaction strings. We cannot move your money, initiate transfers, or view your passwords.
I have messy finances from earlier this year. Can I import them?
Yes! In fact, as part of being an early customer, Liam will personally review your old Excel/CSV sheet and format it for you if you get stuck. Send it over and we’ll get it imported within 24 hours.
Why is it called Tally Hall?
Historically, tally sticks were used to record debts and agreements without relying on complicated banks. We want this app to be a welcoming hall where your numbers are plain, honest, and talliable.
