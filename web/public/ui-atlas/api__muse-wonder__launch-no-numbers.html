Questions

Before you open the books

Who is Tally Hall for?

Freelancers, independent contractors, consultants, and creators who want one simple place for invoices, expenses, receipts, and tax-ready totals — especially if spreadsheets have not stuck.

I’m not an accounting person. Will this make sense?

That’s the point. Tally Hall uses plain language — money in, money out, what’s unpaid, and what you may want to set aside — and keeps categories editable so they match your work.

Does Tally Hall file my taxes or replace an accountant?

No. Tally Hall helps you keep organized records and export tidy summaries. It does not file taxes and does not provide personal tax, legal, or financial advice. Many freelancers share their export with a tax professional.

You just launched. Is it risky to join early?

We’re new, and we’re upfront about it. You can start on the free plan, export your data anytime, and cancel a paid plan whenever you like. Early members also get direct onboarding help while we grow.

Can I bring in past receipts and invoices?

Yes — you can add past income and expenses and upload receipts you already have, so this year’s records can live in one place.

What if I need help?

Use the in-app help link or email us from the address on your account page. As an early member, your questions also help shape our help guides and roadmap.
