FAQ

Questions we actually get.

Who gets a seat?

Someone who sends a message, starts a huddle, or uploads a file during the billing month. People who only read, plus guests and bots, are free. Deactivate someone and they drop off the next invoice.

What if the room goes quiet?

Paid plans bill active people only. A silent month is a $0 invoice. Spark stays free either way. We do not charge rent on an empty room.

Can we switch plans?

Upgrades start immediately and are prorated. Downgrades start on the next cycle. If you leave Kiln, take an export first. Legal hold and the long audit history live on that plan.

Is annual a contract?

Monthly plans cancel anytime. Annual is prepaid for the year, at 20% less, and you keep the room through the term you have paid. Add people as they show up; new seats are prorated.

Do you train models on our chats?

No. Messages are stored so your workspace can search them. We do not sell workspace content, and we do not use it to train models.

Can we move history in?

Hearth and Kiln import channel history from a standard export. Direct messages are opt-in, person by person. Spark starts fresh.

Nonprofit or classroom pricing?

Accredited nonprofits and schools get Hearth at half the per-person rate. Write to hello@ingle.chat and include the accreditation.
