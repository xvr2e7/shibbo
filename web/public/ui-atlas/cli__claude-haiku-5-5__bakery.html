A bakery that runs on patience.

Odd Loaf started in a rented kitchen with a jar of starter we named Gerald. Our founders wanted bread that tasted like it had time to become something, and a neighbourhood that could walk over and grab a loaf before work.

We still do it the slow way. Long ferments, wild yeast, and a few deliberately odd shapes that keep the oven interesting. Every loaf is hand-shaped by the person who mixed it.
