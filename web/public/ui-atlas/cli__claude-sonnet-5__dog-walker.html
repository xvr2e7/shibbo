Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before booking your first walk.

Are your walkers insured and background-checked?
Yes — every walker is bonded, insured, and passes a thorough background check before joining our team.
How do I get access to my home?
You can provide a spare key during the meet & greet, use a lockbox, or share a smart lock code. Whatever's most convenient for you.
Can I request the same walker every time?
Absolutely. Most clients build a relationship with one primary walker, with a trusted backup for sick days or vacations.
What if my dog has medical needs or medication?
No problem — just let us know the details during booking and we'll make sure your walker is fully briefed.
Do you offer last-minute or same-day walks?
We do our best to accommodate same-day requests based on walker availability in your area. Just message us!
