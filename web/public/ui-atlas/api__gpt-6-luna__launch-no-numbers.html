A few good questions

Before you get started.

Who is Tally Hall for?

Freelancers who want a straightforward place to keep track of business income and expenses.

Is Tally Hall brand new?

Yes. We launched last week, so we don’t have customer reviews to share yet. We’d rather be upfront about that while we keep working on the fundamentals.

Does it replace an accountant or tax professional?

No. Tally Hall is for keeping your bookkeeping organized; it isn’t tax or accounting advice. A qualified professional can help with advice specific to your situation.
