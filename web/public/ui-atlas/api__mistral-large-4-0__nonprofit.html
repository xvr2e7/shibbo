Our mission

We plant trees where people actually live — then stay to help them grow.

Rooted started in 2016 with one pickup truck, forty saplings, and a stubborn belief: every neighborhood deserves shade. Today we're a crew of arborists, organizers, and 2,300 volunteers across 38 neighborhoods.

We don't dig and dash. Every tree gets watered, mulched, and pruned for three full years — because a tree only counts if it survives.

  • Always free for residents, schools & small businesses
  • 40+ native, climate-resilient species — right tree, right place
  • Three years of care included with every single tree

How it works

From request to shade in three steps

Step 1

Nominate your spot

Tell us about your block, yard, or schoolyard. We survey utilities, soil, and shade — then match the right tree to the right place.

Step 2

We plant together

Crews of 8–12 neighbors dig, plant, and mulch on scheduled planting days. Tools, gloves, and snacks provided — zero experience needed.

Step 3

We care for 3 years

Watering trucks, seasonal pruning, and a survival guarantee: if a tree dies in its first three years, we replace it free.
