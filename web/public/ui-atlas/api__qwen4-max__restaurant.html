Our Family

From a Beirut courtyard
to your neighborhood

In 1987, Mariam and Joseph Khoury left Beirut with two suitcases, a brass coffee pot, and a notebook of recipes written in Mariam's mother's hand. They opened a twelve-seat dining room and cooked everything themselves. Today their children and grandchildren run the pass — but the notebook still sits by the stove, and nothing on the menu has drifted from it.

  • 1987

    Twelve seats and one Saj

    Mariam & Joseph open the first dining room. The bread oven never cools.

  • 2004

    The second generation

    Son Elias takes the charcoal grill; daughter Nadine guards the mezze — and the secrets.

  • 2019

    Grandchildren at the pass

    Yasmina & Karim add the weekend family feast — every dish still made by hand, daily.
