❦ The Philosophy

Capturing the unscripted grace of your most meaningful day.

With an editorial eye and a documentary heart, I seek out the quiet glances, the joyous tears, and the fleeting in-between moments. Blending medium format film with digital precision to craft imagery that feels as effortless and enduring as your love.

Behind the Lens

Hello, I'm Clara.
Curator of memories.

Based in Paris and available worldwide, I have spent the last decade documenting love stories across sun-drenched Italian villas, misty Scottish highlands, and intimate cityscapes.

My approach is calm, observant, and deeply personal. I believe the best photographs happen when you forget the camera is there, allowing you to be fully present in the magic of your celebration.
