What we do

Complete care for every smile

Whether it's a six-month checkup or a full restoration, we handle it all under one roof — no runaround, no surprises.

Preventive Care

Thorough cleanings, digital X-rays, sealants, and personalized hygiene plans to stop problems before they start.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Professional whitening, porcelain veneers, and bonding designed to brighten your smile naturally.

Restorative Dentistry

Tooth-colored fillings, crowns, bridges, and implants that restore both function and confidence.

Clear Aligners

Discreet, removable orthodontics with 3D treatment previews — see your future smile before you begin.

Pediatric Dentistry

Kid-friendly visits that build healthy habits early, with a gentle approach that keeps little ones smiling.

Emergency Dentistry

Toothache, chip, or knocked-out tooth? Call before noon and we'll see you the same day — guaranteed.

Why Brightside

A calmer kind of dental visit

We founded Brightside on a simple idea: going to the dentist shouldn't feel like a chore. Our office is designed for comfort — warm lighting, noise-cancelling headphones, and a team that explains everything in plain language before any work begins.

We invest in technology that makes care faster and gentler: digital impressions instead of goopy molds, low-dose 3D imaging, and same-day crowns milled right in our office.

  • Transparent, upfront pricing with no surprise bills
  • Most insurance plans accepted, plus an in-house savings plan
  • Evening and Saturday appointments available
  • Sedation options for anxious patients
