Short Bio

A scientist who
keeps a notebook.

Mara Ellison is a fluvial hydrologist who has spent eighteen years wading, gauging, and listening to rivers across the American West — from snowmelt creeks in the Cascades to the silt-heavy bends of the Rio Grande.

Her essays braid field data with personal history: a rating curve becomes a story about her father, a flood hydrograph becomes an argument for letting rivers misbehave. Her work has appeared in Orion, High Country News, Terrain.org and The Atlantic.
