How it works

From murmur to meaning in three steps.

Capture anywhere

Hit the mic, type a line, or paste a link. Murmur accepts voice, text, images of whiteboards, and screen clips without asking you to organize anything first.

Murmur shapes it

Within seconds your raw capture becomes a titled note with headings, a summary, tags, and any tasks it found. You can edit or undo any change.

Come back when you need it

Search by meaning, not just keywords. Pick up a thread weeks later and Murmur will remind you what you were thinking and what's still open.
