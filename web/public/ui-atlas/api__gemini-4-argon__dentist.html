Common Questions

Thoughtful answers before you arrive.

Over 40% of our patients joined us after avoiding the dentist for years. We never rush or lecture. Our suites feature noise-canceling headphones, ceiling-mounted streaming cinema, warm lavender towels, topical numbing gels prior to any computer-regulated anesthetic, and optional nitrous oxide or oral conscious sedation.

Never aggressively. Unlike outdated crown preparations, our biomimetic approach uses ultra-thin contact-lens veneers (0.2mm to 0.4mm) that preserve 90–95% of your natural enamel. You also test-drive a removable 3D-printed prototype on your teeth before any final porcelain is crafted.

Before your appointment, our benefits coordinator verifies your exact PPO breakdown. You receive a clear, itemized digital ledger showing what your plan covers and your exact copay—never a surprise bill months later.

Your first visit includes a full 3D intraoral wellness scan, low-radiation digital imaging, oral cancer and periodontal screening, a gentle Swiss Guided Biofilm cleaning, and an unhurried sit-down consultation with your doctor looking at high-definition photos together.
