A house, not a concept.

Recipes traveled from Zahle to this kitchen in a tin of sumac and a stubborn insistence on doing things slowly.

Noura still seasons the labneh the way her mother did: salt, a ribbon of olive oil, and za’atar crushed that morning. Samir lights the grill before the street is awake. The children grew up folding grape leaves at the same table guests sit at now.

We cook for the room, not for a photograph. Order more mezze than you think you need. Argue gently about who gets the last piece of knafeh. That is the point.
