About

Mara Lindqvist is a hydrologist who has spent twenty years measuring rivers in the western United States and, more recently, in Aotearoa New Zealand. Her research concerns floodplain connectivity and the ways groundwater and surface water trade places along a channel.

The essays here began as field notes. They are attempts to describe what rivers are doing, in language that respects both the physics and the wonder. She writes for people who have stood on a bank and felt that something important was happening just out of sight.

She lives near a small tributary of the Columbia with a dog who dislikes cold water, and she still checks the gauge most mornings.
