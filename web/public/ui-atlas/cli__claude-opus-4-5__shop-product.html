How to Brew

Master the pour-over method in four simple steps.

01

Rinse

Place filter in dripper and rinse with hot water to remove paper taste and preheat ceramic.

02

Dose

Add 20g of medium-fine ground coffee. Shake gently to level the bed.

03

Bloom

Pour 40ml water in slow circles. Wait 30 seconds as CO₂ releases and grounds expand.

04

Pour

Add remaining water in steady spirals. Total brew time: 2:30-3:00. Enjoy.
