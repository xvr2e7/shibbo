FAQ

Good questions, happy answers.

Still wondering about something? Send a note and we'll happily help.

Ask a question
Do you meet dogs before the first walk?

Yes. Every new client starts with a quick meet-and-greet so we can learn your dog's routine, comfort level, leash preferences, and any must-know quirks.

Can you walk reactive or anxious dogs?

Absolutely. We use quiet routes, patient pacing, and force-free handling. We'll discuss triggers and safety plans before booking recurring walks.

What happens in bad weather?

Light rain is usually no problem with proper gear. For heat, storms, ice, or poor air quality, we'll shorten the outing or switch to an indoor potty break and play visit.

Can you give medication or special instructions?

Yes. We can administer simple medications, apply routine care, follow feeding instructions, and respect crate or room preferences with clear written directions.

What areas do you serve?

We currently serve Maplewood, Glenview, Oak Park, and nearby neighborhoods. If you're just outside the area, send your address and we'll do our best to help.

How do I receive walk updates?

After each visit, you'll get a text or email with photos, potty notes, distance, route highlights, and any little details worth sharing.
