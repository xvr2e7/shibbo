A repeatable cup, every morning

The Kiln is tuned for a 1:16 ratio and a single steady pour. Here's the sequence we brew with in the shop.

01

Rinse the filter

Seat a #2 cone filter, rinse it with hot water, and pour the rinse away. This warms the cone so the first pour doesn't lose heat.

02

Bloom

22 g of medium-fine coffee, bed levelled. Pour 60 g of water at 94 °C and wait 40 seconds while the grounds degas.

03

Pour in spirals

Work outward from the centre in slow circles to 360 g total, keeping the water level just below the rim.

04

Drawdown

The bed should flatten out around 2:45. Swirl the carafe once, let it settle, and serve.
