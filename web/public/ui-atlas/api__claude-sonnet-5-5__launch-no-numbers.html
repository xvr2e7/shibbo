Questions

Fair things to ask a new app.

Is Tally Hall a replacement for an accountant?

No. It keeps your books organized and gives you estimates, which makes working with an accountant faster and cheaper. Tax estimates are guidance, not professional tax advice.

You just launched. Should I trust you with my finances?

Be as careful as you’d be with any new tool. Bank connections are read-only, so Tally Hall can’t move money, and you can export all your data at any time. Start with the free trial and a CSV import if you’d like to test before connecting an account.

Who is it for?

Solo freelancers, consultants, and independent creatives who earn income without an employer withholding taxes. It isn’t designed for teams with payroll or inventory.

What happens when the trial ends?

You choose whether to subscribe. If you don’t, you can still export everything you entered.

Something I need is missing. Can I ask for it?

Please do. At this stage, early feedback has real influence on what we build next.
