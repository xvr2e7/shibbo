The method

Built around three habits — not a spreadsheet with a relationship mode.

Most budget apps quietly assume one person does all the work while the other stays politely oblivious. That person gets resentful; the other gets surprised. Tandem gives both of you three small, shared habits instead.

Pick a threshold together — $10, $200, whatever feels like yours. Charges under it flow past silently. Charges over it get a two-tap yes-or-no from both of you. It takes four seconds, and it quietly deletes “wait, what was this?” from your vocabulary.

Ten minutes, once a week, phones down. Tandem walks you through three questions — what came in, what’s next, what we’re dreaming about — and closes with one number you both say out loud. It’s the ritual couples report keeping for years, not weeks.

She earns double; he’s back in school. Tandem splits by income share and actual life, not by a coin flip — and recalculates the moment one of those changes. Nobody keeps score anymore, because the app already did, kindly.
