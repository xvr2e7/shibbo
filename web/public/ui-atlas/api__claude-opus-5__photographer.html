Ciao, I'm Elena

I photograph the in‑between — the glances, the trembling hands, the laughter nobody planned.

Fifteen years, two hundred and forty weddings, and still the same lump in my throat during the vows.

I grew up between olive groves outside Siena with my grandfather's Rolleiflex slung around my neck, and I've never really put a camera down since. My work lives somewhere between documentary and editorial: unhurried, warm, a little bit filmic. I won't pose you into something you're not — I'll simply stay close, stay quiet, and catch the day as it actually felt.

When I'm not shooting, you'll find me developing rolls in a converted pantry, eating too much focaccia, or driving to a coastline I've never photographed before.

The Experience

How we'll work together

01

A long conversation

We start with coffee or a video call — no pitch deck, just your story, your plans and the things that actually matter to you. I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right fit.

02

Gentle planning

Four to six weeks out I help shape your timeline so the light works in your favour, and so you're never rushed out of your own celebration for photographs.

03

The day, and after

I arrive early, stay late, and stay out of the way. A preview gallery lands within 72 hours; your full edited collection within four weeks, with an optional fine-art album.
