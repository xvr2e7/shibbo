May 12–18 · Illustrative data

The weekly product brief

Relay reviewed 248 tickets and found eight recurring themes and three new issues. Here are the three most-mentioned themes.

CSV export for reports · 34 tickets

Customers want to bring report data into spreadsheets for monthly reviews. Several mentioned copying rows by hand.

“Can I download the filtered report as a CSV instead of copying it into Sheets?”
Mobile login timeouts · 21 tickets

Customers reported that verification timed out after they switched to their email app to retrieve a sign-in code.

“By the time I get the code and switch back, I have to start over.”
Clearer team permissions · 17 tickets

Workspace admins struggled to tell which roles could invite teammates or manage billing before assigning them.

“I want our team lead to invite people, but I don’t want to give them access to billing.”
