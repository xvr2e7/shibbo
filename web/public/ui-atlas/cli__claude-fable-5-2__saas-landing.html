    Questions

    Things people ask before connecting.

    Do we need to tag tickets for this to work?

    No. Relay reads the full text of each conversation and groups by the underlying problem. If you already tag, Relay uses your tags as a hint, but it doesn’t depend on them being consistent.

    How is this different from the reporting in our helpdesk?

    Helpdesk reports tell you how support is performing: volume, response time, satisfaction. Relay tells you what customers are struggling with in the product, ranked and explained, written for the people who decide what gets built.

    What happens to customer data?

    Relay connects with read-only access. Personal details such as names, emails, phone numbers, and payment data are redacted before analysis. Your tickets are never used to train models, and you can delete everything at any time.

    Can we review the report before it goes out?

    Yes. Turn on draft mode and the report arrives to an editor first. You can merge or rename themes, add a note, or pin a theme you’re tracking, then send it on.

    What if a theme is grouped wrong?

    Split it, merge it, or move tickets between themes. Relay remembers the correction and applies it to future weeks, so the report gets closer to how your team thinks about the product.
