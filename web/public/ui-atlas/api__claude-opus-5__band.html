The story

Recorded at
low tide

After two years of touring, the four of us drove east with a station wagon full of pedals and no fixed plan. We rented a shed that still smelled faintly of herring, ran a single mic down the length of the room, and played until the generator gave out each night.

“We kept the mistakes. They were the only honest parts.”

Paper Lanterns is about leaving the porch light on for people who may not come back — nine songs for long drives, thin walls and slow forgiveness. Mixed by Nour Haddad. Mastered at Blackwater.
