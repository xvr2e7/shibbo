FAQ

Fair questions

Can my partner see all of my spending?
Only what you choose to share. Each partner has a private space for personal accounts and spending. Shared categories, bills, and goals are visible to both — everything else is yours alone.
We have different incomes. How do splits work?
However you want: 50/50, proportional to income, or a custom ratio per category. Rent can split one way and groceries another. Tandem tracks the running balance and settles up automatically each month.
Do we need a joint bank account?
No. Tandem works great with fully separate accounts, a joint account, or any mix. It creates a shared view on top of whatever banking setup you already have.
Is our financial data safe?
Yes. We use 256-bit encryption, read-only bank connections through trusted providers, and biometric login. Tandem can never move your money, and we never sell your data — our revenue comes from subscriptions, not ads.
What happens if we break up?
We hope you won't, but if you do, either partner can unlink the account at any time. Shared data is archived for both of you, and each person keeps their private space and personal history.
