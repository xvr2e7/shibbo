We walk rain or shine! On wet days, we bring clean microfiber towels to dry off muddy paws and bellies before unclipping. During summer heatwaves (above 85°F) or icy advisories, we adjust walks to shaded grass routes, test pavement temperatures with the back of our hand, and swap intense cardio for indoor puzzle enrichment + shorter relief breaks.
Absolutely. Over 40% of our solo-walk clients are leash-reactive, anxious, or selective. We never use aversive tools (no prong, choke, or shock collars). Instead, we use high-value treats, wide arcs, and quiet low-traffic routes so your dog can decompress safely.
