Why urban trees

Trees are infrastructure for people

In many cities, the neighborhoods with the least tree cover are up to 10°F hotter in summer. We plant where the canopy gap is widest.

🌡️

Cooler streets

Mature street trees lower surface temperatures, cut cooling costs, and reduce heat-related illness during heat waves.

💨

Cleaner air

Urban trees filter particulate pollution near busy roads and schools — where kids breathe it most.

🏘️

Stronger neighborhoods

Every planting is led with residents, not just for them. Neighbors adopt, water, and name the trees on their block.

Our model

From bare sidewalk to shaded street

We don't just plant trees — we commit to three years of care, because a planted tree isn't the goal. A grown one is.

Map the gaps

We use heat and canopy data to find the blocks that need trees most, then knock on doors to ask residents what they want.

Plant together

Neighbors, local schools, and volunteers plant native, climate-ready species on community planting days.

Water & care

Our crews and trained "tree stewards" water, mulch, and prune every tree for its first three summers.

Grow the canopy

We track every tree's survival publicly and reinvest in blocks until the canopy goal — 30% cover — is met.
