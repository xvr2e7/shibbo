Paper Moons

Eleven songs recorded in a rented farmhouse over one long, humid summer. Four friends, two tape machines, and not enough sleep.

Paper Moons
Hollow Static

A record about leaving places you loved.

"Paper Moons" began as a handful of voice memos recorded on tour buses and motel balconies. It grew into something bigger — a collection of songs about distance, memory, and the strange comfort of static on an empty highway radio. Produced by the band and recorded live to tape with minimal overdubs, this is Hollow Static at their rawest.

📀 Out October 18, 2024 — Vinyl, CD & Digital
