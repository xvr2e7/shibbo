The record

Written between checkout and dark.

Vacancy was written in the thin hour between checkout and dark, when every town looks like the last one and the sign still says there’s a room. June Pell’s voice sits close to the glass. The band plays like they’re trying not to wake the next unit over — until they aren’t.

Nine songs. A landline that won’t quite connect. A parking lot with opinions. An ice machine keeping time. A highway record that still believes in staying.

Produced by Mateo Ruiz & June Pell · Mixed by Priya Raman

Liner notes

Recorded mostly live in Units 4 and 11 of the Sundown Motor Lodge, with overdubs at Lantern Room, Los Angeles. Mastered by H. Okonkwo. Cover photograph by Cass Adler, from the balcony at blue hour, unretouched. Strings on “Vacancy (Reprise)” by the West Hall Quartet. All songs by Pell / Ruiz / Adler / Ito. Published by Lowlight Songs.

The band

Four people, one long hallway of night.

Aurora Motel started as a two-piece that could barely keep a PA from humming, and became a band that treats the overnight highway like a studio. Vacancy is their second full-length, after 2024’s Continental and the Lowlight EP.

  1. 2019 Two songs and a broken amp in a Tacoma basement.
  2. 2021 First van, first blown tire, first real chorus.
  3. 2023 Lowlight EP — recorded between shifts.
  4. 2024 Continental, the debut LP.
  5. 2026 Vacancy. Leave the light on.
