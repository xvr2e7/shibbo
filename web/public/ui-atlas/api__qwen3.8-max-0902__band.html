02 — Liner notes

How it was made.

Cannon Beach, OR · Sept 2023

We went to the coast in September, when the tourists were gone and the light started going soft. We rented a lifeguard tower that somebody had converted into a studio — one room, a window on three sides, sand in the carpet forever. The plan was to demo five songs. We came back with the album.

The rule was simple: first takes, real rooms, no click. You can hear the tide under “Sodium Light” if you turn it up, and the squeak of Jude's bass drum pedal on every track, which we eventually decided was just part of the band. June's voice was tracked live with the room mics, which is why the quiet songs sound like she's sitting next to you.

Halfremembered Summer is a record about the things you can't quite get back — old photographs, borrowed sweaters, the exact color of a streetlight at 2 a.m. It's for the drive home with the windows down.

“We don't remember summers —
we remember how they ended.” — from “Porch Light,” track 05

Left the porch light on for a version of you that moved out somewhere around twenty-two. Moths keep asking if I'm doing okay — I tell them, “ask me again in a day.” And the tape keeps rolling past the end of the song, and the hiss is where the memory belongs. If you call after midnight, don't apologize — I've been up rehearsing the sound of the tide.
