How it works

Three quiet steps. You only do the first.

  1. 1

    Murmur

    Hold the key and talk. Or type, paste, or leave it running in a meeting. Half-sentences and tangents are welcome.

  2. 2

    Settle

    Murmur drops the ums, finds the point, and writes a titled note with decisions, to-dos and dates pulled out.

  3. 3

    Recall

    Ask a plain question weeks later. Murmur answers from your own notes and shows you exactly where it came from.

Privacy

Good listeners keep things to themselves.

  • Transcribed on your device

    Speech becomes text on your Mac or phone. Raw audio is deleted once the note is written, unless you choose to keep it.

  • Never used for training

    Your notes are not used to train models, ours or anyone else's. That holds on every plan, including Free.

  • Delete means delete

    Remove a note and it's gone from our servers and backups within 30 days. Export first if you'd like a copy.

Questions

Asked often.

Does Murmur send a bot into my meetings?

No. Murmur listens through your own device's microphone and system audio, so nothing joins the call. It does remind you to tell people you're recording, because you should.

What happens to my audio?

It's transcribed on your device and deleted once the note is written. You can turn on “keep audio” per note if you want to replay the original.

What if Murmur gets something wrong?

Every line in a note links back to the moment in the transcript it came from. Tap to check it, fix it in place, and Murmur keeps your correction.

Can I just type?

Yes. Typed and pasted notes get the same treatment: titles, to-dos, links and answers. Typed notes are unlimited on every plan.

Which languages does it understand?

More than 30, and it copes when you switch language mid-sentence. Notes are written in whichever language you choose.

Can I get my notes out?

Any time, on any plan. Export a single note or your whole library as plain Markdown files.
