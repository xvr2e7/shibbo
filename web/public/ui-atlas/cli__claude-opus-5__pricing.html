FAQ

Questions, answered

How does per-user billing actually work?
You're billed only for members who are active in a given month — anyone who sends a message, joins a huddle, or reads a channel. Add someone mid-cycle and you're charged a prorated amount; deactivate someone and the unused balance lands back as account credit on your next invoice. Guests and single-channel collaborators are always free.
Can I switch plans or billing periods later?
Any time, from Admin → Billing. Upgrades take effect immediately with a prorated charge. Downgrades apply at the end of your current period so you keep what you've paid for, and switching from monthly to annual locks in the lower rate right away.
What happens to our messages on the free plan?
Nothing is deleted. Messages and files older than 90 days are archived rather than removed — they become searchable again the moment you upgrade to Pro or higher, with no import step.
Do you offer a trial of the paid plans?
Yes — 14 days of Pro or Business for your whole workspace, no card required. When the trial ends you drop back to Starter automatically; we never auto-charge you.
Which payment methods do you accept?
All major credit cards and Apple Pay for self-serve plans. Annual contracts of $5,000 or more can be paid by ACH, SEPA, wire, or purchase order with NET-30 terms. Invoices and VAT/GST receipts download from the billing page.
Is Tandem compliant with SOC 2 and GDPR?
Tandem is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, GDPR- and CCPA-ready with a standard DPA available for every plan, and offers HIPAA BAAs plus EU, UK, US, and AU data residency on Enterprise Grid. Our latest reports are in the Trust Center.
Can we self-host or get a discount?
We don't offer self-hosting, but Enterprise Grid supports customer-managed encryption keys so your data stays unreadable without them. Registered nonprofits and accredited schools get 85% off paid plans, and open-source projects get Pro free for public workspaces.
