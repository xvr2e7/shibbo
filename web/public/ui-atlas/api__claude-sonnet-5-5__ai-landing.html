FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find yours? Write to hello@murmur.app.

Does Murmur join my calls as a bot?

No. Murmur captures audio from your own device, so there's no awkward “Notetaker has joined” guest. We do recommend letting people know you're recording, and Murmur can post a friendly heads-up for you.

Where is my audio stored?

Transcription happens on your device whenever possible. Anything that syncs is end-to-end encrypted, and you can delete audio after transcription with a single toggle while keeping your notes.

Do you train AI on my notes?

Never. Your recordings, transcripts and notes are yours alone and are not used to train any models — ours or anyone else's.

How accurate are the transcripts?

Murmur handles accents, multiple speakers and background noise well, and you can fix any word by tapping it. Corrections teach Murmur names and jargon so the next note is even better.

Can I export my notes?

Anytime — Markdown, PDF, plain text or straight to Notion and Obsidian. No lock-in, no export fees.
