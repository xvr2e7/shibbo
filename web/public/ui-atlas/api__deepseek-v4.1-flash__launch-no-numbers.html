What it does

Four things, done properly.

We left out the parts of accounting software that freelancers never touch.

01

Connect once, then forget it

Read-only connections pull in every transaction from your bank and cards. Categorization learns your regulars — the same client, the same coffee shop, the same eleven software subscriptions.

02

Taxes, set aside as you go

Every payment that lands moves a slice into a set-aside figure you can see at a glance. Quarterly estimates update themselves, so April stops being a surprise.

03

Invoices that don't need chasing twice

Send an invoice, get a paid notification, and the income files itself into the right month. No retyping the same number into three places.

04

Hand your accountant a clean file

Profit and loss, expense categories, mileage, contractor payments, and a CSV export. Schedule C-shaped if you're in the US. They'll think you've been doing this all along.

Questions

Things people ask us.

Is it safe to connect my bank?

Connections are read-only and run through a regulated banking-data provider. We can see your transactions — we can't move money, and we never see or store your bank credentials. Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can disconnect or delete your data from settings at any time.

Do you handle 1099s and Schedule C?

If you're in the US, every expense category maps to a Schedule C line, and we track payments to contractors so 1099-NEC season isn't a scramble. To be clear: Tally Hall is a bookkeeping tool, not a CPA firm. Your accountant still signs off.

I already have years of spreadsheets.

Import a CSV and we'll match it against your bank feed, so you're not starting your history from zero. Messy columns are fine — you'll get a chance to review the mapping before anything is saved.

What happens when you start charging?

Anyone who signs up while we're new gets a founding account: free for twelve months after we launch public pricing, then $12 a month if you want to keep going. There's no card on file now, so there's nothing to cancel if you drift away.

It works today. We're onboarding in small batches so that every reply comes from someone who actually writes the code. Join the list and you'll get an invite within a week.
