About the Album

Echoes & Waves represents a bold artistic evolution for our band. This album was recorded over 18 months in studios across three countries, with production from renowned music producers and collaborators.

Each track tells a unique story, exploring themes of connection, transformation, and the human experience. The sonic palette ranges from intimate acoustic moments to expansive electronic soundscapes, creating a rich and immersive listening experience.

We poured our hearts and souls into this project, and we can't wait to share it with you. Whether you're discovering us for the first time or a longtime fan, we hope this album resonates with you.
