Why ALTO

A conference built like good frontend: small surface, deep craft.

ALTO brings 900 people who build for the browser to a converted riverside warehouse for two days. Single-session mornings so everyone shares the same context, two tracks after lunch, and long breaks because the hallway is where half the learning happens.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Anything else, write to hello@alto.conf. A human replies within a day.

Is there a call for papers?+

Yes. The CFP is open until 20 December 2026. We pay for travel and accommodation for every speaker, first-time speakers included, and offer mentoring for anyone who wants it. About a third of the lineup comes from the open call.

Which language are the talks in?+

English, with live human captions on both stages. The lightning talk session on day one accepts talks in Portuguese, captioned in both languages.

Will the talks be recorded?+

Every talk is recorded and published free on our site and YouTube within three weeks of the conference, with edited captions and transcripts. Ticket holders get access to the raw recordings the following week.

What about the workshops?+

Six half-day workshops run on Thursday 13 May at the same venue, each capped at 25 people. They cost €240 plus VAT and are booked separately once the programme is announced in February.

Can I get a refund or transfer my ticket?+

Full refunds until 14 April 2027. After that, you can transfer your ticket to someone else free of charge until 1 May. Team tickets can rename attendees at any time.

Do you have a code of conduct?+

Yes, and it applies to the venue, the parties and the online spaces. There is a response team on-site, reachable by phone or at the registration desk, and we have asked people to leave before.

Is Lisbon expensive in May?+

It is high season, so book early. Our hotel blocks start at €110 a night, and Cais do Sodré, Chiado and Santos are all within walking distance. Lunch out is typically €12–15.
