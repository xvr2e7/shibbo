Why ceramic

Built for the pour,
not for the shelf.

Plastic flexes and steals heat. Metal scalds the bed. Stoneware does neither — it holds a steady thermal curve from bloom to final drawdown, so your extraction stays honest.

Heat that stays put

3.2 mm walls and a thickened base hold the slurry at 92–94°C through the whole brew — no mid-pour temperature crash.

A forgiving geometry

The 60° cone with nine raised ribs keeps channels open and draws down evenly, even when your pour is a little imperfect.

Filter-friendly

Works with standard 01 and 02 cone filters — bleached, unbleached or cloth — with a sealed rim that stops bypass leaks.

Made for daily use

Chip-resistant, lead-free glaze. Dishwasher safe, stain resistant, and glazed on the base so it never scratches your counter.

Anatomy

Every millimetre
has a reason.

Four details we argued about for two years — and the reason the Mora pours the way it does.

1

Sealed filter rim

A precision-turned lip locks the paper in place and prevents side-bypass, so all the water travels through the bed.

2

Nine extraction ribs

Vertical ridges keep airflow beneath the filter for a clean, controlled drawdown between 2:45 and 3:15.

3

Thermal mass base

A thickened lower third stores heat and re-radiates it into the slurry, stabilising extraction across multi-pour recipes.

4

Glazed foot ring

The base is fully finished and lightly cushioned — it won't scratch a carafe, a server, or your kitchen counter.

Made in small batches

The ritual

Three minutes,
four moves.

Our house recipe: 18 g of coffee, medium-fine grind, 300 g of water at 94°C. Start the timer and pour along.

3:00
Ready

Brew timer

Press start and follow the steps on the right. The ring shows your progress through the full 3-minute recipe.

0:00

Bloom — 45 g

Start the timer and pour 45 g of water in a slow spiral. Let the bed swell and release its gas.

0:45

First pour — to 150 g

Pour gently from the centre outwards, keeping the water level steady just below the rim.

1:45

Second pour — to 300 g

Continue in concentric circles. Avoid the paper walls and keep the flow thin and even.

2:45

Drawdown & serve

Swirl the dripper once, let it finish draining by 3:00–3:15, then pour into your favourite cup.

Back to the dripper

The Mora is shaped for standard cone filters — 01 size for a single cup and 02 size for up to 400 ml. Bleached, unbleached and reusable cloth filters all sit properly in the sealed rim.

Dishwasher safe, yes — the glaze is fully vitrified and stain resistant. Please avoid the microwave and direct flame; stoneware heats unevenly there and can craze over time.

Recommended but not essential. The thick walls absorb less heat than thin ceramic, but a quick rinse with hot water before brewing keeps your slurry temperature rock steady.

Every piece is hand-glazed and kiln-fired in small batches, so tone, speckle and surface texture vary a little from one to the next. Those variations are the evidence of the making, not a defect.
