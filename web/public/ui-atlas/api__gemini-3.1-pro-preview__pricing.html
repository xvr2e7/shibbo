Frequently asked questions

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Reach out to our customer support team.

How does billing work?

We charge per active user per month. If you add new users during your billing cycle, we'll prorate the charge. If a user is deactivated, we'll credit your account for the unused time.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your team settings dashboard. Prorated charges or credits will automatically be applied to your next invoice.

What happens at the end of the 14-day trial?

If you don't enter your credit card information before the trial ends, your workspace will automatically be downgraded to the Free Starter plan. You won't lose any data, but you will lose access to premium features.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Yes, we love supporting organizations that make the world better. We offer a 50% discount on the Business plan for qualifying non-profits and educational institutions. Contact our support team to apply.
