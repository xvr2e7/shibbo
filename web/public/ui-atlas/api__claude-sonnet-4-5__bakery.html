The Odd Loaf Story

We're a small-batch bakery in the heart of the neighbourhood, dedicated to the craft of traditional sourdough baking.

Every loaf is made with organic flour, filtered water, and our 5-year-old starter (affectionately named "Bubbles"). We bake fresh every morning, starting at 4am, so you get the warmest, crustiest loaves possible.

Our small operation means limited quantities and lots of love in every loaf. That's why we sell out by noon most days!
