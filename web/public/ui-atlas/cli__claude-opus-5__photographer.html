Hello, I'm Elowen

I photograph the in-between — the second glance, the hand on a back, the laugh that arrives before the toast is finished.

Fifteen years in, I still believe the best frames are the ones nobody arranged. My work lives somewhere between a documentary and a love letter: warm, grain-flecked, and honest about how a day actually felt at 11pm when the band played one more song.

— Elowen

About

Fifteen years,
a little grain

I grew up in a house full of shoeboxes — my mother's photographs, unlabelled and out of order, which is how I learned that the pictures people keep are almost never the formal ones. They're the blurry ones. The ones where someone is mid-sentence.

So that's what I make. I shoot a hybrid of medium-format film and digital, I work quietly, and I will never ask you to do something twice for the camera. I'll hold your flowers, find you a drink, and tell you when your father is crying behind you.

I'm based in Charleston with my partner and a very serious whippet named Bishop. I travel for roughly half the weddings I photograph, and I have never once regretted getting on the plane.
