Questions

The stuff people ask first.

Do we both need our own subscription?

No. One Together subscription covers both partners' accounts, devices and shared pots. You just invite your partner by email and they join the same workspace.

Is it safe to connect our bank accounts?

We connect through read-only providers used by major banks. Tandem can see balances and transactions, but it can never move money, make a payment, or change anything in your accounts. Everything is encrypted with 256-bit TLS in transit and at rest.

What if we don't want to merge everything?

Most couples don't, and that's fine. You choose exactly which accounts feed the shared budget. Everything else stays private — including a separate fun-money budget each of you can spend without a single question.

Can we split unevenly if one of us earns more?

Yes. Choose 50/50, split proportionally by income, or set your own custom percentages per category. You can change the split at any time — Tandem recalculates going forward and never rewrites your history.

What happens if we break up?

It happens, and we've thought about it. Either partner can export the full ledger as CSV, and the account holder can remove the other person without losing their own data. No lock-in, no hostage-taking.

Which countries and banks are supported?

Tandem works with over 12,000 banks and card issuers across the US, Canada, the UK and the EU. If your bank isn't supported yet, you can add transactions manually or import statements and still use every shared feature.
