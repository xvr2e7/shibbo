FAQ

Fair questions.

Anything else? Write to hello@relay.app — a human replies, usually within the hour.

Does Relay modify anything in my help desk?
No. Connections are strictly read-only. Relay never edits, tags, closes, or replies to tickets. Your agents' workflow stays exactly as it is — Relay just listens.
How accurate is the clustering, really?
In blind tests against human triage, Relay's themes matched 94% of the time. Every theme links to its source tickets, so you can audit any claim in one click. You can also merge, rename, or split themes — Relay learns your taxonomy.
What happens to customer data?
Tickets are encrypted in transit and at rest, PII is redacted before analysis, and your data is never used to train shared models. Relay is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR-compliant, and offers EU residency on Scale. Delete your workspace and everything is gone within 30 days.
Why weekly instead of a real-time dashboard?
Because dashboards are where insight goes to be ignored. A weekly rhythm matches how product teams actually plan. That said, Team and Scale plans include spike alerts — if something jumps 3× overnight, you'll hear about it before Monday.
Can I try it on last month's tickets?
Yes — the trial backfills up to 90 days of history, so your first report arrives with real trends, not a cold start. Most teams see their first "oh, wow" theme within ten minutes of connecting.
