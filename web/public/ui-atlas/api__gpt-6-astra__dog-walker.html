Can we meet before the first walk?

Absolutely. Every new friendship starts with a free meet & greet. We’ll talk through your dog’s routine, meet on their home turf, and take a little stroll together. There’s no obligation to book afterward.

Are walks solo or with a group?

Our listed walks are one-on-one, so your dog gets our full attention. If you have more than one dog at home, tell us at your meet & greet and we’ll put together a plan for your little pack.

What if my dog is shy or a little reactive?

Let’s talk about it. We’ll learn their triggers, favorite rewards, and comfort level before agreeing on a route and routine. We’re honest about whether we’re the right fit, and we never rush a new friendship.

Do you walk in all weather?

A little drizzle doesn’t stop the fun. In extreme heat, storms, or unsafe conditions, we’ll discuss a shorter potty break and indoor enrichment instead. Their comfort and safety always come first.

How do I book a regular walking schedule?

Start with a meet & greet request and tell us your preferred days. We’ll confirm neighborhood coverage and availability, then agree on a regular schedule that works for you. Nothing is booked or charged until we’ve confirmed the details together.
