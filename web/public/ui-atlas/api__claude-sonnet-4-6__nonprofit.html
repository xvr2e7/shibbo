Our Mission

Cities need trees as much as they need roads.

We work with city governments, local businesses, and volunteers to plant native trees in underserved urban neighborhoods — where the heat island effect is harshest and green space is rarest.

Community-led: Every planting site is chosen by local residents and neighborhood associations.

Native species only: We plant trees native to each region, ensuring long-term ecosystem health.

5-year guarantee: We monitor and maintain every tree for five years after planting.

Fully transparent: Every tree gets a GPS tag. Donors can see exactly where their trees grow.

Environmental Impact

What 187,000 trees actually do.

Urban trees aren't just pretty. They're critical infrastructure for healthier, more equitable cities.

🌡️

Beat the Heat Island

Our trees reduce local temperatures by an average of 3–7°F in planted neighborhoods, dramatically cutting energy costs and heat-related illness.

💨

Cleaner Air

Our urban forest absorbs an estimated 4,200 tons of CO₂ annually and filters pollutants linked to asthma and respiratory disease.

🌧️

Stormwater Control

Each mature tree intercepts up to 1,500 gallons of stormwater per year, reducing flooding and sewer overflow in dense city blocks.

🐦

Biodiversity Corridor

Native tree species support 70% more native insect life than ornamental trees — rebuilding urban food webs and bird populations.

🏘️

Property & Health Equity

Studies show our neighborhoods see 15% higher property values and measurable drops in crime and stress-related health visits.

👐

Volunteer Hours

Over 68,000 volunteer hours logged since 2014. We're building environmental stewards, not just planting trees.
