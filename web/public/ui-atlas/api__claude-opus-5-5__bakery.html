Why odd?

No two loaves look alike. We like it that way.

"Every loaf is shaped by hand at three in the morning. Some come out round. Some come out… less round."

Odd Loaf started in 2016 in a rented corner of a pub kitchen, with one oven and a starter called Gertrude (now nine, still bubbly). We moved to Caraway Street in 2019 and have sold out pretty much every day since.

We use stone-milled flour from two farms within sixty miles, sea salt, water, and time. That's the whole list. The oddness is free.
