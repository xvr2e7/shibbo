About

Four people, one room,
no click track

Hollow Pines formed in 2019 in a rented farmhouse outside Asheville, North Carolina. What started as field recordings and improvised sessions became something none of them expected — a band that sounds like the space between late afternoon and dusk.

Still Light Between the Trees was recorded over nine days in the shell of a decommissioned chapel in rural Vermont. No overdubs. No isolation booths. Just the room and whatever the room gave back.
