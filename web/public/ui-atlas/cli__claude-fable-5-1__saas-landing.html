What's in the report

Built for the Monday roadmap meeting, not for staring at a dashboard.

Dashboards wait for you to look. Relay shows up with the answer.

Trends, not totals

Every theme is compared to the previous four weeks. You see what's accelerating before it becomes a churn conversation.

Weighted by revenue

Connect your CRM and Relay weights each theme by the plan and ARR of the accounts reporting it. Ten Enterprise tickets outrank a hundred free-tier ones.

Real quotes, cited

Each theme includes the two or three customer quotes that explain it best, linked straight back to the original ticket.

Closed-loop tracking

Ship a fix and Relay watches the theme fade. Your release notes finally come with proof that the problem actually went away.

Your cadence, your channel

Weekly by default, daily if you need it. Delivered to Slack, email, Notion, or Linear with one click to turn a theme into an issue.

Private by design

PII is stripped before analysis. Data stays in your region, never trains anyone's models, and is deleted the moment you disconnect.

Questions

Things people ask before connecting.

Do I need to tag tickets for this to work?

No. Relay reads the full conversation, including customer replies and agent notes, and groups tickets by what they're actually about. Existing tags are used as a hint, not a requirement.

How is this different from the reporting in my helpdesk?

Helpdesk reporting is built for support managers: response times, volume, CSAT. Relay is built for product managers. It answers "what should we build or fix," not "how fast did we reply."

What happens to customer data?

Personal information is removed before any analysis runs. Data is encrypted at rest, stored in your chosen region, and never used to train models. Disconnecting deletes everything within 24 hours.

How accurate is the theme grouping?

In onboarding, Relay shows you its first pass and you can merge, split, or rename themes. After that, it learns from your corrections. Most teams stop adjusting after two or three reports.

Can I see the report before I sign up?

Yes. Connect a helpdesk and Relay generates a report from your last 30 days of tickets within an hour, before the trial clock starts.
