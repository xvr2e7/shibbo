Good questions

The things couples
ask us first.

What if we earn very different amounts?
This is the single most common setup, and it's what Tandem is designed for. Instead of splitting everything 50/50, you can split shared costs proportionally — so the partner earning more contributes more, and both people keep an equal percentage of their income as their own. You can switch to 50/50, a fixed amount, or full pooling at any time.
Do we have to merge all our accounts?
Not at all. Most couples start with one shared account or shared card, and keep their personal accounts private. Tandem only ever shows your partner the accounts you explicitly bring into the shared plan. Private stays private.
Is my banking data secure?
Yes. We use 256-bit encryption in transit and at rest, and read-only connections through regulated open-banking providers — meaning Tandem can never move money out of your accounts. We never sell your data, and there are no third-party ads in the app. You can delete your account and all its data permanently at any time.
What happens when we disagree about a budget?
Changes to the shared plan require both partners to confirm — one person can't quietly redirect the budget overnight. If you can't agree, Tandem shows you the trade-offs side by side (what you gain and what you give up for each option), which tends to make the conversation a lot shorter.
Can we use Tandem before we live together?
Absolutely — it's actually a great low-stakes way to talk about money. Plenty of couples use the free plan to track shared holidays, gifts or a date-night fund long before they share a postcode.
How do we cancel?
In two taps, inside the app, no phone call and no retention flow. If you cancel during your trial you won't be charged at all, and your data stays available on the free plan or can be exported as CSV.
