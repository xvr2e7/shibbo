FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Tally Hall new?

Yes. We launched recently and are onboarding our first users now. We're building in the open and would value your feedback as we go.

Do I need to know accounting?

No. Tally Hall is designed for freelancers who aren't accountants. Plain-language labels and suggested categories do most of the work.

Are the tax estimates official?

No. Set-aside figures are estimates to help you plan. They are not tax advice. For filing decisions, please consult a qualified tax professional.

How is my financial data handled?

Bank connections are read-only, and we never move money on your behalf. You can export or delete your data at any time.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. Cancel from your settings page and you won't be charged again.
