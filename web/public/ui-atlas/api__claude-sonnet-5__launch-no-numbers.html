FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

We're new — so we'd rather be upfront than oversell.

Is Tally Hall really brand new? +
Yes — we launched last week. We don't have a long customer history yet, but the product has been in private testing for months and we're actively building based on feedback from our first users.
Is my financial data safe? +
Yes. We use bank-level 256-bit encryption and never store your bank credentials directly. We also never sell your data — full stop.
Can I import my existing spreadsheets? +
Yes. You can import CSV files from spreadsheets or other bookkeeping tools during onboarding, and our team will personally help you get set up.
What if I need help getting started? +
Email us any time — right now that means talking directly to one of our two co-founders, not a support bot.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes, with one click, no phone calls or retention forms. If you cancel during your trial, you won't be charged anything.
