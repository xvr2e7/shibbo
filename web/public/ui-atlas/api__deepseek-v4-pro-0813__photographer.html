Meet Your Photographer

Elena Hartwell

Based in Northern California, traveling worldwide. I believe in real moments, soft light, and the beauty of unscripted emotion.

350+ Weddings Photographed

Stories told through light & emotion

For over a decade, I've had the honor of documenting love stories across the globe — from intimate elopements in Big Sur to grand celebrations in Tuscany. My approach is unobtrusive yet intentional, blending fine art composition with a documentary eye.

I don't just capture what your wedding looks like — I capture what it feels like. The nervous flutter before the first look, the joyful tears during toasts, the quiet stolen glances between newlyweds. These are the moments that matter most.

"Photography is the art of making moments eternal. Every wedding I shoot reminds me why I fell in love with this craft."
Elena Hartwell
