Combat Climate Change

Trees absorb carbon dioxide and help reduce greenhouse gases. Each tree planted contributes to a cooler, healthier planet for future generations.

💨

Improve Air Quality

Urban trees filter pollutants and produce oxygen. Studies show trees can improve air quality by up to 25% in city neighborhoods.

👥

Build Community

Our planting events bring neighbors together. We create green spaces where people can connect, exercise, and enjoy nature.

Environmental Impact

CO₂ Reduction

Our trees will offset over 45,000 tons of CO₂ over their lifetime, equivalent to taking 9,700 cars off the road for a year.

Oxygen Production

A mature tree produces oxygen for two people annually. Our 15,000 trees provide oxygen for 30,000 people each year.

Water Management

Trees reduce stormwater runoff by 50-80%, decreasing flooding and protecting water quality in urban watersheds.

Urban Heat Reduction

Trees cool cities by 2-8°C through shade and evapotranspiration, reducing energy costs and heat-related illness.

Biodiversity

Urban trees provide habitat for birds, insects, and wildlife, restoring ecosystems in concrete jungles.

Health Benefits

Green spaces reduce stress, improve mental health, and encourage physical activity in urban communities.
