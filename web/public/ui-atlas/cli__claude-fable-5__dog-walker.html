Our Story

Walks by people who'd rather be outside with dogs anyway.

Happy Trails started with one borrowed leash and a neighbor's golden retriever who needed more exercise than her owner's knees could offer. Six years later, we're a small team of walkers who treat every dog like our own — because for forty minutes at a time, they basically are.

Every walker is background-checked, pet first-aid certified, and bonded & insured. We keep our client list small on purpose, so your dog always sees a familiar face at the door.
