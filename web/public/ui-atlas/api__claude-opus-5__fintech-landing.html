Questions

The things couples ask us

Do we need a joint bank account?

Not at all. Ampersand sits on top of whatever setup you already have — fully separate, fully joint, or the common messy middle. If you do have a joint account, we'll track it as its own pot.

Can my partner see everything I spend?

Only what you share. By default, your personal accounts are visible to you alone. You can share a whole account, a single category, or individual transactions — and un-share them later.

How does the income-based split work?

Tell us each take-home pay and we'll calculate a proportional share (say 62/38). Every shared expense is divided by that ratio automatically, and the running "who owes who" balance updates in real time.

Is my banking data safe?

We use read-only connections via regulated open-banking providers, 256-bit encryption in transit and at rest, and we never store your bank credentials. We don't sell data — ever. That's what the $8 is for.

What happens if we break up?

It happens. One tap to unlink and each of you keeps your own account, history and budgets intact — nothing is shared retroactively, and shared data is removed from the other side.
