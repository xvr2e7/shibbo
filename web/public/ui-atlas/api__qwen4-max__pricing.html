Questions, answered

Still curious? Ping us at hello@ripple.chat — we reply fast.

How does per-user billing work?▾
You only pay for active members. When someone joins mid-cycle we prorate their seat; when someone is deactivated, we credit the unused time back to your account automatically. Guests are always free on every plan.
Can I switch between monthly and annual?▾
Anytime. Switching to annual applies the 20% discount immediately and credits the remainder of your monthly cycle. Switching back to monthly takes effect at your next renewal.
What happens when my trial ends?▾
Nothing scary. Your workspace gracefully drops to the Free plan — no messages are deleted, and anything beyond Free limits (like older history) is simply paused until you upgrade. No credit card is required to trial Pro.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?▾
Yes! Registered nonprofits, schools, and open-source communities get 50% off Pro and Business. Apply from your workspace settings under Billing → Discounts, and we'll verify within two business days.
Is my data secure?▾
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Ripple is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Business plans add data residency controls, and Enterprise offers customer-managed encryption keys.
Can I cancel anytime?▾
Of course — cancel in two clicks from your billing settings, no phone calls or retention mazes. Annual plans are refunded pro-rata within the first 30 days, and you can export all your data at any time.
