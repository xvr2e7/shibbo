Questions, answered

Everything teams usually ask before switching.

Can I try the paid plans for free?
Yes. Pro and Business include a 14-day trial with every feature unlocked — no credit card required. When the trial ends, your workspace falls back to the Free plan and nothing is charged.
How do you count billable users?
We count anyone who can log in and post or read messages in your workspace. Guests you've invited are always free, unlimited on Pro and Business. Deactivated members stop counting at the end of the billing cycle.
What happens if I add or remove seats mid-cycle?
Upgrades are prorated to the day — you only pay for the remaining time in your cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period, so nobody loses access mid-workstream.
Can I migrate from Slack or Microsoft Teams?
Absolutely. Our free migration assistant imports channels, direct messages, threads, reactions, files, and user profiles — including timestamps and read states. Most teams complete a full workspace migration in under an hour.
Do you offer nonprofit, education, or startup discounts?
We do. Eligible nonprofits, schools, and students get 40% off, and startups with fewer than 20 people get Pro free for 12 months. Send us a note and we'll sort you out.
How is our data protected?
Everything is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Business plans add SAML SSO, SCIM provisioning, EU/US data residency, configurable retention, and audit logs. We're SOC 2 Type II certified and HIPAA compliant with a BAA available on Business.
Can I pay by invoice or purchase order?
Yes — annual Business contracts can be paid by invoice, wire transfer, or purchase order. We also support annual prepay cards and monthly billing for self-serve accounts.
