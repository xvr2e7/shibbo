Questions

Before you dive in

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. Bank connections are read-only and encrypted end-to-end. Tally Hall can never move or withdraw your money — we only read transaction data to categorize it for you.

You just launched. Should I trust it?

Fair question. We'd rather be upfront than fake reviews we don't have. What we can offer: a free tier with no commitment, fast support from the people actually building it, and a product that's been our own daily bookkeeping tool for months before anyone else touched it.

Does it replace my accountant?

No — Tally Hall keeps your books organized and gives you solid estimates, but we'd always recommend a tax professional for filing. Exporting your clean records to them takes one click.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, no contracts and no cancellation fees. Export your data whenever you'd like, whether you stay or go.
