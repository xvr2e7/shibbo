Questions

The things you’re wondering.

Who is Tally Hall for?

Freelancers, contractors and one-person studios — designers, developers, writers, consultants, photographers, coaches. If you invoice people for a living and currently juggle a spreadsheet, a banking app and a shoebox of receipts, it’s built for you.

Do I still need an accountant?

Not to keep books. Tally Hall records your income and expenses, keeps your categories tidy, and holds a slice of every payment in a tax jar so you know what’s owed. When you want a professional to file or review, the handoff export is built for that — and we’ll point you at people who work with freelancers.

Which countries and currencies?

Today: USD, EUR and GBP, with tax set-aside rates you configure yourself. Tally Hall is a record-keeping tool — it doesn’t file returns with any tax authority, and we won’t pretend otherwise. Bank import is the next piece to ship, with more currencies to follow.

Is my financial data safe?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Bank connections will be read-only — we can see transactions, never move money — and we don’t sell or share anything. We’ll publish our full security notes before bank import rolls out.

What if I don’t like it?

Export everything and walk away — CSV, PDF, and a full JSON dump of your data, no lock-in. If you’ve paid the founder rate, you’ve got 30 days to change your mind for a full refund. We’d rather lose a customer than keep a resentful one.

Why launch with no reviews?

Because we’re a week old, and because a review from someone who doesn’t exist is worse than no review at all. The honest version of this page is the one you’re reading. Give us a month and we’ll replace it with something better.

When do I actually get in?

Invites go out in small batches, roughly twenty a week, starting from the founding list. You’ll hear from us the week before your invite lands, and you can skip your turn if you’d rather wait.
