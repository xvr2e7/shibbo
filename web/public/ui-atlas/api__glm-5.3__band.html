      02LINER NOTES

      Notes from the garage.

      Takes 1 through 4, as-is. The space heater stays in the mix.

      We wrote Palm Reader over eleven nights in a rented garage on Olive Avenue — door up, space heater doing its best, a four-track that only works when it rains. One microphone, no phones, and a house rule that anything the tape caught stayed on the record: the hiss, the sirens on Olive, the dog next door, who honestly has great timing.

      The songs came out somewhere between a road trip and a séance — motels, freeways, palm trees that turn out to be poles with leaves glued on, and the specific loneliness of a vending machine at 2 a.m. We didn’t fix much. If a take felt like the truth, it stayed.

      “Anything the tape caught stayed on the record. The dog is basically the fifth member.” — DARIA, BETWEEN TAKES 2 AND 3
