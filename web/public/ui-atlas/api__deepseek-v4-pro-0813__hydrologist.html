About

I'm a hydrologist with two decades of fieldwork tracing rivers from their headwaters to their deltas — measuring flow, mapping floodplains, and listening to what moving water has to say. My scientific work focuses on riverine ecosystems, sediment transport, and the long-term effects of drought on freshwater systems.

But the rivers I study are not just systems of discharge and load. They are archives of memory, carriers of sediment and story alike. My essays attempt to translate the language of rivers — their meanders, braids, and seasonal rhythms — into a human register, exploring how water shapes geography, history, and the inner landscape of attention.

Ph.D. in Hydrology & Fluvial Geomorphology · Fieldwork across the Mississippi, Colorado, Columbia, and Mekong basins
