FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

Can't find what you're looking for? Call us at (555) 555-0142—a real person will pick up.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! We're welcoming new patients of all ages, and we can usually see you within a week. Emergency patients are seen the same day whenever possible.

What if I'm nervous about dental visits?

Let us know when you book. We'll schedule extra time, explain each step before we do it, and offer comfort options like headphones, nitrous oxide or oral sedation.

How much does a first visit cost without insurance?

Our new patient special is $99 and includes a comprehensive exam, full digital X-rays and a personalised treatment plan. Cleanings are included for Smile Club members.

When should my child first see a dentist?

We recommend a first visit by your child's first birthday or within six months of their first tooth. These early visits are short, fun and focused on building comfort.

Do you offer weekend or evening appointments?

Yes—we're open until 7 PM on Tuesdays and Thursdays and from 8 AM to 2 PM on Saturdays.
