The shade gap

Some streets are up to 10°F hotter than others.

Decades of unequal investment mean low-income blocks often have a fraction of the tree cover of wealthier ones. That gap shows up in summer heat, asthma rates, and energy bills.

A single mature street tree can lower surrounding air temperatures, soak up stormwater, and filter pollution — all while making the sidewalk a place people actually want to be.

How we work

We don’t just plant. We stay.

Listen first

We knock on doors and host block meetings. Residents choose where trees go and which species they want — fruit, flowering, or deep shade.

Plant together

Crews and neighbors plant right-sized, climate-ready trees on planting days, with soil prepared and utilities mapped ahead of time.

Care for three years

Our stewards water, mulch, and prune every tree through its most fragile years — and train neighbors to become block tree captains.
