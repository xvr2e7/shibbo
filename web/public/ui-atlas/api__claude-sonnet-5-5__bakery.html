About Odd Loaf

We'd rather make one hundred and forty loaves properly than four hundred in a hurry.

Odd Loaf started in a rented kitchen with a bag of flour and a jar of starter that refused to behave. Years later the starter (we call her Doris) is still in charge, and the loaves are still a little uneven. We think that's the point.

We mill nothing ourselves and pretend nothing. The flour comes from a mill a couple of hours up the road, the salt is plain, and the only secret is waiting. Every loaf is shaped by hand, proofed overnight and baked hot enough to make the crust sing as it cools.
