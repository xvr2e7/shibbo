Questions, answered

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade any time from your workspace settings. Changes are prorated automatically.

What counts as a member?

Anyone with a full account in your workspace. Guests on Pro are free for up to five guests per paid seat.

Is there a discount for nonprofits or schools?

We offer 50% off Pro for verified nonprofits and educational institutions. Contact us to apply.

What happens to my history if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Messages older than 90 days become hidden and return if you upgrade again.
