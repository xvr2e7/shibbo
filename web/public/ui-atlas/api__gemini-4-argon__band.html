For their third full-length album, Brooklyn four-piece The Velvet Static packed two station wagons with tangled patch cables, a temperamental reel-to-reel machine, and three crates of paperbacks, retreating to an unheated barn in the Catskills.

Rejecting computer screens and quantized grids, Echoes in the Suburbs was captured entirely live to tape. You can hear the radiator clanking at the end of "Screen Door Waltz" and the rain hitting the tin roof during the bridge of "AM Static (1610 kHz)." It is an album about distance, memory, and the strange comfort of coming home.
