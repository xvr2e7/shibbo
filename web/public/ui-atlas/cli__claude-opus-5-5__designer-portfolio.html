How I work

No rigid playbook — but a few habits that reliably lead to better products.

01 — LISTEN

Understand deeply

I start with people, not pixels: interviews, support tickets, analytics and long conversations with engineers.

02 — FRAME

Name the problem

Most design debt is a framing problem. I write it down plainly so the whole team agrees on what we're solving.

03 — MAKE

Prototype early

Rough, interactive, real-data prototypes beat polished mockups. I test in days, not weeks.

04 — SHIP

Stay close to code

I pair with engineers through launch and measure what changed — then iterate on what we learned.

I believe the best interfaces disappear — leaving people with a sense of clarity rather than a memory of the screen.

I studied interaction design at Malmö University, then spent my early career in agencies before moving in-house to lead design at fast-growing product companies. These days I work independently with a small number of teams each year.

Outside of work you'll find me throwing pottery, cycling the coast, or rearranging the same three bookshelves.
