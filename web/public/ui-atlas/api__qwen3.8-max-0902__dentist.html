07 · Questions

Asked often,
answered honestly.

Yes, and that’s fine — about a third of our patients tell us exactly this. We offer a “look only, no tools” first visit, a hand signal that pauses everything instantly, noise-cancelling headphones, and sedation options from nitrous to oral sedation for bigger work. Nobody counts your flossing sins here.

We’re in-network with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife and Guardian, and we file for everyone else as a courtesy. Tomás will tell you your real out-of-pocket figure before treatment starts — not after.

Call the emergency line — a human answers, including weekends. We hold same-day emergency slots every day, and if it can’t wait until Monday we’ll open the studio for you.

Professionally supervised whitening is safe for enamel; we pre-treat sensitivity and use desensitising gels throughout. Results typically hold 18–36 months depending on your relationship with coffee, which we will not judge.

Our full fee guide is published above — we think prices should be public. Treatment over $800 can be split into 0% monthly instalments, and the Pearl Club membership covers routine care entirely for $29/month.

From about age one for a knee-to-knee peek, though most kids start around three. First real visits are free “happy visits” — a ride in the chair, a mirror tour, a sticker, and zero drilling. We’re playing the long game.
