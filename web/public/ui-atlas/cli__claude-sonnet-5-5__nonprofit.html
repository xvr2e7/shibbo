Why trees

Shade is a public health issue.

Neighborhoods with fewer trees can be up to 10°F hotter in summer. Trees are one of the simplest, most lasting ways to fix that.

🌡️

Cooler streets

A mature tree can lower surrounding air temperatures and cut energy bills for nearby homes.

💨

Cleaner air

Leaves capture dust and pollutants, helping kids with asthma and neighbors of every age breathe easier.

🌧️

Fewer floods

Roots and soil soak up stormwater, easing pressure on aging drains during heavy rain.

How we plant

From a request to a thriving tree.

We don't just drop saplings and leave. Every tree gets a plan and a caretaker.

Neighbors ask

Residents request trees on their block, or we map the areas with the lowest canopy.

We choose well

Our arborists match species to soil, space, and climate for long-term health.

We plant together

Community planting days bring volunteers and neighbors out with shovels.

We keep caring

Three years of watering, mulching, and pruning give each tree a strong start.

Our approach

Trees are planted by neighbors, not for them.

Every project starts with conversations. We hire locally, train youth crews in urban forestry, and ask residents which trees they want to live beside.

The result is a canopy people love, protect, and pass on.
