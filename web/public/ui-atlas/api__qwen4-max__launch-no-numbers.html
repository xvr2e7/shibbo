Fair questions

The things you'd want to ask a week-old app

You launched last week. Why should I trust you with my books?
Fair skepticism. Here's what we can offer instead of a track record: your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, exports are free and complete from day one, we never sell or share your data, and we've committed in writing to a 90-day notice-and-export window if the product ever winds down. Start with a month of data and see how it feels — you lose nothing but a few minutes.
Is Tally Hall a replacement for my accountant?
No, and we won't pretend otherwise. Tally Hall keeps clean, categorized records and gives you running tax estimates so filing is faster and cheaper — but it's bookkeeping, not tax advice. Many freelancers will still want an accountant at filing time; we make their job (and their bill) smaller.
What happens after the free year?
Founding members lock in $6/month — half the standard $12 — for as long as they stay subscribed. We'll email you 30 days before your free year ends. If you do nothing, you're never auto-charged: you choose to upgrade or export your data and walk away.
Which countries and currencies do you support?
At launch: USD invoicing and set-aside rates you configure yourself, which works for freelancers anywhere billing in dollars. The tax estimates are rate-based, not jurisdiction-specific — you set the percentage. Multi-currency invoicing is next on the public roadmap.
Can I import my existing mess of spreadsheets?
Yes — CSV import for income and expenses is live, with a column-mapping step so your existing spreadsheet doesn't need reformatting. If your format is weird, email it to us; as a week-old startup, we will personally help you migrate. That's a perk of being early.
Who's behind this?
Two freelancers who got tired of doing books in a spreadsheet named FINAL_v3_REAL.xlsx. We built the tool we wanted, shipped it last week, and we answer every support email ourselves — usually within a day, often within the hour.
