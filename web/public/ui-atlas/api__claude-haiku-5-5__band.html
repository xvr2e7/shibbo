Select a track Halcyon Pines — Soft Machinery

Four people,
one cabin,
no signal.

Halcyon Pines started in a Ohio basement in 2017 and somehow ended up in a Vermont cabin with a broken woodstove and a borrowed upright piano. Soft Machinery is what came out of that winter: songs about small appliances, long drives, and the feeling of being slightly out of sync with the world.

Recorded live to tape, mixed by hand, and pressed on translucent amber vinyl. No click tracks were harmed in the making of this record.
