About

Design is a conversation, not a handoff.

I've spent the last decade embedded inside product teams — from three-person startups to 200-person scale-ups — turning fuzzy problems into shipped, measurable work.

I care most about the unglamorous parts: the empty states, the error messages, the fifth iteration nobody sees. That's usually where the real product lives.

Previously at Pleo and Tutti, and a graduate of The Royal Danish Academy. Based in Copenhagen, working with teams everywhere.

Flows, wireframes, and information architecture — getting structure right before pixels.
