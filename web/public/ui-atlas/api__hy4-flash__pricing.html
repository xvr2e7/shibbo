Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan later?
Absolutely! You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account settings. If you upgrade, you'll be prorated for the remainder of your billing cycle.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover) as well as PayPal and wire transfers for annual enterprise plans.
Is there a discount for non-profits or education?
Yes, we offer a 50% discount for registered non-profit organizations and educational institutions. Please contact our sales team with proof of status to get started.
How does the 14-day free trial work?
You get full access to all Pro features for 14 days. We don't require a credit card to sign up. If you decide to upgrade at the end of the trial, you can add your payment details then.
What happens to my data if I downgrade to Free?
If you downgrade to the Free plan, you will retain access to your messages within the 90-day history limit. Older messages and extra features will be archived but can be restored if you upgrade again.
