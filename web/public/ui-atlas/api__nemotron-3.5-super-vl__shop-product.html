Hand-thrown from high-fired stoneware ceramic, the ClayPour dripper retains heat beautifully for a stable brew temperature and a sweeter, more balanced cup. The conical shape and internal ridges guide water flow for an even extraction, while the large central hole prevents bypass and channeling.

Pairs perfectly with #02 or #04 cone filters. Compatible with most 1–4 cup servers and mugs.

  • Material: High-fired stoneware ceramic
  • Filter size: #02 cone (1–2 cup) / #04 cone (4 cup)
  • Dimensions: approx. 11.5 × 11.5 × 9.5 cm
  • Weight: approx. 320 g
  • Origin: Made in Portugal

Dishwasher safe. For best longevity, hand wash with mild soap and a soft sponge. Avoid sudden temperature changes (do not move directly from freezer to hot water). Microwave safe.

How to Brew

1

Place filter

Insert a #02 cone filter and rinse with hot water to remove paper taste and preheat the dripper.

2

Add coffee

Add medium-fine ground coffee (about 15g for one cup) and shake gently to level the bed.

3

Bloom

Pour just enough water to saturate the grounds. Wait 30–45 seconds for the bloom.

4

Pour

Pour in slow concentric circles, keeping the water level steady until you reach your target weight.

Frequently Asked Questions

What filter size should I use?

Use #02 cone filters for the 1–2 cup size and #04 cone filters for the 4 cup size.

Is it microwave safe?

Yes, the ceramic dripper is microwave safe, though we recommend preheating with hot water instead.

Can I put it in the dishwasher?

Absolutely. It is fully dishwasher safe, though hand washing will preserve the glaze longer.
