Frequently asked questions

Still curious? Here's what people usually ask.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your workspace settings. Changes are prorated automatically.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or students?

Yes — nonprofits and accredited educational institutions get 50% off Pro and Business plans. Contact our team to apply.

What happens after the free trial ends?

Your workspace automatically reverts to the Free plan. No credit card is required to start a trial, and you won't be charged unexpectedly.

Is there a limit on integrations?

Free includes 2 integrations. Pro and above include unlimited integrations, including Google Drive, GitHub, Jira, and more.
