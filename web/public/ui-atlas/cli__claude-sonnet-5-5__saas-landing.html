FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which helpdesks does Relay support?

Zendesk, Intercom, Help Scout, and Front today. Others can be connected through our CSV and webhook import.

How is customer data handled?

Personal details like names, emails, and phone numbers are redacted before analysis. Your tickets are never used to train models, and you can delete all synced data at any time.

Do I need to set up tags or categories?

No. Relay discovers themes on its own and adapts as your product changes. If you do have tags, it can use them as extra context.

Can I change when the report arrives?

Yes. Pick any day and time, send it to email or a Slack channel, and add teammates from product, support, or leadership.

What happens after the trial?

You choose a plan or your account pauses. Nothing is charged automatically, and your reports stay available.
