Our Philosophy

Why we reject “Loud AI.”

We believe AI shouldn't write your thoughts for you—it should help you remember and synthesize what you already lived.

Typical “AI” Workspaces NOISY
  • ✕ Intrusive "Write with AI" popups that replace your personal voice with generic corporate prose.
  • ✕ Rigid databases, properties, and nested folders that turn note-taking into unpaid admin work.
  • ✕ Cloud-locked proprietary formats that hold your life’s archive hostage behind a subscription.
  • ✕ Requires you to remember what to ask a chatbot to find past context.
The Murmur Sanctuary AMBIENT
  • ✦ Never overwrites your prose—only distills, links, and surfaces your own authentic ideas.
  • ✦ One chronological stream. Drop voice notes, links, or essays; taxonomy emerges automatically.
  • ✦ Backed by local `.md` files on your disk. Leave anytime with 100% of your backlinks intact.
  • ✦ Proactive margin whispers bring forgotten notes back to you at the exact moment they matter.
