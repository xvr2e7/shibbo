About Me

Design with intention,
build with empathy

I believe the best products feel like they were designed just for you—because they were. My approach blends rigorous user research with a deep appreciation for craft.

MC

A designer who thinks in systems, not just screens

With over 7 years of experience across B2B SaaS, consumer mobile, and design systems, I've learned that great design is never just about pixels—it's about understanding the entire ecosystem around a product.

I partner closely with engineers, product managers, and researchers to make sure every decision—from information architecture to micro-interactions—serves a clear purpose. I believe in shipping early, learning fast, and iterating with confidence.

Outside of work, I'm a mentor for emerging designers, a frequent speaker at design meetups, and an advocate for accessible design practices. I'm also a dog person, a coffee enthusiast, and a beginner ceramicist.
