Talk to us
Questions

The things people
ask before signing up.

Does Murmur change my words?
Never silently. Murmur writes a clean version alongside your original, and both stay visible. You can edit either, and the raw transcript is always preserved — including the bits where you trailed off and said “anyway”.
What happens to my data?
It's encrypted end-to-end, stored in the region you choose, and never used to train AI models — ours or anyone else's. Pro users can turn on local-first mode, which keeps notes and inference entirely on-device.
Which languages and inputs work?
Voice notes in 42 languages, typed text in any script, pasted emails, images and screenshots, PDFs and meeting recordings. Murmur can translate and summarise across languages in the same note.
Can I get my notes out again?
Always. Export to Markdown, PDF, Notion, Obsidian or plain text at any time — including on the free plan. There's no lock-in and no “contact support to export” nonsense.
How is this different from a voice recorder with AI in it?
A recorder gives you back what you said. Murmur gives you what you meant — connected to the rest of your thinking. The memory layer is the point: ask a question six months later and get a sourced answer instead of a folder to dig through.
