Fair questions

The things couples actually ask us.

Including the awkward one. We like the awkward one.

Absolutely — that's the default, not the exception. Link accounts read-only, mark what's shared, and keep the rest fully private. Many Duet couples never merge a single account and still split everything fairly.

Try the proportional engine: each partner contributes the same percentage of their income to shared costs. It's the mode most income-gap couples settle on — and you can switch modes whenever life changes.

Debt can live as a solo goal, a shared goal, or somewhere in between. Duet tracks the payoff countdown alongside your savings so both of you see the whole picture — without surprise.

Connections are read-only with bank-grade (256-bit) encryption, we're SOC 2 audited, and we never sell data. Either partner can unlink everything with one tap, at any time.

The honest question deserves an honest answer: one tap exports the full history for both of you, shared pots unwind cleanly to accounts you choose, and each person walks away with their data. We hope you never need it. It's there if you do.

Duet is built, deliberately, for two. The rituals, prompts, and split engine are all designed around a pair. We're watching requests for bigger households — join the waitlist from the footer if that's you.
