✳ The wonderfully slow bit

A little flour.
A lot of patience.

Every loaf starts long before you eat it. We mix, fold and ferment slowly for flavour you can’t rush—and a crust worth setting an alarm for.

01

The slow mix

Flour, water, sea salt and our lively starter, Oddette. Nothing artificial, nothing to hide.

02

The long rest

Up to 36 hours of cold fermentation builds deep flavour and an open, tender crumb.

03

The hot part

Baked dark on stone every morning, because pale bread is a missed opportunity.
