A little about me

I'm Anna, a hydrologist based in Madison, Wisconsin. I study how water moves through landscapes, and write about what that movement can teach us about place, time, and belonging.

These essays live somewhere between fieldwork and a long walk. When I'm not working, you'll probably find me near a river, taking the slower way home.
