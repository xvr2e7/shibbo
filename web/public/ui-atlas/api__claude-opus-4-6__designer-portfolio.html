About

A little about me

I'm Alex Chen, a product designer based in San Francisco who believes great design is invisible — it just works.

Over the past 8 years, I've had the privilege of working with incredible teams at companies like Stripe, Figma, and several YC-backed startups. My approach combines deep user empathy with business strategy to create products people actually love using.

When I'm not pushing pixels, you'll find me experimenting with ceramics, hiking the Marin Headlands, or contributing to open-source design tools.

Process

How I work

A proven approach adapted to each project's unique needs and constraints.

Discover

Understanding the problem space through stakeholder interviews, user research, competitive analysis, and data review.

Define

Synthesizing research into actionable insights, user personas, journey maps, and a clear design strategy.

Design

Rapid ideation through sketches, wireframes, and iterative high-fidelity prototypes validated with real users.

Deliver

Polished designs with detailed specs, developer handoff, QA support, and post-launch iteration based on metrics.
