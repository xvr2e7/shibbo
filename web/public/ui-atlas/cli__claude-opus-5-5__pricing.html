Questions, answered

What counts as an active member?

Anyone who has signed in or received a notification in the last 14 days. If someone goes quiet, we automatically credit your account for their seat — no manual cleanup needed.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades apply at the end of your current billing period, and your data stays intact.

Do guests cost extra?

Single-channel guests are free on Team and above — up to 5 per paid member. Multi-channel guests are billed as regular members.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

We offer 85% off Team and Business for qualifying nonprofits and accredited schools. Reach out to our team with proof of status to apply.

What happens when my trial ends?

You'll choose a plan or drop back to Free. Nothing is deleted — messages older than 90 days are just hidden until you upgrade again.
