02 — How I work

Design is
a conversation,
not a deliverable

Great products come from deeply understanding the people who use them. My process is collaborative, iterative, and grounded in real user needs rather than assumptions.

I work best embedded in cross-functional teams early enough to shape the direction — not just polish the pixels at the end.

Figma Prototyping User Research Usability Testing Design Systems Information Architecture HTML / CSS Motion Design
1

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, user research, heuristic reviews — finding the real problem before proposing solutions.

2

Define

Synthesising research into clear problem statements, user flows, and success metrics that align the whole team.

3

Design

Rapid exploration followed by high-fidelity prototypes. Validating with users early and often before investing in polish.

4

Deliver

Working closely with engineers through implementation — redlines, QA, and iterating based on live usage data.

03 — About me

Alex
Chen
Product Designer  ·  San Francisco

Turning complexity
into clarity

I'm a product designer with six years of experience across fintech, healthcare, and enterprise SaaS. I've worked with both early-stage startups and teams inside larger organisations — and I know how to navigate the full spectrum, from scrappy MVP to polished, scalable product.

Before going independent, I was Lead Designer at Foundry, where I helped grow the product from seed to Series B. Before that, I studied Industrial Design at RISD, which gave me a deep appreciation for systems thinking and real-world constraints.

Outside of work, I'm usually exploring generative art, taking apart electronics, or hunting for the best ramen in the Bay Area.
