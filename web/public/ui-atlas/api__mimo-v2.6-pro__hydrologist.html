About

A hydrologist with a notebook problem.

Mara Iversen is a hydrologist and writer based in Portland, Oregon, where she works on the Willamette and Columbia basins. For eighteen years she has measured how rivers move water and sediment — the quiet bookkeeping that decides where a flood goes, where a delta grows, and where a fish can still spawn.

Her essays have appeared in Emergence, The Common, and High Country News, and were collected in A River Is a Sentence (2024). She teaches field hydrology at Portland State and serves on the science advisory board of the Skagit Watershed Council. When the water is low, she is usually standing in it.

Currently: three seasons into a sediment budget for the lower Willamette — and writing an essay about gravel, which is taking longer than the gravel.
