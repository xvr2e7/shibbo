Behind the record

Made in the moments between.

Somewhere, Slowly began with a borrowed guitar, a few unfinished thoughts, and a summer that seemed to last forever.

We made this record in living rooms and quiet studios, letting the songs become whatever they needed to be. It's about the small things that turn out to be everything.
