Brew Guide

Four steps to a
perfect cup

1

Rinse

Pre-wet the filter and warm the dripper with 30 g of hot water. Discard. This seats the filter and primes the ceramic.

2

Bloom

Add 20 g of medium-fine ground coffee. Pour 40 g of 200 °F water and wait 45 seconds. CO₂ escapes; the bed expands.

3

Pour

In slow concentric circles, add remaining water in two or three pours to reach 320 g total. Keep the bed level, never flood it.

4

Draw Down

Total brew time should be 3:00–3:30. If faster, grind finer next time. The spent bed should be flat — a good sign.
