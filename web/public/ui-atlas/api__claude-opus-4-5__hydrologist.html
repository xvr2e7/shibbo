About

🌊

For over twenty years, I have devoted my life to understanding rivers—their moods, their memories, their quiet persistence. As a hydrologist at the Mountain West Water Institute, I study the fluid dynamics and ecological systems of watersheds across North America.

But science alone cannot capture what rivers mean to us. That's why I write. My essays explore the intersection of hydrology, history, and human experience—examining how water shapes not just landscapes, but cultures, economies, and souls.

I hold a Ph.D. in Hydrology from UC Berkeley and live along the banks of the Yellowstone River in Montana, where I paddle, observe, and write.
