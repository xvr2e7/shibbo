Perfect Pour-Over in 4 Steps

1
Rinse & Prep
Place filter in dripper and rinse with hot water to remove paper taste
2
Add Coffee
Add 20-25g of medium-ground coffee and create a small well in the center
3
Bloom
Pour 50g water in circular motion, wait 30-45 seconds for bloom
4
Pour & Enjoy
Continue pouring in slow circles until reaching 350g, total time 3-4 minutes
