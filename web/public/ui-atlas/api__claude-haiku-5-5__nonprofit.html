Our Mission

Cooler, cleaner, greener cities for everyone.

Urban neighborhoods can be up to 10°F hotter than surrounding areas. Tree canopy is one of the most effective ways to bring those temperatures down, and it is still unevenly distributed. Wealthier districts often have leafy boulevards while others have none.

We work with residents, city agencies, and local businesses to close that gap. We choose species suited to each block, plant them with community crews, and care for them through their first critical years.

Equity first

We prioritize neighborhoods with the least shade, the highest heat exposure, and the fewest green spaces.

Right tree, right place

Every species is chosen for soil, sidewalk space, climate resilience, and the wildlife it will support.

Community ownership

Residents help choose locations, plant alongside us, and steward their trees for decades to come.

What We Do

Four programs, one goal: more canopy.

From single street trees to whole neighborhood greening projects, our work meets communities where they are.

Street Tree Planting

We plant new trees along sidewalks, in medians, and in parking strips, working with city arborists on every site.

  • Species selected for local conditions
  • Underground utility checks before digging

Schoolyard Groves

We transform asphalt and bare lots at schools into shaded outdoor classrooms, with student-led planting days.

  • Curriculum tie-ins for science classes
  • Student tree care logs

Tree Care & Stewardship

Young trees need water, mulch, and pruning for their first three years. Our trained crews and volunteers provide that care.

  • Survival monitoring for every tree
  • Watering cooperatives on each block

Canopy Equity Mapping

We publish open heat and canopy maps so residents and officials can see where shade is missing and where to act next.

  • Free public data for every city
  • Annual neighborhood canopy reports
How It Works

From a request to a mature tree.

Here is how a tree gets from a neighborhood wish list to a shade-giving canopy.

1

Request a site

Residents, schools, and businesses nominate locations through our online map or at community meetings.

2

Survey & select

Our arborists check soil, utilities, sun, and space, then choose a species built for that exact spot.

3

Plant together

Trees go in during community planting days with tools, training, and snacks provided.

4

Care for three years

Follow-up visits, watering schedules, and pruning keep young trees healthy until their roots are established.

Questions

Frequently asked.

How do you choose where to plant?

We use a canopy and heat map combined with resident nominations. Priority goes to neighborhoods with low tree cover, high heat exposure, and limited access to parks.

Who takes care of the trees after planting?

Our crews visit every tree at least four times in its first three years. Local residents also join watering cooperatives, and the city's forestry department takes over long-term maintenance.

Can I request a tree outside my own property?

Yes. Most of our trees go on public right-of-way such as sidewalks and medians. You can nominate a site through the map on our website, and we will check it with the city.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Canopy City Trust is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Your gift is tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law, and you will receive a receipt by email.

Can my company sponsor a planting?

Absolutely. Corporate partners can fund a planting day, name a grove, or support our canopy mapping work. Reach out to partnerships@canopycity.example for details.
