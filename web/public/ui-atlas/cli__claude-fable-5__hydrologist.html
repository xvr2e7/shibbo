About

ME

Mara Ellison is a hydrologist and essayist based in Missoula, Montana. For fifteen years she has studied how rivers move water and sediment — first with the U.S. Geological Survey, now as an independent researcher and consultant on river restoration projects across the American West.

Her essays sit at the confluence of field science and personal narrative, and have appeared in Orion, Emergence Magazine, High Country News, and elsewhere. She is at work on a book about rivers that have been buried, straightened, or dammed — and the slow, imperfect art of undoing it.
