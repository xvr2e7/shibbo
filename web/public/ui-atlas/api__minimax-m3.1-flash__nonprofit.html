How it works

Rooted in care,
all the way up.

Good trees need more than a shovel and a sunny day. Our four-step approach makes each one part of a healthy, lasting urban forest.

01

Listen

We ask residents where they walk, gather, play, and feel the heat — then map where a tree can make the biggest difference.

02

Plant

Native trees arrive from local nurseries, planted by trained crews and neighbors with the right soil and space to thrive.

03

Care

For three years, we water, mulch, prune, and check every tree — making sure it survives its first vulnerable seasons.

04

Belong

We return the care to neighbors, helping communities adopt, water, and champion the trees they planted together.
