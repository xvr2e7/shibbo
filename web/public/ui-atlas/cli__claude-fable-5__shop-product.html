Holds heat, start to finish

Thick-walled stoneware stabilizes slurry temperature better than plastic or metal, so the last pour extracts as evenly as the first.

Flow you can feel

A 60° cone, deep spiral ribs, and one wide drain hole let your pour — not the brewer — set the pace. Fast or slow, it's your call.

Thrown, not molded

Every dripper is wheel-thrown and hand glazed in small batches. Slight variations in tone and ribbing mean no two are identical.

A 3-minute brew, the Kumo way

Our starting recipe: 20 g medium-fine coffee, 320 g water at 94 °C. Adjust grind, not ratio.

Rinse & warm

Set a paper filter, rinse with hot water to preheat the ceramic, and discard the water.

Bloom

Add coffee, pour 60 g of water in slow circles, and wait 40 seconds while it degasses.

Pour in pulses

Add the rest in two or three spiral pours, keeping the bed flat and the slurry level steady.

Drawdown

Total brew should finish around 3:00. Faster? Grind finer. Slower? Grind coarser.
