FAQ

Questions & answers

Is Tally Hall only for US-based freelancers?
Right now, yes — Tally Hall is built around US tax rules, including Schedule C, quarterly estimated payments, and 1099 tracking. Support for other countries is on the roadmap.
Do I need an accountant to use this?
Not at all. Tally Hall is designed so you can manage your books yourself without any accounting background. That said, your accountant will love you for using it — the exports are exactly what they ask for at tax time.
How does bank syncing work? Is it secure?
We use bank-grade encryption and connect to financial institutions through Plaid, the same technology used by major finance apps. We have read-only access to your transaction history — we cannot move money or make changes.
Can I import data from my existing spreadsheets or QuickBooks?
Yes. You can import transactions via CSV, and we have a direct import path from QuickBooks Online. Most freelancers are fully migrated in under 15 minutes.
What happens after the 30-day trial?
You'll be asked to pick a plan and add a payment method. If you don't, your account moves to a read-only mode so you can still view and export your data — nothing is deleted.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes, cancel any time from your account settings. No cancellation fees, no questions asked. You keep access until the end of your billing period.
