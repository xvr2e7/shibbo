Hello, I'm Elena

A quiet presence,
a loud love for stories

I believe the best photographs happen when you forget I'm there.

For twelve years I've photographed weddings across the Carolinas, Tuscany, and the Pacific coast, blending into the background so that you can be fully present with the people you love. My style is natural, light-filled and emotional, with a documentary heart and gentle direction when you need it.

Expect a friend with a camera who knows when to step back, when to whisper a prompt, and when to catch your grandmother wiping away a tear.
