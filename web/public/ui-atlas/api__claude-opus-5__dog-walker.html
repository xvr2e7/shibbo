Meet your walker

Hi, I'm Mia — and yes, I have treats in every pocket.

I started Wag & Wander in 2017 after a decade of volunteering at the Riverside rescue centre and realising I was far happier in a muddy field than at a desk. These days I walk around 20 regular dogs a week across Riverside, Maple Park and The Docks.

I'm a Canine Behaviour & Welfare graduate, certified in pet first aid, fully insured through PetSure, and I work on force-free, reward-based principles. Reactive and nervous dogs are genuinely welcome here — we'll go at whatever pace keeps your dog under threshold and happy.

Every dog gets a handwritten profile: favourite routes, scary triggers, harness quirks, how many treats before they'll come back. Nothing is left to memory.

Good questions

Frequently asked

Which areas do you cover?

Riverside, Maple Park, The Docks and Kingsley Green as standard. Anything within a further 5 km is possible with a small $6 travel fee — just ask and I'll check the route against my schedule.

How do you handle keys and home access?

Keys are collected at the meet & greet, labelled with a code only I can match to an address, and kept in a wall-mounted key safe at my home. Key-safes, smart locks and lockboxes at your property all work too — whatever you're most comfortable with.

What happens in bad weather?

We still walk — dogs don't mind drizzle and I own excellent waterproofs. In extreme heat, storms or ice, walks are shortened and swapped for indoor enrichment, sniffy garden games and a drop-in visit instead, at no extra cost.

Is my dog suitable for a group walk?

Most sociable dogs are, but I never assume. We start with a trial walk and introduce group members one at a time. If your dog is happier alone, solo walks are no reflection on them — plenty of my favourites are solo clients.

Are you insured, and what about emergencies?

Yes — full public liability and care-custody-and-control cover through PetSure, plus a current pet first-aid certificate and enhanced background check. In an emergency I'll contact you immediately and take your dog to your registered vet, or the nearest 24-hour practice if time is critical.

How do I pay, and what's the cancellation policy?

Invoices go out fortnightly by email, payable by bank transfer or card. Cancel free up to 24 hours before a walk; inside 24 hours the walk is charged at 50%. Emergencies are always treated with common sense.
