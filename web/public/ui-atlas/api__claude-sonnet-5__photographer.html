About the artist

Hello, I'm Elowen

For over a decade, I've had the honor of documenting love stories across vineyards, coastlines, and quiet chapels. My approach blends candid storytelling with timeless, editorial elegance — so your photographs feel like memory, not performance.

Every wedding is different, and every couple deserves images that feel entirely their own. I shoot with warmth, patience, and an eye trained on the fleeting in-between moments that often matter most.
