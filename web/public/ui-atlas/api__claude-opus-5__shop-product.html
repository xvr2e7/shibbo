Details

Each No. 02 is thrown on the wheel by a single maker, trimmed the next morning, then the ribs are cut by hand with a wire tool. Tiny differences in rib depth and glaze pooling are the point — no two pull a shot of water quite the same way.

  • Food-safe, lead-free glaze; unglazed foot ring
  • Fits Hario 02, Cafec and standard 02 cone filters
  • Sits on most 300–600 ml servers, carafes and wide mugs
Care
Rinse with hot water after brewing; a monthly soak in brewer detergent lifts coffee oils. Dishwasher safe on the top rack, though hand washing keeps the glaze crazing bright. Avoid thermal shock — don't take it from the freezer to a kettle pour.

Why ceramic

Slower to heat. Far slower to let go.

Plastic cones are quick and forgiving; glass is pretty but thirsty for your heat. Fired stoneware sits in between and stays there — a brewer with thermal mass is a brewer that doesn't argue with your recipe.

01

Even extraction

The spiral ribs lift the paper away from the wall so air escapes and water drains from the whole bed, not just the centre channel.

02

Stable temperature

Pre-heated, the body gives back heat during the bloom instead of stealing it — measurably sweeter cups with light roasts.

03

Made to be kept

Thrown, trimmed, ribbed and glazed by hand in a seven-day cycle. It will outlive the kettle, the grinder and probably the kitchen.

The ritual

A four-pour recipe for the No. 02

22 g coffee · 360 g water at 94°C · medium-fine grind · total brew 2:45–3:15.

01

Rinse & warm

Soak the filter and let the cone drink the heat. Discard the water.

0:00
02

Bloom

60 g of water, a gentle swirl, and wait while the bed degasses.

0:00 – 0:45
03

Three pours

Add 100 g every 30 seconds in slow concentric circles to 360 g.

0:45 – 1:45
04

Draw down

Let the bed flatten and settle. Swirl the server, then pour.

2:45 – 3:15
