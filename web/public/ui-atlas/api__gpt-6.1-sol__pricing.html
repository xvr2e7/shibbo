Let’s talk Enterprise

A few things you might be wondering.

Can I try Loop before choosing a plan?

Absolutely. Start with our Free plan, or explore every feature in Pro or Business with a 14-day trial. No credit card required. At the end of your trial, choose a paid plan or continue on Free.

What happens when my team grows?

You can add teammates whenever you need. On a paid plan, new seats are prorated for the remainder of your billing period. If you’re on Free and need more than 10 teammates, you can upgrade to Pro or Business.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel in your workspace billing settings. Upgrades take effect right away; downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits and education?

We do! Eligible nonprofits and educational organizations can receive special pricing. Contact hello@loop.example with a little information about your organization to learn more.
