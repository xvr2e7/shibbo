02 · Capabilities

What I actually do.

End-to-end product design — from the first messy whiteboard to the last pixel handed off.

Before pixels, clarity. I run stakeholder interviews, map jobs-to-be-done, and turn ambiguity into a scoped, testable product thesis. The deliverable isn't a deck — it's a shared decision about what we're building and why.

User researchJTBD mappingOpportunity sizingRoadmap framing

Dense dashboards, mobile flows, empty states, error states — the whole surface, not just the happy path. I design in systems of states and transitions so engineering never has to guess what happens next.

FigmaMotion specsAccessibility (WCAG AA)Edge-case mapping

I build systems teams actually adopt: token pipelines wired to code, contribution models that don't bottleneck on one person, and documentation written for engineers first. Arcline DS cut cross-product design debt by 38%.

Token architectureComponent APIsThemingGovernance

High-fidelity prototypes in Figma, ProtoPie, or plain HTML/CSS when the interaction demands real physics. I test with five users before shipping to five thousand — and I'm comfortable being proven wrong.

03 · About

Maya Chenshe/her · PDX

I started in print, detoured through front-end code, and landed in product design because it's the only discipline where craft and consequence meet. Somewhere along the way I stopped chasing novelty and started chasing clarity.

These days I lead design at Ledgerline, mentor two junior designers, and occasionally teach an interaction design workshop at PNCA. Off-screen: film photography, long trails, and an unreasonable opinion about keyboard switches. I work best with small teams who ship weekly and argue kindly.
