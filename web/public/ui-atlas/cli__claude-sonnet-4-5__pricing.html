Frequently asked questions

Can I try before I buy?

Yes! All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and can arrange invoicing for Enterprise customers.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use bank-level encryption and are SOC 2 Type II certified. Enterprise plans include additional security features.

What happens if I go over my limits?

We'll notify you when you're approaching limits. You can upgrade your plan at any time to get more capacity.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Yes! We offer 50% off Pro plans for verified nonprofit organizations. Contact us for details.
