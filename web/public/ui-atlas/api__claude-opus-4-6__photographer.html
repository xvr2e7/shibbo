Hello, I'm Elara

WEDDING & ELOPEMENT PHOTOGRAPHER

I believe that love is the most beautiful thing in the world, and capturing it is my greatest privilege. Based in the rolling hills of Tuscany and available worldwide, I specialize in creating timeless, editorial images that feel like fine art but tell your unique story.

My approach is gentle and unobtrusive — I'm there to document the stolen glances, the happy tears, the quiet moments between the big ones. Every couple deserves photographs that make their hearts skip a beat, even decades from now.

When I'm not behind the camera, you'll find me exploring flower markets, sipping espresso in hidden cafés, or planning my next adventure with my husband and our golden retriever, Luna.
