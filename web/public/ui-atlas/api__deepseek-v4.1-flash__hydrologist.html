About

I'm a hydrologist and fluvial geomorphologist. For fifteen years I've measured rivers — their discharge, their sediment load, the slow arithmetic of erosion and deposition — mostly in the headwaters of the Columbia basin.

The essays here are the other half of that work. They're for anyone who has stood at a bend and wondered why the water goes that way, and what it was doing there a hundred years before you arrived.
