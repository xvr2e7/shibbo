Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate any charges.

What happens after my trial ends?

After 14 days, you'll be asked to choose a plan. Your data is safe—we keep it for 30 days even if you don't subscribe.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Yes! We offer 50% off Pro plans for registered nonprofits and educational institutions. Contact us to apply.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and for Enterprise plans, we can invoice with NET 30 terms.

Is there a setup fee?

No, there are no setup fees for any plan. Enterprise onboarding assistance is included at no extra cost.

Can I export my data?

Absolutely. You can export all your messages, files, and user data at any time in standard formats.
