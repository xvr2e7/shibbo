Hello, I'm Elise

I photograph the in-between moments.

The breath before the vows. Your father's hand on your shoulder. The way your partner looks at you when they think no one is watching. These are the photographs that grow more precious with every passing year.

For over a decade I've travelled from Tuscan vineyards to Scottish highlands, documenting weddings with a gentle, unobtrusive eye — on film and digital — so you can be fully present in your day.
