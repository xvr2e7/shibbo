How billing works

The invoice shrinks when your team does.

01

Active-only billing

A member counts as active if they send a message or join a huddle that month. Reading doesn't count. Lurkers are free.

02

Prorated to the day

Add someone on the 20th and you pay for eleven days. Remove them and the credit lands on your next invoice automatically.

03

Guests don't cost a seat

Clients, freelancers and agencies join the channels you choose, at no charge on any paid plan.

Questions

Asked in #billing, answered here.

Still unsure? Message us at any hour and a person replies.

What exactly counts as an active member?

Anyone who sent a message, reacted, or joined a huddle during the billing month. Reading channels, receiving notifications and staying signed in do not count.

What happens when Starter hits its 10-member limit?

Nothing breaks. The eleventh invite prompts you to start a 30-day Team trial. If you decline, the invite waits until a spot opens.

Can we switch plans or cancel whenever?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current period, and unused annual time is refunded.

What happens to our history if we downgrade?

It stays safe. Messages older than 90 days are hidden on Starter, not deleted, and reappear the moment you upgrade again. You can export everything at any time.

Do nonprofits and schools get a discount?

Registered nonprofits and accredited schools get Team at 50% off, and student groups get it free. Apply from your workspace billing settings.

Where is our data stored?

Workspaces are hosted in the US by default, encrypted in transit and at rest. Enterprise customers can pin data to the EU, UK, Australia or Japan.
