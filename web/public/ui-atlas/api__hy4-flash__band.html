About The Record

Recorded in an abandoned warehouse in Brooklyn over the harsh winter of 2023, 'Echoes In The Void' is a sonic exploration of isolation, nostalgia, and the beautiful decay of urban landscapes.

Combining shoegaze textures with post-punk rhythms, Velvet Static has crafted their most ambitious and emotionally resonant record to date. Mixed by the legendary Dave Fridmann, this album pushes the boundaries of their signature sound into thrilling new territory.

"A masterclass in atmospheric tension and cathartic release. A definitive statement of intent for the modern underground." — Pitchfork
