Design

Shaped by how water moves

Every curve of the Morrow does a job. Seen from above, the reasoning is easy to follow.

  1. Twelve spiral ribs

    Raised ribs hold the paper away from the wall so air can escape as water goes in. The bed drains evenly instead of stalling at the edges.

  2. One wide opening

    An 18 mm aperture means the dripper never sets the pace. Your grind and your pour do, so there is real room to adjust a recipe.

  3. A thick, rolled rim

    Dense stoneware walls take on heat during the pre-rinse and give it back through the brew, which keeps extraction steady to the last drop.

  4. A handle that stays cool

    The loop is pulled by hand and set clear of the cone, with space for two fingers. You can lift it straight off the carafe mid-drawdown.

A recipe that scales with you

  1. 0:00

    Rinse, then bloom

    Rinse the filter with hot water to warm the dripper, and discard. Add the grounds and pour 60 g of water. Swirl once and let it rest.

  2. 0:45

    First pour

    Pour in slow circles from the centre outwards until the scale reads 190 g.

  3. 1:30

    Second pour

    Keep the same gentle spiral until you reach 320 g. Give the dripper one light swirl to level the bed.

  4. ≈ 3:00

    Drawdown

    Let it drain to a flat, even bed. If it runs much faster, grind finer. If it drags, go coarser.

Questions

Before you brew

Can't find what you need? Write to hello@morrowceramic.example and a person will reply.

Which size should I choose?

If you mostly brew for yourself, the No. 1 is the one. It handles up to 24 g of coffee, enough for a large mug. Choose the No. 2 if you regularly brew for two or more.

Why ceramic instead of plastic or glass?

Stoneware has high thermal mass. Once pre-heated with a rinse, it keeps the brew temperature steady from first pour to last. It is also inert, so it adds no flavour of its own.

Which filters does it take?

Any standard cone-shaped paper filter: size 01 for the No. 1, size 02 for the No. 2. Forty unbleached filters come in the box to get you started.

Will mine look exactly like the pictures?

Close, but not identical. Each piece is glazed by hand, so the speckle, the tone and the way the glaze breaks over the rim will vary a little.

What if it arrives chipped or broken?

Send us a photo within 30 days and we will ship a replacement at no cost. You don't need to return the damaged piece.
