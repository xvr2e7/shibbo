Ask us anything
What neighborhoods do you serve?

We cover Alberta Arts District, Beaumont-Wilshire, Hollywood, Sabin, Concordia and the western edge of the Mt. Tabor area. If you're just outside those lines, reach out anyway — we can often make it work or refer you to someone great.

How many dogs do you walk at once?

Absolutely no more than three, and only if they're well-matched in energy and temperament. Many of our walks are one-on-one, and you can always request a solo walk at the standard rate.

Are you insured and what happens in an emergency?

Yes — we carry full commercial liability insurance and bonding through Business Insurers of the Carolinas. Every walker is Pet First-Aid & CPR certified. We keep your vet's info and a signed release on file, and we'll contact you before any non-emergency care.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule with 24 hours' notice and there's no charge at all. Inside 24 hours we charge 50% of the visit, since that slot was held just for you. Illness and emergencies are handled case by case — just tell us.

Do you walk in the rain?

Rain or shine — this is Portland. We carry slickers, quick-dry towels and spare leashes. During heat advisories or poor air quality from wildfire smoke, we shorten routes and shift to shaded, early-morning or indoor visits at no extra cost.

Can you give medication?

Yes. Oral meds, topical treatments and insulin injections are all fine, and all our walkers are trained in handling them safely. Just include the instructions in your care notes at the meet & greet.
