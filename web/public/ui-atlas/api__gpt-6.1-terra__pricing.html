Questions?
We've got answers.

Still curious about something? Our team is always happy to have a chat ↗

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or switch your plan as your team grows. Get in touch and we'll help you find the right fit.

How does per-user pricing work?

Paid plans are priced per team member, per month. Choose yearly billing for a lower monthly rate, or pay month to month for flexibility.

What happens when I reach the free plan limit?

You can keep using your free workspace, or move to a paid plan when you need more members, history, or features.

Is my team's data secure?

Keeping your conversations safe is important to us. Our paid plans offer additional administrative controls, with more advanced options available for enterprise teams.

Do you offer plans for larger organizations?

Yes. Our Enterprise plan is designed around your organization's needs. Contact our team to talk through the details.
