Ask us anything
Can I try a paid plan before subscribing?

Absolutely. Every workspace can try Pro free for 14 days, with no credit card required. You’ll get access to every Pro feature and can invite your whole team. If you decide not to upgrade, your workspace simply returns to the Free plan.

What counts as a billable team member?

Billable members are teammates with regular access to your workspace. Single-channel guests are free on paid plans. Multi-channel guests and full workspace members count toward your subscription, and you only pay for active members.

Can I change plans or billing frequency later?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or switch between monthly and yearly billing whenever you need. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer nonprofit or education discounts?

We offer discounted plans to qualified nonprofits, accredited schools, and early-stage startups. Get in touch with a little information about your organization and our team will help find the right option.

What happens to our messages if we cancel?

Your workspace and conversations stay intact. At the end of your paid billing period, you’ll move to the Free plan and its 90-day searchable history limit. You can export your data first or upgrade again at any time.

How does Clover keep our work secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with regular backups, granular permissions, and two-factor authentication on every plan. Business adds SAML SSO, automated provisioning, and custom retention controls.
