Can I try relay before choosing a plan?

Of course. Start with Free for as long as you like, or explore Plus or Business with a 14-day free trial. No credit card needed, and your team’s conversations stay with you when you choose a plan.

What happens when my team grows?

Add teammates whenever you need them. On paid plans, new members are billed at your current per-person rate, prorated for the rest of your billing period. You can upgrade your plan at any time.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Absolutely. Manage your subscription in workspace settings. Upgrades take effect right away; cancellations and downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Yes! Eligible nonprofits and educational organizations can get special pricing. Get in touch with our team to tell us about your organization and find the right fit.
