FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Murmur work in languages other than English?

Yes — Murmur currently transcribes and summarizes in 14 languages, with more added regularly based on user requests.

Can I use Murmur without recording audio?

Absolutely. You can type or paste text directly and Murmur will summarize and extract action items the same way it does for voice.

Where is my data stored?

By default, your transcripts are encrypted at rest and in transit. Team and Pro plans can opt into on-device processing so audio never leaves your machine.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, there are no contracts. You can cancel, downgrade, or export all your notes at any time from your account settings.

Does Murmur integrate with my calendar?

Murmur can auto-join meetings from your Google or Outlook calendar and start recording based on rules you set.
