The Approach

Your day moves fast. The photographs should let you slow it down.

I photograph weddings the way they actually feel — the shaky breath before the aisle, the grandmother laughing with her whole body, the quiet two minutes you steal away from the party. No stiff poses, no forced moments. Just gentle direction when you need it, and invisibility when you don't.

Every gallery is edited by hand, one frame at a time, in a warm, true-to-life style that will still feel like you in thirty years.

Behind the Camera

A calm presence on a loud, lovely day.

I picked up my first film camera at sixteen and never really put it down. Eleven years and two hundred forty weddings later, my job still feels like a privilege: I get to stand at the edge of the most important day of your life and pay very close attention.

Couples tell me I feel like a friend who happens to have a camera. That's the whole goal. I'll fix your boutonnière, hold your bouquet, cry at your vows, and disappear when the moment belongs only to you.

  • Featured in Martha Stewart Weddings, Junebug & Green Wedding Shoes
  • Shooting digital and medium-format film on every wedding
  • Second photographer & travel-friendly — passports always ready
— Elena
