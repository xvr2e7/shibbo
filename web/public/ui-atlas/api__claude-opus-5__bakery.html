How it works

Three rules, and we're sorry about all of them.

01

Come early

Doors open at 7:00. The queue starts forming around 6:45, mostly people in slippers. By 9:30 the interesting loaves have usually left the building.

02

Take what's there

We bake forty loaves because that's what fits in the oven. There is no back room. There is no tomorrow's bread today. The Odd One changes weekly and we don't announce it in advance.

03

Or book a shelf spot

Standing orders are held under your name until 9:00am on collection day. Eight spots per day, because that's how many names Dev can remember.

Counter notes

Small print, honestly

  • Cash is nice, card is fine. The card machine sulks below 4°C.
  • Bring a bag or a tote. We wrap in paper; paper is not a handle.
  • Day-old bread goes half price at 11:30 — if any exists, which it rarely does.
  • Crumbs for the ducks: free, by the door, in the tin marked DUCKS.
  • Brenda the starter is nine years old and travels by bike. Sharing by request.
