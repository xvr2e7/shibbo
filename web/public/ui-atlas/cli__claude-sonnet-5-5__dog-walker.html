Good questions

What areas do you serve?

I serve the neighborhood and surrounding areas within a few miles. Send a message with your address and I'll confirm.

What if I need to cancel?

No problem. Just give me 12 hours' notice for a full refund or reschedule.

Do you walk reactive or senior dogs?

Yes! Solo walks are ideal for them. We'll talk through your dog's needs at the meet & greet.

What vaccines are required?

Dogs need to be up to date on rabies, DHPP, and bordetella. Group walks also require spay/neuter after 8 months.

How do you get into my home?

Many clients use a lockbox or hide-a-key. Keys are labeled with a code, never your address.
