Brew & Care

Start with a good rinse.

A simple recipe

Set the dripper on a mug and rinse a #02 paper filter with hot water. Discard the rinse water, then add 20 g of medium-ground coffee. Pour in 45 g of hot water and wait 30 seconds. Continue pouring slowly in circles until you reach 320 g of water total.

What’s included?

One glazed ceramic dripper. Paper filters, mugs, and servers are not included.

Cleaning and care

Let the dripper cool before rinsing. Wash by hand with mild dish soap or place it on the top rack of your dishwasher.
