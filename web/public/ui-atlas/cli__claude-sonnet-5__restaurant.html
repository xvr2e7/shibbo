Our Story

Three Generations, One Kitchen

Beit Sitti — "my grandmother's house" — opened in 1987 when Siham and her husband Georges began serving mezze from their home kitchen to neighbors in Beirut's Mar Mikhael district. Today, their children and grandchildren carry on the tradition in this very dining room.

Every batch of hummus, every tray of kibbeh, and every loaf of saj bread is still made by hand, using Siham's original recipes and olive oil pressed from our family's groves in the Chouf mountains.

— The Khalil Family
