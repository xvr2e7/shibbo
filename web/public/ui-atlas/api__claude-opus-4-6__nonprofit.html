Our Mission

Greener Cities for Healthier Lives

Urban Canopy was founded in 2009 with a simple belief: every person deserves access to the shade of a tree. In too many city neighborhoods — often low-income communities and communities of color — tree cover is sparse, streets are hotter, and air quality suffers.

We work alongside residents, city agencies, and local organizations to plant the right trees in the right places, ensuring they thrive for decades.

  • Prioritize under-canopied and heat-vulnerable neighborhoods
  • Select native and climate-adapted species for resilience
  • Provide ongoing care and maintenance for 3 years post-planting
  • Train local stewards to champion urban forestry
How It Works

From Seedling to Shade

Our proven process ensures every tree we plant has the best chance to grow and benefit the community for generations.

1
📍

Identify the Need

We use satellite data and community input to map neighborhoods with the least tree cover and greatest heat exposure.

2
🤝

Partner & Plan

We collaborate with residents, local groups, and city agencies to choose species and planting sites that work for everyone.

3
🌱

Plant & Nurture

Volunteers and trained crews plant together. We water, mulch, and monitor each tree for three years to ensure it thrives.

Our Impact

Why Urban Trees Matter

Trees aren't just beautiful — they're essential urban infrastructure that makes cities healthier, safer, and more equitable.

🌡️

Cool Down Streets

Mature trees can reduce local temperatures by up to 10°F, combating deadly urban heat islands in our most vulnerable communities.

💨

Clean the Air

Our trees remove over 200 tons of air pollutants annually, helping reduce asthma rates and respiratory illness in city neighborhoods.

🌊

Manage Stormwater

Urban trees intercept millions of gallons of rainwater each year, reducing flooding and easing pressure on aging sewer systems.

🏠

Boost Property Values

Tree-lined streets increase home values by 10-15% and attract businesses, driving equitable economic investment in communities.

🧠

Improve Mental Health

Studies show that access to green spaces lowers stress, reduces anxiety, and fosters community connection among urban residents.

🐦

Support Wildlife

Urban trees create crucial habitat corridors for birds, pollinators, and other wildlife, restoring biodiversity in concrete landscapes.
