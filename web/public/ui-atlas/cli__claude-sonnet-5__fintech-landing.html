FAQ

Questions, answered

Do we need joint bank accounts to use Pear?+
Not at all. Pear connects to both of your individual accounts and combines the view — your money stays exactly where it is.
Can one partner keep some spending private?+
Yes. You can mark individual accounts or categories as personal, so only shared budgets and goals are visible to both of you.
Is my financial data secure?+
We use bank-level 256-bit encryption and read-only connections through trusted financial data partners. Pear can never move or withdraw your money.
What if we break up or separate finances later?+
You can unlink your accounts and split into separate individual plans at any time — your transaction history stays with you.
Does Pear work outside the US?+
We currently support bank connections in the US, Canada, and the UK, with more countries on the way.
