About the Album

Ephemeral Light is an exploration of transience—the moments that slip through our fingers like water, leaving only impressions behind. Written across two winters in a cabin on the Oregon coast, these songs emerged from fog and solitude.

The album weaves together analog synthesizers, layered guitars, and found sounds recorded during coastal walks. Producer Sarah Lowe helped us capture something raw and unpolished, letting imperfections breathe.

"We wanted to make something that felt like a photograph fading in the sun," says vocalist James Ward. "Beautiful because it's disappearing."
