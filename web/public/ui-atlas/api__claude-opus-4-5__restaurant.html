Our Story

Three Generations of Passion

Cedar & Vine began in 1985 when our grandmother, Layla Khoury, brought her treasured recipes from Beirut to share with our community. What started as a small family kitchen has blossomed into a beloved restaurant that still honors her legacy.

Today, her grandson Michel leads the kitchen, while his sister Nadia manages the front of house. Together with their parents and a dedicated team, they ensure every dish tells the story of Lebanese hospitality, warmth, and tradition.

Every ingredient is carefully selected, every spice blend mixed by hand, and every plate prepared with the love that Teta Layla instilled in us. Welcome to our family table.
