Our Mission

Urban heat islands, air pollution, and disappearing green space affect everyone—especially in underserved neighborhoods. Canopy Cities is a nonprofit dedicated to restoring the urban canopy, one tree at a time.

We work with city governments, schools, and volunteers to plant native, climate-resilient trees and provide the long-term care they need to thrive.

  • Native species suited to urban environments
  • Community-led planting and stewardship
  • Measurable impact on air quality and temperature
  • Free trees for residents in priority neighborhoods

How It Works

From bare streets to thriving canopy, here is our process.

1

Assess

We map neighborhoods with the lowest tree cover and highest heat vulnerability.

2

Plant

Community planting days bring neighbors together to plant native trees.

3

Care

Volunteers water, mulch, and monitor young trees for their first three years.

4

Grow

We track canopy growth and report environmental impact back to the community.
