What it does

Less admin. Fewer surprises. One place for the money side of freelancing.

Most bookkeeping tools were built for small businesses with a part-time accountant. Tally Hall is built for one person, with no finance team, who'd rather be working.

Connects to your bank

Link your business account and transactions flow in as they happen. No CSV exports, no retyping receipts on a Sunday.

Sorts expenses for you

Software, travel, equipment, home office. Tally Hall learns your categories and flags only the ones it isn't sure about.

Invoices that chase themselves

Send clean invoices, get paid by card or transfer, and let polite reminders go out on schedule so you don't have to be the one nagging.

Know your tax bill before it arrives

Every payment that comes in, Tally Hall estimates what portion is yours to keep and what to set aside. A running figure, updated daily, so quarterly deadlines stop being a shock. Export a summary your accountant will actually thank you for.

Questions

Things people ask before signing up.

Is it safe to connect my bank to a brand-new app?

Bank connections go through an established third-party provider that handles authentication, so your login never touches our servers. Access is read-only: Tally Hall can see transactions but can never move money. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

What if you shut down?

Fair question for any new company. Your data is exportable at any time as CSV and PDF, so nothing is locked in. If we were ever to wind down, we'd give at least 90 days' notice and keep exports available throughout.

Does it replace my accountant?

No, and we don't think it should. It replaces the part where you hand your accountant a shoebox. Tally Hall keeps everything tidy during the year and produces a report they can work from directly. Many freelancers will spend less on accounting as a result.

How accurate is the tax estimate?

It's an estimate, based on your income, the deductions it has categorised, and the rates you confirm during setup. It's designed to keep you from being badly surprised, not to file your return. Final figures should always come from your accountant or tax software.

Which countries do you support?

We've launched in the United States first. Bank connections work with most major US banks and credit unions. UK and Canada are next on the list, and early users will be the first to know when they open.

What happens when the trial ends?

You pick a plan and add a card, or you don't and your account pauses. We won't charge you anything you didn't knowingly agree to, and your data stays available to export either way.
