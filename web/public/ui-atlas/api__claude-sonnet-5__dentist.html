Ask a Question
Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans and also offer flexible in-house payment options for uninsured patients.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend a checkup and cleaning every six months for most patients, though your dentist may suggest a different schedule based on your needs.

Do you offer emergency appointments?

Absolutely — we reserve same-day slots for dental emergencies such as severe pain, swelling, or injuries. Call our clinic directly for urgent care.

Is teeth whitening safe for enamel?

Yes, our professional whitening treatments are dentist-supervised and formulated to protect your enamel while delivering safe, effective results.

Do you treat young children?

Yes! Dr. Sofia Mendes specializes in pediatric dentistry and our clinic is designed to be a comfortable, friendly space for young patients.
