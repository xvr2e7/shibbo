Questions

The things people ask first.

How long until I see a useful report?

Connecting an inbox takes about four minutes. Relay backfills the last 90 days immediately, so you get a full report with real trend lines within the hour — you don't have to wait a week to see whether it's any good.

Does it work if my team never tags tickets?

That's the normal case, and it's the point. Relay clusters on the content of the conversation rather than your tags. If you do have tags or custom fields, it will use them as hints and still catch the themes that cut across them.

What happens to our customer data?

Relay has read-only access and stores conversation text encrypted at rest. Nothing is used to train shared models, PII is redacted before analysis, and you can purge your workspace at any time. SOC 2 Type II report and DPA available on request.

How is this different from the dashboards in Zendesk?

Those tell you volume, response time and CSAT — how support is performing. Relay tells you what customers are stuck on, in priority order, with quotes and the accounts attached. It's written for the person deciding what to build, not the person staffing the queue.

Can we change the schedule or the cadence?

Yes. Pick any day and time, choose Slack, email or Notion, and send different cuts to different channels — support leadership gets the full digest, each product squad gets only their area.

What if we're a tiny team with low ticket volume?

Below roughly 40 tickets a week, Relay switches to a monthly digest so the themes are statistically meaningful instead of noise. Starter stays free at that size for as long as you like.
