Common questions from cautious partners

Everything you need to know about privacy, security, and setup.

Will my partner see every coffee or personal purchase I make? +
No. That’s the core philosophy of Tandem. When you connect your bank, you decide which accounts or specific transactions belong in your private "Solo" space versus the shared "Joint" space. Personal purchases remain strictly yours unless you choose to share them.
What if one of us earns significantly more than the other? +
Tandem includes an automated Proportional Income Splitter. You simply input your respective incomes (or let the app calculate it from your direct deposits), and shared expenses will automatically be divided in fair proportion to your earnings.
How secure is our financial data? +
We use end-to-end 256-bit AES encryption—the same standard utilized by major national banks. We partner with Plaid to link institutions, meaning Tandem never sees, stores, or sells your banking credentials.
Do we have to open a new joint bank account to use Tandem? +
Not at all! Over 70% of our couples keep their existing individual bank accounts. Tandem acts as an intelligent virtual overlay that balances debts and tracks joint expenses seamlessly without the hassle of opening new bank accounts.
