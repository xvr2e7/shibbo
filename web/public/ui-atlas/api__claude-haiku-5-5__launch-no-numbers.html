FAQ

Common questions

Is Tally Hall a replacement for an accountant?

No. Tally Hall organizes your books and prepares clean records and summaries. For complex situations, such as multi-state filing, payroll, or large deductions, we recommend working with a qualified accountant. Our exports are designed to make that work faster and cheaper.

Who is it for?

Freelancers, contractors, and solo operators who invoice clients and track business expenses. If you have a sole proprietorship, a single-member LLC, or a side business, it should fit well.

Can I export my data if I leave?

Yes. You can export all of your transactions, invoices, and categories as CSV at any time, whether or not you stay subscribed.

Which banks and countries are supported?

We're starting with US-based bank connections and expanding from there. Early access members will be the first to hear about new regions. If your bank isn't supported yet, you can always import a CSV statement.

What does it cost?

Pricing will be finalized before general availability. Early access members will be told about pricing ahead of any charges, and nothing is billed while you're on the waitlist.
