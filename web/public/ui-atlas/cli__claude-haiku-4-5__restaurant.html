Our Story

A Family Tradition

For over 30 years, the Habib family has been serving authentic Lebanese cuisine with recipes handed down from grandmother to mother to daughter. Every dish is prepared with love, the freshest ingredients, and a deep respect for our heritage.

Our kitchen is where tradition meets hospitality. We believe food is more than sustenance—it's a celebration of family, culture, and community.
