About the conference

Not another webinar with snacks.

TagusConf is a single-track, two-day conference for people who build for the web. No parallel tracks you have to choose between, no sponsored keynotes, no 40-slide sales decks. Just sharp technical talks, long coffee breaks and a city that knows how to end a day well.

  • One track, twenty-four talks — every session recorded and published free.
  • Eight hands-on workshops on day two, capped at 30 people each.
  • Diversity tickets and travel grants for every edition.
  • Lunch, coffee and the closing party are included in every ticket.

Venue

An old factory, right on the river.

LX Factory is a former industrial complex turned into studios, bookshops and rooftop bars — ten minutes from the water and twenty from the centre of Lisbon. Exposed brick, big windows, and the 25 de Abril bridge practically in the room.

Getting there
Tram 15E to Alcântara-Mar, or a 25-minute walk along the river from Cais do Sodré. Parking is available on site.
Accessibility
Step-free access to every room, live captioning on the main stage, and a quiet room available all day.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still stuck? Write to hello@tagusconf.pt and a real human will reply.

Is there a livestream?

Yes. Every talk on the main stage is streamed free, and recordings are published on our site about two weeks after the conference. Workshops are in-person only.

Do you offer scholarships or travel grants?

We do. Each edition we reserve 40 free tickets and a small travel budget for people from underrepresented groups in tech. Applications open alongside the second speaker announcement.

What about food and dietary requirements?

Lunch is served on site both days with vegan, vegetarian, halal and gluten-free options. You'll be asked about dietary needs when you register.

Can I get a refund?

Full refunds up to 30 days before the event, and you can transfer your ticket to someone else at any point — just email us the new name.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes. LX Factory is step-free throughout, the main stage has live captioning and an induction loop, and there's a quiet room available for the duration of the conference.

Will there be a code of conduct?

Always. We follow the Contributor Covenant and enforce it. Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behaviour is expected to comply immediately.
