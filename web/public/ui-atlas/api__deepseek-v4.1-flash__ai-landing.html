Why Murmur

Built for the way you actually think

No bot to invite. No notes to type. No "wait, what did we decide?" on Thursday. Murmur listens, and the rest just appears.

Live transcription that actually keeps up

Speaker labels, punctuation, timestamps and accents — resolved in real time across 40+ languages. Murmur learns your team's names, acronyms and jargon the more it listens.

Summaries in your voice

Murmur studies how your team writes and produces recaps you'd actually send — not a wall of bullet points.

Action items, extracted

Owners and deadlines pulled straight from the conversation and pushed to your task tracker.

Ask your archive

"What did we promise the Northwind team in March?" Search a year of meetings in plain English.

Private by default

Encrypted end to end, never used to train models, and deletable in a single tap. Your words stay yours.

How it works

Three steps. Then nothing.

Set it up once and forget it exists — until the recap lands.

01

Connect your calendar

Sign in and Murmur joins your Zoom, Meet and Teams calls automatically. No bot links to paste, no one waiting in a lobby.

02

Let it listen

The transcript appears live as people speak, with names attached. Nothing to press. Nothing to remember. Just talk.

03

Read, search, ship

A clean summary and a list of action items arrive in your inbox before you've refilled your coffee. Every word, permanently searchable.
