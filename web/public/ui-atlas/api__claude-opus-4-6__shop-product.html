1

Rinse Filter

Place a paper filter in the dripper and rinse with hot water. This removes paper taste and preheats the ceramic.

2

Add Coffee

Add 22g of medium-fine ground coffee (about 3 tablespoons). Level the bed with a gentle shake.

3

Bloom

Pour 50ml of 200°F water over the grounds in a spiral motion. Wait 30–45 seconds for the bloom.

4

Pour & Enjoy

Continue pouring in slow, concentric circles until you reach 350ml. Total brew time: 2:30–3:30 minutes.
