About

The band

The Hollow Pines formed on the frost-bitten edge of Montpelier, Vermont, out of campfire sessions and failed attempts to fix a leaking roof. What started as a loose circle of friends became something stranger and more necessary — a sound built from open tunings, reverb-drenched pedal steel, and lyrics drawn from long winters and longer silences.

Glass Meridian is their third full-length, and their most nakedly honest. Produced by the band alongside longtime collaborator Mara Holt, it was cut to two-inch tape over three weeks in January, with minimal overdubs and no click tracks.
