About

I photograph the day
the way you'll remember it.

I'm Elena. For the past eleven years I've been the person at the edge of the room with a camera — watching your grandmother fix your veil, your best friend lose it during the toasts, the two of you sneaking a minute alone behind the barn.

My work is rooted in patience and film-inspired color. I don't orchestrate; I pay attention. The result is a record of your wedding that feels like it was made by someone who was actually there, because I was.

The Approach

How we'll
work together

  1. 01

    A conversation

    We start with a video call or coffee. I want to know how you met, what you're nervous about, and who the people are that matter most. Nothing is scripted.

  2. 02

    A loose plan

    I'll help you build a timeline with real breathing room — enough light, enough margin, and enough space to actually be present with each other.

  3. 03

    The day itself

    I'm quiet, quick, and mostly invisible. Portraits take twenty minutes, tops. The rest of the time I'm just watching for the thing you'd otherwise miss.

  4. 04

    Your gallery

    A curated preview within a week, and the full gallery in six to eight. Hand-edited, printable, and yours to keep forever. Albums are designed together, slowly.
