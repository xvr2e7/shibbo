Frequently asked questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your workspace settings. Prorated credits apply immediately.

What does "per user" mean?

You pay for each active member in your workspace. Guests and deactivated members are not billed.

Is there a free trial for Pro and Business?

Yes, all paid plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes. Contact our sales team to apply for a discounted plan.
