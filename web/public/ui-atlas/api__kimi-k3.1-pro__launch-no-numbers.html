The useful fine print

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Still wondering about something? Write to hello@tallyhall.app. A human will read it.

Do I need to understand accounting?
Not at all. Tally Hall uses plain language and focuses on the essentials: what came in, what went out, who still owes you, and what you may want to reserve for taxes. You can keep things simple or dig into reports when you’re ready.
Can I bring over my existing records?
Yes. You can import income and expenses with a CSV, then review categories before everything is added. Manual entry is also available, so you don’t need to connect a bank account to get started.
Do I have to connect my bank?
No. Manual entry and CSV imports work on their own. If you choose to enable a supported bank connection, it is used to bring transactions into your workspace—not to move money.
Is Tally Hall an accountant or tax advisor?
No. Tally Hall helps you organize records and plan tax set-asides, but it doesn’t replace professional advice. Tax rules vary by location and situation, so consult a qualified professional about your actual return.
What happens when my free trial ends?
You’ll choose whether to subscribe for $14 monthly or $132 yearly. Because no card is required to start, you won’t be charged automatically if you decide Tally Hall isn’t for you.
Why aren’t there customer stories on this page?
Because we launched last week. There aren’t any yet, and we don’t think invented praise is a good substitute. For now, the sample workspace and free trial are the best ways to judge Tally Hall for yourself.
