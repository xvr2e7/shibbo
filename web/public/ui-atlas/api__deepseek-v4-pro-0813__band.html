notes from the band

“driftwood echoes” was written between a frozen lake and a borrowed basement in northern michigan. We let the tape hiss stay, kept the false starts, and built these songs around the sound of winds through birch trees. It's an album about memory, leaving, and staying anyway.

Recorded at Old Farm Studios, Traverse City
Mixed by Eleanor Voss & Nick Calder
Mastered by Juniper Sound Lab
All songs by Starling (Meadow Lark, Jonah Reed, Wren Okafor, Finn Castell)
