FAQ

Questions, answered

Does Murmur work offline? +
Yes — Murmur can record offline and will transcribe and process your notes automatically once you're back online. On supported devices, on-device transcription works fully offline.
What languages are supported? +
Murmur supports transcription and summarization in over 40 languages, with automatic language detection mid-conversation.
Is my data private? +
All recordings and notes are encrypted in transit and at rest. You can also enable on-device-only processing so audio never leaves your phone.
Can I use Murmur for team meetings? +
Absolutely. The Team plan adds shared workspaces, speaker labeling, and collaborative note editing for everyone in the call.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes, there's no lock-in. Cancel anytime from your account settings and keep access until the end of your billing period.
