Good questions

Things people ask us

Which neighbourhoods do you cover?

We walk throughout Cedar Park, Riverbend, Old Mill and Hillcrest, plus anywhere within a 10-minute drive of those areas. Not sure if you're in range? Send us your address and we'll confirm the same day.

How do you handle keys and home access?

We prefer lockboxes or smart locks, but we're happy to hold a key. Keys are stored in a locked safe, tagged with a code rather than your address, and signed in and out for every shift.

What if my dog doesn't get along with others?

That's exactly what solo walks are for. We never force a dog into a group, and our walkers are experienced with reactive and fearful dogs. We'll always be honest about what we think is the right fit.

What happens in bad weather?

Rain, snow and wind are fine — we come prepared with towels and dry off paws before we go. In extreme heat, ice storms or air-quality warnings, we swap the walk for an indoor enrichment and potty-break visit at no extra charge.

Can you give medication?

Yes. Oral medication, topical treatments and insulin injections are all handled by our vet-tech-trained walkers at no extra cost. We just need written instructions on file.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule free of charge with 12 hours' notice. Inside 12 hours we charge 50% of the walk rate, since your walker has already held the slot. Weekly plans can be paused for any full week at no cost.
