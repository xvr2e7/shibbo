The Venue

Located in the heart of Lisbon with stunning riverside views.

Centro de Congressos de Lisboa

One of Europe's most prestigious conference venues, offering state-of-the-art facilities with breathtaking views of the Tagus River and easy access from anywhere in Lisbon.

  • 5 minutes from Belém Tower
  • Direct metro access (Belém station)
  • Free high-speed WiFi throughout
  • Wheelchair accessible
  • On-site restaurants and cafés
  • Outdoor terraces for networking
