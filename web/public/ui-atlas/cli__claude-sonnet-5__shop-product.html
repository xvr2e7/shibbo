Why Kōri

Designed around the pour

Every curve exists to serve one goal: a clean, even extraction, cup after cup.

Spiral Rib Design

Interior ridges lift the filter for consistent airflow and a steady, controllable drip rate.

Heat-Retentive Clay

Thick-walled stoneware holds temperature longer than plastic or glass, slowing bloom cooling.

Single Large Hole

One wide drain lets you control extraction entirely by pour speed, not fixed restriction.

Our Craft

Thrown, trimmed,
fired by hand.

Each dripper begins as raw stoneware on a potter's wheel, trimmed once leather-hard, then bisque-fired before a food-safe glaze is applied by hand.

The result carries small variations in glaze and weight — proof it passed through human hands, not a mold.

  • Lead-free, food-safe glaze
  • Kiln-fired at 1,250°C for durability
  • Dishwasher and microwave safe
  • Made in a small studio workshop
