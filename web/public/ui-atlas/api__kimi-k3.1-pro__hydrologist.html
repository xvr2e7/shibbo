About Mara

Science is how I begin. Attention is how I stay.

I’m a hydrologist and essayist based in western Colorado. For fifteen years, I’ve studied snowmelt, floodplains, and river restoration across the American West.

My essays live in the space between field data and lived experience—where a hydrograph becomes a story about home, and a river reminds us that certainty is rarely the same thing as knowledge.
