Good to know

Questions, answered

Are you insured and trained?

Yes — fully insured and bonded, with current Pet First Aid and CPR certification. Certificates are available on request and I'm happy to share them at the meet & greet.

What happens if it rains?

Dogs still need to go out, so walks happen rain or shine. In severe weather — thunderstorms, ice, or extreme heat — I'll shorten the outdoor portion and make up the time with indoor play, then message you to let you know what we did.

How do I know how the walk went?

After every walk you get a photo, a GPS-tracked route map and a short note covering pace, bathroom breaks and anything worth knowing. Photos and notes go out by text or email, whichever you prefer.

Do you walk dogs in groups?

Only if you'd like. Most clients book solo strolls. Adventure Pack Walks are limited to four dogs and I only group dogs of similar size, energy and temperament after a successful trial walk.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 12 hours before your slot. Inside 12 hours, the walk is charged in full — though if you're unwell or there's an emergency, just tell me and we'll sort it out.

Which areas do you cover?

Maple Grove, Cedar Hills, Riverside and the streets around Fairview Park — roughly a three-mile radius. If you're just outside that, get in touch and I'll see what I can do.

Let's get your dog out the door

Tell me a little about your pup and I'll come back with availability — usually the same day. The meet & greet is always free.
