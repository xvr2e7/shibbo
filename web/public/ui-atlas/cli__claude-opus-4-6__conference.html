Built for the people who build the web.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is there a Code of Conduct? ▼
Yes. We are committed to providing a safe, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone. Our full Code of Conduct will be published on the website and shared with all attendees before the event.
Are the talks recorded? ▼
Yes! All main-stage talks will be professionally recorded and published on YouTube within 4 weeks of the conference, free for everyone.
Can I get a refund? ▼
Full refunds are available until April 22, 2027 (30 days before the event). After that, you can transfer your ticket to another person at no cost.
Do you offer diversity scholarships? ▼
Absolutely. We set aside 50 scholarship tickets for underrepresented groups in tech. Applications open January 2027. Scholarships cover the full ticket price and may include a travel stipend.
What language are the talks in? ▼
All talks and workshops are in English. We will provide live captions on screens throughout the venue.
Is the venue wheelchair accessible? ▼
Yes. The Centro de Congressos is fully wheelchair accessible, including the main hall, workshop rooms, restrooms, and dining areas. Contact us if you have specific accessibility needs.
