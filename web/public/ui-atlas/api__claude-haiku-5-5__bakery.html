How the day goes

The schedule

Bread takes time. Our day is built around the dough, not the other way round.

06:00

Mix & fold

The first dough of the day goes into the bowl. The starter has been fed overnight.

07:00

Shape & proof

Loaves are shaped by hand, then rest in cold proofing baskets until the oven is hot.

08:00

Doors open

Fresh out of the oven, the shelves fill up. The queue usually starts before we do.

12:00

Sold out

Whatever's left goes home with the baker. Reserve ahead if you want a guaranteed loaf.

Our story

Started in a garage, still a bit wonky.

Odd Loaf began with a jar of starter that our founder, Mara, kept on the windowsill for two years before she dared to bake for anyone else. The first loaves were lopsided, slightly flat and wonderful, and the neighbours kept coming back for more.

We're still a small operation. One oven, two bakers, and a counter that's only as big as the front room. We source flour from a mill about forty minutes away and buy our butter from the dairy down the road. That's pretty much the whole business plan.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Why do you sell out so quickly?

We bake around sixty loaves a day, by hand, in one oven. We'd rather run out than bake more and lose the crust quality.

Can I buy bread after noon?

Occasionally there's a little left in the bag by the door, priced to go. It's not guaranteed, and we won't bake extra to make it so.

Do you do wholesale or deliveries?

Local cafés and restaurants can get in touch at hello@oddloaf.example. We don't deliver to homes, but we're only a short walk from most of the neighbourhood.

Is the bread vegan?

Our country loaf, rye, seeded batard and focaccia are vegan. The chocolate and orange loaf and cinnamon knots contain dairy and egg.

Can I bring my own bag or tub?

Please do. Bring a cloth bag and we'll wrap your loaf in paper, which keeps it crisp on the walk home.

How long does it keep?

Two to three days in a bread bin or wrapped in a cloth. Freeze sliced for up to a month. Toast it, don't refrigerate it.
