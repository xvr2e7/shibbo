Why ceramic

Thoughtful down to the last rib

Most drippers are an afterthought. Ours is thrown, glazed and fired by hand — then tuned like an instrument.

Even heat retention

Dense 4 mm walls hold a stable slurry temperature from bloom to draw-down, so flavours stay balanced — never scorched, never sour.

60° spiral ribs

Raised ribs wind up the cone wall, keeping the paper filter off the ceramic and letting air escape for a faster, cleaner flow.

Matte, lead-free glaze

A food-safe satin glaze inside and out resists stains and odours, rinses clean in seconds, and feels beautiful in the hand.

Brew guide

The 4-pour method

A forgiving three-minute recipe. Use 20 g coffee (medium-fine) and 340 g water — it scales for either size.

01

Bloom 0:00 – 0:45

Pour 60 g of water in slow circles, wetting every ground. Wait 45 seconds — the bed should dome gently as it degasses.

02

First pour 0:45 – 1:30

Add 200 g more, starting at the center and spiraling outward. Keep the water level steady and never touch the paper filter.

03

Second pour 1:30 – 2:15

Top up to 340 g with gentle, even spirals. Avoid the very edge — the ribs will handle the draw-down from here.

04

Draw down 2:15 – 3:00

Let it drain fully. Swirl the server, pour, and take a moment — you’ve earned it.
