Good questions, welcome here

A little clarity before you come in.

Still wondering about something? Our care coordinators are real people, and they're happy to help.

Call or text (503) 555-0148
Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes. We're in-network with many PPO plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, and Guardian, and we can file out-of-network claims too. Share your member details before your visit and we'll verify your benefits and provide an estimate of any out-of-pocket costs.

I haven't seen a dentist in years. Is that okay?

Absolutely. You won't get a lecture here. Many of our patients come to us after a long break, and our only focus is where we go from here. We'll move at a pace that feels manageable and prioritize what matters most to your health.

What if I feel anxious about treatment?

Please tell us—before or during your visit. We can agree on a pause signal, explain each step before it happens, offer headphones and blankets, and build in extra time. For more involved care, we'll also discuss appropriate comfort options with you.

Do you see children?

We do, starting with gentle “happy visits” around age one or when the first tooth appears. Our family-friendly approach uses simple language, plenty of encouragement, and no pressure. Parents are always welcome in the treatment room.

Can you help with a dental emergency?

We reserve space for urgent visits whenever possible. Call us at (503) 555-0148 for a toothache, broken tooth, lost crown, or swelling. If you have severe swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding, or a serious injury, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

What payment options are available?

We accept major credit cards, HSA and FSA funds, and CareCredit financing. Before treatment, we'll walk you through costs and options clearly. Our Aster Membership is also available for patients without insurance who want predictable preventive care.
