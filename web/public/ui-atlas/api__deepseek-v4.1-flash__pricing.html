Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before you hit "start free".

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your workspace settings. Changes take effect immediately, and we prorate the difference on your next invoice — no phone calls, no contracts.

What counts as an active user?

Anyone who sends a message, joins a call, or uploads a file during a billing month. Read-only guests and deactivated accounts are always free, so you never pay for people who aren't actually using Huddle.

Is there a free trial on paid plans?

Yes — 14 days of Pro or Business, no credit card required. If you decide not to continue, your workspace automatically drops back to the Starter plan and nothing is charged.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

We do. Registered nonprofits, accredited schools, and open-source projects get 50% off any paid plan. Reach out to our team with a bit of documentation and we'll apply it to your workspace.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You keep full access to your workspace for 30 days after cancellation, and you can export every message and file in an open format for 90 days. After that, your data is permanently deleted from our servers.
