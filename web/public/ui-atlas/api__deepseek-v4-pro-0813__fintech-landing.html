FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions from couples getting started with Together.

Is my financial data secure?
Absolutely. We use bank-level 256-bit AES encryption for all data at rest and in transit. We never store your bank login credentials — we use secure, read-only API connections through trusted partners like Plaid and Yodlee. Your data is never sold to third parties, and you can export or delete everything anytime.
Can my partner and I have different budgets?
Yes! While Together is built around shared budgets, each partner can also have personal budgets and spending categories. You can choose exactly what to share and what to keep private. The flexibility helps couples find the balance that works for their unique situation.
Does it work if we have separate bank accounts?
Definitely! In fact, most Together couples maintain separate accounts and use the app to see their combined financial picture. You can link individual accounts from different banks and the app aggregates everything into one unified view. It's perfect for couples who want financial transparency without merging accounts.
Can I switch from another budgeting app?
Yes! We offer free data migration from popular apps like Mint, YNAB, and EveryDollar. Our import tool can transfer your transaction history, categories, and budget settings in a single click. Plus, our onboarding team is available to help you get set up smoothly.
What happens if we break up?
We know this is an important question. Each partner retains full ownership of their own financial data. If you decide to separate your accounts on the app, you can easily export your transaction history and remove your connection to the shared budget. There's no awkward process — just a few clicks.
