Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

Do I need to know anything about accounting?

No. Tally Hall uses everyday language — "money in," "money out," "set aside for taxes." If you can read a bank statement, you can use it.

Does Tally Hall file my taxes?

No. We help you keep organized records and estimate what to set aside, and we make it easy to hand everything to your accountant or tax software. The tax estimate is a planning guide, not tax advice.

You just launched. Why should I trust you with my books?

Reasonable question. You can start on the free plan, and you can export all of your data whenever you like — so you're never locked in. If Tally Hall isn't right for you, you leave with everything you put in.

What does "founding member" pricing mean?

If you subscribe to Pro during our early period, you keep the $8/month price for as long as your subscription stays active, even if our regular price goes up later.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel from your settings and you'll drop back to the free plan with your data intact.
