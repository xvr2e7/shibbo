Hello, I'm Emma

For over a decade, I've had the privilege of capturing love stories through my lens. Each wedding is a unique journey, and I'm passionate about preserving those precious, fleeting moments that you'll treasure forever.

My approach is natural and unobtrusive, allowing your day to unfold organically while I document the genuine emotions, intimate glances, and joyful celebrations that make your wedding uniquely yours.

Based in the heart of California, I'm available for weddings worldwide. Let's create timeless memories together.
