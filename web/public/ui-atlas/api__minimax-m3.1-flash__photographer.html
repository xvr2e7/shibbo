The story

Since 2016, I've been the
quiet witness to your loudest days.

Solenne was born from a simple conviction: your wedding should not look like anyone else's. I photograph with a quiet, editorial eye — honest light, unhurried moments, and the beautiful disorder of a day you'll never repeat.

Every gallery is hand-edited, graded and delivered as a keepsake you'll pull from a shelf fifty years from now. No templates. No trends. Just your day, remembered honestly.

How it unfolds

Four steps,
one long exhale

01

Consult

A long conversation about your people, your places and the way you want to feel when you look at the photographs.

02

Plan

A shared timeline, a shot list written only for you, and a quiet producer coordinating every supplier on your behalf.

03

Capture

Two artists, documentary instinct, and a gentle hand. You will barely notice us — and that is exactly the point.

04

Deliver

Your gallery arrives in twelve weeks, hand-graded, printed and bound in our Paris studio — and archived forever.
