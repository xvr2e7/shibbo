Our approach

Nervous about the dentist? Most people are.

Around half of our patients tell us they've avoided dental care for years. Nobody here will lecture you. Tell us what worries you and we'll build the appointment around it.

  • Noise-cancelling headphones
  • Weighted blankets
  • Numbing gel before any injection
  • A stop signal, always honored
  • Oral & nitrous sedation
  • Ceiling screens
i.

Time to actually talk

Check-ups are booked for 45 minutes, not 15. You'll see your own x-rays and photos on screen and leave understanding exactly what we found.

ii.

No surprise bills

Every treatment plan comes with a written, itemized fee and your insurance estimate before you decide anything. The price we quote is the price you pay.

iii.

Only what you need

Our dentists are salaried, with no commissions and no upsell targets. If a tooth can be watched instead of drilled, we'll watch it.

Your first visit

What to expect, step by step.

Allow about an hour. Bring your insurance card and a list of any medications. We'll take care of the rest.

  1. A proper hello

    No clipboard in a waiting room. We sit down together, go over your history and hear what brought you in.

    10 minutes

  2. Photos & x-rays

    Low-dose digital x-rays and a quick 3D scan, so you can see what we see. No goopy impressions.

    15 minutes

  3. Exam & cleaning

    A full check of teeth, gums and jaw, followed by a gentle cleaning with your hygienist if time allows.

    30 minutes

  4. Your plan, in writing

    We walk through the options, costs and timing. Take it home, think it over. There's no pressure to book.

    5 minutes

Questions

Things people ask us.

Can't find yours? Call the front desk on (503) 555-0142. A real person answers.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. New patients are usually seen within the week, and we keep a few early-morning and late-afternoon slots for people who can't get away from work.

I haven't been to a dentist in years. Is that a problem?

Not at all, and you're far from alone. We'll start with a relaxed exam, show you where things stand and put together a plan at whatever pace suits you. Nobody will make you feel bad about the gap.

Do you offer sedation?

We offer nitrous oxide (“laughing gas”) and oral sedation for adults. If you choose oral sedation you'll need someone to drive you home. We'll talk through which option suits you at your first visit.

When should my child first see a dentist?

Around their first birthday, or when the first tooth appears. Early visits are short, playful and mostly about getting comfortable in the chair.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Just let us know at least 24 hours ahead by phone or text and there's no charge. Life happens, and we'd rather find you a better time.

Is the studio accessible?

Yes. There's step-free entry from Linden Avenue, an accessible restroom, and two treatment rooms that accommodate wheelchairs. Tell us what you need when booking and we'll have it ready.
