05 — Fair questions

Things people murmur about.

Does Murmur train on my recordings?+

Never. Your audio and notes are end-to-end encrypted and excluded from all model training. You can delete anything permanently at any time, and we purge backups within 30 days.

Do participants know it's recording?+

Yes — Murmur joins calls as a visible participant named "Murmur (notes)" and can announce itself automatically. Consent isn't a feature toggle; it's the default.

What if the audio is messy?+

Bring it on. Murmur handles crosstalk, background noise, accents, and speaker changes. It flags low-confidence passages instead of hallucinating, so you always know what's solid.

Can I use it for lectures or voice memos, not just meetings?+

Absolutely. Record in person, upload files (m4a, mp3, wav, video), or forward a voice memo. Students, journalists, and therapists-on-paperwork are some of our happiest users.

What happens when I hit the free limit?+

Nothing breaks. Your existing notes stay searchable forever; new transcription pauses until next month or until you upgrade. No surprise charges, ever.
