Our Heritage

A Story of Love & Flavor

Three generations of recipes, one unforgettable table

When Grandmother Layla first ground her own spice blends in a stone mortar in the hills of Mount Lebanon, she couldn't have imagined her recipes would one day travel across the ocean. But love has a way of carrying things forward.

In 1987, her son Karim and his wife Nadia brought those handwritten recipe cards to Brooklyn, opening a small restaurant with just eight tables. Today, their children — Sami and Maya — continue the tradition, honoring every recipe while adding their own creative touch.

Every jar of pickled turnips, every batch of freshly baked manakish, every slow-simmered stew carries the warmth of home. This is more than food — it's memory made edible.

— The Khoury Family
