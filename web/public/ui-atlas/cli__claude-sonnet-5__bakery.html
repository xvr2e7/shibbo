Our Story

Started with one jar of flour and water

Odd Loaf began as a Sunday hobby in a one-bedroom kitchen with a starter that refused to die — even through two moves and one very long power outage. We named her Gladys, and she's still the reason every loaf tastes a little different, a little alive.

We bake in small batches because that's the only way we know how to keep the crust right and the crumb open. We sell out because that's the point — come hungry, come early, or come back tomorrow.

— The Odd Loaf bakers
