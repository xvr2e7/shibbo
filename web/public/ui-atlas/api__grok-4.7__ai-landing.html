03 — Method

Three gestures. Then it gets out of the way.

01

Write it wrong.

Half sentences, voice memos, a word you will fix later. Murmur keeps the original. Nothing is cleaned until you ask.

02

Let it file.

People, dates, and shelves are noticed in the margin. A tap accepts them. Silence declines them. Both are fine.

03

Ask later.

Recall is a question, not a hunt. “What did I promise Lena?” is the whole interface.

The quiet part

What it refuses to do.

Most note apps get louder as they get smarter. Murmur is built around the things it will not do.

Streaks

A missed day is not a failure. A notebook is not a gym, and we don’t count the days you were elsewhere.

Badges

Nothing pulses, pings, or tallies what you haven’t read. There is no unread number to clear.

Uninvited summaries

Murmur will not rewrite a page you didn’t ask about. A summary is a request, not a default.

Shared by default

A notebook is private until you put it on a shelf with someone. Sharing is a deliberate act.

Questions

Asked plainly.

Does Murmur train on my notes?

No. A page is read only when you ask about it. Your notebook is not training data, and it is not used to improve a model for anyone else.

What happens when I’m offline?

Writing and editing work without a connection. A new recall question waits until you’re back, then answers from the pages already on your device.

Can I leave?

Yes. Markdown and PDF export are on every plan, including the free one, and they don’t expire. You get the originals, not just the tidy version.

Will it write my notes for me?

Only if you ask for a draft. Drafts are marked as drafts. The daily work is filing, linking, and answering — not taking the pen.

Who is it for?

Anyone whose thoughts arrive out of order: editors, researchers, students, founders, and people who still prefer a page to a dashboard.
