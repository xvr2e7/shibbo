How it works

Set up in one evening.
Calm for the rest of the year.

01

Invite your partner

One link, two accounts, one shared space. Each of you connects your own banks and cards. Your personal view stays personal.

02

Decide what’s “ours”

Tag categories as shared or personal, pick your split rule, and set a couple of goals. Tandem suggests sensible defaults to start.

03

Let it run

Transactions sort themselves. Splits recalculate. You get one gentle Sunday recap, and a settle-up button when it’s time.
