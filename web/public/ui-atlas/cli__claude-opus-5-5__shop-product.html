Care

Dishwasher safe on the top rack. Pre-heat with hot water before brewing — stoneware holds heat beautifully once warm. Avoid sudden shocks from freezer to boiling.

Why every one looks a little different

Each Arc is thrown on the wheel and dipped by hand. Expect gentle variation in glaze pooling, small iron speckles, and a unique drip line — the marks of something made by a person, not a press.

Small details, slower mornings.

Three minutes, four pours.

Use the timer to follow along — each step lights up as you go.

  1. 0:00

    Bloom

    Pour 50 g of water to saturate the grounds. Give a gentle swirl and let the gas escape.

  2. 0:40

    First pour

    Spiral slowly from the center out to 150 g, keeping clear of the paper's edge.

  3. 1:20

    Second pour

    Top up to 300 g in small circles. Keep the water level steady and consistent.

  4. 2:00

    Drawdown

    Swirl once and let it drain. You're looking for a flat bed, finishing around 3:00.
