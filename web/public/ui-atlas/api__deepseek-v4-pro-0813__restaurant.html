Our Story

From Beirut to Your Table

✦
"Every dish tells the story of our family."

Rooted in Tradition, Nourished by Love

Beit Jeddi means "My Grandfather's House" in Arabic — and that's exactly what this restaurant is. It began in 1987 when our grandfather, Youssef, brought his mother's recipes from the mountains of Mount Lebanon to this city with nothing but a suitcase full of spices and a dream.

Today, his daughter Layla and her children carry those same recipes forward — the seven-spice blend hand-ground each morning, the hummus whipped to silkiness, the kibbeh shaped by hand just as they were taught. We don't cut corners. We don't use shortcuts. We cook the way our family always has: with patience, with pride, and with love.

Meet the Family

Three Generations of Cooking

We are a family first and a restaurant second. Every dish carries the hands that taught us how to make it.

✦
👨‍🍳

Youssef — The Founder

Grandfather Youssef started it all in 1987 with his mother's recipe book and a dream. He still visits the kitchen every morning to check the spice blend.

👩‍🍳

Layla — Head Chef

Daughter of Youssef, Layla masters the art of Lebanese home cooking. Her grape leaves are legendary, and her hummus has won hearts across the city.

👨‍👧‍👦

The Next Generation

Layla's children — Karim, Rania, and Omar — bring fresh ideas while honoring tradition. From modern plating to warm hospitality, they carry the torch.
