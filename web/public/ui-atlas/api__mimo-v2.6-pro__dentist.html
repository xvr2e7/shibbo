Good questions

Everything you might be wondering.

Do you take my insurance, and will you bill it for me?
We're in-network with most major PPO plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife and Guardian. We'll verify your benefits before your first visit, submit claims on your behalf, and give you a written estimate of what you'll owe — so there are never any surprises.
I'm genuinely nervous about the dentist. How do you help?
You're in very good company — roughly a third of our new patients tell us the same thing. We start with a chat only, no instruments. We use topical numbing before any injection, offer nitrous oxide or oral sedation, and we work in small steps with a stop signal you control at any moment.
What does a new patient visit actually involve?
Plan for about 75 minutes. We'll take low-dose digital X-rays and a 3D scan, complete a comprehensive exam including an oral cancer screening, and — if you'd like — a gentle cleaning. Then we review everything together with photos on screen and discuss any treatment options at your pace.
How quickly can I be seen in an emergency?
We hold emergency appointments open every weekday and on Saturday mornings. Call before 10am and we'll almost always see you the same day. For severe facial swelling, uncontrolled bleeding or trauma, please call us immediately or visit the nearest emergency department.
Do you offer payment plans for larger treatment?
Yes. We offer interest-free in-house payment plans for treatment up to $3,000, and extended financing through CareCredit and Cherry for larger cases. Our treatment coordinator will walk you through every option before you commit.
Is teeth whitening from a dentist really different?
Significantly. Professional whitening uses a higher-concentration gel with custom-fitted trays and desensitising agents, which means a brighter, more even result with far less sensitivity than shop-bought strips. We also check that whitening is appropriate for your enamel before we begin.
