Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll be prorated for the remainder of your billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the next billing period.

What happens after my trial ends?

After your 14-day Pro trial ends, you'll be moved to the Free plan unless you choose to subscribe. No credit card required for the trial, and you won't lose any data.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits?

Absolutely! We offer 50% off for verified nonprofits and educational institutions. Contact our sales team with your organization details to get started.

Is my data secure?

Security is our top priority. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We're SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and undergo regular third-party security audits.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and wire transfers for Enterprise plans. All payments are processed securely through Stripe.

Can I export my data?

Yes, you can export all your messages, files, and workspace data at any time from your admin settings. We support JSON and CSV export formats.
