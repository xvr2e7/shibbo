    § 05THE SMALL PRINT ANSWERS, IN PLAIN TYPE

    Asked before every first issue.

    Q.01Which tools does Relay read from?+

    Zendesk, Intercom, Help Scout, Front, Freshdesk — or any shared mailbox over IMAP. Anything else, bring a CSV export and we’ll take it from there. Your whole queue, not a sample: closed tickets, merges, the works.

    Q.02How does it tell signal from noise?+

    Duplicates collapse into one theme. Spam, password resets and one-line rants get folded in quietly but never headline. Every theme carries a cluster-confidence score, and only themes clearing both volume and ARR-impact thresholds make the page. You can tune the thresholds — and we show you what moved when you do.

    Q.03Will customers be identifiable in the brief?+

    PII is scrubbed before anything reaches the page. Quotes appear as first name, role and company unless the customer opted in to more. Every number in the brief traces back to source tickets, so you can audit us ticket by ticket.

    Q.04Why weekly, and not real-time?+

    Because a dashboard you check every day is a dashboard you ignore by Friday. The brief is a decision document, not a notification feed — it lands Monday at nine and fits in the time your team’s coffee takes to cool.

    Q.05What if we disagree with the ranking?+

    Good — that’s a roadmap debate with evidence. Weights (ARR, frequency, recency) are visible and adjustable, and every claim on the page links to its receipts. Argue with the numbers, not with vibes. Anything else, write to hello@relay.press — it lands in our own queue.
