About

I'm Nora Halloran, a surface-water hydrologist in western Montana.

For fifteen years I've waded into rivers with a current meter to find out how much water is going by. It is a simple question that turns out to touch everything: snow, law, salmon, concrete, and who gets to stay in their house.

I write here a few times a year, slowly, about one river at a time. Some of these essays have also appeared in High Country News and Orion.
