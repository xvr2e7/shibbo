The story

Recorded in an orchard shed

Velvet Static began in the winter of 2022, in a drafty orchard shed outside Portland, Oregon — four friends, one borrowed 8-track, and a space heater that only worked on Tuesdays. They recorded everything you hear on Glass Orchard right there among the apple trees: vocals at dawn, drums in the barn, tape hiss left in on purpose.

Three years, two EPs and one very patient landlord later, the shed has become a studio — but the band still records with the windows open. Ten songs about the strange, sweet ache of growing up somewhere green. Press play; the orchard is listening.
