Design with intent.

I believe great product design isn't just about how things look—it's about how they work. With over 6 years of experience, I partner closely with engineers and PMs to ship solutions that are technically feasible, business-aligned, and genuinely delightful.

My approach is deeply rooted in qualitative research, rapid prototyping, and rigorous usability testing. I sweat the micro-interactions just as much as the information architecture.
