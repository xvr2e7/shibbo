Bringing Nature Back to Our Streets

Urban Canopy was founded in 2015 with a simple belief: every neighborhood deserves the shade, beauty, and clean air that trees provide. Too often, it's the communities that need trees most—those facing heat islands, pollution, and lack of green space—that have the fewest.

We partner with local governments, community organizations, and residents to plant native trees where they'll make the biggest difference. But we don't just plant and leave—we provide ongoing care and education to ensure every tree thrives for generations.

Why Urban Trees Matter

Trees do more than beautify our streets—they're essential infrastructure for healthy, resilient cities.

🌡️

Cool Our Cities

Urban trees can reduce local temperatures by up to 10°F, combating dangerous heat islands and lowering energy costs for cooling.

💨

Clean the Air

A single mature tree absorbs 48 pounds of CO₂ per year and filters harmful pollutants, improving respiratory health for everyone nearby.

🏠

Strengthen Communities

Studies show tree-lined streets increase property values, reduce crime, and create gathering spaces where neighbors connect.

🦋

Support Wildlife

Native trees provide critical habitat for birds, pollinators, and other urban wildlife, creating corridors of biodiversity.

💧

Manage Stormwater

Tree roots and canopies capture rainwater, reducing flooding and preventing pollutants from reaching our waterways.

🧠

Boost Mental Health

Access to green spaces reduces stress, anxiety, and depression while improving focus and overall well-being.
