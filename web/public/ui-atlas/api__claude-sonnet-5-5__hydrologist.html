About

Mara Lindqvist is a hydrologist who studies how rivers rise, shrink, and sometimes vanish, with a focus on intermittent streams in semi-arid basins. She has spent fifteen years working with streamflow records, from installing gauges to arguing about what their numbers mean.

Her essays began as field notebooks and grew into a way of explaining the science to people who live beside rivers but rarely see them in data. She lives in western Montana, a short walk from the confluence of two small rivers, and is working on a book about the history of the stream gauge.
