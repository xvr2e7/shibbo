Our Mission

We believe that every city deserves a green canopy. Urban trees clean the air we breathe, reduce city temperatures, manage stormwater, and provide essential habitats for local wildlife.

At CanopyCity, we partner with local communities, schools, and businesses to plant and maintain trees in neighborhoods that need them the most. Together, we are cultivating a sustainable and equitable future for urban environments.
