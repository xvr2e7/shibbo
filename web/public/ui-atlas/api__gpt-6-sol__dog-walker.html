Good to know

Questions? We’re all ears.

Don’t see what you’re looking for? Send us a note and we’ll be happy to help.

Do you walk dogs of all ages and sizes?

We’d love to hear about your dog. Tell us their age, energy level, and any special needs so we can discuss a walk that’s comfortable for them.

Can you accommodate a regular schedule?

Yes, recurring walks are welcome, subject to availability. Let us know which days and times you’re hoping for when you get in touch.

What happens if the weather is bad?

We’ll keep your dog’s comfort and safety first. If conditions aren’t suitable for a walk, we’ll contact you to discuss the best option.

How much does a walk cost?

Pricing depends on the walk length and your location. Send us a quick note with what you need, and we’ll share the options before you book.
