Three odd things about us

01

We only bake once

The oven goes on at four and off at ten. Whatever we make, we make once — no afternoon batch, no second chance, no apology.

02

Thirty per cent rye

Stone-milled, and a thirty-six hour cold ferment in the back kitchen. It makes the crumb open and the crust properly dark. Odd, but better.

03

Six loaves held back

Regulars with a standing order get one set aside. Six a day are kept for people who can't be at the door at eight. Ask at the counter.

Come early, or come Friday

We're on the corner opposite the old laundry, behind the green door with the chalkboard on it. No seats, no coffee machine, no queue system — just a shelf and a card reader.
