Our Mission

Urban Roots is dedicated to creating greener, more sustainable cities by planting trees in urban neighborhoods that need them most.

We believe that every community deserves access to the environmental, health, and social benefits that trees provide—from cleaner air and cooler temperatures to stronger community bonds.

Through partnerships with local governments, businesses, and community organizations, we're building a movement to transform concrete landscapes into thriving green spaces.

🌱

How It Works

📍

Identify

We work with communities to identify areas with limited tree coverage and high need.

🤝

Partner

Local volunteers, businesses, and city officials join forces to plan planting events.

🌳

Plant

Community members come together to plant native trees that will thrive for decades.

💚

Nurture

We provide ongoing care and monitoring to ensure every tree reaches its full potential.
