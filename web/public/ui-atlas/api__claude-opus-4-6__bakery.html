Why Odd Loaf?

No two loaves are ever the same

Each one shaped by hand, guided by our starter (her name is Mildred, she's 12 years old), and baked with care in our neighbourhood oven.

🌾

Organic Flour

Stoneground at a local mill, sourced from regenerative farms within 100 miles of the bakery.

⏳

Slow Fermented

48 hours minimum. No shortcuts, no commercial yeast. Just flour, water, salt, and time.

🤲

Hand Shaped

Every loaf touched by human hands. We shape before dawn and bake as the sun rises.

From dough to door

A day at Odd Loaf

Every loaf follows the same patient rhythm. Here's what a single day looks like behind our flour-dusted doors.

4:00 am
🌙

Shape & Score

Cold-proofed doughs are pulled, gently shaped by hand, and scored with a razor-sharp lame.

5:00 am
🔥

Into the Oven

Stone deck oven, steamed heavily for that blistered crust. 45 minutes at 500°F.

7:00 am
☀️

Doors Open

Loaves cool on wooden racks. The aroma pulls in early birds from the street.

~12:00 pm
✨

Sold Out

Every loaf, gone. We clean up, feed Mildred, and start the next batch of dough.
