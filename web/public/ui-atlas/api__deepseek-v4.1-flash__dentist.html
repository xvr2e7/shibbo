Get in touch
Do you accept my dental insurance?

We're in-network with Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, Guardian, United Concordia and several regional plans. Send us a photo of your card and we'll verify your benefits and estimate your out-of-pocket cost before your first visit — free of charge.

I'm nervous about the dentist. What are my options?

A lot, actually. We offer nitrous oxide, oral sedation, noise-cancelling headphones, weighted blankets and genuinely unhurried appointments. You can pause at any time by raising your left hand — no questions asked.

How much does a first visit cost?

New patient exams — including digital X-rays, a comprehensive exam and a cleaning — start at $99 without insurance when you book online. If you're covered, we'll run your benefits first so there are no surprises.

Do you see children?

Yes, from age one. Dr. Nandal leads our pediatric team, and appointments are designed to be short and playful — with a sticker wall that has never once failed us.

What happens in a dental emergency?

Call us first at (555) 018-4420. We hold same-day emergency slots open every weekday and can usually see you within a few hours for pain, swelling or a broken tooth.

Where do I park?

Free covered parking in the Cedar Row garage — enter from 3rd Avenue. We validate your ticket at the front desk, so it costs you nothing.
