05 / The useful bits

Questions?
We’ve got you.

Still wondering about something? Drop us a note. A real human will reply.

Who is VIEWPORT for?

Anyone making the web: frontend developers, designers, accessibility specialists, students, creative technologists, and the curious folks in between. You don’t need to know everything to belong here.

Will the talks be recorded?

Yes. Attendees will receive access to session recordings after the conference. We’ll also share slides and useful links wherever speakers are happy for us to do so.

Is the venue accessible?

Arroz Estúdios has step-free access to the main event spaces. If you have a specific access requirement, please email us before booking so we can make your visit as comfortable as possible.

What if I can’t make it?

You can transfer your ticket to someone else up to seven days before the event. Email us with the new attendee’s details and we’ll take care of the swap.

Will there be food for me?

Absolutely. Lunch and refreshments are included, with vegetarian and vegan options. Tell us about allergies or dietary needs when you register.
