Welcome to our table

A Legacy of Flavor

Our story began three generations ago in a small village nestled in the Lebanese mountains. What started as a humble family kitchen quickly became a local gathering place, renowned for its warmth, hospitality, and incredible food.

Today, Al-Zaytoun brings the essence of our heritage to you. Every dish is carefully crafted using recipes passed down through generations, the freshest locally sourced ingredients, and traditional cooking techniques that honor our roots.

When you dine with us, you aren't just a guest—you become part of our family.

- The Haddad Family
