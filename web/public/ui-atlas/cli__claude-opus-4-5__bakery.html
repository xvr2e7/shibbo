A Little Odd

Good bread takes time. We think that's a feature, not a flaw.

Every loaf starts with our 8-year-old starter named Gus, local flour from small farms, and 24+ hours of slow fermentation. No shortcuts, no commercial yeast, no rush.

We bake small batches because that's what fits in our oven. When it's gone, it's gone—come back tomorrow and we'll have more.
