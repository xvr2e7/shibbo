FAQ

Questions & answers

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. We use Plaid for bank connections — the same infrastructure used by Venmo, Robinhood, and thousands of other financial apps. Your bank credentials are never stored on our servers. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

Do I need to know accounting?

Not at all. Tally Hall is designed for people who don't think in debits and credits. Everything is presented in plain language — income in, expenses out, here's what's left.

Can I use this alongside QuickBooks?

Absolutely. Many freelancers use Tally Hall day-to-day and sync to QuickBooks when their accountant needs it. The Pro plan includes a direct QuickBooks integration.

What happens after the free tier limit?

You'll get a heads-up when you're approaching 50 transactions. Your existing data stays accessible — you just won't be able to import new transactions until you upgrade or a new month starts.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, no contracts. You can downgrade or cancel your plan at any time. Your data remains available for export for 90 days after cancellation.

I have more questions.

We'd love to hear from you. Reach out at hello@tallyhall.app and a real human (our founding team) will get back to you within a few hours.
