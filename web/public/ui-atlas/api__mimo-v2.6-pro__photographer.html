The studio

Hello, I'm Elena —
I make photographs
you can feel again.

I grew up in a farmhouse outside Siena where my grandmother kept every letter my grandfather ever wrote her in a biscuit tin. That tin taught me what photography is for: not the perfect pose, but the proof that it happened — the light, the nerves, the ridiculous laughter.

My approach is quiet. I'll never spend your wedding directing you like a film set. I'll learn your family's names, know which aunt will cry first, and be in the right room at the right moment. The result is a gallery that feels less like a photoshoot and more like a memory you can hold.

The experience

Unhurried,
considered,
human.

From the first email to the album on your coffee table — here's exactly how we'll work together.

01

The first conversation

Before you book

We'll talk for an hour — no pitch, no pressure. I want to hear how you met, who's coming, what you're nervous about. If we're not the right fit, I'll happily recommend someone who is.

02

Planning & timeline

Three to six months out

Together with your planner, I build a photography timeline around light — not just logistics. Family group lists, travel, sunset times, and a rain plan you'll never need but will be glad to have.

03

The wedding days

Calm, quiet presence

I arrive early, stay late, and move like a guest rather than a crew. No flash during vows, no shouting directions, no twenty-minute photo sessions stealing you from your own party.

04

Your gallery & album

Four to eight weeks later

A private online gallery, a hand-designed heirloom album, and print-ready files. I stay in touch long after — most of my couples send me baby photos two years later.
