Hydrologist & River Ecologist

About

I am a hydrologist and fluvial geomorphologist with over a decade of research experience studying river systems across North America and East Asia. My work focuses on the intersection of water quality, riparian ecology, and hydrological modeling, with particular interest in how climate change and human land use alter river behavior and function.

Through both scientific research and long-form writing, I explore the profound connections between rivers and human society—examining how these vital waterways have shaped our civilizations, sustained our ecosystems, and continue to present both challenges and opportunities in an uncertain future.
