About Cassette Skies

Recorded in a converted garage during the winter of 2023, Cassette Skies is a love letter to analog imperfection. Fuzz, tape hiss, and midnight drives through neon-lit suburbs.

The Velvet Static return with their most intimate collection yet—six tracks of reverb-drenched guitars, drum machines, and whispered harmonies that feel like finding a forgotten mixtape in your glovebox.

Headphones recommended. Best listened to at dusk.
