Welcome to Beit Teta — "Grandmother's House"

Our story began in the sun-drenched village of Douma, high in the Batroun mountains, where Teta Salma taught us that you never measure olive oil with a spoon—you measure it with your heart.

Today, her grandchildren run the kitchen and the dining room. Our sumac is still harvested by cousins in the Chouf mountains, our pita balloons fresh out of the stone hearth every ten minutes, and our labneh is strained in-house over 48 hours.

Whether you are joining us for a quick morning manousheh with mint tea or a four-hour Sunday family feast, our table is yours.

— The Khoury Family

24-Hour Marinated

Our meats are marinated in wild oregano, seven-spice, garlic, and cold-pressed olive oil before hitting live charcoal.

Single-Estate Lebanese Oil

Every dish is finished with cold-pressed olive oil and wild za'atar imported directly from our family's groves in North Lebanon.

Meant to Be Shared

True to Lebanese tradition, our dishes arrive at the center of the table as they are ready from the hearth.
