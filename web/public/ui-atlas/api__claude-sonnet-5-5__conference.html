Miradouro means “viewpoint” — a place you climb to for a better look at the city. This is that, for the frontend.

We bring together engineers, designers and curious people who care about building interfaces that are fast, accessible and a pleasure to use. No vendor pitches, no framework wars — just practical talks, honest war stories and long hallway conversations.

Two days at the river, in a city that knows how to host. Come for the talks, stay for the sunset.

( Venue )

On the banks of the Tagus

Pavilhão do Tejo in Parque das Nações: big windows, bigger sky, and a short walk to the water.

Pavilhão do Tejo

Getting there

Oriente metro and rail station is a five-minute walk. The airport is roughly 15 minutes away by metro — no taxi needed.

Where to stay

We have partner rates at three hotels within a ten-minute walk. Book through your ticket confirmation to unlock them.

Accessibility

Step-free throughout, quiet room, live captions on stage, and gender-neutral bathrooms. Tell us what else you need.

( FAQ )

Good questions

Anything else? Write to hello@miradouro.example.

What language are the talks in?

English. Live captions are displayed on stage for every session.

Will talks be recorded?

Yes. Recordings and slides are published within two weeks, and Conference ticket holders get early access.

Is there a code of conduct?

Absolutely. Everyone is expected to be kind and respectful. Our team is on site and reachable throughout both days; details are in the code of conduct linked in the footer.

Do I need a visa to attend?

That depends on your nationality. We provide invitation letters on request once you hold a ticket.

Are there options for dietary requirements?

Yes — vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and halal options are available. Tell us when you register.

Can I bring my kids?

Children are welcome. We offer a quiet family area and a small childcare grant; ask us and we’ll help out.
