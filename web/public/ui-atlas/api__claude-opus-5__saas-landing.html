FAQ

Reasonable questions.

Does this replace our ticket tags?

No — it makes them optional. Relay clusters on the content of the conversation, so your report stays accurate even when agents forget to tag, or when a theme emerges that your taxonomy never anticipated. If you do maintain good tags, Relay uses them as an extra signal.

How accurate is the clustering?

On benchmark accounts, roughly 94% of tickets land in a theme a human reviewer agrees with. More importantly, everything is auditable: each theme shows its source tickets, and you can split, merge or rename a theme in one click. Corrections carry forward to future weeks.

What happens to customer data?

PII is redacted before any model sees it. We're SOC 2 Type II, support EU data residency, sign DPAs and BAAs, and never train models on customer data. You can delete your workspace and all derived data at any time, permanently, from the settings page.

How long does setup take?

About four minutes. Authorize your helpdesk, pick a delivery channel, choose a send time. Backfill of historical tickets runs in the background and your first full report arrives the following Monday — or immediately, if you don't want to wait.

We're a 6-person startup. Is this overkill?

Probably, if you're reading every ticket yourself — keep doing that, it's better. Relay starts earning its keep around 300–500 tickets a month, which is usually the point where nobody can hold the whole queue in their head anymore.
