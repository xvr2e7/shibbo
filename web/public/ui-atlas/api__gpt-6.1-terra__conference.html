06 / Good to know

A few quick
answers.

Who is OFFSCREEN for?

Frontend developers, designers, creative coders, and anyone curious about making a better web. Whether you’re just starting out or have been building for years, you’re welcome here.

What language are the talks in?

All talks and on-stage sessions will be held in English.

Is food included in my ticket?

Yes! Your ticket includes lunch, coffee, and snacks on both days. We’ll share more information about dietary requirements closer to the event.

Where exactly is the venue?

We’re putting the finishing touches on our Lisbon venue. Sign up for updates below and we’ll let you know as soon as it’s announced.

How can I get in touch?

Drop us a line at hello@offscreenconf.com. We’d love to hear from you.
