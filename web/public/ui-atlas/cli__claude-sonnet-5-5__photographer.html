Hello, I'm Elena

I photograph the feeling of your day.

For twelve years I've stood quietly at the edges of weddings — the nervous laugh before the aisle, a grandfather's tearful look, the dance floor at midnight. I believe the best photographs happen when you forget I'm there.

My approach is documentary at heart, with gentle direction only when it helps. You'll receive a gallery that feels like your day, not a staged version of it.
