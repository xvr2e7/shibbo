Venue

Pavilhão Tejo, Belém

A light-filled riverside hall in Belém, ten minutes from the Jerónimos Monastery and right on the Tejo waterfront. One auditorium, a sunny terrace for breaks, and the best custard tarts in the world around the corner.

  • Avenida da Índia 142, 1300-299 Lisboa
  • 15 min by tram (15E) or train from the city center
  • Fully step-free access; quiet room available both days
  • Lisbon airport is 25 minutes away by metro + train

FAQ

Good questions

Will the talks be recorded?

Yes. Every talk is professionally recorded and published free on our channel about four weeks after the conference. Attendees get early access.

Is the conference in English?

Yes, all talks are in English with live captions. The organizing team also speaks Portuguese, Spanish, and French if you need help.

What's your refund policy?

Full refund until April 30, 2027; 50% until May 21. After that, tickets are non-refundable but freely transferable to another person at any time — just email us the new name.

Is the venue accessible?

Fully. Step-free entry, accessible restrooms, reserved front-row seating, live captions on every talk, and a quiet room on both days. Tell us about any specific needs when you register and we'll make it work.

Do you have a code of conduct?

Yes, and it's enforced. All attendees, speakers, and sponsors agree to it when registering. The full text and the reporting contacts are linked in the footer and printed on your badge.

Where should I stay in Lisbon?

Belém is quiet and close to the venue; Cais do Sodré and Baixa are lively and 15 minutes away by tram. We've negotiated discounted rates at three partner hotels — the booking codes are in your ticket confirmation email.

Can my company sponsor?

We'd love that. Sponsorships keep ticket prices down and fund the scholarship program. Write to sponsors@azulejoconf.example for the prospectus.
