Issue #47 — The Big Idea

Everything you need, 15 minutes from your door.

Paris promised it. Melbourne mapped it. Now Portland measured it — residents in complete 15-minute neighbourhoods drive 42% less, report higher life satisfaction, and spend more locally. The secret wasn’t magic. It was three boring, brilliant moves: legalise corner shops, connect the side streets, and plant a tree every 15 metres.

🍽️ Explain it at dinner: “A great neighbourhood isn’t about living downtown — it’s about not needing to leave yours for daily life.”
