How the walk works

No contracts, no mystery. Just a steady routine your dog learns to love — and a paper trail (of the photo variety) for you.

01

The free meet & greet

We come to you, learn your pup's quirks, the leash spot, the treat drawer, and the exact ear-scratch technique that wins them over. Twenty minutes, zero obligation.

02

Doorstep pickup

Your walker arrives in the booked window, keys in our insured lockbox system or a hello at the door. You get a "paws up, we're off!" text.

03

The walk itself

Sniff-heavy routes, shaded park loops, hydration breaks, and GPS tracked the whole way. sniffing counts as exercise, obviously

04

The report card

Photos, route map, pee tally, treat count, and a mood rating — in your inbox before you've even closed the laptop. Fresh water topped up on the way out.
