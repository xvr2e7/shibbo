A day at Odd Loaf

Why we sell out: one oven, one bake, zero shortcuts.
04:30

The mix

Yesterday's levain meets today's flour. The starter, Brenda, gets fed first. She's older than the business.

06:45

First bake

84 loaves in, steam on, ears up. The whole block smells like a good decision.

07:00

Doors open

Regulars queue from 6:50. We pretend not to notice. The buns go first — they always go first.

~11:30

Last loaf

Somebody buys it, usually while apologising to the person behind them. A small daily drama.

12:00

Gone

Shelves empty, ovens off, aprons hung. We nap, then feed Brenda. See you at 7.
