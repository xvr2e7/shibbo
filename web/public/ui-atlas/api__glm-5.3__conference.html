03The venueBELÉM, BY THE WATER

Under the chimneys, by the river

We’re holding the whole thing inside Central Tejo — the old thermoelectric power station on the Belém waterfront, four chimneys and all, sitting directly under the 25 de Abril bridge. One room, 400 seats, brick everywhere, and the Tejo outside for the long lunches.

When the talks end, you walk out straight onto the river promenade. The day-one tasca night happens in Alfama; the day-two farewell drink happens wherever the sun is still up.

  • Tram 15E — Praça do Comércio → Belém, then a 4-minute walk under the bridge
  • MAAT — Central Tejo, Av. Brasília, Belém, Lisboa
  • June in Lisbon: 28°C and light that makes designers emotional
