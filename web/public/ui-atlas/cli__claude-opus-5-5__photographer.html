The person behind the lens

Light, laughter & the in-between

For over a decade I've been chasing the soft, unscripted seconds that make a wedding yours — the shaky breath before the vows, your grandmother's hand on your cheek, the dance floor at 1am.

My approach is part documentary, part fine art. I'll guide you gently when it matters and disappear when it doesn't, so you can be fully present while I quietly gather the story.
