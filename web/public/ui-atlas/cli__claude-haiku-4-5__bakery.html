About Odd Loaf

Welcome to your neighbourhood's favourite sourdough destination. Since opening our doors, we've been committed to crafting exceptional sourdough using time-honoured techniques and the finest ingredients.

Our sourdough starter has been carefully maintained for years, developing complex flavours that can't be rushed. Each loaf is hand-shaped and baked to golden perfection in our wood-fired oven.

We believe in quality over quantity—we bake enough to meet demand, but not so much that anything sits around. That's why we often sell out by noon, and why our regulars know to arrive early.
