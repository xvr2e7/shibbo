The Approach

Your wedding is not a photoshoot. It's a day you'll spend the rest of your life trying to remember.

I photograph the way a room feels when your father sees you for the first time. The pause before the vows. The laugh you didn't plan. My work is quiet, warm and unhurried — I'd rather be a friend with a camera than a director with a shot list.

Based in California and travelling wherever love takes me, I photograph a limited number of weddings each year — usually around fifteen — so that every couple receives the presence, patience and care their day deserves.

About

Hi, I'm Elowen.

I believe a photograph should feel like a memory, not a performance — so I spend the day looking for the real thing.

I grew up on film, in a house where every drawer was full of half-finished rolls and my grandmother's handwriting on the back of them. That's still how I work: slowly, attentively, with an eye for the moments that disappear in a heartbeat.

On the day, you'll barely notice me. I'll be there for the tears, the chaos, the quiet minute you steal alone together — and I'll hand you back a story that sounds exactly like you.
