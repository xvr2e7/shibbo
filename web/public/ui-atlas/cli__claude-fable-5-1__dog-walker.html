Our Story

Started with one rescue pup and a lot of leash time

Happy Trails began in 2017 when founder Jordan Reyes adopted Biscuit, a stubborn beagle mix who needed a lot of walking. Neighbors started asking if Jordan could walk their dogs too — and the rest is history.

Today we're a small, tight-knit team of certified walkers who treat your dog like our own. No franchises, no rotating strangers — just the same friendly faces your pup knows and trusts.

FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

We currently walk dogs in Maplewood, Riverside, Oak Hill, and the surrounding neighborhoods within about a 6-mile radius of downtown. Not sure if you're covered? Just ask — we're growing!

Absolutely. Happy Trails is fully insured and bonded, and every walker passes a thorough background check before joining the team. We're also certified in Pet First Aid & CPR.

Rain or shine, we show up! In extreme heat, cold, or storms, we shorten walks for safety and fill the remaining time with indoor play and cuddles. You'll always be notified of any changes.

We keep things small and personal — walks are solo or with dogs from the same household only, unless you specifically request a buddy walk with a compatible pup.

Life happens! Cancel or reschedule free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice via text, email, or our app. Same-day cancellations may incur a 50% fee.

Most clients use a coded lockbox or smart lock, which we recommend. We can also hold a labeled key in our secure system. Keys are never marked with your address.
