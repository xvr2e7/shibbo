The Photographer

Hi, I'm Elena

I've spent the last nine years photographing weddings across coastlines, vineyards, and quiet backyard ceremonies — and every single one has taught me something about how people love each other.

My approach is unobtrusive and instinctive. I'm there to notice the things you'll want to remember: the look before the vows, the laugh mid-toast, the quiet exhale after it's all over.

Based in the Hudson Valley, available worldwide for destination weddings.
