Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Chat with us.

How does per-user billing work?

You're only billed for active members. If someone is deactivated mid-cycle, we automatically credit the unused time to your next invoice. Guests on Starter and above are always free.

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing, and downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle. If you cancel, your workspace moves to the Free plan and your data stays intact.

What happens to our messages if we downgrade to Free?

Nothing is deleted. Messages older than 90 days are hidden on the Free plan, and everything reappears instantly if you upgrade again.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes — eligible nonprofits and educational institutions get 50% off Starter and Business. Apply from your workspace settings and we'll verify within two business days.

Is my data secure?

All plans include encryption in transit and at rest, and Relay is SOC 2 Type II certified. Business adds SSO and retention controls; Enterprise adds audit logs, DLP integrations, data residency, and HIPAA compliance.
