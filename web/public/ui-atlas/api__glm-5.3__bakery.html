    House rules — enforced, lovingly

    The rules, such as they are.

    • 01.

      First come, first warm.

      No pre-orders, no holds, no favours. The queue is the whole point — it's where the news gets exchanged.

    • 02.

      We bake what the starter allows.

      It's nine years old, it's moody, and it doesn't take requests. Some weeks that means rye. Some weeks it means we find out on the day.

    • 03.

      Cash box, card, or a decent trade.

      Last month a man paid in transparent gages.* Best trade of the year, and he got two loaves and a knot for them.

    • 04.

      When it's gone, it's gone.

      There is no secret back shelf. The bread you can smell but cannot see does not exist. Come before ten, or come on Friday for a chat instead.

    • 05.

      Dogs are welcome.

      The bench out front seats three and a dog. The starter likes the attention.

    * since confirmed to be excellent plums. the trade stands.
