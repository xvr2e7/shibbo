01 — The idea

A conference about what people see.

Miradouro is Portuguese for viewpoint: the hilltop terraces where Lisbon stops to look at itself. We borrowed the word for two days about the view layer: the pixels, the paint, the focus ring, the font that loads a beat too late. Less framework horse-racing, more of the work that survives it.

  1. 01

    One track

    Everyone sees the same talks, so the hallway conversation starts from the same place.

  2. 02

    Long breaks

    Thirty minutes minimum. The best part of a conference is never on the stage.

  3. 03

    Show the work

    Every talk includes real code, real numbers or a real mistake. Ideally all three.

  4. 04

    Live captions

    All sessions are captioned in English by human stenographers, on screen and on your phone.

04 — Venue

A warehouse on the Tejo.

Armazém da Luz

A converted 1920s riverside warehouse in Marvila, east of the old centre. High brick vaults, daylight from both ends, and a quay where lunch is served if the weather behaves. In May, it usually does.

Address
Cais da Luz 16, Marvila, 1950 Lisboa
Metro
Fifteen minutes on foot from the nearest red-line station, or five on the free conference shuttle that loops every ten minutes from 08:00.
Airport
Lisbon airport is about twenty minutes away by taxi or metro.
Access
Step-free throughout, with a quiet room, a prayer room, gender-neutral toilets and a hearing loop at the stage.
Stay
Partner hotel rates in Marvila and Alfama are sent with your ticket confirmation.

06 — Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything missing? ola@miradouro.example reaches a human.

What language are the talks in?

English, with live human captioning on a screen beside the stage and on your own device. Hallway Portuguese is encouraged and gently coached.

Who is this conference for?

People who build interfaces for the web: frontend and design engineers, designers who code, accessibility specialists. Talks assume you write HTML, CSS and JavaScript for a living but not that you use any particular framework.

Can I get a refund or transfer my ticket?

Full refunds until 1 March 2027. After that, tickets can be transferred to another person free of charge until 1 May. Just send us the new name.

Will the talks be recorded?

Yes. Ticket holders get captioned recordings two weeks after the event; they become public for everyone in the autumn.

Is there childcare?

Free on-site childcare for ages 2–10 is available on both days with certified, English- and Portuguese-speaking carers. Places are limited, so tell us when you book.

Do you have a code of conduct?

We do, and we enforce it. Miradouro is for everyone who is kind to everyone else. Trained staff in yellow lanyards are on the floor at all times, and reports can be made in person or anonymously by text.

Can my company sponsor?

Yes: coffee, captions, childcare and scholarships are all funded by sponsors. There are no sponsor talks and no badge scanning. Ask for the one-page deck at parceiros@miradouro.example.
