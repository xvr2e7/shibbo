FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

You just launched. Why should I trust you with my finances? +

A fair question — we won't pretend otherwise with invented testimonials. What we can offer: bank connections are read-only through an established banking data provider, your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can export everything and delete your account at any time. We'd rather earn trust than claim it.

Can Tally Hall move money or pay bills from my account? +

No. Access is strictly read-only. Tally Hall can see your transactions to categorize them, but it has no ability to initiate transfers or payments of any kind.

Does this replace my accountant? +

It replaces the spreadsheet and the shoebox, not professional advice. Tally Hall keeps your records clean and estimates what to set aside; many users still hand the year-end export to an accountant for filing. If you file yourself, the summaries map to the categories your tax forms ask for.

I'm months behind on my books. Is it too late to start? +

That's exactly who we built this for. You can import up to twelve months of transaction history and sort it with quick swipes — most of it gets auto-matched, and the stragglers take one sitting to clear.

What happens to my data if I cancel? +

You can export a complete copy of your ledger as CSV or PDF before (or after) you cancel, and request full deletion whenever you like. Your books are yours, not a hostage.

Why is it called Tally Hall? +

Because keeping a tally shouldn't feel like a chore — it should feel like the good part of the show. Also, we like the name.
