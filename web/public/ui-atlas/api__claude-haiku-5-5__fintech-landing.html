FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Email us and a real human will reply.

Can my partner see everything in my accounts?

No. You choose what to share. Joint accounts and shared categories are visible to both of you, while individual accounts and transactions can stay private. Duet only ever reads account data; it cannot move money.

Do we both need a paid plan?

Only one of you needs a paid plan to unlock Duet Plus features for the household. The other partner can use the free Starter features at no cost, and the shared budget works for both of you.

What if we're not splitting 50/50?

That's common. You can split by fixed percentages or by each partner's share of income. You can also set different splits for different categories, like rent versus dining out.

Does it work if we have separate bank accounts?

Yes. Many couples keep separate accounts and a shared one for bills. Duet connects to 12,000+ banks and credit unions, so you can track everything in one place without merging finances.

What happens if we break up?

Duet makes it straightforward to separate. You can export your full history, unlink shared accounts, and divide goals and balances. Each partner keeps their own personal data.

How is my data protected?

Bank connections use read-only, encrypted access, and your data is encrypted at rest. We never sell your data, and you can delete your account and all associated data at any time from settings.
