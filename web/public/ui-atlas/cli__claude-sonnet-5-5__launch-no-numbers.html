Questions, answered

Who is Tally Hall for?

Freelancers and independent professionals — designers, developers, writers, consultants, and anyone running a business of one.

Is Tally Hall new?

Yes. We launched recently and are actively improving the product. Early users get a direct line to the team and real influence over what we build next.

Does it replace my accountant?

No. Tally Hall keeps your records organized so working with an accountant is faster and easier, but it doesn't provide tax or legal advice. Tax set-aside figures are estimates.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export your records at any time.
