FAQ

Questions, answered

Still curious? We've got you.

Do we need joint bank accounts to use Twogether?+
Not at all. Most of our couples keep separate accounts. Twogether just creates a shared view on top of whatever setup works for you.
Is my financial data safe?+
Yes. We use bank-level 256-bit encryption and read-only connections, meaning we can never move or withdraw your money.
Can one partner hide transactions from the other?+
You control exactly what's shared. Mark personal accounts as private, or share everything — the choice is always yours.
What if we break up?+
You can unlink your account instantly at any time. Your data stays private to you, and shared goals can be split or closed out.
Can I try it before paying?+
Yes! Starter is free forever, and both paid plans include a 14-day free trial — no card required to begin.
