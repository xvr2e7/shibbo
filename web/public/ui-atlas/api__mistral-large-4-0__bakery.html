    ✳ how the oddness happens

    Four acts, no intermission.

    1. 4:00AM

      Feed the starter

      Our rye starter gets its morning snack at 4am sharp. It's been eating since 2019 and it is thriving, thank you for asking.

    2. 5:30AM

      Shape the odd ones

      Every loaf is folded by hand and slightly wrong on purpose. Symmetry is for baguettes. These loaves have character.

    3. 7:00AM

      Into the oven, out the door

      Doors open at 7:00 sharp. First come, first fed. The bake list is chalked daily and the loaves go in that order — roughly.

    4. 12:00PM

      Gone

      When the last loaf leaves, we chalk SOLD OUT on the door and go home to sleep. Tomorrow, it happens all over again.
