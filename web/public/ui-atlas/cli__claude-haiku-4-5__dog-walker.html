About Happy Paws

Your Dog Deserves the Best

At Happy Paws, we believe every dog deserves personalized attention and care. With over 5 years of experience in professional dog walking, our certified walkers treat your furry family members with the same love and care we give our own pets.

We understand that dogs need more than just bathroom breaks—they need exercise, mental stimulation, and social interaction. Our walkers are trained in dog behavior and safety protocols to ensure every walk is enjoyable and stress-free.

Whether your pup is a young enthusiast or a senior citizen, we have the perfect walking plan tailored to their personality and energy level.

  • ✓ All dog sizes and temperaments welcome
