    About the record

    We wrote it in a lake house in February with no heat, one good space heater, and a borrowed piano that was a quarter-tone flat.

    Slow Weather began as a folder of voice memos: rain on a tin roof, a neighbor's wind chimes, Ines humming over the dishwasher. We stopped trying to fix the wobble and built the songs around it.

    The result is the warmest, strangest thing we've made — fuzzy guitars next to chamber strings, drum machines next to a kid's xylophone, and a lot of room to breathe.
