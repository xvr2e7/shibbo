Good questions

A few things you might be wondering.

Do we need a joint bank account?

No. Together is designed around the plans you share, not how you hold your money. You can make a shared budget while keeping individual accounts.

Can we still have our own spending money?

Absolutely. A shared plan can include space for individual spending, so you can work toward common goals without accounting for every coffee.

What if we’ve never budgeted together?

That’s a great place to start. Begin with the expenses and goals that matter to you both, then adjust your plan as life changes.
