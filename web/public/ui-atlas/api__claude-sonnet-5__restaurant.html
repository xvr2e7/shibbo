Our Story

A Kitchen Built on Family & Tradition

Zaytoun & Zaatar began in 1987 when Teta Souad started selling her stuffed grape leaves and fresh pita from a small window in Beirut. Today, her children and grandchildren carry on her exact recipes — no shortcuts, no substitutes.

Every morning we bake our own bread, pickle our own vegetables, and press our own olive oil. What you taste on your plate is the same thing we've been serving our own family for over three decades.

— The Haddad Family
