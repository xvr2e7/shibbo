About Elara

A quiet eye in the most beautiful chaos

Based between the Hudson Valley and wherever love takes me, I've spent over a decade photographing weddings with an instinct for stillness — finding the held breath before the first dance, the trembling hands during vows, the grandmother who cries at the back of the room.

My style is intimate and unhurried. I bring the same calm to a ceremony as I do to your engagement session. The result is a collection of images that feel lived-in — naturally yours.
