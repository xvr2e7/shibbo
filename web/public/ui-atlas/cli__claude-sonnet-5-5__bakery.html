Why "Odd" Loaf?

The first loaf we ever pulled from the oven was lopsided, split down one side and absolutely delicious. The neighbours called it odd. We kept the name.

We use organic flour from a small mill, water, salt and a starter that's been fed every day for years. No additives, no shortcuts, no second bake in the afternoon.

How to get a loaf

It's simple, but you do have to be a bit early.

Walk in

No pre-orders and no delivery. Just come by and join the queue; it moves quickly and smells wonderful.

Come early

Weekday mornings are calmest. Saturdays sell out fastest, so aim for before 9:30.

Bring a bag

We wrap in paper, but a tote makes us happy and saves a bag.

Freeze the rest

Slice what you won't eat in two days and freeze it. Toast straight from frozen.
