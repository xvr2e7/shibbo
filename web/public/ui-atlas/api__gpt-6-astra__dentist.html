Let’s clear things up.

Are you accepting new patients?

Absolutely. We welcome new patients of all ages. Request a visit online or call our team, and we’ll help you find a time that works. If you have previous dental records, we can help coordinate their transfer.

What should I expect at my first visit?

A friendly hello, time to get to know each other, and a thorough look at your oral health. We’ll review your history, discuss any concerns, and explain any recommended care before moving forward. Please bring your photo ID, insurance card, and a list of medications.

Do you accept dental insurance?

We work with many major PPO dental plans. Coverage varies, so call us with your plan details before your visit. We’ll help check your benefits and explain estimated out-of-pocket costs before treatment.

What if I’m nervous about the dentist?

You’re not alone, and you don’t have to hide it. Tell us when you book so we can make a little extra time. We’ll explain each step, agree on a pause signal, and go at a pace that feels comfortable for you.
