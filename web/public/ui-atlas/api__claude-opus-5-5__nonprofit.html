Why trees

Tree cover follows income. We're changing the map.

In most cities, wealthier neighborhoods have up to twice the canopy of low-income ones. That gap shows up as hotter homes, higher energy bills, more asthma, and fewer places to simply sit outside.

  • Cooler blocks and lower summer energy costs
  • Cleaner air and less stormwater flooding
  • Paid green-job training for local young adults

How it works

Planting is the easy part.
Growing takes a block.

A tree needs about three years of care to take root for good. Our model is built around the neighbors who'll walk past it every day.

01

Listen

Residents request trees and choose species with us at block meetings. No tree goes in without a "yes" from the people who live there.

02

Plant

Saturday mornings, our arborists lead volunteer crews to cut sidewalk pits, amend soil, and plant 2-inch caliper trees properly.

03

Tend

Every tree gets a neighbor steward, a watering bag, and text reminders. Our crews prune and check health for the first three years.

Give shade

Every $60 puts one tree in the ground, and keeps it alive.

Your gift covers the sapling, soil, sidewalk cutting, a watering bag, and three years of professional care. 86¢ of every dollar goes straight to planting and stewardship.

Monthly donors, our Root Circle, make it possible to promise neighbors we'll come back.

Free for residents

Want a tree on your block?

Homeowners and renters can request a free street tree. We'll check the site, confirm utilities, and help you pick the right species. Renters: we'll reach out to your landlord with you.
