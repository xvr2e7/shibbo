FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Murmur.

How does the AI transcription work?
Murmur uses state-of-the-art speech recognition to transcribe your voice in real-time. Simply tap the microphone, start speaking, and your words appear as text instantly. The AI automatically adds punctuation, identifies speakers in multi-person conversations, and can even summarize key points afterward.
Is my data private and secure?
Absolutely. Your notes are end-to-end encrypted, meaning only you can access them. We use industry-standard AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Murmur never sells your data, never shows ads, and never trains AI models on your private notes without explicit opt-in consent.
Can I import notes from other apps?
Yes! Murmur supports one-click import from Evernote, Notion, Apple Notes, Google Keep, and Markdown files. Your existing notes will be automatically tagged and organized by the AI, so you can pick up right where you left off.
What happens if I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel at any time. If you cancel your Pro or Team plan, you'll maintain access until the end of your billing period. After that, your account reverts to the Free plan. All your notes remain safe and accessible — nothing is deleted.
Does Murmur work offline?
Yes, Murmur works fully offline. You can capture notes, write, and organize without an internet connection. When you reconnect, everything syncs automatically across your devices. AI features that require cloud processing will queue and run when you're back online.
