Get Tickets View Schedule

Why Lisbon?

Frontend Conf Lisbon brings together the European web community for two days of deep dives into CSS, JavaScript, accessibility, performance, and design systems.

Expect practical talks, hands-on workshops, and plenty of time to connect over pastéis de nata and Atlantic views.

What to expect

  • 20+ speaker sessions across two stages
  • Full-day optional workshops
  • Lunch, coffee, and an afterparty included
  • Beautiful venue in the heart of Lisbon
