About the Album

"Electric Daydreams" is the third studio album from Lunar Moths, recorded over 18 months in a converted lighthouse on the Oregon coast. The album explores themes of memory, transformation, and finding beauty in the spaces between waking and sleeping.

Blending shoegaze textures with electronic production and folk-inspired songwriting, the band has created their most ambitious and personal work to date. Each track was crafted during the golden hours of dawn and dusk, capturing the liminal energy that defines their sound.
