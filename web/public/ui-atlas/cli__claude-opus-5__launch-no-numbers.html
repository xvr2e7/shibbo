How it works

Set it up on a Sunday. Spend twenty minutes a month after that.

Most freelance bookkeeping tools are small-business accounting suites with the hard parts left in. Tally Hall only does the three things a sole proprietor actually needs.

  1. Connect or import

    Link a bank or card account through Plaid, or drag in CSV statements — including back-dated ones, so you can catch up on a year you've been avoiding.

  2. Confirm the categories

    Transactions arrive pre-sorted into IRS Schedule C categories. You correct the ones it got wrong; it remembers the merchant and stops asking.

  3. Know your number

    A running estimate of federal income and self-employment tax for the current quarter, so the 15th is never a surprise. Export the whole year as CSV, JSON or a PDF summary.

Honest about where we are

What Tally Hall can't do yet.

A one-week-old product has gaps. Finding them two days after you've imported three years of statements is worse than reading about them now.

  • Mobile apps

    iOS in beta

    The web app works on a phone, but it isn't a native experience. An iOS build is in TestFlight; Android hasn't started.

  • Bank coverage

    Partial

    Most US banks and card issuers connect fine. Some credit unions and newer neobanks don't yet — CSV import is the fallback, and it's not a bad one.

  • Non-US taxes

    2027

    Tax estimates assume a US sole proprietor or single-member LLC filing a Schedule C. You can still use Tally Hall as a plain ledger anywhere; the tax number just won't apply to you.

  • Multi-currency

    Planned

    One currency per account today. If you invoice in EUR and bank in USD, you'll be doing that conversion by hand for now.

  • Payroll & contractors

    Not planned

    The moment you're running payroll you've outgrown us, and you should use a tool built for it. We'd rather say so than build a bad version.

  • Filing your return

    Not planned

    Tally Hall gets your books filing-ready and hands them off. It does not submit anything to the IRS, and it isn't a substitute for a CPA.

The obvious objection

"Why would I trust my books to an app that's eight days old?"

Fair. We can't offer you a decade of track record, so we've tried to make the downside of being early as small as we can.

01 — Your data leaves freely

A full export is one click, always

Transactions, invoices, receipts and categories, as CSV and JSON, from the settings page. No support ticket, no retention flow, no paid tier required. If we're not working out, you walk away with everything.

02 — Read-only by design

We can't move your money

Bank connections run through Plaid in read-only mode: Tally Hall can see transactions and cannot initiate transfers. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

03 — A real refund, not a credit

60 days, money back

Cancel inside 60 days of your first payment and we refund it to your card, no questions and no exit interview. Annual plans are refunded pro rata after that.

04 — If we fail, you get warning

60 days' notice and a working exporter

Startups die. If Tally Hall shuts down we commit to 60 days' notice, exports kept running to the last day, and no final-month charge. It's in our terms, not just on this page.

Questions

Asked and answered.

You really have no reviews at all?

None. We opened sign-ups on 3 November 2026. There are people using it — friends, former clients, people from the beta — but nobody has used Tally Hall through a full quarter yet, so any testimonial we published would be about a first impression rather than whether it actually holds up.

When we have real reviews, they'll go on this page with full names and the dates they were written. Until then this section stays empty.

Can I move my books over from another tool?

Yes, if you can get a CSV out of it — which you can from QuickBooks, Wave, FreshBooks, Xero and a spreadsheet. The importer maps columns on screen and previews the result before anything is saved. Historical categories come across as free-text tags you can remap in bulk.

Will this handle my taxes if I'm an S-corp / have employees / file abroad?

No. Tax estimates are built for US sole proprietors and single-member LLCs filing a Schedule C. S-corp distributions, payroll withholding and non-US regimes are out of scope and we'd rather you knew now. The ledger, invoicing and export still work regardless of where you file.

Who sees my financial data?

You, anyone you explicitly invite, and the small team that operates the service when you ask us for help with something. We don't sell data, we don't share it with advertisers, and we don't train models on your ledger. Bank connections are read-only.

What happens to my account if Tally Hall shuts down?

You get at least 60 days' written notice, exports stay available until the last day, and you aren't charged for the final month. We've put that in the terms of service so it's an obligation rather than a promise on a marketing page.

Is support actually a human?

For now it's literally the people writing the code, at hello@tallyhall.app. Expect a reply within one business day. If you'd rather talk to whoever decides what gets built, that's founders@tallyhall.app.
