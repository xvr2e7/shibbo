"We were snowed in for three weeks with a broken woodstove, two reel-to-reel decks, and eleven rolls of magnetic tape that smelled like cedar smoke."

Where the Fog Settles was born between blizzards in Brooklin, Maine. Rather than aiming for polished perfection, we let the temperature of the room dictate the tuning of our acoustic guitars and felt pianos.

You can hear the wind rattling the timber framing during the quiet bridge of "Ember Days," and the snowmelt dripping into iron gutters at the close of "Low Tide Bells." It is an archive of survival, kinship, and silence.
