New Album

Dissolving Light

Born from late-night sessions in a converted lighthouse on the Oregon coast, Dissolving Light is a meditation on impermanence, connection, and the spaces between words. Layered synths meet raw guitar tones, carried by vocals that feel like coming home to a place you've never been.
