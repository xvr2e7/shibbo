Brew Guide

Four steps to a better cup

A simple starting recipe for 300 g of water and 20 g of medium-fine coffee. Adjust to taste.

Rinse

Place a paper filter in the dripper and rinse with hot water. This removes paper taste and preheats your cup.

Bloom

Add 20 g of ground coffee and pour 50 g of water. Wait 30 seconds while the coffee degasses.

Pour

Pour the remaining water in slow, even circles over 2:30, keeping the water level steady.

Drawdown

Let the coffee drain fully. A finish around 3:30 to 4:00 is a good target for most beans.

FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

Will it work with any paper filter?

It's designed for size 02 cone filters. Size 01 will fit but may sit high on the rim; size 04 is not recommended.

Can I use it with a metal filter?

Yes. The cone accepts most stainless and mesh filters, though you may notice a slightly fuller body in the cup.

Is the glaze safe for hot water and dishwashers?

Yes. The glaze is food-safe and lead- and cadmium-free. Hand rinsing is recommended to keep the finish bright over time.

Why is it heavier than other drippers?

The thick stoneware walls hold heat through the entire brew, which keeps extraction even from first pour to last.

What if it arrives chipped?

Contact us within 14 days with a photo and we'll send a replacement at no cost. Shipping damage is covered by the two-year warranty.
