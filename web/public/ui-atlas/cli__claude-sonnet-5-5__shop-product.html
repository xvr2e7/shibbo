A simple recipe

  1. Rinse & preheatWet the filter with hot water, then discard.
  2. Bloom · 0:0015 g medium-fine coffee, 45 g water at 94°C. Wait 40 seconds.
  3. Pour · 0:40Spiral slowly to 250 g in two stages.
  4. Finish · 2:45Let it drain, swirl, and enjoy.

Questions

Which filters should I use?

Any standard #2 cone paper filter. Both sizes also work with most reusable metal cones.

Is the glaze food safe?

Yes. Lead- and cadmium-free, and tested to FDA and LFGB standards.

Can I put it in the dishwasher?

Absolutely. Top rack recommended to keep the glaze looking its best.

Each piece looks slightly different. Why?

Every dripper is hand-glazed and kiln-fired, so subtle variation in tone is part of the character.
