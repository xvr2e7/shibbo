Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Talk to a human.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Pick Glow or Blaze and use every feature for 14 days. No credit card required. When the trial ends you can choose a plan or drop down to Spark. Nothing gets deleted either way.

Who counts as a billable person?

Anyone who sends a message, joins a huddle or reacts in a billing month. Members who stay silent are free, and so are deactivated accounts. Guests on Blaze and Bonfire are billed at one-third the rate.

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your billing period. Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days.

What happens to my history if I go back to Spark?

Older messages are hidden, not deleted. Upgrade again within a year and everything reappears exactly where you left it.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits and schools?

We do: 50% off Glow and Blaze for registered nonprofits, open-source projects, and accredited educational institutions. Send us a note and we'll set it up the same day.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards on every paid plan. Annual Blaze and Bonfire customers can also pay by invoice with ACH or wire transfer.
