Good questions

Can my partner see everything I spend?

Only what you choose to share. Anything in a private space stays visible to you alone.

What if we split bills unevenly?

Set any ratio, by percentage or by income, and change it whenever life does.

Is my bank data safe?

Connections are read-only and encrypted. We can't move your money, and we never sell your information.

Does it work for unmarried or long-distance couples?

Absolutely. Tandem works for any two people sharing financial life, wherever they live.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in two taps and keep your data on the free plan.
