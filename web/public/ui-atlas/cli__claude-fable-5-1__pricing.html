Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Talk to our team.

How does per-user pricing work?

You're billed for each active member in your workspace. Guests and deactivated accounts are free. If someone joins mid-cycle, we prorate the charge, and if they leave, you receive a credit toward your next bill.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes. You can move to annual billing at any time and we'll apply the discount immediately. Moving from annual to monthly takes effect at the end of your current term.

What happens when my trial ends?

If you don't add a payment method, your workspace simply drops back to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted, but messages older than 90 days become hidden until you upgrade again.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

We do. Registered nonprofits and educational institutions get Pro at 50% off and Business at 30% off. Apply from your workspace billing page with proof of status.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards, plus ACH and wire transfer for annual Business and Enterprise plans. Invoicing with net-30 terms is available on Enterprise.

Can I cancel at any time?

Absolutely. Cancel from your billing settings and you'll keep paid features until the end of your billing period. We don't charge cancellation fees.
