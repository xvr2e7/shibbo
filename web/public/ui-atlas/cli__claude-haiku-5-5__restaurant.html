Our Story

Three generations, one table

Sahara began in 1994 as a small corner kitchen with eight seats, a wood-fired oven, and a promise to cook the food we grew up eating. Today, our family still gathers every morning to make the bread, roll the grape leaves, and marinate the lamb the same way our grandmother taught us.

We believe good food is a form of hospitality. Every plate is made to be shared, every guest is treated like family, and nobody leaves hungry.
