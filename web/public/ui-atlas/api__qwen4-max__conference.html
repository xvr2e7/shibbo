01 · About

Maré means tide — and the web keeps moving too. MARÉ brings the people building the frontend of the next decade to Lisbon's riverside for two dense, single-track days.

No vendor pitches disguised as talks. No five parallel tracks forcing you to choose. Just practitioners sharing what actually shipped: rendering strategies, modern CSS, reactivity models, accessibility, performance, and the tools that hold it all together. Between sessions: strong coffee, warm pastéis, and a rooftop with a view of the 25 de Abril bridge.

Run by a non-profit collective of Lisbon engineers. All talks recorded, captioned, and released free after the event.

Questions, answered

What language are the talks in?
All talks are in English. Live captions run on the main screen throughout, and recordings are published with edited captions and transcripts within two weeks.
Is there a code of conduct?
Yes — a strict one, enforced. MARÉ is for everyone who builds the web, regardless of background or experience level. Report anything to any crew member (yellow lanyards) or conduct@mareconf.dev. We act, we don't deliberate.
Can I get a refund or transfer my ticket?
Full refunds until May 1, 2026, no questions asked. After that, tickets are freely transferable to another person — just email tickets@mareconf.dev with the new attendee's name.
How do the diversity scholarships work?
40 full tickets are reserved for students and people from groups under-represented in tech. The application is a short form (no essay marathons), opens February 1, and decisions go out by March 1. Travel grants of up to €150 are available on request.
Will talks be recorded?
Every talk, panel, and lightning session is recorded and released free on our channel after the event. The rooftop mixer and tram afterparty are strictly off the record. 🤫
What about food and dietary needs?
Breakfast, lunch, and two coffee breaks each day are included, always with vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free options. Tell us about allergies at checkout and the kitchen will have you covered — including gluten-free pastéis.
Do you help with visas or hotels?
We issue visa invitation letters within 48 hours of a ticket purchase — request one at visas@mareconf.dev. We've also negotiated rates with three hotels in Alcântara and Santos; the codes arrive with your ticket confirmation.
