✳ Side A / Side B

Songs for the long way home.

Best enjoyed at golden hour, through slightly broken speakers, with nowhere urgent to be.

Our second record is a little wilder around the edges. Fuzzed-out guitars, close-harmony choruses, and field recordings from eleven rain-soaked days in a converted barn.

✳ Behind the record

A little less polish.
A lot more pulse.

We arrived at the barn with thirty voice memos, two broken amps, and no real plan. We left with the most honest thing we’ve ever made.

There was no chasing perfection. We kept the chair squeaks, the rain on the roof, and the take where June laughs halfway through the last chorus. That’s where the songs lived.

— Glass Meadow, x
