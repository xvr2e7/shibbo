Questions

Frequently asked

Are you accepting new patients?+

Yes! We're happily welcoming new patients of all ages. Most new patients can be seen within the week, and emergencies are seen the same day.

I'm really nervous about the dentist. Can you help?+

Absolutely — this is one of our specialties. We offer nitrous oxide, oral sedation, noise-cancelling headphones, and agreed-upon "pause" signals. Just let us know when booking and we'll schedule extra time.

What should I bring to my first appointment?+

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card (if applicable), and a list of medications. You can complete your forms online beforehand to save time.

At what age should my child first see a dentist?+

We recommend a first visit by your child's first birthday or within six months of their first tooth. Early visits are short, playful, and help build trust.

Do you offer payment plans?+

Yes. We partner with CareCredit® and Sunbit for flexible financing, including 0% interest options for qualified patients. We'll always share a written estimate first.
