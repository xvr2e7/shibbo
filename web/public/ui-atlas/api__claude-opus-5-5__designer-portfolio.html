How I work

No rigid playbook — but most projects move through these four rhythms, often looping back more than once.

01

Listen

Interviews, shadowing, support tickets and data. I want to understand the job before touching the interface.

02

Frame

Turn findings into a sharp problem statement and success metrics the whole team agrees on.

03

Make

Sketch wide, prototype fast, test early. High-fidelity only when it helps answer a real question.

04

Ship & learn

Pair with engineering through launch, then measure, iterate, and document what we learned.

About

I believe good software feels like a quiet room — everything you need, nothing shouting for attention.

I started as a print designer in Lagos, moved into front-end development, and found my home in product design somewhere in between. That mix still shapes how I work: I care about type and rhythm as much as about state diagrams and edge cases.

Outside of work I mentor early-career designers through ADPList, run a small risograph studio, and swim in the Atlantic whenever the weather allows.
