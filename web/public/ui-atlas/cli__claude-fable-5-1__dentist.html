About Our Practice

A dental home where you're known by name, not chart number.

Brightwater Dental was founded in 2008 by Dr. Elena Marsh with a simple idea: dental care should feel unhurried, honest, and genuinely comfortable. Eighteen years later, we're a team of four dentists and twenty-two team members caring for three generations of local families.

We invest in technology that makes visits easier, like same-day crowns and 3D imaging, but we're proudest of the things that don't show up on a brochure: calling you after a procedure, remembering your kid's soccer season, and never recommending treatment you don't need.

  • Transparent, itemized treatment plans with no surprise bills
  • Same-day CEREC crowns, no temporaries or second visit
  • Warm blankets, noise-cancelling headphones, and streaming TV in every room
  • Fluent care in English, Spanish, and Mandarin

Common Questions

Good to know before your visit.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of current medications. You can save time by completing our new-patient forms online when we text you a confirmation. Plan for about 75 minutes for your first comprehensive exam and cleaning.

Do you see children, and at what age should they start?

Yes! We recommend a first visit by age one or within six months of the first tooth. Early visits are short, playful, and focused on getting your child comfortable in the chair.

I'm nervous about the dentist. What options do you offer?

You're not alone, and we take it seriously. We offer nitrous oxide ("laughing gas"), oral sedation, and a go-slow approach where you set the pace. Many anxious patients also appreciate our noise-cancelling headphones and weighted blankets.

What if I don't have dental insurance?

Our Brightwater Smile Plan is an in-house membership that includes two cleanings, exams, and X-rays per year plus 20% off all other treatment for a flat annual fee. We also offer 0% financing for larger plans.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes. Call us before 3pm on weekdays and we will see you the same day. Existing patients can reach our after-hours line through the main number for urgent guidance.

Where do I park?

Free covered parking is available in the Harbor View garage directly beneath our building. Take the elevator to the second floor and we're the first suite on your left.
