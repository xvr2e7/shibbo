Finance tools built

Shared & personal budgets

Create joint budgets for shared expenses while keeping personal spending private. See who spent what, without the judgment.

Joint goal tracking

Save for a home, a honeymoon, or an emergency fund together. Watch your progress grow in real time and celebrate milestones.

Auto-sync transactions
