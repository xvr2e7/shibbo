Our Impact

Every tree we plant makes a measurable difference in our urban environments and communities

🌍

Climate Action

Our trees absorb over 12,000 tons of CO2 annually, directly combating climate change and reducing urban heat islands by up to 5°F.

💨

Cleaner Air

Trees filter harmful pollutants, removing over 60 tons of air pollutants each year and improving respiratory health for city residents.

🏘️

Community Building

We focus on underserved neighborhoods, creating green spaces that bring communities together and increase property values by 15%.

🦋

Biodiversity

Urban forests provide crucial habitats for birds, insects, and wildlife, supporting biodiversity even in the heart of cities.

💧

Water Management

Our tree canopies intercept stormwater, reducing runoff by 20% and preventing millions of gallons from overwhelming city systems.

❤️

Health & Wellbeing

Studies show that access to urban green spaces reduces stress, encourages physical activity, and improves mental health outcomes.

Current Projects

Discover where we're planting and the communities we're transforming

🌳

Downtown Green Corridor

📍 Portland, OR

Transforming a 2-mile stretch of downtown with native oak and maple trees, creating a shaded pedestrian pathway and wildlife corridor.

🌲

School Yard Forests

📍 Chicago, IL

Planting 5,000 trees across 50 public schools to provide outdoor learning spaces and reduce playground temperatures by 15°F.

🌴

Urban Oasis Initiative

📍 Phoenix, AZ

Creating micro-forests in vacant lots to combat extreme heat, featuring drought-resistant trees adapted to desert climates.
