Odd Loaf began with an overenthusiastic starter and a kitchen that was much too small. Now we’ve got a slightly bigger kitchen, and the same obsession.

We work with the dough, not the clock. Long, slow fermentation. Good flour. A hot oven. Every loaf shaped by actual, slightly floury hands. No shortcuts. No two quite alike.
