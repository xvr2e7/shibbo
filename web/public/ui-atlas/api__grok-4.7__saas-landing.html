02 / Method

Connect once. Read every Friday.

No new tags. No agent workflow. Relay only needs a read-only view of conversations you already store.

  1. 01

    Connect the inbox

    One helpdesk, or a shared mailbox. Most teams finish before lunch. Revoke access from the helpdesk whenever you like.

  2. 02

    Weigh the week

    Relay clusters by intent, then weights repeats, plan, and whether the theme spiked after a release. Password resets stay quiet unless they surge.

  3. 03

    Deliver the brief

    A Thursday draft if you want to cut or pin a theme. Friday morning it goes to email, Slack, or both — the same link, every week.

07 / Questions

Before you connect an inbox.

Which helpdesks can Relay read?

Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, Gorgias, Help Scout, Front, and a shared mailbox via forwarding. If your queue lives somewhere else, the mailbox path usually covers it.

Does it reply to customers, or file tickets back?

No. Relay requests read-only scopes. It does not post internal notes, change status, or email anyone. The only things it writes are the brief, and the draft you edit.

How does it avoid turning noise into a theme?

It clusters by intent, then weights repeat reporters, plan, and sudden jumps after a release. One-off password resets stay in an appendix unless they spike. Thin clusters are labeled thin.

Where does the report go?

Email, Slack, or a stable link. PDF if you need it in a board packet. The Thursday draft is optional — some teams send Friday untouched.

Is ticket data used to train a general model?

No. Tickets are processed for your workspace and not used to train foundation models. You can delete a workspace, and you can revoke the helpdesk token without writing to us first.

Can I correct it when it merges two problems?

Yes. Split, merge, pin, or kill a theme in the draft. Relay remembers the correction next week instead of reintroducing the same bad cluster.
