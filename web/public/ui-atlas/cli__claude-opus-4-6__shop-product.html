  1. Boil & RinseHeat water to 93–96°C. Place a paper filter in the dripper and rinse with hot water to remove paper taste and pre-heat the ceramic.
    2. \n
  2. Dose & LevelAdd 18 g of medium-fine ground coffee (about the texture of table salt). Gently shake to level the bed.
    3. \n
  3. BloomPour 40 g of water in a slow spiral. Wait 30–45 seconds while CO₂ off-gasses. You'll see the bed rise and bubble.
    4. \n
  4. Pour in StagesIn two or three pours, add water up to 300 g total. Pour in slow, steady concentric circles, avoiding the edges of the filter.
    5. \n
  5. Draw Down & EnjoyTotal brew time should be around 2:30–3:00. Remove the dripper, swirl the carafe, and enjoy the clarity.
    6. \n
\n

\n \n \n \n \n \n \n
DailyRinse with hot water after each use. A quick rinse while the ceramic is warm prevents oil buildup.
WeeklyWash with warm water and mild dish soap. Avoid abrasive sponges on the glaze.
Deep CleanSoak in a solution of 1 tbsp baking soda per litre of hot water for 30 min. Rinse thoroughly.
Stain RemovalFor stubborn coffee stains, a paste of baking soda and water left for 15 min works well.
StorageStore in a dry place. Avoid stacking without padding — the unglazed foot ring can scratch other ceramics.
