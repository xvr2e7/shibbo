Questions

The things couples ask us first

Can my partner see my personal spending?
No. Accounts you mark as personal are invisible to your partner — they see only your agreed contributions to the shared pot. You choose what gets shared when you link each account, and you can change it at any time (we'll tell both of you when visibility changes, so nothing goes dark quietly).
Do we both need our own account?
Yes, and that's the point. Each of you gets your own login and your own private space, then you link the two into one shared household. One Mutual Plus subscription covers both people — you'll never be asked to pay twice.
What if our finances aren't merged at all?
That's the most common setup in our beta. Mutual works entirely on top of separate accounts: you nominate which costs are shared, pick a split, and Mutual tracks who has paid what and squares it up on payday. A joint bank account is optional.
How does splitting by income work?
You each enter your take-home pay (you can keep the exact number private and share only the resulting percentage). Mutual converts it into a proportional share of every shared cost, so the same rent takes the same bite out of each paycheck. Override any single bill whenever you want.
Is my banking data safe?
Connections are read-only — Mutual can never move your money. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, we use regulated open-banking providers rather than storing your credentials, and we don't sell or broker your data to anyone, ever. You can revoke a connection or delete your account in two taps.
What happens if we break up?
Relationships change and your money shouldn't be held hostage. Either partner can unlink the household at any time. You each keep your own accounts, your own history, and a full export of the shared records — including who contributed what, which turns out to matter. No ransom, no lock-in, no awkward email to support.
