From our mother's kitchen.
To your neighborhood.

In our family, “have you eaten?” has always meant “I love you.” There was never a recipe book. Just a pinch of this, a taste of that, and enough food for whoever knocked on the door.

We opened Beit to keep that feeling alive. The recipes are the ones we grew up with. The welcome is the one we brought from home. And there’s always room for one more.
