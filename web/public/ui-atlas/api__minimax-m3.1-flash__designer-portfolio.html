03 — How it works

A process with
no theatre.

Four weeks of focused work, then we build. Every stage ends with something you can see, click or read — never a deck that goes nowhere.

01

Listen

Stakeholder interviews, support-log mining and a proper look at the analytics. I want to know where the product is actually losing people.

02

Frame

Problem framing, principles and a single direction. One strong idea beats three safe ones, and I will argue for it.

03

Make

Weekly prototypes in front of real users. Flows, interface, motion, content — refined in the open with engineers already in the room.

04

Ship & learn

Handover that engineers thank you for, plus instrumentation so we can tell what actually worked once it's live.

05 — About

Designer first.
Listener always.

I'm Mira, a product designer based in Copenhagen. I started in editorial illustration, drifted into interaction design because I liked the constraint, and have spent the last nine years making software for regulated, complicated industries — banking, healthcare, logistics.

My favourite work happens in the gap between what a company says it wants and what a person actually needs. I care about typography, about writing good error messages, and about teams that argue about the work instead of about the work.

Away from the screen I run a tiny letterpress studio in Nørrebro, and I am slowly learning to make a decent espresso.
