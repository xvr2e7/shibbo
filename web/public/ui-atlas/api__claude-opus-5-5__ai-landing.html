05 — Questions

Asked, answered.

Does my audio leave my device?

Transcription happens entirely on your iPhone or Mac. Summaries use a private model; text is encrypted in transit, never stored on our servers after processing, and never used for training.

Can it handle accents and multiple languages?

Yes — Murmur understands 40+ languages and switches mid‑sentence if you do. It learns names and jargon you use often, so “Siobhán” and “Kubernetes” come out right.

Does it work offline?

Recording and transcription work fully offline. Distilled notes are generated the moment you’re back online, and they’ll be waiting for you.

Can I record meetings?

You can. Murmur separates speakers, and on the Chorus plan it can join calls on Zoom, Meet and Teams. We always recommend letting people know they’re being recorded.

What if I cancel?

Your notes are yours. Export everything as Markdown, plain text or audio at any time — even on the free plan.
