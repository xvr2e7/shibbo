02 — Approach

How I work

A simple loop, repeated until the product is boring in the best way.

01

Find the real question

Interviews, support logs and analytics, synthesized into one sentence the whole team can repeat.

02

Sketch wide, cut fast

Many cheap options in the first week. Prototypes in front of users before anything gets polished.

03

Design in the material

I work closely with engineers, in real data and real constraints, so what’s designed is what ships.

04

Measure, then simplify

Every launch has a metric and a review date. The best follow-up is often removing something.

03 — About

I believe good product design is mostly about taking things away.
