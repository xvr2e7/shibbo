Questions

Answers, before you ask in #pricing.

Still unsure which plan fits? Send us a message and a human will reply — usually within a couple of hours.

Talk to a human
What counts as an "active member"?
Only people who sign in and send or read messages during the billing period. Deactivated accounts and guests you invite for a single channel don't count toward your seat total — we bill for them separately at $2 per guest, capped at the cost of a full seat.
Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?
Anytime. Moving to annual applies the 20% discount immediately and we credit the unused portion of your monthly cycle. Switching back takes effect at your next renewal date, so you never lose time you've already paid for.
Do you charge per message or per minute?
No. Messages, huddles, calls and searches are unmetered on every paid plan. Your invoice is seats × plan price, and that's it — the only additions are optional add-ons you switch on yourself.
What happens when the Free plan runs out of history?
Nothing disappears. Older messages stay stored and visible to admins, but search is limited to the most recent 10,000 messages. Upgrading to Pro restores full search across your entire archive instantly — there's no re-indexing wait.
Is there a discount for non-profits or education?
Yes — registered non-profits get 50% off Pro and Business, and accredited schools get Pro free for up to 200 members. Apply from your workspace billing page with proof of status and it's approved within two business days.
How does billing work when the team grows or shrinks?
We prorate automatically. Add five people on a Tuesday and you're charged for the remaining days of the cycle only. Remove them and we add credit to your account for your next invoice. We also flag workspaces paying for seats nobody has used in 60 days.
What's your refund policy?
Annual plans come with a 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. After that we refund unused full months on request. Monthly plans can be cancelled any time and simply stop renewing.
