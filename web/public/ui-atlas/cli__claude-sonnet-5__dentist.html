Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often from our patients.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans and will help you understand your coverage before treatment. We also offer in-house financing for uninsured patients.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend a checkup and cleaning every six months for most patients, though your dentist may suggest a different schedule based on your specific oral health needs.

Do you offer emergency appointments?

Yes, we reserve same-day slots for dental emergencies such as severe pain, broken teeth, or injuries. Call our office directly for urgent care.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Absolutely. Our professional whitening treatments are performed under dentist supervision using safe, clinically tested methods that protect your enamel and gums.

Do you treat children?

Yes, our pediatric dentistry services are designed specifically for younger patients, with a gentle approach that helps build positive, lifelong dental habits.
