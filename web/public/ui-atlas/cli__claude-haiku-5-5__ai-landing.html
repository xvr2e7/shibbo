Features

Less typing. More remembering.

Everything you say or hear becomes something you can find, act on, and build from.

◐

Listens, then writes it up

Record a meeting or a walk-and-talk memo. Murmur transcribes accurately and structures the result into headings, decisions, and next steps.

⌕

Ask your notes anything

"What did we decide about pricing in October?" Murmur searches across every note you've ever taken and answers with links to the source.

✦

Action items that follow through

How it works

Three steps. Zero filing.

Murmur fits into the way you already think, without a new system to learn.

01

Capture

Tap record, or let Murmur join your calendar meetings automatically. Voice memos work offline and sync later.

02

Refine

Notes are organized into clear sections with summaries, tags, and linked people or projects. Edit anything you like.

03

Recall

Search by meaning, not just keywords. Murmur surfaces the right note the moment you need it, even if you forgot the exact words.
