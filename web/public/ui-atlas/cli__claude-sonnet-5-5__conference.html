About

A conference for people who care about the details.

Azulejo Conf is a single-track event for frontend developers, designers and engineers who ship interfaces for a living. No sales pitches, no filler: just practical talks that you can use on Monday morning.

Expect deep dives into modern CSS, rendering performance, accessible design systems, web platform APIs and the human side of building for the browser. Between sessions, enjoy pastéis de nata, long lunches and sunset drinks above the Tagus.
