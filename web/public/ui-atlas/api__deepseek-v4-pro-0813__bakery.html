Our Story

From a tiny kitchen to your neighbourhood corner

Odd Loaf started in 2019 with one second-hand oven, a very hungry cat, and a stubborn belief that bread should taste like it did a hundred years ago — slow, wild, and full of character.

We still mix every batch by hand. Our starter is fed daily, never rushed. We shape each loaf individually, proof it in willow baskets, and bake it on stone. No shortcuts, no additives, no batch over 40 loaves.

🍞

Why "Odd Loaf"? Because no two loaves are ever the same. And neither are the people who love them. Come as you are — leave with something warm.
