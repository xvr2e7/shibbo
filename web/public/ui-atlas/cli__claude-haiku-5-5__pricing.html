Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your admin settings. Upgrades take effect immediately, and we prorate the difference on your next invoice.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

Every Pro workspace starts with a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to begin.

What counts as a team member?

Any user who is active in your workspace during the billing period. Guests invited to shared channels are free on all plans.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes. Registered nonprofits and accredited educational institutions receive 50% off Pro. Contact our sales team to apply.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export all workspace data at any time. After cancellation, we keep your data for 30 days before permanent deletion, so you can reactivate without losing anything.

Do you offer refunds?

Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are refundable for the current period if you cancel early.
