Our story

Three generations, one kitchen.

In 1978, Mariam Haddad left the village of Koura with two suitcases — one for clothes, one for her mother's recipe book. She opened twelve seats on Atlantic Avenue and started cooking for whoever walked in.

Forty-six years later her son Nabil still lights the charcoal at seven in the morning, her daughter Rima still rolls the kibbeh by hand, and the recipe book — now held together with tape and olive oil — is still on the shelf above the pass.
