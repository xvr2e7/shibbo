Questions you might ask each other

Do we need a joint bank account to use DuoSpend?

Not at all! Over 60% of DuoSpend couples keep separate bank accounts. You simply connect your individual cards, mark which expenses are shared, and DuoSpend tracks who owes what—or helps you settle up at the end of the month.

Can my partner see every single thing I buy?

Only what you choose to share. Accounts can be set to "Shared by Default" (great for joint credit cards) or "Private by Default" (great for personal checking accounts where you only swipe specific transactions into the shared pool).

How is this different from Splitwise or Venmo?

Splitwise requires manual entry for every coffee and grocery run. DuoSpend syncs automatically with 12,000+ banks, categorizes your spending into a real forward-looking budget, and helps you save for long-term couple goals together.

Is our financial data safe?

Yes. We use Plaid for read-only bank connections with 256-bit encryption. DuoSpend never stores your bank login credentials, cannot move money without your explicit authorization, and never sells your data or shows you ads.
