Questions, answered

Still curious? Chat with us. Fittingly, we reply fast.

How does per‑user billing work? +

You're billed for each active member in your workspace. Add people anytime and we'll prorate the charge. If a member is inactive for 14+ days, we automatically credit their seat back to you.

Are guests and bots counted as members? +

No. Single‑channel guests are free on all paid plans, and you get 5 free multi‑channel guests per paid member. Bots and integrations never count toward your seat total.

Can I switch plans or cancel later? +

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your admin settings in a couple of clicks. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period, and annual plans can be refunded pro‑rata within 30 days.

What happens to my history on the Free plan? +

Messages older than 90 days are hidden, never deleted. Upgrade at any time and your entire history becomes searchable again instantly.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education? +

Absolutely. Qualifying nonprofits get Team free for up to 250 members, and accredited schools receive 85% off Business. Apply from your workspace billing page.
