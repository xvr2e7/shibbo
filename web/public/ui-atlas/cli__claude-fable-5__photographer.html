About Elena

Photography that feels like the day itself

I believe the best wedding photographs are the ones no one posed for — your grandmother's hand on yours, the laugh between vows, the hush right before the doors open. For the past nine years I've been documenting weddings with a quiet, editorial eye: present when it matters, invisible when it counts.

I shoot a blend of digital and medium-format film, delivering galleries that feel timeless rather than trendy. My couples tend to care about light, honesty, and each other — in roughly that order reversed.
