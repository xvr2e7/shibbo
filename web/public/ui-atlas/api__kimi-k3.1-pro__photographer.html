03 · Hello, I’m Mara

The calm presence
behind the camera.

Part quiet observer, part hopeless romantic, always looking for the honest moment.

I believe the best photographs happen when you feel completely at ease. So I’ll offer gentle direction when you need it, disappear when you don’t, and never ask you to perform your own love story. The result is a collection that feels artful, effortless, and unmistakably yours.

04 · The experience

You live it.
I’ll keep it.

From our first conversation to your final gallery, the experience is personal, considered, and refreshingly uncomplicated.

01 / BEFORE

We get to know you.

We’ll talk about what matters, what doesn’t, and how you want the day to feel. I’ll help shape a photography timeline that leaves room to actually enjoy it.

02 / ON THE DAY

You stay present.

No stiff posing or hours away from your favorite people. Just intuitive guidance, a steady presence, and space for all the beautiful unpredictability.

03 / AFTER

You feel it again.

A preview arrives within 48 hours. Your complete, carefully edited story follows in an online gallery, with heirlooms made to live beyond a screen.
