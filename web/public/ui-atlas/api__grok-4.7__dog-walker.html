Questions we hear on the first walk.

Do you walk reactive or senior dogs?

Yes. Reactive dogs get solo walks and a slower introduction. Seniors get shorter loops, more sniff time, and extra water stops.

What if it rains?

We still go, with jackets and towels, unless there’s lightning or unsafe ice. You’ll get a heads-up if we need to switch to an indoor play visit.

How do keys and access work?

Lockbox, garage code, or a labeled key we store in a locked case. We never share access details, and we text when we arrive and leave.

Which neighborhoods do you cover?

We currently walk within about 15 minutes of downtown. If you’re on the edge, send your cross streets and we’ll tell you honestly.
