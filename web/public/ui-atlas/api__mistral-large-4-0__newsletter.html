Mandatory parking minimums add tens of thousands of dollars to every new home. We ran the numbers on 100 recent projects — and looked at what happened in the three cities that simply deleted the rule.

The math on priority lanes is embarrassingly simple, so why are they so rare? A short history of the politics of paint, and the street that finally got it right.

Maps don’t change cities — meetings do. A field guide to the public hearings where the lines on the map actually move, and who shows up to move them.
