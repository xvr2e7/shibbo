Brew guide

A reliable recipe in about three minutes.

Slide to your batch size and we'll do the math at a balanced 1:16 ratio.

20g coffee
40ml bloom
1.3cups
Medium-fine grind, like table salt. Water at 94–96 °C.

  1. Rinse & preheat 0:00

    Fold the filter into the dripper, rinse with hot water to remove paper taste, and warm your mug. Discard the water.

  2. Add coffee & bloom 0:00 – 0:40

    Add ground coffee, level the bed, and pour twice its weight in water. Let it bloom and release gas.

  3. Pour in circles 0:40 – 2:15

    Pour slowly in widening spirals, avoiding the filter edges, until you reach your target weight.

  4. Drawdown & serve 2:45 – 3:15

    Let the bed drain completely. Give the cup a swirl and enjoy while it's hot.

Questions

Good to know.

Which filters does it use?

Any standard cone-shaped paper filter. Use #01 for the Single and #02 for the Double. Metal and cloth cone filters work too.

Will it fit my mug?

The flange sits securely on mugs and servers with an opening of 8 cm or wider, which covers most standard mugs and carafes.

Are the glaze colors exactly as pictured?

Close, but not identical. Glazes shift slightly with every kiln firing, so expect a little natural variation in tone and sheen.
