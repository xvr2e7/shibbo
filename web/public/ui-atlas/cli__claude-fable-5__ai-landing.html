Your second memory, minus the upkeep

Most note apps make you do the organizing. Murmur does it the moment you stop typing.

Capture at the speed of thought

Type, dictate, or forward an email. Murmur transcribes voice notes instantly and cleans up your half-finished sentences without losing your voice.

Organized while you sleep

Notes are automatically grouped, titled, and cross-linked by meaning — not keywords. Related ideas find each other, even weeks apart.

Ask instead of search

“What did I decide about pricing in March?” Murmur answers in plain language and cites the exact notes, so you can trust what it tells you.

Daily digest

Every morning, a two-minute summary of yesterday's notes: decisions made, loose threads, and the action items you promised yourself.

Private by design

Your notes are encrypted at rest and never used to train models. Export everything as plain Markdown anytime — your words stay yours.

Everywhere you think

Mac, Windows, iOS, Android, and the web — with offline capture that syncs the moment you're back online.

Three steps, zero ceremony

Murmur works the way scattered thoughts actually arrive.

Murmur it

Hit the hotkey or just start talking. Capture takes two seconds, from anywhere, without breaking your flow.

Let it settle

Murmur titles the note, files it with its relatives, and links it to everything it echoes — past meetings, old ideas, saved reading.

Ask it back

When you need it, ask in plain language. Murmur retrieves the thought, the context around it, and what you said you'd do about it.
