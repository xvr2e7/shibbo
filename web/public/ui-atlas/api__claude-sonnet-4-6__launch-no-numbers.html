freelancers actually work

No accountant degree required. Tally Hall handles the busywork so you can focus on your craft.

📄

Beautiful Invoicing

Create polished, branded invoices in seconds. Send via email or a shareable link, and get notified the moment a client views or pays.

💸

Expense Tracking

Snap a photo of a receipt or forward a confirmation email. Tally Hall categorizes it automatically and keeps it ready for tax time.

⏱️

Time Tracking

Log hours directly in the app and turn them into line items on an invoice with one click. No more awkward back-of-the-envelope calculations.

Tax Estimates

See a running quarterly tax estimate based on your real income and deductions — so you're never blindsided come April.

Bank Sync
