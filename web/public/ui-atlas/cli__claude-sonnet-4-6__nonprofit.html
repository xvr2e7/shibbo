Our Mission

Urban heat is a
solvable problem

Densely built neighborhoods can be 10°F hotter than leafy suburbs. That heat falls hardest on communities with the fewest resources. UrbanCanopy closes that gap — one tree at a time.


We partner with city governments, community groups, and local volunteers to identify where trees are needed most, plant species adapted to urban conditions, and care for them until they thrive.

48,214 trees growing right now
How It Works

From street corner
to canopy

Our four-step process ensures trees survive long after planting day.

01
Map the Need
We analyze heat maps, tree canopy data, and community input to target the blocks where shade matters most.
02
Select the Species
Our urban ecologists choose native trees suited to local soils, climate, and the specific challenges of city streets.
03
Plant with the Community
Residents join volunteer crews to dig, plant, and water on planting days — building ownership from day one.
04
Care & Monitor
We track every tree for three years with regular watering, mulching, and health checks until it stands on its own.
Why Trees

The science of shade

A single mature street tree delivers measurable benefits to everyone around it.

🌡️
Cooler Streets
Urban tree canopy reduces surface temperatures by up to 10°F, cutting heat-related illness in densely built neighborhoods.
💨
Cleaner Air
Trees absorb CO₂, ozone, and fine particulate matter — improving respiratory health near busy roads and industrial zones.
🌧️
Flood Resilience
Root systems and leaf litter slow stormwater runoff, reducing street flooding and sewer overflow during heavy rain.
🐦
Urban Wildlife
Native trees create corridors for birds, pollinators, and insects — bringing biodiversity back into the city landscape.
