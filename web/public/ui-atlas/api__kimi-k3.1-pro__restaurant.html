Our story

It all began with Teta Noura.

In 1987, Noura and Elias Haddad arrived in London with two suitcases, a cedarwood box of spices and a recipes notebook softened by years of use. They missed the noisy lunches of Beirut, so they started making their own.

What began around a six-seat table grew into Baytna. Today, their children and grandchildren run the kitchen together. The room is bigger, but Teta’s rules remain: taste everything, waste nothing and never let a guest leave hungry.

“The secret is simple. Cook like someone you love is coming home.”
— The Haddad family
