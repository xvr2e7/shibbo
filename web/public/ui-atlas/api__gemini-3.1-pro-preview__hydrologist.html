Dr. Elias Thorne

Hydrologist & Essayist

I am a research hydrologist based in the Pacific Northwest, studying the complex dynamics of watershed ecosystems and fluvial geomorphology. My scientific work focuses on how moving water shapes the land over deep time.

When I am not in waders measuring streamflow or analyzing topological models, I write essays about the cultural, ecological, and poetic currents of the world's waterways. To me, a river is never just a body of water; it is a memory of the landscape, a border, a lifeline, and an unceasing argument with gravity.
