Questions

FAQ

Do tickets include workshops?+

Only the "Full Experience" ticket includes workshop access. Blue Ticket and Remote Pass holders can watch main stage talks but not join hands-on sessions.

Will talks be recorded?+

Yes — all main stage talks are recorded and released to attendees within two weeks. Workshops are not recorded.

Is there a code of conduct?+

Absolutely. We enforce a zero-tolerance harassment policy. The full code of conduct is linked in your ticket confirmation email.

Can I transfer my ticket to a colleague?+

Yes, free of charge up to 7 days before the event. Just email hello@edgefront.conf with the new attendee's details.

Do you offer visa invitation letters?+

Yes, once your ticket is purchased, request a letter from our team and we'll send one within 2 business days.

Seats are going fast.

Last year sold out six weeks early. Lock in your spot for two days by the river, and skip the waitlist.
