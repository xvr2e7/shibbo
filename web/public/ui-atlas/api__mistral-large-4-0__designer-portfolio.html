02 — AboutBerlin, DE

Mara VossStudio — 2025

I turn ambiguous problems into clear, shippable products — and I've been doing it for eight years.

Currently Senior Product Designer at Klarheit, a Berlin fintech, where I lead design for the freelancer banking product used by 200k+ businesses. Before that: Northwind Studio, shipping work for clients in health, retail and SaaS.

My favourite ground is the messy middle — research synthesis, flows, edge cases and the unglamorous screens everyone else skips. I prototype early, test with real users, and stay in the build until it ships.

Off the clock: film photography, bouldering, and a slowly growing shelf of Braun products.

What I do

Discovery, positioning and roadmaps. I help teams decide what to build — and what to leave out — before a single pixel is drawn.

From flows and wireframes to polished, dev-ready interfaces. Every screen earns its place, and every state is designed — including empty and error.

Token-first component libraries with documentation and governance, so six product teams can ship consistently without a bottleneck.

High-fidelity prototypes that feel real, validated with moderated tests before engineering writes a line of code.
