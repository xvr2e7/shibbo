Good Questions

Frequently asked (by very good dogs)

What areas do you serve?
We cover inner Portland — including Laurelhurst, Irvington, Sellwood, Hawthorne, Alberta Arts and surrounding neighborhoods — plus trail pickups within a 15-minute drive. Not sure if you're in range? Send a message and we'll check!
Is my dog walked alone or with other dogs?
Daily Strolls and Big Adventures are always solo — your dog gets 100% of the attention. Trail Day Hikes are small groups of up to three dogs, carefully matched by size, energy and play style (and only with your OK).
What if it's raining?
This is Portland — we walk rain or shine! We keep walks shorter in extreme weather, stick to shaded or sheltered routes as needed, and always towel-dry your pup before heading inside. If weather is genuinely unsafe, we'll swap for an indoor play-and-enrichment visit at no extra cost.
How do you get into my home?
Most clients provide a lockbox code or smart-lock access, which is stored securely and never labeled with your address. Keys are coded, never copied, and returned any time you ask.
Can you handle reactive or anxious dogs?
Absolutely — some of our favorite clients are the "tricky" ones. We're Fear-Free trained, we plan quiet routes and off-peak times, and we go at your dog's pace. The free Meet & Greet lets us build a plan together before the first walk.
What's your cancellation policy?
Life happens! Cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours ahead at no charge. Same-day cancellations are 50% of the walk fee. If we ever need to cancel, your walk is free and we'll make it up to your pup with extra treat time.
