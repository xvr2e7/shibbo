Our Story

Started with one starter, named Doreen.

Odd Loaf began in a one-bedroom kitchen in 2019, when our baker Mara started feeding a sourdough starter she named Doreen out of boredom and curiosity. Friends asked for loaves. Then friends of friends. Then there was a line down the block.

We moved into a proper (tiny) storefront in 2021, but kept the same habits: small batches, long ferments, and baking only what we can make well — nothing more. Doreen is still with us, a little older and a lot more sour.
