02 — How I work

A method built for
shipping, not slides.

Every engagement moves through the same four movements — but the depth flexes with what the product actually needs. No ceremony for ceremony's sake.

Typical cycle — 6 to 12 weeks
01

Frame

Interviews, support logs and analytics until the real problem is smaller and sharper than the brief.

Week 1–2 · Discovery
02

Explore

Wide divergence on purpose — flows, models, language. Then a ruthless convergence on one direction.

Week 2–5 · Concepts
03

Prototype

High-fidelity enough to feel real, tested with the people who'll use it every day. Motion included.

Week 5–9 · Validation
04

Ship & learn

In the repo with engineers, in the release, and back with users six weeks later to see what moved.

Week 9+ · Delivery

03 — About

I like the part where
it gets difficult.

Most of my work lives in the messy middle — where the domain is regulated, the data is unreliable and the users are experts who don't have time to be taught anything new. That's exactly where design earns its keep.

I work embedded with a product trio, close to engineering, and I write as much as I draw. Before going independent I led design at two venture-backed platforms and spent four years in agency work — enough to know that the best ideas survive contact with production, or they weren't the best ideas.
