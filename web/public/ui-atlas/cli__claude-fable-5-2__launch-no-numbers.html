Four jobs, done as you go.

Most freelance bookkeeping is the same handful of tasks, usually left until the night before a deadline. Tally Hall turns them into a few minutes a week.

Send the invoice

Write an invoice, send it, and see at a glance which ones are paid, due, or late.

Log the expense

Add what you spent and attach the receipt. Each expense gets a category, so nothing needs sorting later.

Set tax aside

Pick a rate once. Every payment that lands is split into what to hold back and what you can use.

Hand it over

At year end, export income and expenses in a tidy file your accountant can work from.

Fair questions

Do I need to know any accounting?

No. Tally Hall asks for things you already know: who paid you, what you spent, and roughly how much tax to hold back. It does the adding up.

Does it replace my accountant?

No. It keeps your records in order through the year, so your accountant gets clean figures instead of a folder of receipts.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Your books are yours, and you can export your income and expenses whenever you like.

How new is it, really?

Very. Tally Hall opened in November 2026. Expect a small, focused product with some rough edges, and expect it to change as early users tell us what they need.
