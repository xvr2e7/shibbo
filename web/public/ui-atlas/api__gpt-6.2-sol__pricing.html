Pricing questions

How trials, plan changes, and member billing work.

What happens after my trial?

After 14 days, you can choose a paid plan or switch to Starter. You won’t be charged automatically.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can change your plan from your workspace settings. If you downgrade, your current plan stays active through the end of your billing period.

How does per-member billing work?

Paid plans are priced per workspace member. Yearly prices are shown as a monthly equivalent, but you pay for the full year up front.
