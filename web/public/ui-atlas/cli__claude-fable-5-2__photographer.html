03 · Approach

I work like a guest who happens to carry two cameras.

Most of the day I am out of the way, watching. I step in for twenty minutes of portraits and a short list of family photographs, then give you back to your party.

I started in newspaper photography, where nobody waits for you to be ready, and it shaped how I work at weddings. I do not restage moments and I will not pull you away for an hour at sunset. The photographs come from paying attention.

Every wedding is shot on a mix of colour film, black and white film and digital. Film handles daylight and skin beautifully. Digital takes over once the candles are lit and the dance floor fills.
