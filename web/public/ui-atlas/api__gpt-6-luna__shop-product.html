Details & care

Hand-finished stoneware with a food-safe, lead-free glaze. Dishwasher safe; for a longer life, wash gently by hand. Paper filters sold separately.

How to brew

Place a size 02 cone filter inside and rinse with hot water. Add 20g of medium-ground coffee, then pour 300g of water slowly in concentric circles. Brew time: about 2½–3 minutes.

Shipping & returns

Orders ship from our studio within 1–2 business days. If it isn't quite right, return it unused in its original packaging within 30 days.
