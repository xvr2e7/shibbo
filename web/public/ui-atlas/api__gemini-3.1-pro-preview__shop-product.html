Crafted for Heat Retention

Unlike plastic or thin glass, our thick-walled stoneware absorbs and holds heat during the brewing process. This prevents the slurry temperature from dropping too rapidly, allowing for higher extraction yields and highlighting the sweeter, more complex notes of your specialty coffee.

Each piece is individually wheel-thrown, meaning slight variations in glaze and form are naturally occurring, making your dripper entirely unique.

The Perfect Pour

1. Rinse & Warm

Place a filter in the dripper and pour hot water through it. This removes paper taste and pre-heats the thick ceramic walls.

2. The Bloom

Add 20g of medium-fine coffee. Pour 40g of water over the grounds and wait 30 seconds to allow trapped gases to escape.

3. Slow Pour

Starting from the center, pour water in slow, concentric circles until you reach 300g total weight. Let drain and enjoy.
