Our Story

From a kitchen in Beirut to your table.

Layla and Karim Haddad arrived in London in 1987 with two suitcases, a copper pot, and a notebook of recipes written in Layla’s mother’s handwriting. They started with six tables. Word spread — not because of advertising, but because neighbours kept bringing neighbours.

1987
Six tables & a dream

Dar Layla opens on Cedar Lane. Layla cooks, Karim serves, their children fold napkins after school.

2004
The second generation

Son Rami introduces the charcoal grill he learned in Tripoli. Daughter Maya bakes the first saj bread in-house.

Today
Still family, still handmade

Granddaughter Noor, 19, now makes the baklava with Layla every morning at 7am. The notebook is still in the kitchen.

Three Generations

The hands behind
your dinner.

No head-office recipes. No frozen shortcuts. Just family — arguing lovingly about exactly how much lemon goes in the tabbouleh.

Founder · Grandmother

Layla Haddad, 78

Still tastes every hummus batch before service. Her rule: “If you wouldn’t serve it to your grandchildren, don’t serve it at all.”
