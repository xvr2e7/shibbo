How is this different from a support ticket summary?
A summary tells you what happened. Relay finds patterns across conversations, compares them over time, adds account and customer context, and recommends where product should pay attention next.
Do we need to change our tagging process?
No. Relay works with unstructured conversations and your existing metadata. You can keep your current tags, but nobody needs to maintain a new taxonomy for Relay to work.
Can I verify what the AI finds?
Yes. Every theme links directly to its supporting conversations and customer quotes. You can inspect the evidence, correct a theme, or merge related themes before sharing your report.
Which tools does Relay connect to?
Relay supports Intercom, Zendesk, and Help Scout as support sources. Reports can be delivered to Slack, email, or Notion, and insights can be sent to Linear or Jira.
How quickly will we get our first report?
Most teams connect their workspace in about two minutes. Relay can generate an initial report from recent history, then deliver a fresh report automatically every Monday.
What happens to our customer data?
Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited to your workspace, and your conversations are never used to train shared models. You can disconnect sources and delete your data at any time.

Weekly product report · July 8–14

New teams are hitting a wall before their first win.

Onboarding friction rose this week, while exports and permissions continue to create avoidable support work. These are the three themes worth your team’s attention.

1,284Conversations analyzed
5Themes identified
3Recommended priorities
01 · Onboarding · High confidence46 conversations · +32%

Inviting teammates is too hard to find

New workspace owners expect to invite their team directly from the empty dashboard. Instead, they discover the setting only after contacting support. The issue is most common among workspaces created in the last seven days.

Recommended next stepAdd a member-invite action to the empty dashboard and test making it available to all workspace roles.
“I shouldn’t need an admin just to add my designer.”
02 · Exports · Growing31 conversations · +18%

Large exports are timing out

Customers exporting more than 10,000 rows are disproportionately affected. Several reported retrying the same export multiple times before contacting support.

Recommended next stepMove large exports to a background job and email a download link when the file is ready.
“The CSV works for a small project, but our full export never finishes.”
03 · Slack alerts · Positive signal21 conversations · +100%

Slack alerts are becoming a daily habit

Positive mentions doubled after last week’s release. Customers specifically called out faster response times and not needing to monitor the dashboard manually.

Recommended next stepHighlight Slack alerts during onboarding and test additional controls for alert frequency.
“The new alert caught an issue before our customers did. That’s exactly what we needed.”
