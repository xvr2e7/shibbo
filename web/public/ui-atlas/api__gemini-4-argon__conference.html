// 03. THE SETTING

LX Factory, Under the 25 de Abril Bridge

Hosted inside a restored 1846 industrial textile complex in Alcântara. Expect brick-vaulted industrial stages, specialty Portuguese roasters pulling unlimited bicas, and a wooden tram shuttle straight from Cais do Sodré.

🚋 Tram 15E & Shuttle

12 minutes from downtown Chiado. Dedicated vintage Carris tram charter for attendees every morning.

🥐 Pastéis & Bica Bar

Warm custard tarts delivered hourly from Alcântara bakeries paired with local specialty espresso.

⛵ Tagus River Boat

Day 1 concludes with a sunset catamaran cruise along Belém and Terreiro do Paço with live synth sets.

⚡ 10Gbps Hack Lounge

Dedicated quiet courtyard with ergonomic seating, pair-programming monitors, and hardware debugging stations.
