Good to know

Questions people ask us

Still curious about something? Just call — we're happy to talk it through.

Do you see new patients?

Absolutely. We welcome new patients of all ages and have appointments available most weeks within 10 business days. Emergency and urgent cases are usually seen the same day — call us and we'll make it work.

What does a new patient visit cost?

Most new patient exams with X-rays are around $95–$150 before insurance, and a cleaning typically runs $85–$130. We'll give you the exact numbers when you book so there are no surprises at the counter.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we'll note it, block extra time, and start with a low-pressure conversation. We offer topical numbing before every injection, nitrous oxide, and a "no-pressure" first visit where nothing is done unless you want it.

Do you treat children?

Absolutely — we welcome kids from around age one so they can grow up comfortable with the dentist. We offer children's cleanings, fluoride varnish, sealants and early orthodontic guidance.

Which insurance do you accept?

We're in-network with Delta Dental, Premera, Regence, Cigna and several PPO plans, and we're happy to work with your out-of-network benefits. Bring your card to your first visit and we'll verify everything for you.

How long do appointments take?

A new patient visit is typically 45–60 minutes so we can do a full exam, X-rays and a cleaning without rushing. Routine cleanings and check-ups take about 40 minutes. Emergency visits are typically 30–45 minutes.
