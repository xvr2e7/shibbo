Features

Built for the people between support and product.

Relay is opinionated about one thing: making customer feedback easy to act on.

Root-cause clustering

Groups tickets that describe the same underlying problem, even when customers use completely different words.

Trend detection

Flags spikes within hours of a release or incident, so you know whether something just broke or has been quietly building.

Revenue-weighted priority

Pulls ARR and plan data from your CRM so each issue shows how much money is attached, not just how many people mentioned it.

Close the loop automatically

When a linked Jira or Linear issue ships, Relay drafts a note to every customer who reported it, for your team to approve.

Verifiable claims

Every number in a report links to the underlying tickets. No black boxes, and no PM has to take the summary on faith.

PII redaction by default

Emails, card numbers, and addresses are stripped before analysis. Relay is SOC 2 Type II and never trains models on your data.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to change how my support team works?

No. Relay reads tickets where they already live. Your agents keep using the same helpdesk, macros, and workflows.

How accurate is the grouping?

Relay typically places more than 90% of tickets in the correct cluster in our internal evaluations. Every cluster links to its source tickets, so you can correct mistakes in seconds and Relay learns from the change.

What happens to customer personal data?

Names, emails, phone numbers, payment details, and addresses are redacted before any analysis. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never use your tickets to train shared models.

Can I send the report somewhere other than email?

Yes. Team plans deliver to Slack channels, Notion, or any webhook. You can set different schedules for different audiences, like a daily digest for support leads and a weekly brief for product.

How long does the first report take?

Connecting takes a few minutes. Relay backfills up to 90 days of history, so your first report is usually ready within the hour.
