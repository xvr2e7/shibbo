What to expect on your first visit

New patients

Your first visit.

What happens at my first appointment?

We’ll talk through your dental history and any concerns, examine your teeth and gums, and take X-rays if needed. Before you leave, we’ll explain what we found and discuss next steps together.

What should I bring?

Bring a photo ID, your dental insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications you take. If you have recent dental X-rays, ask your previous office to send them ahead.

Can I check my insurance before booking?

Yes. Call us with your plan details and we can help check your benefits and explain any estimated out-of-pocket costs.
