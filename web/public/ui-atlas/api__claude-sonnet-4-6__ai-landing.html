Features

Everything your notes need to work for you

From raw voice to polished notes in seconds. Murmur does the heavy lifting so you can stay in the moment.

🎙️
Real-time Transcription
Speak naturally and watch your words appear instantly. Murmur handles accents, technical terms, and fast speech with 99% accuracy.
✦
AI Summaries
Get a crisp TL;DR of any recording in one click. Perfect for long meetings, lectures, or brain dumps you don't have time to re-read.
📋
Action Item Extraction
Murmur automatically pulls out tasks, deadlines, and decisions from your notes and turns them into a clean to-do list.
🔍
Semantic Search
Find anything across all your notes instantly. Search by meaning, not just keywords — "that idea about the logo from last month" just works.
🔗
Integrations
Push notes to Notion, Linear, Slack, or Google Docs automatically. Murmur slots into the tools you already use.
🌍
40+ Languages
Transcribe and summarize in English, Spanish, French, Mandarin, Japanese, and dozens more. Switch languages mid-sentence, no problem.
How it works

From voice to insight in three steps

No setup required. Open Murmur, hit record, and you're done.

01
Hit Record
Tap the mic button on any device — phone, desktop, or browser tab. Murmur starts transcribing the moment you speak.
02
AI Processes
Our AI cleans up the transcript, structures it into sections, identifies key points, and extracts action items automatically.
03
Review & Share
Edit, tag, and share your polished notes. Send a summary to Slack or export to any tool you use in one click.
