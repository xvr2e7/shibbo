Our Mission

Trees aren't a luxury — they're infrastructure.

In many cities, tree cover tracks almost exactly with income: wealthier neighborhoods are blanketed in shade while lower-income blocks bake under asphalt, 10–15°F hotter on summer days. We exist to close that gap.

We work block by block with residents, schools, and local governments to plant the right species in the right place, then return for years of pruning, watering, and care so every tree actually survives to maturity.

Our Programs

Where the work actually happens

From planting days to long-term stewardship, these are the programs that keep the canopy growing.

Community Planting

Neighborhood Planting Days

We bring saplings, tools, and expert arborists to residential streets, letting neighbors plant trees on their own blocks.

Youth Education

Schoolyard Forests

Students design, plant, and track mini-forests on school grounds while learning ecology, climate science, and stewardship.

Long-Term Care

3-Year Tree Stewardship

Every tree we plant gets scheduled watering, mulching, and pruning visits for three years to ensure it reaches maturity.

How It Works

From sapling to canopy

A simple process designed to keep trees alive long after the planting day ends.

1

Site Survey

Our arborists assess soil, sunlight, and species fit for each block before anything is planted.

2

Community Sign-Up

Residents request trees for their street and commit to light upkeep like watering in dry weeks.

3

Planting Day

Volunteers and staff plant trees together, with training on proper technique and mulching.

4

3 Years of Care

We return on a schedule to prune, inspect, and replace any trees that don't take root.
