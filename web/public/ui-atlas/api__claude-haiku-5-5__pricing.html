Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you need? Chat with our team.

Can I try a paid plan before I commit?

Yes. Pro and Business include a 14-day free trial with full access to every feature in that tier. No credit card is required to start, and you'll be reminded before the trial ends.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are billed once per year at a 20% discount compared to monthly billing. You can switch between monthly and annual at any time, and unused time is credited toward your next invoice.

What counts as a team member?

A team member is any person with an active account in your workspace. Guests invited to specific channels count as members on Pro and Business. Deactivated accounts don't count toward your seat total.

What happens to my messages if I downgrade to Free?

Nothing is deleted. Messages older than 90 days become hidden but are restored immediately if you upgrade again. You can also export your full history at any time.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or schools?

Yes. Registered nonprofits, accredited schools, and public institutions are eligible for up to 50% off Business. Reach out through the contact form and we'll confirm eligibility within two business days.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2+ and at rest with AES-256. We're SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Enterprise customers can choose data residency and manage their own encryption keys.
