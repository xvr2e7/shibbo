What it is

A conference for people
who ship the front of the web.

No parallel tracks to agonise over, no vendor keynotes, no 45-minute product demos dressed up as a talk. Everyone sees the same programme and argues about it over lunch.

Azulejo started in 2023 as 80 people in a co-working basement in Alcântara. It has grown, but the rules haven't changed: talks are selected from an anonymised CFP, every speaker is paired with a coach for three rehearsals, and half of the programme is reserved for people who have never spoken at a conference of this size.

  • One track. 24 talks, 20–35 minutes each, plus six lightning slots.
  • Real constraints. Case studies with numbers, budgets and the parts that went badly.
  • Hallway first. Long breaks by design — 30 minutes minimum, outdoors when Lisbon allows.
  • Open afterwards. Every talk is recorded, captioned and published free within three weeks.

Questions

Good to know.

Anything missing? Write to hello@azulejoconf.dev — a human answers within two working days.

What language are the talks in?

English, all of them. Live captions in English are projected beside the stage both days, and the published videos ship with English and Portuguese subtitles.

Are the talks recorded?

Yes. Every talk is recorded and published free on the web within three weeks, captioned and with transcripts. Ticket holders get a private link the Monday after the conference.

Is there a code of conduct?

There is, it is enforced, and it has three named contacts who are on site both days wearing bright yellow lanyards. Reports can also be made anonymously through a form linked in the attendee email.

What about food and dietary needs?

Lunch and all breaks are included. Every stall has vegan and gluten-free options as standard; halal and kosher meals, plus anything else you need, are arranged if you tell us in the ticket form by 20 April.

Can you help with a visa invitation letter?

Yes. Buy a ticket, then fill in the visa support form and we'll issue a signed invitation letter within three working days. If a visa is refused, we refund the ticket in full, no questions.

How accessible is the venue?

Step-free throughout — entrance, stage, all toilets and reserved step-free seating with companion seats. Hearing loop in the main hall, ASL/LGP interpretation on request by 1 April, a quiet room open both days, and service animals welcome.

Is there a remote or streaming ticket?

No stream. We'd rather spend the budget on captioning and scholarships, and the free videos land three weeks later anyway.

When does the CFP close?

Round two closes 15 January 2027. Submissions are reviewed anonymously; speaker names are only revealed after the programme is set. Selected speakers get travel, three nights' accommodation and three rehearsal sessions with a coach.
