How I work

Four habits

01

Research first

I start with conversations and messy spreadsheets, not Figma. Ten user calls will teach you more than a month of guessing.

02

Systems thinking

Every screen is a decision that repeats. I look for the pattern underneath so the tenth feature costs less than the first.

03

Craft in the details

Timing, empty states, error copy, the moment after a tap. That's where a product stops feeling like software and starts feeling inevitable.

04

Ship and learn

I'd rather have a real thing in front of real people on Friday than a perfect prototype in a review deck for another quarter.
