Our Story

Cedar House is a family-run Lebanese restaurant founded in 1998 by siblings Nadia and Samir Haddad. Our recipes come straight from our grandmother’s kitchen in the Bekaa Valley—slow-cooked stews, hand-rolled grape leaves, and bread baked fresh every morning.

We believe food is the heart of the family, and every plate we serve is made with the same care we give our own loved ones. Welcome to our table.
