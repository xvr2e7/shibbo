Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about your first visit.

Your comprehensive 60-minute visit includes ultra-low radiation HD digital X-rays, 3D intraoral digital photography, oral cancer screening, periodontal gum measurement, and an unhurried, one-on-one consultation with your doctor. If your gums are healthy, we also complete your gentle ultrasonic cleaning in the same visit.
We offer several levels of relaxation: light Nitrous Oxide ("laughing gas") which wears off immediately so you can drive home, prescription oral conscious sedation for deeper calm, and our sensory comfort menu (weighted blankets, noise-canceling headphones, and ceiling monitors). We also use computer-controlled local anesthesia which eliminates the painful pinch of traditional needles.
Yes, we work with nearly all PPO dental plans (including Delta Dental, MetLife, Cigna, Guardian, Aetna, Premera, and United Healthcare). Our insurance coordinators verify your exact benefits before your appointment to ensure you understand co-pays down to the dollar.
We reserve dedicated emergency slots in our clinical schedule every single business day. If you call us before 2:00 PM with acute tooth pain, trauma, or a broken tooth, we guarantee a same-day evaluation to relieve your discomfort.
In the hands of an experienced provider like Dr. Vance, modern Invisalign algorithms can resolve over 90% of orthodontic issues including crossbites, overbites, deep crowding, and gaps—often 30% faster than traditional bracket braces, with no dietary restrictions or broken wire emergencies.
