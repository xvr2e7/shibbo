Dr. Mara Voss
Hydrologist · Writer · Field Scientist

I am a research hydrologist at the Pacific Northwest Water Institute, where I study how rivers respond to land use change, dam removal, and shifting precipitation patterns.

Alongside my scientific work, I write essays for a general audience about what rivers can teach us — about deep time, about policy failure, and about the peculiar beauty of water moving downhill.

My writing has appeared in Orion, High Country News, and The American Scientist. I hold a PhD in fluvial geomorphology from the University of Washington and an MSc from Uppsala University in Sweden.
