Why ceramic

Designed around heat, flow, and the small ritual of the morning.

Thermal mass

6mm walls stay steady at brewing temperature, so the last pour extracts like the first. No cold spots, no sour edges.

Spiral ribs

Raised ribs lift the filter off the wall, giving air a path out and water an even path down through the bed.

One wide bore

A single 14mm opening means flow is set by your pour, not the dripper. Fast and bright or slow and syrupy — your call.

From our studio

Thrown by two hands, fired twice, tested with a hundred cups.

Every Kōen starts as 900 grams of local stoneware on Mara's wheel. We trim the ribs by hand while the clay is leather-hard, bisque fire, glaze, then fire again to 2,232°F.

Before a design leaves the studio, we brew with it every morning for a month. The Kōen took eleven prototypes to get the bore and rib height just right.

Brew guide

Our everyday recipe

A forgiving starting point. Adjust grind finer if it tastes thin, coarser if it tastes bitter.

Coffee20 g
Water320 g
Ratio1 : 16
Temp94°C / 201°F
GrindMedium-fine
0:00

Rinse & heat

Seat a filter, pour hot water through to rinse paper and warm the ceramic. Discard.

0:00 – 0:45

Bloom

Add grounds, pour 50 g in a gentle spiral. Let it puff and release CO₂.

0:45 – 1:45

Main pour

Pour steadily in slow circles to 200 g, then pause. Continue to 320 g.

~3:00

Drawdown

Give a light swirl. The bed should finish flat and drain around the 3-minute mark.

0:00
