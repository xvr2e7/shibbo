Our Story

A kitchen that never learned to cook for just two.

When Mariam Haddad arrived from Deir el Qamar with three children and a suitcase that was mostly spices, she did what Lebanese grandmothers do: she fed everyone. Neighbours, coworkers, the mail carrier. Eventually someone said, you should open a restaurant — and she finally listened.

Today her son Elias runs the charcoal grill, his wife Rana bakes the bread each morning, and their daughter Layla keeps the dining room humming. Sitto still comes in every Thursday to make the shish barak by hand, and still tells us we use too little garlic.

Everything is made here, from the labneh we strain overnight to the pickled turnips glowing pink on every table.

— The Haddad family
