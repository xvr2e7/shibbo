Can I try Huddle before paying?

Absolutely. The Free plan is yours for as long as you like. You can also try Pro free before choosing a paid plan.

Can I change my plan later?

Of course. Upgrade or downgrade whenever your team needs change. Your conversations and files stay right where they are.

How does billing work?

Paid plans are priced per user. Choose monthly billing for flexibility or yearly billing to save 20%. Yearly plans are billed upfront.

What happens when my trial ends?

We’ll let you know before it ends. You can choose a paid plan or continue using Huddle on the Free plan.

Is my team’s data secure?

Yes. We use encryption in transit and at rest, and Business plans include additional administrative and security controls.
