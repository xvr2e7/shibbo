About the Photographer

I stand back so you can
simply be together.

I am a documentary-style wedding photographer based between the coast and the city. My approach is quiet and observant: I spend the day listening, moving softly, and waiting for the glances, laughter and small gestures that tell the real story.

Every couple receives a considered, cinematic edit, delivered with care and a sense of how the day felt, not just how it looked.
