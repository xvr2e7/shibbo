01 — About

Small room.
Big opinions.

MARÉ is a deliberately small, deliberately intense frontend conference.

Two stages, one main hall and a courtyard full of people who write code for a living. No sales pitches, no sponsor keynotes, no panels about “the future of work”. Just honest talks about the details: how a layout holds together, how an animation feels, how a system survives contact with a product team.

Lisbon is our second home and a perfect host — a city of hills, tiled façades, late light and excellent coffee. Come for the conference; stay for the walk down to the Tagus at sunset.

Format

40-minute talks, hard stops, live Q&A in the courtyard.

Audience

Front-end engineers, designers who code, design-system maintainers.

Language

English, with a few sessions in Portuguese. Live captions in English.

Code of conduct

Strictly enforced. Read it before you come.

04 — Hands-on labs

Friday afternoon, bring a laptop.

Three 90-minute workshops, capped at 40 seats each. Add a lab to your ticket for €60, or wait until Friday morning for any remaining seats.

LAB 01

Animation without the jank

Drive interfaces with the Web Animations API, FLIP techniques and compositor-only properties. You will leave with a 60fps transition you actually understand.

Sofia Almeida90 min · €60
LAB 02

Ship a design system in 120 minutes

07 — Questions

Before you ask.

Still unsure? Email hello@mareconf.pt and a human (usually Rui) will reply within a day.

Get tickets ↗
Will the talks be recorded?

All main-stage talks are recorded in 1080p with professional audio and published two weeks after the conference, free for ticket holders. Workshops and lightning talks are not recorded.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes. The main hall, courtyard and roof terrace are step-free, with accessible restrooms on both levels and reserved seating at the front. Live English captions run on the side screens for every session. If you need anything specific, email us and we will arrange it.

Which language are the talks in?

English, with a handful of sessions in Portuguese. The full programme marks the language of each talk. Slides are usually in English.

Can I transfer my ticket or get a refund?

Tickets can be transferred free of charge until 10 September 2026. You get a full refund up to 17 August, and 50% back until 10 September. After that we will happily move you to the next edition.

What about food and coffee?

Lunch is included on both days, with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options, plus a coffee bar that runs from doors open to the last session. Dinner is not included, but we will reserve tables nearby for anyone who wants one.

Will there be jobs or sponsorship booths?

A small hallway track of eight partner stands, open to all attendees and without a pay-to-play keynote slot. We keep it that way.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes — and it is enforced. Harassment of any kind results in removal without refund. Read it here, and talk to any crew member wearing a red lanyard if you need help.

Get the low tide letter

Speaker announcements, the final programme, video clips from last year and a discount code for early birds. One email a month. No noise.
