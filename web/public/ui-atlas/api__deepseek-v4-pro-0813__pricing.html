Frequently asked questions

How does the 14-day free trial work?

When you sign up for the Pro plan, you get 14 days of full access with all features enabled. No credit card is required upfront. At the end of the trial, you can choose to subscribe or your account will automatically downgrade to the free Starter plan.

Can I change plans or cancel at any time?

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any point from your billing settings. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. If you cancel, you'll retain access until the end of your current paid period.

What counts as a "user" in pricing?

A user is any team member with an active account on your workspace. Guests and viewers with limited access are not counted as billable users on the Pro plan (up to 50 guests). On Enterprise, guests are unlimited and also not billed.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?

Yes! We offer a 50% discount on the Pro plan for registered nonprofits, K-12 schools, and accredited universities. Contact our sales team with your credentials to apply. Enterprise pricing is customized based on your organization's needs.

Is my data secure? Where is it stored?

We take security seriously. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols. By default, data is stored in the United States. Enterprise customers can choose their data region (US, EU, APAC) to meet compliance requirements.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and ACH bank transfers for Pro plans. Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice with net-30 or net-60 terms. All payments are processed securely via Stripe.
