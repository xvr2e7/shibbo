Neighborhood-led tree planting

Planting shade where the city needs it most.

Urban Canopy plants, waters, and cares for trees in neighborhoods with the fewest parks and the hottest summers. Every tree is planted with the people who will live beside it.

Trees are infrastructure.

We treat urban canopy the way cities treat roads and water: as essential, unequally distributed, and worth investing in properly.

🌡️

Cooler streets

Tree-lined blocks can be several degrees cooler than bare pavement, easing heat stress for children, elders, and outdoor workers.

🫁

Cleaner air

Trees filter particulates and absorb carbon, improving air quality where traffic and industry are concentrated.

🤝

Stronger neighbors

We plant in partnership with residents, so each tree comes with a local caretaker and a reason for people to gather.

What we do

From street trees to school orchards, our programs match the right tree to the right place, and the right people to care for it.

Street Trees

Sidewalk Planting

Native shade trees planted along residential streets, with root barriers and tree guards designed for dense city soil.
