From Beirut to Your Table

In 1987, our grandmother Layla arrived with little more than her mother's handwritten recipes and a dream. She opened a small kitchen on Oak Street, serving the dishes of her homeland to anyone who walked through the door.

Today, her grandchildren continue her legacy. Every morning, we prepare our hummus from scratch, our bread bakes fresh throughout the day, and our spice blends follow recipes passed down through four generations.

We believe food is more than sustenance—it's how we share our culture, our history, and our love. When you dine with us, you become part of our family.

— The Khoury Family
