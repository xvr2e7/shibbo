About Odd Loaf

Started by baker Morgan Chen in 2019, Odd Loaf brings back the art of traditional sourdough baking to our neighbourhood. Using only the finest organic grains, a 25-year-old sourdough starter, and time-honored fermentation techniques, each loaf is a labour of love. We keep our operation small and neighbourhood-focused, ensuring every loaf is baked to perfection and served fresh.
