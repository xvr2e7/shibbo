Dr. Sarah Chen

Hydrologist & River Writer

About

Dr. Sarah Chen is a hydrologist with over 15 years of experience studying river systems across North America and Asia. Her research focuses on fluvial geomorphology, watershed dynamics, and the ecological impacts of water management. Beyond academic publications, Sarah writes essays that bridge scientific understanding and humanistic inquiry, exploring rivers not just as hydrological systems but as vital ecosystems and cultural landscapes.

When not in the field or at her desk, Sarah can be found paddling rivers, reading history, and photographing water in all its forms. She holds a Ph.D. in Hydrology from UC Berkeley and is currently an Associate Professor at the University of Washington.
