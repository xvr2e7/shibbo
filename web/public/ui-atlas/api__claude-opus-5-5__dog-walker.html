FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

Are you insured?

Yes! Happy Tails is fully insured and bonded, and certified in pet first aid and CPR. Documents are available on request.

Do you walk dogs in groups?

We offer both solo walks and small group walks of no more than 3 dogs. Group walks are only arranged with dogs who've been carefully matched for size and temperament, and always with your permission.

How do you handle my house keys?

Keys are labeled with a code (never your address) and stored securely. We're also happy to use lockboxes or smart locks.

What happens if it rains?

We walk rain or shine! In severe weather, we'll swap the walk for indoor play time and a quick potty break to keep everyone safe. Every pup gets a towel-dry before we leave.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel at no charge up to 12 hours before a scheduled walk. Later cancellations are billed at 50%.

How do I pay?

We accept card, Venmo, Zelle, and cash. Regular clients are invoiced weekly.
