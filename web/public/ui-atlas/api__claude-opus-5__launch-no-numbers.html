What it actually does

Small app. Opinionated about the boring parts.

Tally Hall does freelance bookkeeping and nothing else. No payroll, no inventory, no 40-tab dashboard you'll never open.

⇄

Bank feeds that reconcile

Connect your business account and Tally Hall matches payments to invoices on its own. You confirm the handful it isn't sure about.

#

Invoices & polite nagging

Send an invoice in under a minute, then let automatic reminders do the awkward follow-up at 7, 14 and 30 days overdue.

%

Tax set-aside

Tell it your rate and it quietly tracks what to hold back from every payment, so quarter-end isn't a surprise.

▣

Receipts by photo

Snap it, and the amount, date and vendor get read off automatically and attached to the matching transaction.

No data resale, ever

Getting started

Fifteen minutes, then ten minutes a week.

STEP 01

Connect an account

Link your business bank account read-only, or upload a CSV if you'd rather not connect anything yet. Both work fine.

STEP 02

Teach it your categories

Sort your first fifty transactions. After that it guesses correctly most of the time and you're just confirming.

STEP 03

Check in on Fridays

A weekly email tells you what came in, what's overdue, and how much tax to hold back. Clear the queue, close the tab.

Questions you should be asking

Fair concerns about a week-old app

Should I really trust my books to something this new?

Cautiously, and with a backup. The maths is simple and well-tested, your data is encrypted and backed up hourly, and you can export everything at any moment. But we won't pretend eight days of production use is the same as eight years. If your situation is complicated, keep your accountant in the loop for the first few months.

What happens if you shut down?

We're a two-person company funded by subscriptions, not investors, so the realistic failure mode is "not enough customers" rather than "spectacular flameout". If we wind down, our terms commit us to 6 months' notice and a complete export of your data in CSV and PDF. You'd leave with everything you put in.

Which banks do you support?

Around 2,000 banks across the US, UK, Canada and the EU through our open-banking provider. If yours isn't on the list, CSV import works for every bank, and it's the first thing we'll add when someone asks.

Does Tally Hall file my taxes?

No. It tracks income, expenses and what you should set aside, and it produces the reports your accountant or tax software needs. Filing is still on you.

Is there anything obviously missing?

Yes, and we'd rather tell you now: no multi-currency invoicing, no mobile app (the web app works on a phone, but it's not lovely), no mileage tracking, and VAT/GST reporting is still rough. All four are on the public roadmap with rough dates.

Who's behind this?

Two people who spent years freelancing and lost too many Sundays to spreadsheets. We're based in Lisbon, we answer our own support email, and there's no sales team to hand you off to.
