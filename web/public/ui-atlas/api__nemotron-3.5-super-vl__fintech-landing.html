Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Is Duet secure?

Yes. We use bank-level encryption (256-bit AES) and never sell your data. Your financial information is protected with industry-standard security practices.

Can I use it without linking a bank account?

Absolutely. You can add transactions manually if you prefer not to connect your bank, though automatic syncing is more convenient.

What happens if we break up?

Either partner can leave the shared workspace. You’ll both retain access to your personal data, and shared history can be exported before leaving.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! Duet Plus comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.
