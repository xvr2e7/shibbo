Details & dimensions

Made from glazed ceramic with a gently ribbed interior for an even extraction. Designed to brew 1–2 cups. Approximately 4.5 in wide and 3.5 in high.

How to use

Place the dripper over your favorite mug, add a cone filter and freshly ground coffee, then slowly pour hot water in circles. Take your time and enjoy.

Care instructions

Rinse after each use. Dishwasher safe, though we recommend a gentle hand wash to keep it looking lovely for longer.
