About

I like the unglamorous parts of product design.

Empty states. Error copy. The settings screen nobody volunteers for. I’ve found that the quality of a product lives in those edges — and that fixing them is usually the fastest way to earn a team’s trust.

Before design I studied industrial engineering, which is a long way of saying I’m happiest with a whiteboard, a flow diagram, and a stubborn constraint. I now lead product design at a mid‑stage fintech, and take on one or two outside projects a year.

Outside of work: slow coffee, long walks with an old dog named Pepper, and a mild obsession with Japanese train signage.

How I work

A process that survives contact with reality.

Loose enough to adapt, tight enough that you always know what week three looks like.

01

Frame

Interviews, support tickets, a week in the data. We agree on the problem before anyone opens Figma.

02

Sketch

Cheap, fast, plural. Ten directions on paper beats one beautiful dead end.

03

Prototype

Clickable, in front of real users within days. Opinions are tested, not debated.

04

Ship & watch

Pair with engineers through build, instrument it, and keep editing after launch.
