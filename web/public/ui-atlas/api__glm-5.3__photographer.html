A note, in her own hand

read before writing
Maren Falkb. 1987, Aarhus
At work since2016, one wedding, a gift
CamerasContax 645 · Leica M6
Everything elseavailable light

I’ve photographed two hundred and fourteen weddings, and the frames I love most were never on anyone’s list. A father fixing his cufflink for the tenth time. Grandmother’s hands, smoothing the dress. The exact second the room runs out of chairs — and nobody minds.

I work quietly, dress like a guest, and stay past the last dance. No shot lists, no wrangling, no “everyone smile at me now.” I’d rather hand you one photograph that makes you cry than four hundred that make you say “oh — that’s nice.”

— Maren

The working day

no shot lists, ever
09:0401

The quiet morning

Coffee steam, a window’s worth of light, everyone speaking softly. I photograph the dress while it’s still hanging, the letters before they’re opened, the hands that won’t stop shaking. These are the pictures that vanish first if nobody is paying attention.

14:3002

The ceremony

Long lenses, complete silence, zero interruption. I won’t move a single soul or ask for a moment twice. If a stranger’s shoulder crosses your kiss — that’s the honest geometry of a real day, and I’ll already be somewhere better.

23:1003

The last song

I stay to the very end. Flash off the ceiling, grain up, everyone sweaty and unglamorous and entirely themselves. Years later, these are the frames people ask about — never the posed ones. Always the true ones.
