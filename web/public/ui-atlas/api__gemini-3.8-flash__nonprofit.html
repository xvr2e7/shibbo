Our Science & Purpose

Trees are urban emergency responders

Zip codes with fewer trees experience up to 15 degrees higher temperatures during heatwaves. We focus our planting where canopy inequality is starkest.

Crush Heat Islands

Asphalt, dark roofs, and lack of shade turn urban avenues into ovens. Mature canopy shade cools sidewalks by up to 20°F while leaves cool air through evapotranspiration.

Up to 12°F cooler microclimates

Filter Particulate Air

Urban trees capture tons of fine dust, ozone, and vehicle exhaust particulates every year, dramatically reducing childhood asthma rates in dense traffic corridors.

48 lbs CO₂ sequestered / tree / year

Neighborhood Wellbeing

Nature in eyesight lowers cortisol levels, creates beloved gathering spaces, and reduces aggressive vehicle speeds along neighborhood streets.

Proven mental health uplifts

How We Work

Targeted planting initiatives

We don't plant trees in random clearings. We deploy rigorous heat-mapping data to build canopy in historically disinvested blocks.

Sidewalk Corridors

Cool Corridors

Cutting through impenetrable pavement to build fortified soil vaults that allow roots to thrive without lifting sidewalks, creating continuous shaded pedestrian routes.

Learn More
Youth Education

Schoolyard Canopies

Replacing black asphalt schoolyards with micro-forests, outdoor classrooms, and fruit orchards where children learn ecological stewardship while staying safe from UV rays.

Learn More
Community Stewardship

Tree Guardians

A sapling planted is useless without care. We train local residents with watering backpacks, mulch kits, and a small monthly stipend to steward young saplings during crucial year 1-3.
