About the Album

Recorded over eighteen months in a converted warehouse studio, "Echoes in the Static" marks a departure from our previous work. We wanted to capture the feeling of late-night drives through empty cities, the hum of analog equipment, and the space between radio stations.

This album explores the tension between nostalgia and innovation, blending warm vintage synths with crisp modern production. It's our most personal work yet, born from countless hours of experimentation and a shared love for the sounds that raised us.
