Questions, answered.

Does Murmur record without people knowing?

No. Murmur shows a clear recording indicator and can post a notice in meetings. You're responsible for getting consent where the law requires it, and we make that easy.

Is my data used to train AI models?

Never. Your audio and notes are encrypted and belong to you. Delete them any time and they're gone from our servers.

Which languages are supported?

Over 40 languages for transcription, with summaries available in the language of your choice.

Can I use Murmur offline?

Yes. Record offline on mobile and Murmur will transcribe and summarize as soon as you're back online.

Can I cancel anytime?

Of course. Cancel in two clicks from settings and keep access through the end of your billing period.
