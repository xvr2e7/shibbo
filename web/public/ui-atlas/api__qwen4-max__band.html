About the record

Two winters. One boathouse. Forty-one rolls of tape, three of them usable.

“We wanted to record the sound of a room remembering a party. The confetti still on the floor, the amp still humming, nobody left to hear it.”

HOLLOW LIGHT is the second full-length from Velvet Static — Mara Quinn (voice, guitar), Theo Ashworth (bass, tape loops), and June Okafor (drums, modular). Where 2023's Low Ceilings whispered, this one glows: eight slow-burning songs built from fuzz pedals, broken spring reverbs, and a choir recorded through a telephone.

Tracked live to a 1/2" machine with no click, no grid, and one working window. Mixed by the band. Mastered by Heba Kadry. The orange marble pressing is limited to 500 copies, hand-numbered.
