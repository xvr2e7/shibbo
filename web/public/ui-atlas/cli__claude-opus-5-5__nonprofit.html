Why trees

The hottest blocks are the ones with the fewest trees.

On a July afternoon, an unshaded sidewalk can be dozens of degrees hotter than one under a mature oak. That gap shows up in energy bills, asthma rates, and emergency rooms.

Trees are one of the cheapest, longest-lasting tools a city has. We put them where they'll do the most good.

  • Cooler streets and lower summer energy costs
  • Cleaner air and less stormwater runoff
  • Safer, more walkable, more beautiful blocks
How it works

Planting is day one. Caring is the job.

A sapling in the ground isn't a tree yet. We stay with every tree for three years — watering, pruning, and checking in — until it can stand on its own.

Listen

We map heat and canopy data, then knock on doors. Residents choose where trees go — and which ones.

Plant

Volunteers and neighbors plant native, climate-ready species on community planting days each spring and fall.

Water

Paid youth crews water every tree weekly through its first three summers — the make-or-break years.

Track

Every tree gets a tag and a record. We publish survival data openly, every year, good news or not.
