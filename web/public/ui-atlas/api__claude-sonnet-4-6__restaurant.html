Our Story

Cooked with love
since 1998

"In Lebanon, we say 'baytna' — our home. When Mama Rima opened these doors, she wanted every guest to feel exactly that."

What started as a small family kitchen in Beirut became a gathering place for the community. Today, Rima's daughter Lara carries on the tradition, still using the same handwritten recipe book, still grinding spices fresh every morning.

Lara Khalil

Head Chef & Co-Owner
