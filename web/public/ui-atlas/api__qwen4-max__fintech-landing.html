Fair questions

The things couples ask us.

Do we both need the app?+
Yes — two logins, one pot. You each see everything shared in real time, and your personal accounts stay visible only to you. No more “can you screenshot the balance?” texts.
We earn very different amounts. Will it feel fair?+
That’s exactly what Honeypot was built for. Proportional splitting means you each contribute the same percentage of your income, so you each keep the same percentage for yourselves. And if you prefer a different arrangement, custom splits take one tap.
What if our income is irregular?+
Freelancers, servers, commission folks — welcome. Percentage-based top-ups mean the pot fills proportionally whenever money actually arrives, not on a rigid schedule that assumes a salaried life.
Is our financial data safe?+
Bank connections are read-only and encrypted with 256-bit AES. We never sell your data, never show you ads, and you can export or delete everything at any time. Our business model is $6 a month, not your spending habits.
We’re not married — is this still for us?+
Absolutely. Moving in together, engaged, dating-and-splitting-rent, or fifty years married — Honeypot works for any two people building a shared life. No ring required.
Can we hide purchases from each other?+
Fun money is nobody’s business but yours — that’s the point. Plus there’s a surprise-gift mode that quietly masks a purchase from the shared feed for up to 30 days. We protect romance as a core feature.
