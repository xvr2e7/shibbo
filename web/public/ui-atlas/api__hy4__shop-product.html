Unlike plastic or thin glass, dense ceramic holds a consistent temperature throughout the entire brew. Deep spiral ribs lift the paper filter away from the walls to prevent sticking and ensure even gas escape, while the oversized bottom hole rewards a steady, mindful pour.
Dishwasher safe, though we recommend gentle hand washing to preserve the matte glaze over time. Avoid abrupt temperature changes. Do not use on direct stovetop heat. Thermal Stability

Dense stoneware retains heat far better than glass or plastic, keeping your slurry at the optimal 200–205°F from bloom to drawdown.

Controlled Flow

Engineered ribbing prevents filter suction, while the single large aperture gives you complete command over your extraction time.
