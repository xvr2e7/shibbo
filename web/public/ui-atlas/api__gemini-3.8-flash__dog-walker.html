Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our keys, bad weather routines, and safety protocols.

How do you handle home keys and apartment access?
We offer two secure options: you can provide a lockbox code at your residence, or entrust us with a physical key. All keys are tagged with randomized internal numeric IDs (never your name or address) and locked inside a secure company key safe when not in active use.
What happens if it rains, snows, or is extremely hot?
We walk in all weather conditions! During severe storms or extreme heat/cold, walks are converted to quick relief breaks combined with indoor mental stimulation, puzzles, and indoor playtime so your pup stays safe and comfortable. We always wipe paws thoroughly upon return.
Are you fully insured and background-checked?
Yes, absolutely! Every single walker on our team is fully bonded, carries comprehensive Pet Care Insurance, and completes a thorough federal background check. Furthermore, our team is certified in Pet First Aid & CPR.
What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?
Life happens! You can cancel or reschedule any walk free of charge up to 7:00 PM the evening prior to the scheduled walk. Same-day cancellations within 3 hours of the appointment incur a nominal 50% fee.
Will my dog have the same walker every day?
Yes! Consistency builds trust. Your pup will be assigned a primary dedicated walker. If they are ever sick or on scheduled vacation, our vetted secondary backup walker will step in—and you will be informed well in advance.
