FAQ

Reasonable questions

Do you train models on my recordings?

No. Your audio and transcripts are never used to train any model, ours or a third party's. Processing happens in an isolated environment and raw audio is deleted on a schedule you control — down to immediately after processing.

Does everyone on the call know it's recording?

Yes. The Murmur bot announces itself on join, appears in the participant list, and the desktop app shows a persistent recording indicator. You can also enable region-specific verbal consent prompts.

What if the meeting is in person?

Open Murmur on iOS or Android and tap record. Multi-speaker diarization works on room audio too — we tested it in genuinely terrible cafés.

Which tools does it connect to?

Google Calendar, Outlook, Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack, Notion, Linear, Asana, Jira, HubSpot and Salesforce — plus a REST API and webhooks on Pro and above.

Can I cancel whenever?

Any time, from settings, no email required. You keep export access to everything you've recorded for 60 days after cancelling.
