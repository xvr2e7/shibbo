Recipes from Teta's Kitchen

Our story began in the cedar-scented mountains of Bsharri, gathered around a worn copper pot. When the Haddad family brought those handwritten recipes across the ocean, we brought the tradition of the mezze with us.

At Cedar & Olive, we don't just serve food; we invite you into our home. Every dish is a memory, the bread is always warm, and there is forever room for one more at the table.
