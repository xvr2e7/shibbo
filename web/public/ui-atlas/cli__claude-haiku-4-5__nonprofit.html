About GreenCity

Our Mission

GreenCity is dedicated to transforming urban neighborhoods by planting native trees that clean our air, reduce temperatures, and create beautiful spaces for our communities.

Since 2018, we've planted over 50,000 trees in 12 cities across North America, working with local communities and partners to ensure every tree thrives.

We believe that green spaces are not a luxury—they're essential infrastructure that improves health, reduces inequality, and builds stronger communities.
