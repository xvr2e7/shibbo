Why TEJO

A conference
that respects
your time.

We started TEJO because most conferences ask you to choose between six rooms and regret five of them. Here there's one stage, one shared experience, and a schedule curated for depth rather than volume — with long breaks built for actual conversations.

  • 40-minute talks, not 20-minute sprints
  • Every session captioned & recorded
  • Curated hallway track with matching app

Performance that ships

Real numbers from real products. We'll dig into rendering, budgets, and the boring wins that move Core Web Vitals more than any framework migration.

Design systems, honestly

Adoption, governance, tokens across platforms, and the politics of shipping a component library nobody asked for. Case studies, not manifestos.

Accessibility as craft

Beyond checklists: keyboard models, screen-reader patterns, inclusive motion, and how to make accessibility part of your design and QA loop.

“The web is the only platform we get to keep. TEJO is about treating it with the respect it deserves — and leaving Lisbon with things you can ship on Monday.”
— Inês Carvalho, Programme Director, TEJO

Email the team
Is the conference single-track?
Yes. Every talk happens on the main stage, so you never have to choose. Workshops run in parallel with the long lunch break on Day 1 and the afternoon block on Day 2 — Premium tickets include both.
Will talks be recorded?
Every session is recorded with live captions and published within three weeks. Ticket holders get permanent access; after that, talks go public on our channel and podcast feed.
What's the refund and transfer policy?
Full refunds until 11 April 2026. After that, you can transfer your ticket to a colleague at any time, free of charge, up to 48 hours before doors open. Just reply to your confirmation email.
How accessible is the venue?
LX Factory is step-free throughout, with accessible toilets on every floor, reserved seating near the stage, a quiet room, and live captions on the main screen. Tell us what you need in your registration form and we'll make it happen.
Do you offer diversity and student tickets?
We reserve 40 tickets at a reduced rate (€120) for people from under-represented groups in tech and for full-time students. Applications open on 2 February 2026 and close when the allocation is gone.
Is TEJO beginner-friendly?
Very. You don't need to be a framework specialist — talks are chosen for clear reasoning and practical takeaways rather than novelty. The hallway track is famously welcoming, and we run first-timer meetups on both mornings.
Can I bring a plus-one to the party?
Yes. Party add-ons are €35 and include food, drinks and the azulejo workshop. Note that the closing rooftop party on Day 2 is ticket holders only unless you've added a plus-one.
