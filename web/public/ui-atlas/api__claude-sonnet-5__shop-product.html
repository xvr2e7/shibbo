Care Instructions +

Crafted for a calmer morning

The Terra Dripper's steep walls and single large hole give you full control over your pour: slow down for a brighter, more floral cup, or pour steadily for a fuller body.

Unlike plastic or metal drippers, glazed stoneware holds heat evenly throughout the brew, keeping your water temperature more stable from first bloom to final drawdown.

Every piece is fired twice at high temperature, making it dense, chip-resistant, and safe for dishwashers, microwaves, and freezers.
